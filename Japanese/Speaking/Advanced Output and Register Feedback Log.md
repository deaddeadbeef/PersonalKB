# Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log

> Use this during [[Phase 5 — Advanced]] when you record yourself, test a phrase with a tutor or native speaker, or compare your output against a native model. This is for learner-output feedback; clip-quality problems still belong in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].

## How To Use This Log

Add one short entry whenever an output attempt reveals a register, keigo, pitch, rhythm, nuance, idiom, filler, or professional-phrasing issue worth carrying forward.

1. Name the situation and the source model.
2. Record what you tried to say.
3. Record the feedback or mismatch.
4. Write the corrected version or next test.
5. Link the entry back to the weekly review.

Do not wait for perfect feedback. A self-recording compared against a replayable native model is useful. Human or tutor feedback is required when the issue is social direction, keigo, business tone, humor, or implication.

## Register Targets

| Target | What To Log | Evidence Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keigo direction | Whether the respected person, speaker, or in-group/out-group relation was handled correctly | Tutor/native feedback or a trusted formal model |
| Business phrasing | Whether the phrase is polite, natural, and not over- or under-formal | Workplace audio, business course audio, tutor/native check |
| Idioms and proverbs | Whether the expression fits the situation and tone | Native example, tutor explanation, or transcripted use |

## Return-To-Practice Rule

An output pattern can return to normal practice when:

- The corrected version has a clear source or feedback route.
- The issue is not only "sounds bad" but named: pitch, rhythm, register, keigo direction, wording, filler timing, idiom use, or nuance.
- You have repeated the corrected pattern aloud after the feedback.
- If a local clip itself is suspect, it is recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] instead of only here.
- The next [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] names how the fix was reused.
