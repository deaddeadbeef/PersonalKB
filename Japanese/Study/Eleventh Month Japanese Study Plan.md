# Eleventh Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the first month of [[Phase 5 — Advanced]]. The job is to make native-source work active and accountable: every week needs one exact news, podcast, lecture, interview, documentary, drama, anime, or song segment with transcript, subtitles, lyrics, article text, show notes, lesson context, or tutor notes; one Block 1 local precision drill set; one pronunciation, rhythm, source-label, subtitle/transcript, or summary-accuracy check; and one summary, transcription, shadowing, or mining proof without English as the main scaffold.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 11 execution path. It follows [[Tenth Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 1 of [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Tenth Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Ten Success Criteria|Month 10 success criteria]] and the [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery#Phase 4 Checkpoint|Phase 4 checkpoint]] are real. If native-speed Phase 4 listening still becomes passive background, repeat the relevant Week 41-44 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 45

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 1 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one exact native-source segment from [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]] with transcript, subtitles, lyrics, article text, show notes, lesson context, or tutor notes.
3. Open [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 1, [[Phase 5 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]], [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]], [[Advanced Listening Resources]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
4. Decide the first proof before the second study day: summarize, transcribe, shadow, or mine one short section.
5. Name one checking route for the week: subtitle/transcript alignment, rhythm, reading, pitch, source-label meaning, or summary accuracy.

Default route: use a short news item, podcast excerpt, interview answer, documentary paragraph, drama scene, anime scene with Japanese subtitles, or song lyric section. Keep the first segment short enough to replay on at least three days.

## Month-Eleven Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 1 assignment | Keep the month on Phase 5 Block 1 until native-source proof is real |
| Segment before volume | Choose one exact segment before adding optional immersion |
| Text support is required | Use transcript, subtitles, lyrics, article text, show notes, lesson context, or tutor notes |
| Local clips are precision drills | Use [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 1 for source labels, media titles, lyrics labels, and register warnings |
| Native source is the authority | Local clips support source work; they never prove advanced listening |
| Output proof is visible | End each week with a summary, transcription, shadowing target, or mined sentence |
| Feedback route is named | Use [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] when a summary, recording, or mined sentence exposes a fix to carry forward |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the native source or a reference route, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the native source.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Native source | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 45 | [[Advanced Listening Resources]] and source selection | Block 1 source-label clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] | One news, podcast, lecture, interview, documentary, or article/audio segment with text support | Name the source and summarize one short section without English as the main scaffold |
| 46 | [[NHK World — News Listening Practice]] or a comparable news/article audio pair | Block 1 news/source clips plus page-level examples | One news item with article text, captions, transcript, or notes | Transcribe or shadow 20-60 seconds and check subtitle/transcript alignment |
| 47 | [[Anime and Drama — Immersion Listening]] or another scene-based source | Block 1 anime/drama/register-warning clips plus page-level examples | One drama/anime/native scene with Japanese subtitles or notes | Identify one line that should not be copied into your own speech and explain why |
| 48 | [[Music — Learning Through Japanese Songs]] or Block 1 consolidation | Block 1 music/lyrics clips or weak source-label clips | One lyric section, podcast excerpt, news item, or scene repeated from earlier in the month | Mine three useful lines or summarize the segment and choose the next Phase 5 block target |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Month 11 is about building the Phase 5 native-source habit, not collecting many sources.

## Week 45

Goal: choose one stable native source and make the first segment accountable.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Advanced Listening Resources]].
3. Use the Block 1 source-label clips in [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native-source segment with text support.
5. Summarize one short section without English as the main scaffold.

Minimum proof: one exact native source, one exact segment, and one short summary.

If you cannot name the segment, the week has become passive immersion. Restart with a shorter source.

## Week 46

Goal: use news or article/audio material to connect speed, text, and summary accuracy.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[NHK World — News Listening Practice]] or a comparable text-supported news/audio source.
3. Use Block 1 news/source clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay one news item or article/audio pair with text visible.
5. Transcribe or shadow 20-60 seconds and check where the text support confirms or corrects your hearing.

Minimum proof: one 20-60 second transcription or shadowing target with the checking route named.

If full-speed news is too much, reduce the segment to one sentence and keep the source stable.

## Week 47

Goal: use scenes as listening material without copying risky speech into your own output.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Anime and Drama — Immersion Listening]].
3. Use Block 1 anime/drama/register-warning clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay one short scene with Japanese subtitles, transcript, or notes.
5. Identify one line that is useful for listening but risky for production, then explain the character, setting, register, or gender/style reason.

Minimum proof: one scene line classified as safe-to-mine, recognition-only, or do-not-copy.

If you cannot explain the register, do not mine the line for output.

## Week 48

Goal: consolidate Phase 5 Block 1 by mining, summarizing, or shadowing from one native source without switching tools.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Music — Learning Through Japanese Songs]] or repeat the weakest Block 1 source page.
3. Repeat only the weakest Block 1 clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same lyric section, podcast excerpt, news item, or scene.
5. Mine three useful lines, summarize the segment, or shadow one section, then choose the next Phase 5 block target.

Minimum proof: one mined-line set, summary, or shadowing target plus the next block decision.

If songs distort normal speech rhythm, keep them for lyric mining and use a spoken source for pronunciation proof.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 1 assignment from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Add an [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] entry if a recording, summary, mined sentence, or register warning produced a carry-forward fix.
5. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
6. Name the exact next native-source segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 2 because several native sources were sampled once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 1 source page
- 1-3 Block 1 local clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named native source
- 1 summary sentence, transcription fragment, shadowed line, or mined line

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not count passive immersion as Phase 5 proof.
- Do not switch native sources every day.
- Do not let local clips replace the native-source segment.
- Do not mine anime, drama, or songs into production without register/context checks.
- Do not advance to Block 2 without [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Eleven Success Criteria

Month eleven counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 1 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 1 native-source segment or segments;
- Block 1 local clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one source-label, subtitle/transcript, rhythm, reading, pronunciation, register-warning, or summary-accuracy issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one native source and exact segment can be named without hunting;
- one short native segment can be summarized without English as the main scaffold;
- one 20-60 second section can be shadowed or transcribed with text support;
- one scene, lyric, or media line can be classified as safe-to-mine, recognition-only, or do-not-copy;
- the next Phase 5 block target is chosen from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue to Block 2 in [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]]. Block 2 moves into professional register, full keigo, workplace/service audio, and output logged in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]].

Before starting Block 2, choose one workplace, service, interview, formal speech, business course, tutor/native role-play, or recorded real interaction with a clear role and feedback route.
