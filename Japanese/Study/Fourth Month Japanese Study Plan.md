# Fourth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the first month of [[Phase 3 — Expansion]]. The job is to enter N4 grammar without turning it into silent pattern study: every week needs one authentic segment, one Block 1 local drill set, one source-backed pronunciation or rhythm check, and one spoken proof target.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 4 execution path. It starts Phase 3 after [[Third Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 1 of [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Phase 2 Weekly Review#Phase 2 Exit Check]] is real. If Phase 2 output is still shaky, repeat the relevant Week 9-12 work in [[Third Month Japanese Study Plan]] before starting this page.

## Before Week 13

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 3 — Expansion]].
2. Open [[Phase 3 Authentic Audio Spine]] and choose one primary source with transcript, lesson text, subtitles, or tutor context.
3. Open [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 1 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
4. Keep [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 3 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] available.
5. Name the exact Block 1 authentic segment before the second study day.

Default route: use Genki II, Irodori A2/B1, a graded N4 lesson, or a tutor/native recording where the target grammar appears in full sentences.

## Month-Four Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 1 assignment | Keep the month on Block 1 until the evidence is real |
| Full sentence before pattern label | Hear the grammar in a sentence before memorizing the English explanation |
| Local clips are controlled drills | Use [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] Block 1 to isolate forms and direction |
| Authentic audio is the model | Compare rhythm and pronunciation against course, tutor, or native audio |
| One check per week | Check one rhythm, reading, action direction, or suspect-clip issue through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | Te-form review plus [[N4 Grammar — Conditional Forms]] | Block 1 te-form and conditional clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] | Dialogue or lesson segment with one visible conditional pattern | Say two conditional sentences without reading |
| 14 | [[N4 Grammar — Passive and Causative]] | Block 1 passive/causative clips plus page-level examples | Course/tutor segment where affectedness or make/let meaning is clear | Say one passive and one causative sentence, then identify who acts and who is affected |
| 15 | [[N4 Grammar — Giving and Receiving]] | Block 1 giving/receiving clips plus page-level examples | Dialogue with あげる, もらう, or くれる in context | Say two social-direction sentences and explain the direction aloud |
| 16 | Block 1 consolidation | Repeat only weak Block 1 clips | Same authentic segment or a shorter related segment | Say three target-pattern sentences without reading and fill [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Phase 3 blocks can take more than one week.

## Week 13

Goal: connect te-form review to conditional sentence rhythm.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Review one te-form pattern or one conditional pattern.
3. Use Block 1 te-form and conditional clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same authentic segment with the target pattern visible in text.
5. Compare one clause ending or sentence rhythm feature.

Minimum proof: say two conditional sentences without reading and name the authentic model used.

If conditionals feel like four English labels, shorten the target to one pattern and repeat it across more days.

## Week 14

Goal: hear action direction and affectedness before producing passive and causative forms.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[N4 Grammar — Passive and Causative]].
3. Use Block 1 passive/causative clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a course, tutor, or native segment where the affected person or make/let meaning is clear.
5. Compare who acts, who is affected, and how the verb ending carries that direction.

Minimum proof: say one passive and one causative sentence, then explain the action direction aloud.

If the forms are readable but not speakable, stop adding new forms and repeat fewer examples.

## Week 15

Goal: make giving and receiving grammar social-direction grammar, not vocabulary trivia.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one section from [[N4 Grammar — Giving and Receiving]].
3. Use Block 1 giving/receiving clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a dialogue where あげる, もらう, or くれる appears in context.
5. Compare the social direction and the sentence rhythm.

Minimum proof: say two social-direction sentences and explain who benefits or receives help.

If the direction is fuzzy, return to short sentence pairs and keep the same authentic segment.

## Week 16

Goal: consolidate Block 1 before moving to longer N4 sentences.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Repeat only the weakest Block 1 local clips.
3. Replay the same authentic segment or one shorter related segment.
4. Produce one conditional, one passive/causative, and one giving/receiving sentence.
5. Fill [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] and decide whether Block 1 repeats.

Minimum proof: say three target-pattern sentences without reading and record the exact authentic segment, local clips, and check target.

If any proof item is missing, repeat Block 1 with a shorter authentic segment.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 1 assignment from [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next authentic segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 2 because the pages were read once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current N4 grammar page
- 1-3 Block 1 local clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named authentic segment
- 1 spoken target-pattern sentence from memory

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not memorize N4 grammar labels before hearing full sentences.
- Do not move to passive/causative until at least one conditional pattern is speakable.
- Do not treat local TTS as the authority for rhythm or connected speech.
- Do not add random native media before the weekly authentic segment is stable.
- Do not advance to Block 2 without [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Four Success Criteria

Month four counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 1 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 1 authentic segment or segments;
- Block 1 local clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one rhythm, reading, action-direction, or suspect-clip issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- two conditional sentences can be said without reading;
- one passive and one causative sentence can be produced with action direction explained;
- two giving/receiving sentences can be produced with social direction explained;
- three Block 1 target-pattern sentences can be said without reading.

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue with [[Fifth Month Japanese Study Plan]]. It wraps Block 2 in [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and moves into longer N4 sentences, potential/volitional forms, and Core 1000 output.

Before starting Block 2, open [[Phase 3 Audio Coverage Map]] and choose the exact graded or transcripted authentic segment that will anchor longer sentence practice.
