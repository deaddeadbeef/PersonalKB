# Japanese Learning Dashboard

> Start here when you sit down to study Japanese. This page turns the vault into an operating system: what to learn next, what to ignore for now, and how to tell whether the week worked.

## Current Strategy

The goal is systematic Japanese learning, not browsing every interesting Japanese resource at once. Use one course spine, one review system, one daily input habit, and this vault as the map.

For the full content order, use [[Japanese#Start Here]]. The dashboard is for daily execution; the Japanese hub is the table of contents for the whole learning sequence.

## Current Stage

**Active phase:** [[Phase 1 — Foundation]]

Stay here until the checkpoint is real:

- Read hiragana without sounding out each symbol.
- Read katakana slowly but correctly.
- Know the first 100 survival words well enough to recognize them in short sentences.
- Produce a basic self-introduction from memory.
- Maintain a daily review habit for at least 10 consecutive days.

Do not spend serious time on N3 grammar, keigo, business Japanese, native news, or advanced kanji yet. They stay visible in the vault for later, but they are not part of the current work queue.

## Daily Loop

### Minimum Day: 30 Minutes

| Time | Action | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | Reviews | Finish due Anki/WaniKani/Bunpro items before adding new ones |
| 15 min | Course spine | One textbook or Irodori lesson section; write answers, do not just read |
| 5 min | Audio | Use [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] and one segment from [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]] |

### Good Day: 60 Minutes

| Time | Action | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 min | Reviews | Keep the pile small enough that tomorrow is possible |
| 20 min | Course spine | New grammar or exercises |
| 10 min | Vault review | Read one linked vault note and write 2-3 recall questions |
| 15 min | Listening or shadowing | Repeat clips from [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] aloud; prioritize sound over speed |

### Weekly Review: 20 Minutes

Every Sunday, open [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] and record:

- What did I finish?
- What is still fuzzy?
- Which cards are failing repeatedly?
- Did I hear or read Japanese every day?
- What is the one next lesson for the coming week?

## Progress Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Kana complete | Read all hiragana and katakana rows from memory, including dakuten and small や/ゆ/よ combinations |
| First grammar | Explain は, が, を, に, で with one example each |
| First output | Say a 30-second self-introduction without reading |
| First listening habit | 10 days with at least 5 minutes of beginner Japanese audio, including [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] and [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]] |
| Ready for Phase 2 | All [[Phase 1 — Foundation|Phase 1 checkpoint]] items are true |
