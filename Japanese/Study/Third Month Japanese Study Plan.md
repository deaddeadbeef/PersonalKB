# Third Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for Weeks 9-12 of Japanese study. The job is to finish [[Phase 2 — Building Blocks]] with kanji attached to spoken words, pronunciation checked against real models, and beginner interactions practiced as heard Japanese instead of memorized English prompts.

## Purpose

This page is the month-three execution path. It completes Phase 2 after [[Second Month Japanese Study Plan]].

Use it after the Week 5-8 success criteria are true. If particles, forms, counters, or daily-life vocabulary are still not speakable, repeat the relevant week in [[Second Month Japanese Study Plan]] before starting Week 9.

## Before Week 9

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Week 9 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Open [[Phase 2 Audio Coverage Map]] and confirm which kanji, pronunciation, and conversation pages you will use.
3. Keep [[Phase 2 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] available.
4. Choose the exact course, NHK, tutor, or native segment for Week 9 before the second study day.
5. Keep the same source route from Month 2 unless [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]] shows it is not working.

Do not start isolated kanji memorization before choosing the spoken words and authentic audio that will anchor them.

## Month-Three Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Spoken word first | Kanji work starts from words you can hear and say |
| One weekly Phase 2 assignment | Copy only the current week from [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]] |
| One authentic segment | Name the exact course, NHK, tutor, or native segment before drilling |
| Local clips are controlled drills | Use [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]] to isolate kanji words, difficult sounds, scripts, and polite service lines |
| Authentic audio is the model | Compare against official/course/native audio through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Week 9

Goal: understand kanji as a writing system without disconnecting characters from sound.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Kanji — How Kanji Work]], [[Radicals — The Building Blocks]], or [[Kanji Learning Strategies]].
3. Use the Weeks 9-10 kanji-as-spoken-words block in [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay course or native vocabulary audio for words you already know how to say.
5. Compare one word reading only: do not add isolated character readings yet.

Minimum proof: explain five kanji-linked words and say them as words without losing pronunciation.

If kanji recognition becomes detached from sound, stop adding characters and return to spoken vocabulary.

## Week 10

Goal: read the first N5 kanji set as words, not isolated symbols.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study a small chunk of [[Kanji N5 Essentials]].
3. Use the Weeks 9-10 first N5 kanji-word block in [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay native/course examples containing the target words.
5. Check one uncertain reading through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] before adding it to review.

Minimum proof: read and say a small N5 kanji vocabulary set in words, with any uncertain readings checked against a real model.

If readings feel arbitrary, reduce the kanji set and keep every item inside a word or sentence.

## Week 11

Goal: fix high-impact sounds while turning daily scripts into speakable Japanese.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one section from [[Pronunciation — Difficult Sounds for English Speakers]] or [[Conversation Patterns — Daily Interactions]].
3. Use the Weeks 11-12 interaction block in [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]] plus one sound-focused page clip.
4. Replay one repeatable daily-interaction dialogue.
5. Compare one feature only: ら行, ん, long vowels, small っ, devoicing, or sentence rhythm.

Minimum proof: shadow one daily script without reading and log the one pronunciation feature that changed after listening to the authentic model.

If the script is memorized but sounds unnatural, keep the same dialogue and shorten the shadowing segment.

## Week 12

Goal: make polite shop and restaurant language usable without overproducing keigo.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study [[Conversation Patterns — Shopping and Restaurants]], [[Keigo — Teineigo (Polite)]], [[Social Register — When to Use What]], or [[Shadowing — Technique and Practice Guide]].
3. Use the Weeks 11-12 shopping and polite-service block in [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a service, shop, restaurant, or polite dialogue from the current spine.
5. Role-play the same situation aloud without reading.

Minimum proof: complete a short polite shop or restaurant role-play and name the authentic model that shaped the rhythm.

If politeness feels like memorized labels, stay with です/ます and one service exchange. Recognition comes before advanced keigo production.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Copy the next week from [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]] into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next authentic segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. If the evidence is missing, repeat the same week with a shorter authentic segment.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current kanji, pronunciation, or interaction page
- 1 local clip or clip set from the current Phase 2 week
- 1 sentence or phrase from the named authentic segment
- 1 spoken word, sentence, or role-play line from memory

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not learn kanji as isolated English keywords with no spoken word.
- Do not memorize onyomi and kunyomi lists before hearing useful words.
- Do not treat local clips as the final model for ら行, ん, small っ, devoicing, register, or service dialogue.
- Do not turn polite language into advanced keigo study yet.
- Do not move to Phase 3 just because the pages were read silently.

## Month-Three Success Criteria

Month three counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]] has evidence for Weeks 9-12;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names one authentic segment per week;
- Week 9-12 local clip sets from [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]] were used;
- at least one pronunciation, rhythm, kanji-reading, or register feature was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- five kanji-linked words can be explained and pronounced as words;
- a small N5 kanji vocabulary set can be read and said aloud;
- one daily script can be shadowed without reading;
- one polite shop or restaurant role-play can be completed with natural です/ます timing.

## Phase 2 Exit

After these success criteria are true, run the full [[Phase 2 Weekly Review#Phase 2 Exit Check]].

Do not move to [[Phase 3 — Expansion]] until the Phase 2 exit check is real. Before starting Phase 3 content, open [[Fourth Month Japanese Study Plan]], [[Phase 3 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]], [[Phase 3 Audio Coverage Map]], and [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]].
