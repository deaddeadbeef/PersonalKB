# Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder

> Use this during [[Phase 3 — Expansion]]. The job is to keep N4 grammar, 1000-word listening, pitch awareness, media input, and register recognition tied to one replayable authentic segment, one local block, one source-checked pronunciation or reading issue, and one proof row.

## How To Use

1. Start Phase 3 only after the [[Phase 2 — Building Blocks|Phase 2 checkpoint]] is real.
2. Choose one primary source from [[Phase 3 Authentic Audio Spine]] and one pronunciation or pitch-reference route.
3. At the start of each week, copy the current block assignment into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
4. Run [[Daily Audio Loop]] on each study day.
5. At the end of the week, fill [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] and add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].

Phase 3 blocks can take more than one week. Do not advance because the block was read once. Advance when the authentic segment, local block, pitch or reading check, and output proof are all real.

## Advancement Rule

Move to the next block only when the current block has real evidence:

- the authentic source and exact segment are named;
- the authentic segment was replayed on at least three days;
- the local block was repeated on at least three days;
- one short segment was shadowed, summarized, transcribed, or mined for sentences;
- one pitch, accent, kanji-reading, rhythm, media, or register issue was checked through a source-backed route;
- normal proof was recorded in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]];
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before returning to daily practice.

If any item is missing, repeat the same block next week with a shorter authentic segment.

## Block 4 Safety Rule

Block 4 is where local clips are easiest to overtrust. Treat local pitch, media, and register clips as prompts only.

| If the target is... | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Pitch or accent | [[Phase 3 Pitch Accent Practice Path]] plus OJAD, NHK, Forvo, course audio, tutor feedback, or native audio |
| Media rhythm | The exact transcripted or subtitled segment you are shadowing |
| Keigo or register recognition | Tutor/native/course dialogue or a clearly contextualized source |
| Local clip quality | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] and [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] |
