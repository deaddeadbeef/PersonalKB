# JLPT Overview — N5 to N1
The Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) is the standard benchmark for Japanese ability. Five levels, N5 (easiest) to N1 (hardest).
## Level Breakdown
### N5 — Basic Japanese
- **Kanji:** ~100 | **Vocabulary:** ~800 | **Grammar:** ~40 points
- **Can do:** Read simple sentences, understand slow spoken Japanese on familiar topics
- **Study time:** ~150 hours from zero
- **Key resources:** Genki I (Ch. 1-12), Tae Kim basics
### N4 — Elementary Japanese
- **Kanji:** ~300 | **Vocabulary:** ~1,500 | **Grammar:** ~60 points
- **Can do:** Read everyday texts, follow conversations at natural speed on daily topics
- **Study time:** ~300 hours total
- **Key resources:** Genki II, Tobira prep
### N3 — Intermediate Japanese
- **Kanji:** ~650 | **Vocabulary:** ~3,750 | **Grammar:** ~90 points
- **Can do:** Read newspaper headlines, follow most conversations, express opinions
- **Study time:** ~500 hours total
- **Key resources:** Tobira, Kanzen Master N3
### N2 — Upper Intermediate
- **Kanji:** ~1,000 | **Vocabulary:** ~6,000 | **Grammar:** ~170 points
- **Can do:** Read novels and newspapers, follow lectures, function in a Japanese workplace
- **Study time:** ~800 hours total
- **Milestone:** Required for most Japanese companies hiring foreigners
### N1 — Advanced
- **Kanji:** ~2,000 | **Vocabulary:** ~10,000 | **Grammar:** ~200+ points
- **Can do:** Near-native reading, understand nuance and implied meaning, academic Japanese
- **Study time:** ~1,200+ hours total
- **Milestone:** Required for graduate school in Japan, some government positions
## Test Format

| Section | N5/N4 | N3/N2/N1 |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Vocabulary & Kanji | ✓ | ✓ |
| Grammar & Reading | ✓ | ✓ |
| Listening | ✓ | ✓ |
| Time | 80-105 min | 105-170 min |
## Test Schedule
- **July** and **December** each year (some locations December only)
- Registration opens ~3 months before
