# Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder

> Use this for Weeks 5-12 of [[Phase 2 — Building Blocks]]. The job is to keep N5 mechanics tied to one authentic segment, one local drill set, one pronunciation or reading check, and one weekly proof row.

## How To Use

1. Start Phase 2 only after the [[Phase 1 — Foundation|Phase 1 checkpoint]] is real.
2. Keep the same primary authentic source from [[Phase 2 Authentic Audio Spine]] unless the weekly review shows it is not working.
3. At the start of each week, copy the matching assignment row into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
4. Run [[Daily Audio Loop]] on each study day.
5. At the end of the week, fill [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]] and add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].

Do not turn Phase 2 into silent grammar reading. Every week needs heard Japanese, spoken repetition, and one checked detail.

## Advancement Rule

Move to the next week only when the current row has real evidence:

- the authentic source and exact segment are named;
- the authentic segment was replayed on at least three days;
- the local clip set was repeated on at least three days;
- one short segment was shadowed or produced aloud;
- one pronunciation, rhythm, counter, kanji-reading, pitch, or register feature was checked;
- normal proof was recorded in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]];
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].

If any item is missing, repeat the same assignment next week with a shorter authentic segment.

## Common Adjustments

| Problem | Adjustment |
| --- | --- |
| The authentic segment feels too fast | Keep the source, shorten the exact segment, and repeat it more days |
| The course lesson does not perfectly match the vault page | Use the closest overlap, then let the local clips isolate the missing detail |
| A counter or kanji reading sounds uncertain | Check [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] before memorizing it |
| Local clip and native/official audio disagree | Treat the local clip as suspect until checked or replaced |
| Week 11 or 12 feels like memorized lines only | Add one tutor/native model or official dialogue before treating the role-play as proof |
