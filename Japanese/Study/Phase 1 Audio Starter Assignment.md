# Phase 1 Audio Starter Assignment

> Use this for the first week of [[Phase 1 — Foundation]]. It turns the audio system into a concrete assignment before you have enough Japanese to choose segments confidently.

Use [[First Week Japanese Study Plan]] for the day-by-day execution. After Week 1, continue with [[First Month Japanese Study Plan]] and [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]] for Weeks 2-4.

## Pick One Source

Choose one row and ignore the other two for the first week.

| If you are using... | Week 1 authentic target | Local target | Comparison target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Genki I | First lesson audio from GENKI official audio or OTO Navi | Week 1 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | Vowels, long vowels, は/へ/を, and small っ |
| Irodori A1 | First Starter/A1 lesson task audio from Irodori official audio or online course | Week 1 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | Vowels, long vowels, greetings, and polite timing |
| No course chosen yet | First beginner lesson from NHK WORLD-JAPAN Easy Japanese | Week 1 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | Greeting rhythm, vowel length, and one sentence you can shadow |

Default choice if you have not picked a course: use NHK for one week, then decide in [[Resources Index — Textbooks, Apps, and Tools]].

## Seven-Day Starter Loop

Use [[Daily Audio Loop]] every day. Keep the assignment small enough that you can repeat it.

| Day | Local drill | Authentic model | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Play the first Week 1 local clip set | Pick and play the first authentic segment | Fill the current assignment table |
| 2 | Repeat vowels and particle traps | Replay the same authentic segment with text/context | Shadow one short phrase |
| 3 | Repeat long-vowel and small-っ clips | Replay the same segment blind first | Compare one timing feature |
| 4 | Repeat the hardest local clip only | Replay the same authentic phrase or sentence | Say it once without reading |
| 5 | Repeat Week 1 local clip set | Replay the same segment or the next tiny segment | Record one fuzzy sound |
| 6 | Repeat only the clips that still feel weak | Replay the authentic model and shadow | Decide whether any local clip is suspect |
| 7 | Quick local review | Replay the week's authentic segment | Fill [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

Do not add more audio sources during this week. If the segment is too hard, shorten it instead of switching resources.

## Success Criteria

The first week counts if all of these are true:

- one authentic source was used across at least three days;
- one local Week 1 clip set was repeated across at least three days;
- one phrase or sentence was shadowed aloud;
- one pronunciation feature was compared;
- one row was added to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]];
- any suspect local clip was routed to [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
