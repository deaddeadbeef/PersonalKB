# First Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the first four weeks of Japanese study. The job is to finish [[Phase 1 — Foundation]] with a stable routine, one course/source route, one local audio ladder, one authentic audio model per week, and visible proof that pronunciation is being checked against human or official audio.

## Purpose

This page is the month-one execution path. It keeps you from browsing the whole vault after the first week.

Use [[First Week Japanese Study Plan]] for Days 1-7. Then return here for Weeks 2-4 and use [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]] to copy the current week's assignment into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].

## Month-One Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One course/source route | Use Genki, Irodori, NHK, or another chosen beginner source consistently enough to compare pronunciation |
| One weekly assignment | Copy only the current week from [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]] |
| One named authentic segment | Name the exact lesson, skit, timestamp, tutor recording, or native model before drilling |
| Local clips are drills | Use [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] for repetition, not as the final pronunciation authority |
| Authentic audio is the model | Compare against official/course/native audio through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip sounds wrong, stop drilling it and route it to [[Pronunciation Correction Log]]. Do not keep repeating a suspect clip because it is already in the vault.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[First Week Japanese Study Plan]], [[Hiragana Complete Guide]], [[Writing Systems Overview]] | Week 1 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | First Genki, Irodori, NHK, tutor, or native beginner segment | One greeting or short phrase shadowed aloud |
| 2 | [[Katakana Complete Guide]], [[Writing Systems Overview]] | Week 2 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | Next segment from the same source, preferably with katakana, classroom, travel, or request words | Three katakana words said with Japanese timing |

## Week 1

Do not improvise this week. Use [[First Week Japanese Study Plan]] day by day.

By the end of Week 1:

- one authentic source is chosen;
- the exact first segment is named;
- Week 1 local clips have been repeated on at least three days;
- one phrase has been shadowed aloud;
- one pronunciation feature has been compared;
- [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] and [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] both have evidence.

## Week 2

Goal: make katakana usable without losing hiragana.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Read or drill one katakana row.
3. Use Week 2 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the next authentic segment from the same source.
5. Compare loanword timing, long vowels, or clipped English habits.

Minimum proof: say three katakana words with Japanese timing and write the exact authentic model used.

If katakana is still too slow, repeat Week 2 instead of moving to grammar-heavy work.

## Week 3

Goal: connect survival words to the first sentence frames.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small chunk from [[Core 100 — Survival Japanese]] or [[N5 Grammar — Sentence Patterns]].
3. Use Week 3 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a short dialogue, request, or survival-phrase segment.
5. Compare です/ます rhythm, particles, or polite request timing.

Minimum proof: shadow five survival phrases aloud and identify one rhythm or particle feature that changed after listening to the authentic model.

If the phrases are not speakable yet, keep the same source and shorten the segment.

## Week 4

Goal: produce the first self-introduction from memory.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Read [[Conversation Patterns — Greetings and Introductions]] or [[Self-Introduction Template]].
3. Use Week 4 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay an introduction, greeting, tutor, or native model segment.
5. Practice the same self-introduction aloud.

Minimum proof: say a 20-30 second self-introduction from memory and record which authentic model shaped the timing.

Do not expand the script until the short version is smooth.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Copy the next week from [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]] into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next authentic segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. If the evidence is missing, repeat the same week with a shorter authentic segment.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes kana, survival phrase, or sentence reading
- 1 local clip or clip set from the current week
- 1 sentence or phrase from the named authentic segment
- 1 short note only if the target changed or a clip sounds suspect

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## Source-Switch Rule

Stay with the same primary source for the whole month unless it is unusable.

Switch only when one of these is true:

- the audio is unavailable or unreliable;
- the lesson sequence is clearly too hard after shortening the segment;
- the source does not provide enough context to understand what you are repeating;
- a tutor or native recording becomes the better stable reference.

When you switch, write the reason in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] so the month still has a trace.

## Month-One Success Criteria

Phase 1 month one counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] has evidence for Weeks 1-4;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names one authentic segment per week;
- Week 1-4 local clip sets from [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] were used;
- at least one pronunciation or rhythm feature was compared each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- kana can be read without constant chart lookup;
- the 20-30 second self-introduction can be said from memory.
