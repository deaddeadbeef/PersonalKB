# Seventh Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the first month of [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]]. The job is to make N3 grammar audible in native-speed or official-course Japanese: every week needs one repeatable transcripted segment, one Block 1 local drill set, one rhythm or pronunciation check, and one shadowing or sentence-chain proof.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 7 execution path. It follows [[Phase 3 Block 4 Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 1 of [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Phase 3 Block 4 Japanese Study Plan#Block-Four Success Criteria|Phase 3 Block 4 success criteria]] are real. If pitch checks, media/register recognition, or the Phase 3 exit check are still incomplete, repeat the relevant Week 25-28 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 29

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 1 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one exact native-speed, advanced course, tutor, or native-speaker segment with transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson text, or tutor-provided notes.
3. Open [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 1, [[Phase 4 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
4. Name the first multi-clause sentence you will shadow before the second study day.
5. Pick one formal expression, clause link, quotation pattern, or older/newer grammar contrast to check through a source-backed route before the end of Week 31.

Default route: use an intermediate/advanced course dialogue, Irodori B1-style audio, a transcripted podcast or news segment, a tutor/native recording, or a short native segment where longer clauses, reporting patterns, or formal expressions appear in context.

## Month-Seven Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 1 assignment | Keep the month on Phase 4 Block 1 until N3 grammar is hearable in real speech |
| Transcript before speed | Use text support so particles, clause endings, and quotation markers can be checked |
| Local clips isolate grammar | Use [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 1 for formal expressions, conjunctions, nominalization, quotation, and level contrasts |
| Native-speed audio is the model | Compare rhythm and phrasing against course, tutor, or native audio |
| Sentence chain is the output | End each week with one shadowed multi-clause sentence or one produced N3 sentence chain |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 29 | [[N3 Grammar — Formal Expressions]] | Block 1 formal-expression clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] plus page-level examples | Transcripted speech, course audio, tutor recording, or formal segment with the target expression visible | Shadow one formal-expression sentence and explain where the formal rhythm differs from casual speech |
| 30 | [[N3 Grammar — Complex Conjunctions]] | Block 1 complex-conjunction clips plus page-level examples | Longer clause-chain segment with reason, contrast, addition, sequence, or concession | Shadow one multi-clause sentence without losing particles |
| 31 | [[N3 Grammar — Nominalization and Quotation]] | Block 1 nominalization and quotation clips plus page-level examples | Interview, narration, lesson, or tutor segment with こと, の, と, という, or と思う | Produce one reported-speech or nominalized sentence chain |
| 32 | [[Grammar — Comparison Across Levels]] and Block 1 consolidation | Repeat only weak Block 1 clips | Same native-speed segment or one shorter related segment | Shadow one multi-clause sentence and produce one N3 sentence chain |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Month 7 is about hearing N3 grammar inside real sentence rhythm, not reading every pattern once.

## Week 29

Goal: hear formal expressions as rhythm and register, not just vocabulary.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[N3 Grammar — Formal Expressions]].
3. Use the Block 1 formal-expression clips in [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same transcripted formal, course, tutor, or native segment with text visible.
5. Shadow one formal-expression sentence and mark the clause ending or formal phrase.

Minimum proof: shadow one formal-expression sentence and explain where the rhythm or register differs from a casual version.

If formal expressions become silent reading, stop adding patterns and shadow one sentence repeatedly.

## Week 30

Goal: keep particles and clause endings intact as N3 conjunctions make sentences longer.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[N3 Grammar — Complex Conjunctions]].
3. Use Block 1 complex-conjunction clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a transcripted segment with reason, contrast, addition, sequence, or concession.
5. Shadow one multi-clause sentence and say the connector separately afterward.

Minimum proof: shadow one multi-clause sentence without losing particles or clause endings.

If the sentence is too long, reduce it to one connector and one following clause.

## Week 31

Goal: make nominalization and quotation audible enough to report, explain, or summarize.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[N3 Grammar — Nominalization and Quotation]].
3. Use Block 1 nominalization and quotation clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay an interview, narration, lesson, tutor, or native segment where reported speech or nominalization is visible in text.
5. Produce one sentence using こと, の, と, という, or と思う.

Minimum proof: produce one reported-speech or nominalized sentence chain and name the authentic model used.

If quotation timing blurs, compare only direct quotation and indirect quotation for the day.

## Week 32

Goal: consolidate Phase 4 Block 1 by comparing older grammar with N3 forms and producing one controlled sentence chain.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Grammar — Comparison Across Levels]].
3. Repeat only the weakest Block 1 clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native-speed segment or one shorter related segment.
5. Shadow one multi-clause sentence, then produce one N3 sentence chain from memory.

Minimum proof: shadow one multi-clause sentence and produce one N3 sentence chain without reading.

If production collapses, keep the same segment and reduce the proof to one connector plus one quotation or nominalization pattern.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 1 assignment from [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next native-speed or official-course segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 2 because the pages were read once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 1 N3 grammar page
- 1-3 Block 1 local clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named native-speed or official-course segment
- 1 shadowed line or one N3 sentence-chain fragment from memory

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not turn N3 grammar into silent reading.
- Do not let local clips replace the native-speed or official-course model.
- Do not choose a segment without text support for this first Phase 4 month.
- Do not add more formal patterns before one sentence can be shadowed.
- Do not advance to Block 2 without [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Seven Success Criteria

Month seven counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 1 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 1 native-speed or official-course segment or segments;
- Block 1 local clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one clause rhythm, quotation timing, formal-expression, pronunciation, reading, or suspect-clip issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one formal-expression sentence can be shadowed with register/rhythm noted;
- one multi-clause sentence can be shadowed without losing particles;
- one reported-speech or nominalized sentence chain can be produced;
- one N3 sentence chain can be produced from memory after shadowing a native-speed or official-course model.
