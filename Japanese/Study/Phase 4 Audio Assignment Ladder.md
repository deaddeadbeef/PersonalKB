# Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder

> Use this during [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]]. The job is to keep N3 grammar, native-speed listening, keigo, business register, cultural expressions, and longer output tied to one replayable native-speed segment, one local block, one source-checked register or pronunciation issue, and one proof row.

## How To Use

1. Start Phase 4 only after the [[Phase 3 — Expansion|Phase 3 checkpoint]] is real.
2. Use [[Seventh Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 1 execution path.
3. Use [[Eighth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 2 execution path.
4. Use [[Ninth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 3 execution path.
5. Use [[Tenth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 4 execution path.
6. Choose one repeatable source from [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]] and one register or pronunciation reference route.
7. At the start of each week, copy the current block assignment into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
8. Run [[Daily Audio Loop]] on each study day.
9. If the block involves keigo, business, service language, or formal role-play, open [[Phase 4 Keigo and Register Production Checklist]] before producing it.
10. At the end of the week, fill [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] and add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].

Phase 4 blocks can take more than one week. Advance only when the native-speed segment, local block, register or pronunciation check, and active output proof are all real.

## Advancement Rule

Move to the next block only when the current block has real evidence:

- the native-speed or official-course source and exact segment are named;
- the authentic segment was replayed on at least three days;
- the local block was repeated on at least three days;
- one short segment was shadowed, transcribed, summarized, role-played, or mined for sentences;
- one pronunciation, pitch, reading, rhythm, register, keigo, business, idiom, or cultural-context issue was checked through a source-backed route;
- normal proof was recorded in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]];
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before returning to daily practice.

If any item is missing, repeat the same block next week with a shorter authentic segment.

## Register Safety Rule

Phase 4 is where local clips are most likely to create false confidence. Use local clips for repetition, not social judgment.

| If the target is... | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Keigo production | [[Phase 4 Keigo and Register Production Checklist]] plus tutor/native/course role-play |
| Business phrase rhythm | Workplace/service audio, business course dialogue, tutor role-play, or native feedback |
| Idiom or seasonal expression | Native/tutor/course context that explains when the expression fits |
| Pitch, kanji reading, or pronunciation | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]], OJAD, NHK, Forvo, course audio, tutor feedback, or native recording |
| Local clip quality | [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before the clip returns to daily practice |
