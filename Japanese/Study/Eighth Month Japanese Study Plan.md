# Eighth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the second month of [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]]. The job is to make N3 kanji and adult-life vocabulary hearable in text-supported native-speed or official-course Japanese: every week needs one repeatable news, workplace, weather, daily-life, tutor, or course segment, one Block 2 local drill set, one kanji reading or pronunciation check, and one short spoken summary using five target words.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 8 execution path. It follows [[Seventh Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 2 of [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Seventh Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Seven Success Criteria|Month 7 success criteria]] are real. If N3 grammar is still silent reading, or if the native-speed segment was not repeated and reviewed, repeat the relevant Week 29-32 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 33

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 2 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one exact news, workplace, weather, daily-life, tutor, or course segment with transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson text, or tutor-provided notes.
3. Open [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2, [[Phase 4 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
4. Name the first short segment you will summarize before the second study day.
5. Pick one N3 kanji reading and five adult-life target words to check, hear, and reuse aloud before the end of Week 35.

Default route: use a short NHK-style news item, Irodori B1-style audio, a workplace/course dialogue, a weather report, a tutor/native recording, or a daily-life segment where the target words appear in text.

## Month-Eight Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 2 assignment | Keep the month on Phase 4 Block 2 until N3 kanji and adult vocabulary are hearable in real speech |
| Spoken words before isolated kanji | Study kanji through words, compounds, and sentences before reviewing character meanings alone |
| Text support is required | Use transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson notes, or tutor notes so readings can be verified |
| Local clips isolate readings and vocabulary | Use [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2 for N3 kanji words, workplace terms, and weather/nature terms |
| Native-speed audio is the model | Compare rhythm and phrasing against course, tutor, broadcaster, or native audio |
| Summary is the output | End each week with one short spoken or written summary using five target words aloud |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 33 | [[Kanji N3 Essentials]] as spoken words | Block 2 N3 kanji clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] plus page-level examples | Course/native vocabulary audio, short news item, tutor recording, or article with audio/text support | Check one N3 kanji reading in a word and say five target words aloud |
| 34 | [[Thematic Vocabulary — Work and Office]] | Block 2 workplace clips plus page-level examples | Workplace, business, daily-life, or course dialogue with text support | Summarize a workplace segment and reuse five target words aloud |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Month 8 is about hearing adult-life words inside real context, not memorizing every kanji or vocabulary table once.

## Week 33

Goal: make N3 kanji study audible by attaching readings to real words and compounds.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Kanji N3 Essentials]].
3. Use the Block 2 N3 kanji clips in [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same text-supported course, tutor, news, or native segment.
5. Check one kanji reading in a word, then say five target words aloud.

Minimum proof: one N3 kanji reading checked through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]], plus five target words said aloud from memory.

If the kanji page becomes character-only study, stop adding characters and return to one spoken compound.

## Week 34

Goal: understand workplace vocabulary as adult-life speech, not as an isolated list.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Thematic Vocabulary — Work and Office]].
3. Use Block 2 workplace clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a workplace, business, daily-life, tutor, or course segment with text visible.
5. Summarize the segment in one or two short sentences and reuse five work words aloud.

Minimum proof: one workplace summary plus five target words reused aloud.

If the segment contains register-sensitive business language, check the role and formality through [[Phase 4 Keigo and Register Production Checklist]] before producing it freely.

## Week 35

Goal: hear weather, nature, and seasonal words in real information flow.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Thematic Vocabulary — Nature and Weather]].
3. Use Block 2 nature/weather clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a weather report, news item, travel segment, daily-life segment, tutor recording, or course dialogue with text visible.
5. Summarize the weather or nature information and reuse five target words aloud.

Minimum proof: one weather or nature summary plus five target words reused aloud.

If the audio is too fast, keep the same segment and summarize only the first sentence or headline.

## Week 36

Goal: consolidate Phase 4 Block 2 by using N3 kanji, work words, and weather/nature words in one controlled summary habit.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Repeat the weakest section from [[Kanji N3 Essentials]], [[Thematic Vocabulary — Work and Office]], or [[Thematic Vocabulary — Nature and Weather]].
3. Repeat only the weakest Block 2 clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native-speed segment or one shorter related segment.
5. Give one short summary and reuse five target words without reading.

Minimum proof: one short summary and five target words produced without reading.

If production collapses, reduce the summary to one sentence and reuse only three words, then repeat the same source next week.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 2 assignment from [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next news, workplace, weather, daily-life, tutor, or course segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 3 because the pages were read once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 2 page
- 1-3 Block 2 local clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named authentic segment
- 1 kanji reading, workplace word, or weather/nature word said aloud

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not turn N3 kanji into silent character review.
- Do not let local clips replace the native-speed or official-course model.
- Do not choose a segment without text support for this vocabulary-heavy month.
- Do not summarize in English only; say the target Japanese words aloud.
- Do not advance to Block 3 without [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Eight Success Criteria

Month eight counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 2 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 2 native-speed or official-course segment or segments;
- Block 2 local clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one N3 kanji reading, adult-life word, rhythm issue, pronunciation issue, or suspect-clip issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one N3 kanji reading can be checked in a real word;
- five workplace words can be reused aloud after a source-backed segment;
- five weather or nature words can be reused aloud after a source-backed segment;
- one short news, workplace, weather, or daily-life segment can be summarized without reading.

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue to [[Ninth Month Japanese Study Plan]]. It wraps Block 3 of [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and moves into keigo, business register, seasonal greetings, idioms, number culture, and register-checked role-play.
