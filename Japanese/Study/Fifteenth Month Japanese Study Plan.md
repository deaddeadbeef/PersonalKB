# Fifteenth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this after [[Fourteenth Month Japanese Study Plan]]. The job is to turn the Month 14 next-cycle decision into a four-week refinement cycle: one bottleneck, one native source, one local diagnostic set, one proof type, and one weekly decision recorded in [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].

## Purpose

This page is the first post-Block-4 refinement cycle for [[Phase 5 — Advanced]]. It does not start a new phase and it does not reset to broad study.

Use it only after the [[Fourteenth Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Fourteen Success Criteria|Month 14 success criteria]] are real. If Month 14 did not produce an exact source segment, bottleneck, and review cadence, repeat Week 60 before starting this page.

## Before Week 61

Do this once:

1. Open the Month 14 next-cycle decision and copy the exact bottleneck into [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].
2. Choose one primary native source from [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Advanced Listening Resources]], [[NHK World — News Listening Practice]], lecture, interview, podcast, drama, documentary, tutor recording, workplace audio, or a course source with stable audio.
3. Name the exact segment: timestamp, scene, article/audio pair, transcript section, subtitle range, lesson unit, recording name, or show-note section.
4. Choose one local diagnostic route from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 4 or the weak earlier Phase 5 block.
5. Choose one proof type: shadowing, transcription, summary, mined-line reuse, role-play recording, pitch/reading check, register feedback, or retest score/error log.
6. Copy the current assignment from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].

Default route: keep the same bottleneck for four weeks. Change the source only if the segment is too long, lacks a stable replay route, or does not expose the bottleneck.

## Refinement-Cycle Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One bottleneck | The month attacks the Month 14 bottleneck instead of browsing advanced resources |
| One native source | Use one stable source segment enough times to hear change |
| Local clips diagnose | Use [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] to isolate the problem, not to replace native listening |
| Proof type stays stable | Track the same proof type for at least two weeks before changing it |
| Feedback is mandatory for output | Use [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] when the proof involves speaking, summary, register, or role-play |
| Pronunciation uncertainty is routed | Check [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] and record suspect clips in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] |
| Weekly review decides | [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] chooses the next source, not discomfort or novelty |

If the source is authentic but too hard to work actively, shorten it. Do not replace it with local clips.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic model | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 61 | Lock the refinement target | Block 4 diagnostic clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] or the weak Phase 5 block | One exact native segment from the Month 14 decision | Bottleneck, source, proof type, and review cadence named |
| 62 | Repair the bottleneck with source support | Only clips that expose the chosen bottleneck | Same segment, shortened if needed, with transcript, subtitles, notes, article text, or tutor/course context | One shadowing, transcription, summary, or checked explanation target |
| 63 | Test output or active reuse | Local clips only after the native model is replayed | Same source plus one output/register/reference model if needed | One recording, mined-line reuse, tutor/native check, or source-backed correction |
| 64 | Retest and choose the next cycle | Only clips needed for the retest | Original segment plus one fresh adjacent segment or JLPT-style/native pair | Retest result and next four-week decision recorded |

If the bottleneck changes every week, the source is too broad or the proof type is too vague.

## Week 61

Goal: turn the Month 14 decision into a small repeatable cycle.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Re-read the Month 14 next-cycle decision.
3. Replay the exact native segment.
4. Use 3-8 local diagnostic clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
5. Write one line naming the bottleneck and what will prove improvement.

Minimum proof: one current assignment in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]] and one Week 61 row in [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].

If the exact source cannot be named, do not start Week 62.

## Week 62

Goal: repair the bottleneck using the native source as the authority.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Replay the same segment or a shorter excerpt from it.
3. Use only local clips that expose the active bottleneck: speed, vocabulary, grammar, register, pitch, stamina, topic knowledge, or transfer.
4. Check the issue against transcript, subtitles, notes, course audio, tutor/native feedback, OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, or a native recording.
5. Produce one shadowing, transcription, summary, or explanation target.

Minimum proof: one source-backed fix and one active-use attempt.

If the fix depends on guessing from local audio alone, it is not fixed.

## Week 63

Goal: test whether the repair transfers into output or active reuse.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Replay the native model before local clips.
3. Record or write one short output tied to the bottleneck.
4. If the bottleneck is register, keigo, stance, pitch, or naturalness, log the attempt in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]].
5. Check one correction through a tutor/native speaker, trusted course, transcript/subtitle route, or reference audio.

Minimum proof: one output or reuse attempt with a named feedback or reference route.

If output feels too hard, shrink it to one sentence or 20 seconds. Do not avoid it for the whole week.

## Week 64

Goal: retest and choose the next refinement cycle.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Re-run the original source task, source-backed check, or paired JLPT-style/native comparison.
3. Add one fresh adjacent segment only after the original segment has a retest result.
4. Compare Week 64 to Week 61 using the same proof type.
5. Choose the next cycle: repeat the same bottleneck, move to the next bottleneck, shift to output/register work, shift to pitch/reading repair, or choose a new N2/N1 source.

Minimum proof: a retest result and one next-cycle decision with exact source, bottleneck, proof type, and review cadence.

If the retest result is vague, repeat Week 64 with a shorter segment.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep the current assignment in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]] updated.
4. Add an [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] row when output, register, pitch, or naturalness is involved.
5. Send suspect local clips to [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before returning them to daily practice.
6. Name the next exact source segment before adding any new advanced resource.

The cycle is successful when the same bottleneck has visible before/after evidence.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current source segment
- 1-3 diagnostic clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence shadowed, transcribed, summarized, checked, or reused
- 1 line in [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]]

This preserves the cycle. It does not replace the weekly retest.

## What Not To Do

- Do not start a fresh resource because the current bottleneck is uncomfortable.
- Do not use local clips as the pronunciation authority for Phase 5.
- Do not change proof type every day.
- Do not treat passive listening as evidence.
- Do not skip [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] when output or register is the target.
- Do not leave suspect pronunciation, pitch, or reading issues outside [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].

## Month-Fifteen Success Criteria

Month fifteen counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] names the same primary bottleneck for the cycle;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact native source segment and proof type;
- [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] clips were used only as diagnostics or precision drills;
- at least one Week 61 baseline and Week 64 retest use the same proof type;
- one source-backed pronunciation, pitch, reading, register, grammar, or naturalness check was completed;
- output or active reuse, if relevant, was recorded in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]];
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one next-cycle decision names the exact source, bottleneck, proof type, and review cadence.

## Next Cycle

After these success criteria are true, continue with another four-week refinement cycle. Keep the same structure:

- one bottleneck;
- one native source;
- one local diagnostic set;
- one proof type;
- one weekly review decision.

Good next cycles:

- repeat the same source with a harder proof type;
- move from comprehension to spoken summary;
- move from spoken summary to tutor/native feedback;
- isolate pitch or reading with reference audio;
- use a new N2/N1 source only after the old bottleneck has a retest result.
