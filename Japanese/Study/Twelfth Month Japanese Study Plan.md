# Twelfth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the second month of [[Phase 5 — Advanced]]. The job is to make full keigo and professional register usable without guessing: every week needs one exact workplace, service, interview, formal speech, business-course, tutor/native role-play, or recorded real-interaction model; one Block 2 local precision drill set; one uchi-soto, social-direction, phrase-rhythm, over-formality, under-formality, or naturalness check; and one register-appropriate output logged in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]].

## Purpose

This page is the Month 12 execution path. It follows [[Eleventh Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 2 of [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Eleventh Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Eleven Success Criteria|Month 11 success criteria]] are real. If Phase 5 work is still mostly passive source sampling, repeat the relevant Week 45-48 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 49

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 2 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one exact professional-register model from [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]]: workplace audio, service interaction, interview, formal speech, business course, tutor/native role-play, or recorded real interaction.
3. Name the role map before drilling: speaker, listener, in-group, out-group, customer/client, superior, peer, or subordinate.
4. Open [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2, [[Phase 5 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]], [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]], [[Keigo — Overview and Register System]], [[Keigo — Sonkeigo (Honorific)]], [[Keigo — Kenjōgo (Humble)]], [[Business Japanese — Workplace Communication]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
5. Decide the first output proof before the second study day: role-play, meeting line, phone line, email-to-spoken rewrite, service reply, or business summary.
6. Name the feedback route: tutor, native speaker, trusted business course, replayable native model, or self-recording compared to a human/native model.

Default route: use one business course dialogue, customer-service exchange, workplace scene, interview answer, formal speech excerpt, or tutor role-play prompt. Keep it short enough to repeat on at least three days.

## Month-Twelve Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 2 assignment | Keep the month on Phase 5 Block 2 until professional-register proof is real |
| Role before phrase | Do not drill keigo until the speaker, listener, and social direction are named |
| Human/native model required | Keigo and business naturalness cannot be proven by local TTS alone |
| Local clips are precision drills | Use [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2 for forms, rhythm, and phrase recall after the model is chosen |
| Output proof is mandatory | Every week ends with one recording, role-play, spoken rewrite, phone line, meeting line, or summary |
| Feedback route is named | Log the model, mismatch, corrected version, and next test in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] |
| Over-formality is a problem | Do not treat more keigo as automatically safer or more natural |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip feels unnatural, socially risky, or different from the model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep practicing from the human/native model.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic model | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 49 | [[Keigo — Overview and Register System]] and role mapping | Block 2 register-map clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] | One professional-register model with a clear speaker/listener relationship | Name the uchi-soto map and record one safe polite baseline line |
| 50 | [[Keigo — Sonkeigo (Honorific)]] | Block 2 sonkeigo clips plus page-level examples | Formal speech, interview, service, business-course, or tutor/native model where someone else's action is elevated | Record three honorific lines and mark who is being elevated |
| 51 | [[Keigo — Kenjōgo (Humble)]] | Block 2 kenjogo clips plus page-level examples | Workplace, client, service, or tutor/native model where your side or in-group is lowered | Record one role-play or phone line using humble forms without lowering the wrong person |
| 52 | [[Business Japanese — Workplace Communication]] and Block 2 consolidation | Block 2 business-output clips or weak keigo clips | Business course dialogue, workplace audio, service interaction, formal meeting, interview, or tutor/native role-play | Log one register-appropriate output in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] and choose the next Phase 5 block target |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Month 12 is about safe professional output, not memorizing every keigo form at once.

## Week 49

Goal: make the relationship map explicit before producing keigo.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Keigo — Overview and Register System]].
3. Use the Block 2 register-map clips in [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same professional-register model.
5. Name the speaker, listener, uchi/soto relation, and target politeness level.
6. Record one safe polite baseline line and compare its rhythm to the model.

Minimum proof: one role map and one recorded polite baseline line.

If you cannot name who is being raised or lowered, do not add sonkeigo or kenjogo yet.

## Week 50

Goal: produce honorific language only when another person's action should be elevated.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Keigo — Sonkeigo (Honorific)]].
3. Use Block 2 sonkeigo clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay one formal, service, interview, business-course, or tutor/native segment where the model elevates someone else.
5. Record three short honorific lines and mark who is being elevated.

Minimum proof: three sonkeigo lines with the respected person named.

If the line accidentally elevates yourself or your in-group, log the correction in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]].

## Week 51

Goal: produce humble language without lowering the wrong person.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Keigo — Kenjōgo (Humble)]].
3. Use Block 2 kenjogo clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay one workplace, client, service, or tutor/native segment where the speaker lowers self or in-group.
5. Record one role-play, phone line, or status update using humble forms.

Minimum proof: one humble-output recording with the lowered person or group named.

If the role map changes mid-scenario, rewrite the output before recording again.

## Week 52

Goal: consolidate Block 2 by making one business or service output natural enough to carry forward.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Business Japanese — Workplace Communication]].
3. Repeat only the weakest Block 2 clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same business course, workplace, service, formal meeting, interview, or tutor/native model.
5. Record one email-to-spoken rewrite, meeting line, phone line, service reply, or business summary.
6. Log the model, mismatch, corrected version, and next test in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]].

Minimum proof: one register-appropriate output entry with a feedback route.

If the output is grammatically correct but socially stiff, over-formal, casual, or unnatural, keep it as the Week 52 target until the next test is named.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 2 assignment from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Add one [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] entry for the week's recording, role-play, spoken rewrite, or register correction.
5. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
6. Name the exact next professional-register model before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 3 because the forms were recognized in a table.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 2 keigo or business page
- 1-3 Block 2 local clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 line from the named professional-register model
- 1 recorded or spoken line with the role map named

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly output proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not treat local TTS as the authority for keigo, business tone, or professional naturalness.
- Do not produce sonkeigo or kenjogo before naming the speaker, listener, and social direction.
- Do not assume more keigo is always safer.
- Do not copy formal speech, customer service, or workplace drama lines without context.
- Do not advance to Block 3 without [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] and [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] evidence.

## Month-Twelve Success Criteria

Month twelve counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 2 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 2 professional-register model or models;
- Block 2 local clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one uchi-soto, social-direction, phrase-rhythm, over-formality, under-formality, or naturalness issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one role map can be named without hunting;
- one polite baseline line can be recorded and compared to a human/native model;
- three sonkeigo lines can be produced with the respected person named;
- one kenjogo role-play, phone line, or status update can be produced without lowering the wrong person;
- one business or service output is logged in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] with model, mismatch, corrected version, and next test;
- the next Phase 5 block target is chosen from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue to [[Thirteenth Month Japanese Study Plan]]. It wraps Block 3 of [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and moves into idioms, filler timing, stance, implication, pitch, and advanced grammar nuance.

Before starting Block 3, choose one native conversation, drama scene, podcast excerpt, tutor example, pitch reference, or course segment where the nuance is audible in context.
