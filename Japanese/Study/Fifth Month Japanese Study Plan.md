# Fifth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the second month of [[Phase 3 — Expansion]]. The job is to make longer N4 sentences audible and speakable: every week needs one transcripted authentic segment, one Block 2 local drill set, one rhythm or pronunciation check, and one spoken summary target.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 5 execution path. It follows [[Fourth Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 2 of [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Fourth Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Four Success Criteria|Month 4 success criteria]] are real. If Block 1 grammar is still readable but not speakable, repeat the relevant Week 13-16 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 17

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 2 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Keep the same primary authentic source from Block 1 unless [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] gave a concrete reason to change it.
3. Choose one exact graded, intermediate, course, tutor, or native-speaker segment with transcript, lesson text, subtitles, or a tutor-provided script.
4. Open [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2, [[Phase 3 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
5. Name the first longer sentence you will shadow before the second study day.

Default route: use Genki II, Irodori A2/B1, a graded N4 lesson, a transcripted story, or a tutor/native recording where longer sentence chains, potential forms, volitional forms, or 1000-word vocabulary appear in context.

## Month-Five Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 2 assignment | Keep the month on Block 2 until longer sentence proof is real |
| Transcript before speed | Use audio with text so particles and clause endings can be checked |
| Local clips isolate the mechanics | Use [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2 for sentence chains, potential forms, volitional forms, and output vocabulary |
| Authentic audio is the model | Compare connected speech, pause placement, and sentence rhythm against course, tutor, or native audio |
| Summary is the output | End each week with one short spoken summary, not only repeated clips |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 17 | [[N4 Grammar — Compound Sentences]] | Block 2 compound-sentence clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] | Transcripted segment with two or more linked clauses | Shadow one longer sentence without losing particles |
| 18 | [[N4 Grammar — Potential and Volitional]] potential forms | Block 2 potential clips plus page-level examples | Dialogue or lesson segment with can-do expressions | Say three can-do sentences and explain what is possible |
| 19 | [[N4 Grammar — Potential and Volitional]] volitional/intention forms plus [[Core 1000 — Conversational Fluency]] | Block 2 volitional clips and opinion/reason vocabulary | Segment with intention, plan, opinion, reason, or explanation language | Say one intention sentence and one reason sentence from memory |
| 20 | Block 2 consolidation | Repeat only weak Block 2 clips | Same authentic segment or a shorter related segment | Shadow one longer sentence and summarize one segment in three short sentences |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Block 2 is about sentence control under audio pressure, not checking off pages.

## Week 17

Goal: keep particles and clause endings intact as sentences get longer.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[N4 Grammar — Compound Sentences]].
3. Use the Block 2 compound-sentence clips in [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same authentic sentence chain with text visible.
5. Shadow one longer sentence, then say the clause connectors aloud separately.

Minimum proof: shadow one longer sentence without losing the particles, then name the exact authentic model used.

If long sentences collapse, shorten the sentence instead of speeding up.

## Week 18

Goal: make potential forms sound like usable can-do statements, not just conjugation tables.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study the potential-form section in [[N4 Grammar — Potential and Volitional]].
3. Use Block 2 potential clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a course, tutor, or native segment with can-do expressions.
5. Produce one can-do sentence about yourself and one about another person.

Minimum proof: say three potential-form sentences and explain what is possible in each one.

If 食べられる and passive-looking forms blur together, stop adding examples and compare only two short sentences.

## Week 19

Goal: connect volitional/intention forms to reasons, opinions, and first Core 1000 output.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study the volitional/intention section in [[N4 Grammar — Potential and Volitional]].
3. Choose five useful words from [[Core 1000 — Conversational Fluency]].
4. Use Block 2 volitional clips and opinion/reason vocabulary from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
5. Replay one authentic segment containing intention, plan, opinion, reason, or explanation language.

Minimum proof: say one intention sentence and one reason sentence from memory.

If Core 1000 becomes a silent word list, reduce the target to five words and reuse each one aloud.

## Week 20

Goal: consolidate Block 2 into one repeatable shadowing and summary routine.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Repeat only the weakest Block 2 local clips.
3. Replay the same authentic segment or one shorter related segment.
4. Shadow one longer sentence.
5. Summarize the segment aloud in three short sentences.

Minimum proof: shadow one longer sentence and produce a three-sentence summary without reading.

If summary output is too hard, keep the same segment and reduce the proof to three shorter sentences.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 2 assignment from [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next authentic segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 3 because the pages were read once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 2 grammar or Core 1000 page
- 1-3 Block 2 local clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named authentic segment
- 1 spoken sentence or one summary line from memory

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not treat long sentences as reading-only material.
- Do not change authentic sources just because the current segment is difficult.
- Do not trust local TTS for connected-speech rhythm without comparing it to the authentic model.
- Do not add Core 1000 words without reusing them aloud.
- Do not advance to Block 3 without [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Five Success Criteria

Month five counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 2 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 2 authentic segment or segments;
- Block 2 local clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one longer-sentence rhythm, particle, potential/volitional ending, reading, or suspect-clip issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one longer compound sentence can be shadowed without losing particles;
- three potential-form sentences can be said and explained;
- one intention sentence and one reason sentence can be produced from memory;
- one authentic segment can be summarized aloud in three short sentences.

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue to Block 3 in [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]]. Block 3 moves into practical travel, health, date/time, onomatopoeia, and N4 kanji vocabulary.

Before starting Block 3, open [[Phase 3 Audio Coverage Map]] and choose the exact practical dialogue, short article/audio pair, or tutor recording that will anchor practical-domain vocabulary.
