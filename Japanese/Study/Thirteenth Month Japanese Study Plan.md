# Thirteenth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the third month of [[Phase 5 — Advanced]]. The job is to make nuance audible before it becomes output: every week needs one exact native conversation, drama scene, podcast excerpt, tutor example, pitch reference, trusted course segment, or grammar-in-context model; one Block 3 local precision drill set; one idiom-fit, filler-timing, stance, implication, pitch, reading, or grammar-nuance check; and one reused expression, pitch-sensitive sentence, or explanation logged with its reference route.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 13 execution path. It follows [[Twelfth Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 3 of [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Twelfth Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Twelve Success Criteria|Month 12 success criteria]] are real. If professional register still lacks a model, recording, and feedback route, repeat the relevant Week 49-52 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 53

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 3 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one exact nuance model from [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]]: native conversation, drama scene, podcast excerpt, tutor example, pitch reference, trusted course segment, or grammar-in-context model.
3. Name the nuance target before drilling: idiom fit, filler timing, stance, implication, pitch contrast, reading, or grammar contrast.
4. Open [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 3, [[Phase 5 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]], [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]], [[Idioms and Proverbs — ことわざ]], [[Common Filler Words and Discourse Markers]], [[Pitch Accent — Common Patterns]], [[Grammar — Comparison Across Levels]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
5. Decide the first proof before the second study day: explain in context, reuse in a checked sentence, shadow the line, compare pitch, or record a short output attempt.
6. Name the reference route: native transcript/subtitles, tutor/native feedback, trusted course audio, OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, or a native recording.

Default route: use one conversation excerpt, podcast exchange, drama scene with subtitles, tutor example, course dialogue, or pitch reference item. Keep the first target short enough to replay on at least three days.

## Month-Thirteen Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 3 assignment | Keep the month on Phase 5 Block 3 until nuance proof is real |
| Context before reuse | Do not reuse idioms, fillers, stance markers, or pitch-sensitive items without context |
| Human/native model required | Nuance, implication, pitch, and idiom fit cannot be proven by local TTS alone |
| Local clips are prompts | Use [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] Block 3 to isolate forms after the model is chosen |
| Reference route is named | Every target needs a transcript/subtitle, tutor/native/course, OJAD/NHK/Forvo, or native-recording check |
| Output stays small | Reuse one expression or pitch-sensitive item in one checked sentence before expanding |
| Feedback is logged when risky | Use [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] when tone, stance, humor, implication, or pitch could mislead |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with a pitch reference, native model, or tutor/native feedback, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the source-backed route.

## Week 53

Goal: treat idioms and proverbs as context-dependent signals, not decorations.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Idioms and Proverbs — ことわざ]].
3. Use the Block 3 idiom clips in [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native, tutor, or course example where the expression appears or is explained.
5. Explain the situation, tone, and whether the expression is safe for your own output.

Minimum proof: one idiom or proverb classified as safe-to-use, recognition-only, or do-not-use-yet.

If you cannot describe the context, do not mine the expression for output.

## Week 54

Goal: hear fillers and discourse markers as timing and stance, not random naturalness words.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Common Filler Words and Discourse Markers]].
3. Use Block 3 discourse-marker clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay one conversation turn from a podcast, interview, drama scene, tutor example, or course dialogue.
5. Shadow one short turn and explain what one filler or discourse marker does: hesitation, agreement, softening, contrast, topic shift, summary, or stance.

Minimum proof: one shadowed turn plus one timing/stance explanation.

If the marker sounds natural in the source but awkward in your voice, log the mismatch in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]].

## Week 55

Goal: make pitch, reading, and rhythm checks source-backed.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Pitch Accent — Common Patterns]].
3. Use Block 3 pitch prompts from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Check one pitch-sensitive word or phrase against OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, tutor/native recording, trusted course audio, or a native source sentence.
5. Say the item in one sentence and record the reference route.

Minimum proof: one pitch or reading item with its reference route named.

If the local clip and the reference route disagree, treat the local clip as suspect until [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] records it.

## Week 56

Goal: consolidate Block 3 by using one nuanced grammar or expression choice in context.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Grammar — Comparison Across Levels]].
3. Repeat only the weakest Block 3 clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native, course, or tutor segment where a contrast is clear.
5. Reuse one nuanced expression, pitch-sensitive item, discourse marker, idiom, or grammar contrast in a checked sentence.
6. Log the reference route and next test in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] if the output is socially or prosodically risky.

Minimum proof: one checked sentence plus the next block decision.

If the explanation is only an English translation, repeat the week with a shorter source.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 3 assignment from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Add an [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] entry if a reused expression, recording, pitch item, or nuance choice produced a carry-forward fix.
5. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
6. Name the exact next nuance model before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not advance to Block 4 because the advanced expression was recognized once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 3 idiom, filler, pitch, or grammar page
- 1-3 Block 3 local clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 line from the named native/tutor/course model
- 1 explanation, pitch check, shadowed line, or checked sentence

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly nuance proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not treat local clips as the authority for idiom fit, filler timing, implication, or pitch.
- Do not reuse idioms, drama lines, or fillers because they sound advanced.
- Do not skip the reference route for pitch-sensitive words.
- Do not call a grammar contrast learned if you can only translate both forms into English.
- Do not advance to Block 4 without [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Thirteen Success Criteria

Month thirteen counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 3 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 3 native/tutor/course model or models;
- Block 3 local clips from [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one idiom-fit, filler-timing, stance, implication, pitch, reading, or grammar-nuance issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one idiom or proverb can be classified as safe-to-use, recognition-only, or do-not-use-yet;
- one discourse marker or filler can be explained by timing or stance in a native turn;
- one pitch or reading item has a source-backed reference route;
- one nuanced expression, pitch-sensitive item, discourse marker, idiom, or grammar contrast was reused in a checked sentence;
- the next Phase 5 block target is chosen from [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue to [[Fourteenth Month Japanese Study Plan]]. It wraps Block 4 of [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and moves into N2/N1 benchmark and refinement days: JLPT-style listening, real-input transfer, bottleneck diagnosis, and long-term maintenance.

Before starting Block 4, choose one JLPT-style listening item and one real native input segment on the same topic, role, or function.
