# Second Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for Weeks 5-8 of Japanese study. The job is to start [[Phase 2 — Building Blocks]] without letting N5 grammar become silent table-reading: every week needs one authentic segment, one local drill block, one spoken proof target, and one pronunciation or reading check.

## Purpose

This page is the month-two execution path. It sits between [[First Month Japanese Study Plan]] and the full [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it after the Phase 1 month-one success criteria are true. If kana, the first 100 words, or the self-introduction are still shaky, repeat the relevant part of [[First Month Japanese Study Plan]] before starting Week 5.

## Before Week 5

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 2 — Building Blocks]].
2. Open [[Phase 2 Authentic Audio Spine]] and keep the same source route from Phase 1 if it worked.
3. Open [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Copy Week 5 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
5. Keep [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 2 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] available.

Default if the Phase 1 source was too loose: pick one course route now, preferably Genki I or Irodori A1. Use NHK only if you can name the exact lesson segment and replay it across multiple days.

## Month-Two Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Grammar counts only when heard and used | Do not mark a grammar page done until you have heard examples and said at least one |
| One weekly Phase 2 assignment | Copy only the current week from [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]] |
| One authentic segment | Name the exact course, NHK, tutor, or native segment before drilling |
| Local clips are controlled drills | Use [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]] to isolate particles, forms, counters, and vocabulary |
| Authentic audio is the model | Compare against official/course/native audio through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Week 5

Goal: hear particles as sentence direction, not as English prepositions.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section of [[N5 Grammar — Particles]].
3. Use Week 5 of [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same authentic segment with visible particles.
5. Compare one particle job only: topic, subject, object, destination, time, location, means, or addition.

Minimum proof: hear five short sentences and identify the key particle job without translating word by word.

If particles are still guessed from English word order, repeat Week 5 with shorter sentences.

## Week 6

Goal: make common verb and adjective forms audible before pushing speed.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one verb-form or adjective chunk.
3. Use Week 6 of [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a lesson segment with visible forms.
5. Compare one ending or rhythm feature: ます, て, た, ない, い-adjective, or な-adjective.

Minimum proof: say five polite sentences and one adjective description, then compare their rhythm with the authentic model.

If forms collapse during speech, reduce the number of forms. Use fewer patterns more often.

## Week 7

Goal: stop importing English number timing into Japanese.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study [[N5 Grammar — Time and Counting]] or one counter group.
3. Use Week 7 of [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a scheduling, classroom, shopping, or tutor segment with numbers.
5. Check one irregular reading through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] before memorizing it.

Minimum proof: say one short schedule and three counted objects without pausing to translate from English numbers.

If a counter reading feels arbitrary, do not brute-force it. Check the model and keep the example in a sentence.

## Week 8

Goal: turn daily-life vocabulary into small speakable routine sentences.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study a small chunk from [[Core 500 — Daily Life Vocabulary]], [[Thematic Vocabulary — Food and Drink]], or [[Thematic Vocabulary — Home and Daily Life]].
3. Use Week 8 of [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay a food, home, restaurant, or routine segment from the same source route.
5. Shadow one short sentence or exchange.

Minimum proof: narrate three daily routine sentences aloud and shadow one matching authentic segment.

If vocabulary grows faster than listening, stop adding words and repeat the same segment until the sentence chunks are audible.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Copy the next week from [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]] into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next authentic segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. If the evidence is missing, repeat the same week with a shorter authentic segment.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current N5 page or sentence reading
- 1 local clip or clip set from the current Phase 2 week
- 1 sentence or phrase from the named authentic segment
- 1 spoken sentence from memory

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not read grammar tables silently and call the week complete.
- Do not add kanji-heavy work before Week 9 unless the word is already speakable.
- Do not switch authentic sources because the first replay feels too hard.
- Do not memorize counter readings that have not been checked against an authentic or reference source.
- Do not keep drilling a local clip that sounds wrong; log it first.

## Month-Two Success Criteria

Month two counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]] has evidence for Weeks 5-8;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names one authentic segment per week;
- Week 5-8 local clip sets from [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]] were used;
- at least one pronunciation, rhythm, particle, form, counter, or reading feature was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- five heard particle sentences can be analyzed without English word-order guessing;
- five polite sentences and one adjective description can be said aloud;
- one short schedule and three counted objects can be said with Japanese timing;
- one daily routine or restaurant segment can be shadowed.

## Next Month

After these success criteria are true, continue Weeks 9-12 in [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]]. The next block keeps kanji tied to spoken words first, then moves into pronunciation, daily interactions, shopping, restaurants, and polite register.

Before starting Week 9, open [[Phase 2 Audio Coverage Map]] and confirm which kanji, pronunciation, and conversation pages will supply local clips and which authentic segment will be the model.
