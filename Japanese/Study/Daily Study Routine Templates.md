# Daily Study Routine Templates

> Pick the smallest routine you can actually repeat. Daily continuity matters more than heroic weekend sessions.

## How To Choose

Use the 30-minute routine as the default. Use the 60-minute routine when you have energy. Use the 2-hour routine only during an intentional push. Every routine uses [[Daily Audio Loop]] for the audio block.

## Daily Audio Rule

Audio counts only when both layers happen:

| Layer | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Local drill | One current-phase local clip or clip set |
| Authentic model | One named official, native, tutor, or source-backed segment |
| Comparison | One pronunciation, rhythm, pitch, reading, or register feature |
| Routing | Normal proof goes to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]]; suspect local clips go to [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] |

## 30-Minute Daily

| Time | Activity | Tools |
|------|----------|-------|
| 10 min | Due reviews | Anki / WaniKani / handwritten review |
| 15 min | Course spine | Genki, Irodori, or your chosen textbook |
| 5 min | Current phase audio | [[Daily Audio Loop]]: one local clip set plus one named authentic segment |

Use this on ordinary days. Do the reviews first; they are the part that decays if skipped.

## 1-Hour Daily (Steady Progress)

| Time | Activity | Tools |
|------|----------|-------|
| 15 min | Due reviews plus 3-10 new cards | Anki / WaniKani |
| 20 min | Course spine | Exercises, not passive reading |
| 10 min | Vault review | One page from the current phase |
| 15 min | Listening or shadowing | [[Daily Audio Loop]] with the current phase's local ladder and authentic spine |

Use this when you want steady progress and still want the next day to be sustainable.

## 2-Hour Daily (Intensive)

| Time | Activity | Tools |
|------|----------|-------|
| 20 min | Reviews | Finish due cards first |
| 30 min | Course spine | New lesson plus exercises |
| 20 min | Kana/kanji or sentence mining | Only from material you understand |
| 20 min | Listening with transcript | [[Daily Audio Loop]] plus transcript or lesson context |
| 15 min | Output | Self-intro, journal, or tutor prep |
| 15 min | Cleanup | Add cards, note questions, choose tomorrow's task |

Use this sparingly. If it creates a review backlog, reduce new cards.

## Weekly Review

Once per week, open the current phase review page and record what actually happened: [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]], [[Phase 2 Weekly Review]], [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]], [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]], or [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].

Check:

- Did I study at least 5 days?
- Did reviews stay manageable?
- Which grammar point is still unclear?
- Which local clip and native-course segment did I repeat enough to improve?
- Did I add the weekly row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]]?
- What is the next small page or lesson?

## SRS Rules

1. Reviews before new cards.
2. Add fewer cards than you think you can handle.
3. Make cards from things you already understand.
4. Rewrite failed cards instead of brute-forcing them.
5. Keep total daily reviews low enough that tomorrow is not intimidating.

For Phase 1, 3-10 new cards/day is enough. The goal is habit formation, not a huge deck.

## Minimum Viable Study Day

When the day is busy, do only this:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes kana or sentence reading
- 1 minute listening from [[Daily Audio Loop]]: one local clip plus one authentic sentence

This keeps the chain alive without pretending every day is ideal.
