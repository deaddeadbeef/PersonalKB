# Daily Audio Loop

> Use this every study day. The local clip is the drill; the authentic source is the pronunciation model; the evidence log keeps the habit honest.

## The Loop

| Step | Action | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Choose the current phase and exact target | [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]] |
| 2 | Play one local clip set for 1-5 minutes | [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]], or [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]] |
| 3 | Play one authentic segment once for sound, then once with text or context | [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 2 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 3 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]], or [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]] |
| 4 | Shadow one short part aloud | Same authentic segment |
| 5 | Compare one feature only | Vowel length, small っ, particle reading, ら行, ん, pitch drop, rhythm, register, or naturalness |
| 6 | Route the result | [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] for normal proof; [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] for suspect local clips |

If the authentic segment is not named, the audio loop is incomplete.

If this is your first week, start with [[Phase 1 Audio Starter Assignment]] before choosing anything else. For the rest of Phase 1, use [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

For Phase 2, use [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]] before choosing page-level clips. It keeps particles, forms, counters, kanji readings, and beginner interactions tied to exact authentic audio.

For Phase 3, use [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]] before choosing page-level clips or media. It keeps N4 grammar, longer sentences, pitch checks, and intermediate listening tied to a replayable source.

For Phase 4, use [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] before choosing native-speed segments, keigo role-plays, or page-level clips. It keeps register-sensitive output tied to human, official-course, tutor, or native models.

For Phase 5, use [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] before choosing native-source segments, output targets, or local precision drills. It keeps native media, professional register, pitch, nuance, and N2/N1 benchmark work tied to evidence instead of passive immersion.

## Minimum Day

Use this when time is tight:

1. Play one local clip.
2. Play one authentic sentence.
3. Say the sentence once.
4. Mark the current assignment in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] if it changed.

This is enough to preserve the listening habit. It is not enough to add a weekly proof row unless the exact source and segment are named.

## Standard Day

Use this for the normal 5-15 minute audio block:

| Time | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 2 min | Local drill: repeat the current phase clip set |
| 5-10 min | Authentic model: listen once blind, once with text or lesson context |
| 2 min | Shadow one phrase, sentence, or timestamp range |
| 1 min | Compare one feature and choose whether to continue, check, or log a suspect clip |

## Weekly Hand-Off

At the end of the week:

1. Open the current phase review page.
2. Fill the weekly audio evidence table.
3. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
4. Choose next week's exact source segment before adding new material.

## Stop Conditions

Stop drilling a local clip and use [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] when:

- the displayed Japanese and audio do not match;
- the word has an ambiguous reading and no reading hint;
- a particle, counter, name, kanji reading, or title sounds wrong;
- the local rhythm conflicts with an official, native, tutor, OJAD, NHK, or Forvo reference;
- the clip teaches keigo, register, or a full sentence you plan to memorize and no authentic model is assigned.
