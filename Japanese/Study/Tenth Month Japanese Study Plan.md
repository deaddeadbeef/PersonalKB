# Tenth Month Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the fourth month of [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]]. The job is to make native-speed listening repeatable instead of passive: every week needs one short replayable native-speed segment with transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson notes, or tutor notes; one Block 4 local drill set; one discourse, filler, rhythm, or source-label check; and one shadowing, transcription, or summary proof.

## Purpose

This page is the Month 10 execution path. It follows [[Ninth Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 4 of [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Ninth Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Nine Success Criteria|Month 9 success criteria]] are real. If keigo, business phrases, cultural expressions, and role/direction checks are still unstable, repeat the relevant Week 37-40 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 41

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 4 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one exact native-speed podcast, news, drama scene, tutor recording, advanced course segment, or short article/audio pair with transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson notes, or tutor notes.
3. Open [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 4, [[Phase 4 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]], [[Advanced Listening Resources]], [[Common Filler Words and Discourse Markers]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
4. Decide the first output proof before the second study day: shadowing, transcription, or summary.
5. Pick one filler, stance marker, discourse turn, source label, rhythm issue, or suspect clip to check before the end of Week 42.

Default route: use a short transcripted podcast excerpt, NHK-style news segment, drama scene with subtitles, tutor/native recording, advanced course segment, or article/audio pair. Keep it short enough to replay on at least three days.

## Month-Ten Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 4 assignment | Keep the month on Phase 4 Block 4 until native-speed proof is real |
| Segment before immersion | Choose one named segment before adding optional passive listening |
| Text support is required | Use transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson notes, or tutor notes for checking |
| Local clips isolate discourse | Use [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 4 for source labels, fillers, stance markers, and discourse turns |
| Native-speed audio is the model | Local clips support the segment; they never replace it |
| Output proof rotates | Shadow, transcribe, or summarize one short segment each week |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

## Four-Week Path

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 41 | [[Advanced Listening Resources]] and source selection | Block 4 source-label clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] | One short native-speed segment with text support | Name the source, timestamp or lesson segment, and shadow one line |
| 42 | [[Common Filler Words and Discourse Markers]] fillers and stance markers | Block 4 filler clips plus page-level examples | Same segment or a shorter related segment where filler timing is audible | Identify one filler, stance marker, or discourse turn and explain its timing |
| 43 | Shadowing or transcription | Repeat only weak Block 4 clips | Same segment, slowed/replayed only as needed, then normal speed | Shadow or transcribe 20-60 seconds with text support |
| 44 | [[Study Roadmap — Intermediate to Advanced]] and Phase 4 consolidation | Repeat only weak Block 4 clips | Same native-speed segment or one short related segment | Summarize one short segment and choose the Phase 4 exit or repeat target |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Month 10 is about controlled native-speed stamina, not random immersion volume.

## Week 41

Goal: choose a stable native-speed source and make the first segment repeatable.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Advanced Listening Resources]].
3. Use the Block 4 source-label clips in [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native-speed segment with text support.
5. Shadow one line or sentence and write the exact source, episode, article, scene, lesson, or timestamp.

Minimum proof: one named native-speed segment and one shadowed line.

If you cannot name the exact segment, the week has not started yet.

## Week 42

Goal: hear fillers, stance markers, and discourse turns as timing, not vocabulary lists.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Common Filler Words and Discourse Markers]].
3. Use Block 4 filler and discourse clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native-speed segment or one shorter related segment.
5. Mark one filler, stance marker, or discourse turn in the transcript, subtitles, notes, or your own transcription.

Minimum proof: one filler, stance marker, or discourse turn identified with timing and function.

If fillers become isolated vocabulary, stop adding clips and return to the actual segment.

## Week 43

Goal: turn native-speed comprehension into a visible shadowing or transcription proof.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Repeat only the weakest Block 4 clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
3. Replay the native-speed segment once for gist, once with text support, and once for output.
4. Shadow or transcribe 20-60 seconds.
5. Check one rhythm, pronunciation, or suspect-clip issue through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].

Minimum proof: one 20-60 second shadowing or transcription target with the checking route named.

If 60 seconds is too much, reduce to 20 seconds and keep normal-speed playback as the model.

## Week 44

Goal: consolidate Phase 4 Block 4 and decide whether Phase 4 is ready to continue or needs another repeat block.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Study Roadmap — Intermediate to Advanced]].
3. Repeat only the weakest Block 4 clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay the same native-speed segment or one short related segment.
5. Summarize the segment and choose the next target: repeat Block 4, review a weak Phase 4 block, or prepare for Phase 5.

Minimum proof: one short summary plus one decision about the next Phase 4 or Phase 5 target.

If the summary is vague, keep the same segment and transcribe one more sentence before advancing.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 4 assignment from [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next native-speed segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not move to Phase 5 because the pages were read once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 4 page
- 1-3 Block 4 local clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named native-speed segment
- 1 shadowed line, transcription fragment, or summary sentence

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not count passive background listening as the Block 4 spine.
- Do not browse many native sources before repeating one short segment.
- Do not let local clips replace the native-speed model.
- Do not choose a segment without text or note support for this month.
- Do not move to Phase 5 without [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] evidence.

## Month-Ten Success Criteria

Month ten counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 4 month;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 4 native-speed segment or segments;
- Block 4 local clips from [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one filler, stance marker, discourse turn, rhythm issue, source-label issue, pronunciation issue, or suspect-clip issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one native-speed source and exact segment can be named without hunting;
- one filler, stance marker, or discourse turn can be identified in real speech;
- one 20-60 second segment can be shadowed or transcribed with text support;
- one short native-speed segment can be summarized and tied to the next Phase 4 or Phase 5 decision.

## Next Phase

After these success criteria are true, open [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] and the [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery#Phase 4 Checkpoint|Phase 4 checkpoint]]. If the checkpoint is not real, repeat the weakest Phase 4 block with a shorter segment.

If the checkpoint is real, prepare Phase 5 by opening [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]], [[Phase 5 Audio Coverage Map]], and [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] before starting native-source output work.
