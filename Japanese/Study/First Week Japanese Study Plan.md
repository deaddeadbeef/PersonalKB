# First Week Japanese Study Plan

> Use this for the first seven days of Japanese study. The point is not to finish a lot of pages; it is to build the daily loop: reviews, one course spine, kana, one local audio drill, one named authentic segment, and one small proof record.

## Before Day 1

Do this once:

1. Open [[Japanese Learning Dashboard]].
2. Choose one course/source route from [[Authentic Audio Source Setup]].
3. Open [[Phase 1 Audio Starter Assignment]].
4. Copy its Week 1 table into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
5. Keep [[Daily Audio Loop]], [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] available for the week.

Default if you have not chosen a course: use the first NHK WORLD-JAPAN Easy Japanese beginner lesson for one week, then decide whether to continue with NHK, Genki, or Irodori in [[Resources Index — Textbooks, Apps, and Tools]].

## Week 1 Rules

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| One course/source only | Prevents setup from replacing study |
| One exact authentic segment | Makes pronunciation and rhythm checkable |
| One local clip set | Keeps Obsidian audio useful without making TTS the authority |
| One comparison feature per day | Avoids trying to fix every sound at once |
| One proof row at the end | Keeps the week from becoming vague listening |

If the authentic segment is too hard, shorten it. Do not switch sources midweek unless the source is unusable.

## Seven-Day Plan

| Day | Main study | Local audio | Authentic audio | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Set up the course/source and read the first part of [[Hiragana Complete Guide]] | Week 1 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | Choose and play the exact first segment from [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]] | Fill [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]] |
| 2 | Continue hiragana recognition; read kana aloud slowly | Repeat vowels and particle traps from Week 1 local clips | Replay the same authentic segment with text or lesson context | Shadow one short phrase aloud |
| 3 | Review hiragana rows that are slow; add a few cards only if reviews are light | Repeat long-vowel and small-っ clips | Replay the same segment blind once, then with text | Compare one timing feature |
| 4 | Read [[Writing Systems Overview]] and return to hiragana drills | Repeat only the hardest local clip | Replay one authentic phrase or sentence | Say it once without reading |
| 5 | Start [[Katakana Complete Guide]] lightly; do not rush | Repeat Week 1 local clip set | Replay the same segment or the next tiny segment from the same source | Record one fuzzy sound or reading question |
| 6 | Review kana and one short phrase from the course/source | Repeat only weak clips | Shadow the authentic model once | Decide whether any local clip is suspect |
| 7 | Open [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] | Quick Week 1 local review | Replay the week's authentic segment | Add one row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

## Minimum Day

Use this when the day is busy:

1. Do 5 minutes of reviews.
2. Read one kana row or one short phrase.
3. Play one local Week 1 clip.
4. Play one sentence from the named authentic segment.
5. Mark nothing new unless the current assignment changed.

This preserves the habit. It does not count as weekly proof unless the exact authentic segment and local clip set are already named.

## What Counts As Success

The first week counts when all of these are true:

- one authentic source and exact segment are named;
- the same authentic source was used on at least three days;
- Week 1 local clips were repeated on at least three days;
- one phrase was shadowed aloud;
- one pronunciation feature was compared through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]];
- one summary row was added to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]];
- suspect local clips, if any, were routed to [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].

## What Not To Do

- Do not browse advanced grammar, keigo, native news, or N2/N1 pages this week.
- Do not add many SRS cards before reviews are stable.
- Do not treat local TTS as the final model for pronunciation.
- Do not change sources because a repeated segment starts feeling boring.
- Do not continue drilling a local clip that sounds wrong; log it first.
