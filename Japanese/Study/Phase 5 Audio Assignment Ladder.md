# Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder

> Use this during [[Phase 5 — Advanced]]. The job is to keep advanced listening, professional register, native media, pitch, nuance, and N2/N1 direction tied to one exact native-source segment, one local precision block, one source-backed pronunciation or register check, and one output or comprehension proof.

## How To Use

1. Start Phase 5 only after the [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery|Phase 4 checkpoint]] is real.
2. Use [[Eleventh Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 1 execution path.
3. Use [[Twelfth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 2 execution path.
4. Use [[Thirteenth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 3 execution path.
5. Use [[Fourteenth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the Block 4 execution path.
6. Use [[Fifteenth Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the first post-Block-4 refinement cycle after Month 14 names the bottleneck, source, proof type, and review cadence.
7. Choose one repeatable source from [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]] and one output or register feedback route.
8. At the start of each week, copy the current block assignment into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
9. Run [[Daily Audio Loop]] on each study day, keeping local clips to a short precision drill.
10. If the block involves output, keigo, professional phrasing, humor, implication, or social stance, open [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] before treating the attempt as learned.
11. At the end of the week, fill [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]] and add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].

Phase 5 blocks can take more than one week. Advance only when native-source work, local precision drills, feedback, and active proof are all real.

## Advancement Rule

Move to the next block only when the current block has real evidence:

- the native source and exact segment or timestamp are named;
- the native segment was replayed or mined on at least three days;
- the local block was repeated on at least three days without replacing native-source work;
- one short segment was shadowed, transcribed, summarized, discussed, mined, role-played, or reused in output;
- one pronunciation, pitch, reading, rhythm, register, keigo, business, idiom, filler, humor, implication, or naturalness issue was checked through a source-backed route;
- Phase 5 output or register work, if present, was recorded in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]];
- normal proof was recorded in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]];
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before returning to daily practice;
- next week's exact native segment is chosen before adding new advanced material.

If any item is missing, repeat the same block next week with a shorter native segment.

## Native-Source Safety Rule

Phase 5 is where local clips are most likely to feel productive while avoiding the hard work. Use local clips as prompts, not as proof.

| If the target is... | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Native-speed comprehension | Replayable human-recorded native source with text, subtitles, transcript, article text, lyrics, show notes, or tutor/native context |
| Keigo or business output | Workplace/service audio, business course dialogue, tutor/native feedback, formal speech, or recorded role-play |
| Humor, idiom, filler, implication, or stance | Native/tutor/course context that shows when the expression fits |
| Pitch, reading, or pronunciation | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]], OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, course audio, tutor feedback, or native recording |
| JLPT-to-real-listening transfer | JLPT-style item plus real native input on the same topic or function |
| Local clip quality | [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before the clip returns to daily practice |
