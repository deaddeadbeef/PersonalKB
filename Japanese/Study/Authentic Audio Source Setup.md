# Authentic Audio Source Setup

> Use this once before the first week of study, then update it only during weekly review. The goal is not to collect Japanese audio; it is to keep one reliable human or official pronunciation model beside the local Obsidian drills.

## Decision Rule

Pick one primary authentic audio source for the current phase. Keep it until the phase review proves it is not working.

| Current situation | Primary source | Use it for | Do not do this |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You bought or chose Genki | [GENKI official audio through OTO Navi](https://genki3.japantimes.co.jp/en/intro/index.html) | Dialogue, vocabulary, practice, reading, and workbook listening audio | Skip the recordings and treat Genki as a silent grammar book |
| You want a free official course | [Irodori official textbook/audio](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/about.html) or [Irodori online course](https://www.irodori-online.jpf.go.jp/guide/) | Can-do lessons, daily-life communication tasks, streamed or downloaded MP3 audio | Run Irodori and Genki in parallel during Phase 1 |
| You have no course spine yet | [NHK WORLD-JAPAN Easy Japanese](https://www.nhk.or.jp/lesson/en/) | One beginner lesson at a time: skit, phrase, drill, and pronunciation support | Play many lessons once without shadowing |
| You are in Phase 3 or later | The current phase authentic spine plus a transcripted course, tutor recording, or native segment | Repeatable shadowing, transcription, sentence mining, and output checks | Replace the spine with random native media |
| You need pitch or reading verification | [OJAD](https://www.gavo.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/ojad/), NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, course audio, or tutor/native feedback | Word reading, pitch pattern, and suspect local-clip checks | Treat local TTS pitch as authoritative |

Local MP3 clips are the drill layer. The authentic source is the model.

## Setup Checklist

1. Choose the course or source in the table above.
2. Install the required app or bookmark the exact course page.
3. Choose the current lesson, unit, skit, or segment before opening the local ladder.
4. Open the matching assignment ladder when one exists: [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]], [[Phase 2 Audio Assignment Ladder]], or [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
5. Open the matching local ladder: [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 2 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]], [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]], or [[Phase 5 Local Audio Practice]].
6. Open the matching authentic spine: [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 2 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 3 Authentic Audio Spine]], [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]], or [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]].
7. Keep one pronunciation reference route ready: [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]], OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, course audio, tutor feedback, or native feedback.
8. Record the exact source and segment in the current weekly review and [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].

## Daily Use

| Time | Action | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-5 min | Play the local clips for the current page or phase ladder | You repeated one controlled sound, word, phrase, or sentence |
| 5-20 min | Play the authentic segment once blind, once with text, then shadow one short part | You heard human-recorded or official-course Japanese before memorizing the phrase |
| 1-2 min | Compare one feature: vowel length, small っ, particle reading, ら行, ん, pitch drop, rhythm, or register | You know what to fix next |
| 1 min | Log the source and segment in the current weekly review or [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] | The next session can restart without browsing |

When time is short, keep one authentic sentence and one local clip. Do not skip audio completely.

## Weekly Proof

Each weekly review should answer these:

- Which authentic source did I use?
- What was the exact lesson, skit, episode, scene, timestamp, or recording?
- Which local clip set did it support?
- Can I shadow one short segment without reading?
- Which pronunciation, pitch, rhythm, or register issue did I check?
- What is next week's exact audio segment?

The segment must be replayable. If you cannot name the segment, it does not count as the week's pronunciation model.

After the weekly review, add one row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]]. The log keeps the running proof trail; the weekly review explains the local details.

## When Sources Disagree

Use this priority order:

1. Human-recorded official-course audio, tutor/native recording, or public broadcaster lesson for phrase rhythm.
2. OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, course audio, or tutor/native feedback for word readings and pitch checks.
3. Local TTS only for short repeatable drills after the reading is known.

If a local clip sounds wrong, use [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and run the checks in [[Japanese Audio QA Status]]. Do not keep drilling a suspect clip just because it plays.
