# Phase 3 Block 4 Japanese Study Plan

> Use this after [[Sixth Month Japanese Study Plan]] to finish [[Phase 3 — Expansion]]. The job is to make pitch checks, filler timing, media rhythm, and register recognition source-backed: every week needs one repeatable authentic segment, one Block 4 local drill set, one reference check, and one shadowing or recognition proof.

## Purpose

This page is the Phase 3 Block 4 execution path. It follows [[Sixth Month Japanese Study Plan]] and wraps Block 4 of [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

Use it only after the [[Sixth Month Japanese Study Plan#Month-Six Success Criteria|Month 6 success criteria]] are real. If practical vocabulary, onomatopoeia, or N4 kanji readings are still isolated lists, repeat the relevant Week 21-24 work before starting this page.

## Before Week 25

Do this once:

1. Open [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]] and copy Block 4 into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log#Current Assignment]].
2. Choose one short intermediate or native segment with transcript, subtitles, lyrics, lesson text, or tutor-provided notes.
3. Open [[Phase 3 Pitch Accent Practice Path]], [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] Block 4, [[Phase 3 Audio Coverage Map]], [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]], and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
4. Choose one pitch/accent reference route: OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, course audio, tutor feedback, or a native-speaker recording.
5. Name the exact first shadowing segment before the second study day.

Default route: use Genki II, Irodori A2/B1, a graded intermediate lesson, a short YouTube segment with Japanese subtitles, a short drama/anime scene with transcript/subtitles, a song line with checked lyrics, or a tutor/native recording. Keep it short enough to replay several times.

## Block-Four Rules

| Rule | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One Block 4 assignment | Keep the block stable until pitch/media/register proof is real |
| Reference before local pitch | Check pitch or accent through [[Phase 3 Pitch Accent Practice Path]] before trusting local pitch clips |
| Segment before media binge | Use one repeatable text-backed segment, not random immersion |
| Local clips are prompts | Use [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] Block 4 to isolate pitch, filler, media, and register labels after the authentic model is clear |
| Recognition before production | Hear register and media cues before trying to speak like the source |
| Weekly proof is required | End each week with [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] and one row in [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] |

If a local clip disagrees with the authentic model or reference source, quarantine it in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] and keep using the authentic model.

| Week | Main content | Local audio | Authentic audio | Output proof |

| 25 | [[Pitch Accent — Introduction]], [[Pitch Accent — Common Patterns]], [[Phase 3 Pitch Accent Practice Path]] | Block 4 pitch prompts from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] only after source checks | Reference audio plus one short sentence from the primary segment | Check one pitch/accent item and explain the drop or contrast |

If a week is overloaded, repeat it. Block 4 is about hearing and verifying subtle cues, not collecting more media.

## Week 25

Goal: build a pitch-check habit without treating TTS as the pitch authority.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Pitch Accent — Introduction]], [[Pitch Accent — Common Patterns]], or [[Phase 3 Pitch Accent Practice Path]].
3. Check one word or minimal pair through the chosen pitch/accent reference route.
4. Use the matching Block 4 pitch prompt from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] only as a repetition drill.
5. Say the word with one particle or inside one short sentence, then compare it to the source again.

Minimum proof: check one pitch/accent item and explain the drop, contrast, or uncertainty in [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]].

If the reference check takes over the whole session, stop at one item.

## Week 26

Goal: hear filler words and discourse markers as timing devices, not vocabulary decorations.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Common Filler Words and Discourse Markers]].
3. Use Block 4 filler and discourse clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
4. Replay one short conversation, lesson, tutor, or native segment where the cue appears in context.
5. Shadow one line and mark where the filler, sentence-end particle, or connector changes the timing.

Minimum proof: shadow one line and identify one filler or discourse timing cue.

If fillers become forced production, switch back to recognition and shadowing.

## Week 27

Goal: move from beginner-style audio to one controlled intermediate or native segment without losing the text-backed study loop.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Choose one source from [[Intermediate Listening Resources]] or [[YouTube Channels for Japanese Learners]].
3. Keep the segment short and replayable, with transcript, subtitles, lesson notes, or visible context.
4. Use Block 4 media/source-label clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] only to name the source type and speed.
5. Summarize the segment aloud in one to three short sentences.

Minimum proof: summarize one short segment and name the source type, speed, and support text.

If listening becomes passive, shorten the segment and require one shadowed line.

## Week 28

Goal: recognize media rhythm and register cues before entering Phase 4.

Daily shape:

1. Finish due reviews.
2. Study one small section from [[Anime and Drama — Immersion Listening]], [[Music — Learning Through Japanese Songs]], or [[Keigo — Overview and Register System]].
3. Replay one short scene, song line, service/register segment, or tutor/native example with text or context.
4. Use only the weak Block 4 local clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]].
5. Explain one register cue, media-rhythm cue, or reason the source is not a speaking model.

Minimum proof: explain one register or media-rhythm cue and fill the [[Phase 3 Weekly Review#Phase 3 Exit Check|Phase 3 exit check]].

If a scene or song is too hard, replace it with a shorter subtitled or tutor-provided segment.

## Weekly Handoff

At the end of each week:

1. Fill [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]].
2. Add one summary row to [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]].
3. Keep or update the Block 4 assignment from [[Phase 3 Audio Assignment Ladder]].
4. Decide whether any suspect local clips need [[Pronunciation Correction Log]].
5. Name the exact next segment before the second study day of the new week.

The weekly review decides the next action. Do not move to [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]] because the Block 4 pages were read once.

## Minimum Day

Use this on busy days:

- 5 minutes due reviews
- 5 minutes current Block 4 page
- 1 Block 4 local clip from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]]
- 1 sentence from the named authentic segment
- 1 source-backed pitch, rhythm, media, or register note

This preserves continuity. It does not replace the weekly proof.

## What Not To Do

- Do not trust local pitch clips without a reference check.
- Do not treat YouTube, anime, drama, or music as passive immersion for this block.
- Do not speak like anime, song lyrics, or rough casual media unless a tutor/native/course source confirms the register.
- Do not add more pitch items than you can check.
- Do not start [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]] without [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] evidence and the Phase 3 exit check.

## Block-Four Success Criteria

Block 4 counts when all of these are true:

- [[Phase 3 Weekly Review]] has evidence for the Block 4 work;
- [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]] names the exact Block 4 authentic segment or segments;
- Block 4 local clips from [[Phase 3 Local Audio Practice]] were used on at least three days per week;
- at least one pitch/accent, filler timing, media rhythm, register, or suspect-clip issue was checked each week;
- suspect local clips, if any, were recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]];
- one pitch/accent item was checked through [[Phase 3 Pitch Accent Practice Path]];
- one filler or discourse timing cue can be identified in a real line;
- one short intermediate or native segment can be summarized aloud;
- one media or register cue can be explained without copying the source as a speaking model;
- the [[Phase 3 Weekly Review#Phase 3 Exit Check|Phase 3 exit check]] is filled.
