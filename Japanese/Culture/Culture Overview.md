## Key Cultural Concepts

### Uchi/Soto (内/外) — In-group/Out-group ![[cultov-002-uchi-soto.mp3]]
The fundamental social divide. Language changes based on whether you're speaking about/to someone in your group or outside it.

### Tatemae/Honne (建前/本音) — Public/Private Face ![[cultov-003-tatemae-honne.mp3]]
![[nontbl-047-tatemae.mp3]]
![[nontbl-048-honne.mp3]]
What you say publicly vs what you really think. Japanese communication is often indirect — reading between the lines is a skill.

### Wa (和) — Harmony ![[cultov-004-wa.mp3]]
![[nontbl-049-wa-harmony.mp3]]
Maintaining group harmony drives communication patterns. Direct refusal is rare; indirect signals are common.

### Amae (甘え) — Mutual Dependence ![[cultov-005-amae.mp3]]
![[nontbl-050-amae.mp3]]
A uniquely Japanese concept of presuming on others' goodwill. Affects how requests and favors are communicated.
