# Phase 1 Audio Coverage Map

> Use this page when asking: "What should I listen to while studying this Phase 1 page?" It maps the required [[Phase 1 — Foundation]] sequence to local clips, authentic audio, and pronunciation QA.

## Coverage Rule

Every Phase 1 study item needs three things:

| Layer | Source | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local drill | [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] or page-embedded MP3 clips | Repeat small sounds, words, and sentences inside Obsidian |
| Authentic model | [[Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine]] | Keep human-recorded or official-course Japanese as the pronunciation model |
| QA rule | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] | Decide when a clip is safe, suspect, or needs correction |

Planning pages do not need embedded audio. They need to route clearly to the audio layers. Drill pages and content pages should either embed clips directly or point to the matching weekly clip set.

## Gap Policy

These are not gaps:

- A planning page with zero embedded clips, if it routes to the audio stack.
- A copyrighted native source linked externally instead of mirrored into the vault.
- A local TTS clip used only after [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] says it is safe for the job.

These are real gaps:

- A required Phase 1 page with no embedded clips and no route to local/authentic audio.
- A local clip in a daily path that sounds wrong or lacks a reading hint for ambiguous text.
- A learner-facing instruction that says "listen to audio" without naming the page or source.
