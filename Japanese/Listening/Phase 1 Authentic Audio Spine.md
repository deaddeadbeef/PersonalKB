# Phase 1 Authentic Audio Spine

> Use this page to choose the human-recorded or official-course audio for [[Phase 1 — Foundation]]. Pair it with [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]]: native audio gives the pronunciation model, while local clips give short repeatable drills.

## The Rule

Pick exactly one native-audio spine for the first month:

| If you chose... | Use this audio | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genki I | [GENKI official audio through OTO Navi](https://genki3.japantimes.co.jp/en/intro/index.html) | Structured textbook/workbook audio for dialogues, vocabulary, practice, readings, and listening comprehension |
| Irodori A1 | [Irodori Starter / A1 audio](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/about.html) or the [Irodori online course](https://www.irodori-online.jpf.go.jp/guide/) | Free Japan Foundation course with audio around real-life communication tasks |
| No course chosen yet | [NHK WORLD-JAPAN Easy Japanese](https://www.nhk.or.jp/lesson/en/) | 48 short beginner lessons with skits, drills, pronunciation practice, and cultural context |

Do not run all three at once. The point is consistency and repeat exposure, not collecting resources.

If this is the first week, use [[Phase 1 Audio Starter Assignment]] to choose the first exact source segment and copy it into [[Authentic Audio Evidence Log]]. For Weeks 2-4, use [[Phase 1 Audio Assignment Ladder]].

## Daily Pairing

Use this pairing on any Phase 1 study day:

| Time | Audio layer | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-5 min | [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] | Drill the current week's kana, particle, phrase, or self-introduction clips |
| 5-15 min | This authentic spine | Play the current course or NHK lesson audio once blind, once with text, then shadow one short segment |
| 1 min | [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]] or daily note | Log one phrase you can now say and one sound that still needs work |

When time is short, keep the local drill and play one native sentence. Do not skip audio completely.

## Four-Week Native Audio Plan

| Week | Native-audio focus | Local companion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First course unit or NHK lesson: greetings, names, basic classroom phrases | Week 1 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] for vowels, は/へ, を, long vowels, and small っ |
| 2 | Next course unit: katakana-heavy words, classroom or travel basics | Week 2 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] for コーヒー, タクシー, ホテル, and requests |
| 3 | First short dialogues and request phrases | Week 3 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] for survival words and polite requests |
| 4 | Self-introduction dialogue and review audio | Week 4 of [[Phase 1 Local Audio Practice]] for XはYです and self-introduction blocks |

The native source does not have to match the local clip exactly. It just has to keep human Japanese in your ear while the local clips make specific sounds easy to repeat.

Use [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] when a local clip, course recording, dictionary entry, or your own recording disagree.

Use [[Phase 1 Audio Coverage Map]] when you need to match a required Phase 1 page to the correct native and local audio layer.

## Quality Bar

Treat audio as usable for Phase 1 only if it meets these conditions:

- The source is an official course, publisher audio, public broadcaster lesson, tutor recording, or native speaker recording.
- The clip has text, transcript, or lesson context you can check.
- The speech is slow or segmented enough to shadow without guessing.
- You can replay the exact same segment across multiple days.
- You are not using auto-generated social-media captions as the source of truth.

## Weekly Proof

At the end of each week, record evidence in [[Phase 1 Weekly Review]]:

- Which native source did I use?
- Which lesson or audio segment did I repeat?
- Can I shadow one short segment without reading?
- Which local clip fixed a pronunciation detail I missed in native audio?
- What is next week's exact audio segment?
