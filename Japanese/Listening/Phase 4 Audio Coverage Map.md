# Phase 4 Audio Coverage Map

> Use this page when asking: "What should I listen to while studying this Phase 4 page?" It maps the required [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]] sequence to local clips, native-speed audio, and pronunciation/register QA.

## Coverage Rule

Phase 4 audio support has three layers:

| Layer | Source | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local drill | [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] plus embedded MP3 clips on the current Phase 4 page | Repeat N3 grammar, kanji vocabulary, keigo, business, culture, and discourse-marker examples inside Obsidian |
| Authentic model | [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]] | Keep native-speed, human-recorded, or official-course Japanese as the pronunciation, rhythm, and register model |
| QA rule | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] + [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] | Decide when a clip is safe, suspect, fixed, or replaced |

Use [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] for daily execution. It is a curated subset of existing Phase 4 clips, ordered by block so N3 grammar, kanji, keigo, business, culture, and discourse timing do not require page-level clip hunting. Use [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] when you need the copy-ready block assignment, evidence target, and advancement rule. For Block 1, use [[Seventh Month Japanese Study Plan]] when you need the learner-facing daily path through N3 formal expressions, clause chains, nominalization, quotation, and grammar comparison. For Block 2, use [[Eighth Month Japanese Study Plan]] when you need the learner-facing daily path through N3 kanji, work vocabulary, nature/weather vocabulary, reading checks, and source-backed summaries. For Block 3, use [[Ninth Month Japanese Study Plan]] when you need the learner-facing daily path through keigo, business phrases, cultural expressions, role checks, and register-safe production. For Block 4, use [[Tenth Month Japanese Study Plan]] when you need the learner-facing daily path through native-speed source selection, filler timing, discourse turns, and shadowing, transcription, or summary proof. The authentic model stays primary because register, idioms, discourse timing, and longer clauses cannot be learned from local TTS alone.

## Gap Policy

These are not gaps:

- A strategy, culture, or listening-resource page with low clip count, if it routes to [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]] and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
- A copyrighted native source linked externally instead of mirrored into the vault.
- A local clip used for repetition after the authentic model and QA rules make its job clear.

These are real gaps:

- A Phase 4 page that asks for listening, shadowing, keigo, business, or register practice without naming the local/authentic layer.
- A keigo, business, idiom, pitch, or discourse-timing claim that relies on TTS alone.
- A suspect local clip that is not recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before it returns to daily practice.
- A Phase 4 week with no [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] evidence for the native-speed segment, local clip set, and register or pronunciation check.
