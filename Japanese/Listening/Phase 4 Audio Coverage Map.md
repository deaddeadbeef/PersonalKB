# Phase 4 Audio Coverage Map

> Use this page when asking: "What should I listen to while studying this Phase 4 page?" It maps the required [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]] sequence to local clips, native-speed audio, and pronunciation/register QA.

## Coverage Rule

Phase 4 audio support has three layers:

| Layer | Source | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local drill | Embedded MP3 clips on the current Phase 4 page | Repeat N3 grammar, kanji vocabulary, keigo, business, culture, and discourse-marker examples inside Obsidian |
| Authentic model | [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]] | Keep native-speed, human-recorded, or official-course Japanese as the pronunciation, rhythm, and register model |
| QA rule | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] + [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] | Decide when a clip is safe, suspect, fixed, or replaced |

Phase 4 does not need a separate local ladder yet. The required content pages already contain page-level clips. What Phase 4 needs is a stronger native-speed model because register, idioms, discourse timing, and longer clauses cannot be learned from local TTS alone.

## Gap Policy

These are not gaps:

- A strategy, culture, or listening-resource page with low clip count, if it routes to [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]] and [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]].
- A copyrighted native source linked externally instead of mirrored into the vault.
- A local clip used for repetition after the authentic model and QA rules make its job clear.

These are real gaps:

- A Phase 4 page that asks for listening, shadowing, keigo, business, or register practice without naming the local/authentic layer.
- A keigo, business, idiom, pitch, or discourse-timing claim that relies on TTS alone.
- A suspect local clip that is not recorded in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] before it returns to daily practice.
- A Phase 4 week with no [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] evidence for the native-speed segment, local clip set, and register or pronunciation check.
