# Phase 5 Local Audio Practice

> Use this during [[Phase 5 — Advanced]] when the daily routine says "Phase 5 local drills." This is the precision-drill ladder for native media labels, full keigo, business register, idioms, discourse timing, pitch checks, and advanced grammar nuance.

## Audio Standard

Use [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] to choose the current block. Use [[Phase 5 Authentic Audio Spine]] as the pronunciation, rhythm, register, and comprehension authority. The local clips below are short precision drills inside Obsidian.

Phase 5 is not a local-TTS phase. Do not count these clips as proof of advanced listening. Proof comes from replayable native-source work, shadowing, transcription, summary, output, tutor/native feedback, and entries in [[Advanced Output and Register Feedback Log]] or [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].

For pitch, idioms, professional phrasing, humor, keigo, and register nuance, local clips are prompts only. Check the item against native audio, OJAD/NHK/Forvo, tutor feedback, official-course audio, or a native-speaker recording before treating it as learned.

Use [[Phase 5 Audio Assignment Ladder]] when you need the copy-ready current assignment, advancement rule, and native-source safety rule. Use [[Phase 5 Audio Coverage Map]] when you need the full page-to-audio inventory.

## Session Loop

Run this loop for 10 minutes on a minimum Phase 5 day or 30 minutes on a good day:

1. Play one native-source sentence or short segment from the week's source.
2. Play 3-8 local clips from the matching block below.
3. Say each local item back once without reading.
4. Shadow, transcribe, or summarize one native-source sentence.
5. Record or write one output line when the week includes register or nuance work.
6. Log the exact source, local drill set, and output/feedback route in [[Phase 5 Weekly Review]].

Keep local work small. If native-source work disappears, stop local drilling and return to the spine.

## Block 4 - Benchmark and Refinement Days

Goal: use local drills only to diagnose the bottleneck, then return to native-source work.

Use this block when studying [[JLPT Overview — N5 to N1]], [[Study Roadmap — Intermediate to Advanced]], or a Phase 5 week where the problem is not one page but the system.

| Bottleneck | Local drill | Native-source proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | 3-5 discourse markers from Block 3 | Shadow one fast line until it is repeatable |
| Register | 3-5 phrases from Block 2 | Record one role-play and compare to a human/native model |
| Pitch or reading | 3-5 pitch prompts from Block 3 | Check OJAD/NHK/Forvo/tutor or native recording |
| Media vocabulary | 3-5 source labels from Block 1 | Summarize one native segment with text support |
| Grammar nuance | 3-5 comparison clips from Block 3 | Explain the difference in one original sentence |

End-of-block proof: name the bottleneck for next week and choose the exact source segment that attacks it.
