# Phase 2 Local Audio Practice

> Use this during [[Phase 2 — Building Blocks]] when the daily routine says "Phase 2 page clips." This is the local clip ladder for N5 mechanics: particles, verb forms, adjectives, time, counters, first kanji words, and basic interactions.

## Audio Standard

Use [[Phase 2 Authentic Audio Spine]] as the pronunciation model. The local clips below are controlled drills for repetition inside Obsidian.

Do not treat local clips as the only authority. If a clip sounds different from your course audio, NHK lesson, tutor, or native speaker recording, stop using it and follow [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy#Correction Workflow]].

This page does not replace the content pages. It gives you one ordered path through selected clips so Phase 2 practice does not turn into clip hunting.

Use [[Phase 2 Audio Coverage Map]] when you need the full page-to-audio inventory.

## Session Loop

Run this loop for 5 minutes on a minimum day or 15 minutes on a good day:

1. Play one authentic sentence from the week's course, NHK, tutor, or native-speaker segment.
2. Play 3-8 local clips from the matching block below.
3. Say each item back once without reading.
4. Shadow the local clip once while reading.
5. Close the page and produce one short sentence from memory.

Keep the set small. Phase 2 improves when particles and forms become speakable, not when every table has been played once.
