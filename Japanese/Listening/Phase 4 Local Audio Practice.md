# Phase 4 Local Audio Practice

> Use this during [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]] when the daily routine says "Phase 4 clips." This is the local clip ladder for N3 grammar, N3 kanji, adult-life vocabulary, keigo, business register, cultural expressions, and discourse timing.

## Audio Standard

Use [[Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine]] as the pronunciation, rhythm, and register model. The local clips below are controlled drills inside Obsidian.

Phase 4 includes register-sensitive Japanese. Do not produce keigo, business phrases, idioms, or seasonal greetings from local clips alone. Check the role, social direction, and human/native/course model through [[Phase 4 Keigo and Register Production Checklist]] before using them freely.

Do not treat local clips as the only authority. If a clip conflicts with your native-speed source, course audio, tutor, or native-speaker recording, stop using it and follow [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy#Correction Workflow]].

Use [[Phase 4 Audio Assignment Ladder]] to decide which block is active and what proof is required.

Use [[Phase 4 Audio Coverage Map]] when you need the full page-to-audio inventory.

For Block 1, use [[Seventh Month Japanese Study Plan]] as the learner-facing wrapper around the N3 grammar clips below, so native-speed or official-course audio stays the model and local clips remain controlled drills.

## Session Loop

Run this loop for 10 minutes on a minimum Phase 4 day or 25 minutes on a good day:

1. Play one native-speed or official-course sentence from the week's source.
2. Play 5-10 local clips from the matching block below.
3. Say each item back once without reading.
4. Shadow one native-speed sentence and one local clip.
5. Produce one summary line, N3 sentence chain, or register-checked role-play line.
6. Log one exact target in [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]].

Keep the local set short. Phase 4 improves when local clips make native-speed speech more hearable, not when TTS replaces the native model.
