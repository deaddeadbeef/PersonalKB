# Listening Overview

Listening is often the hardest skill for Japanese learners. The language is spoken quickly, particles are swallowed, and pitch accent carries meaning.

## Why Listening Is Hard
- **Speed:** Native speakers average 7.5 syllables/second
- **Particle reduction:** は, を, を are often barely pronounced ![[listen-019-particles-ha-wo.mp3]]
- **Homophones:** Many words sound identical (橋/端/箸 — hashi means bridge/edge/chopsticks) ![[listen-020-hashi-hashi-hashi.mp3]]
- **Pitch accent:** Distinguishes otherwise identical words
- **Contractions:** ている → てる, ておく → とく ![[listen-021-teiru-teru-teoku-toku.mp3]]
