# Phase 4 Authentic Audio Spine

> Use this page during [[Phase 4 — Intermediate Mastery]]. Pair it with [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] so native-speed training stays anchored while N3 grammar, keigo production, business register, cultural expressions, and sustained conversation become repeatable.

## The Rule

Keep one repeatable native-speed source and one pronunciation/reference route. Phase 4 can use real native media, but it still needs a controlled segment you can replay, shadow, transcribe, and compare against the local drills.

| Source role | Use | Good options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary native-speed spine | The segment you repeat across several days | A transcripted podcast, news segment, drama scene, tutor/native-speaker recording, or intermediate/advanced course audio |
| Register model | Formal, business, keigo, and polite workplace language | Tutor/native-speaker role-play, course business dialogue, service interaction, or workplace audio with text |
| Pronunciation reference | Word, pitch, rhythm, and reading checks | OJAD, NHK accent dictionary, Forvo, course audio, tutor feedback, or a native-speaker recording |
| Light immersion | Extra exposure after the main segment is done | [[Advanced Listening Resources]], [[NHK World — News Listening Practice]], [[Anime and Drama — Immersion Listening]], or a podcast episode you do not mine deeply |

Do not count passive background listening as the spine. The spine is the exact segment you can name, replay, shadow, and review.

## Daily Pairing

Use this pairing on any Phase 4 study day:

| Time | Audio layer | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3-5 min | [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] or current Phase 4 page embeds | Repeat N3 grammar, kanji vocabulary, keigo, business, culture, or discourse-marker clips inside Obsidian |
| 15-30 min | This authentic spine | Listen once for gist, once with text or notes, then shadow or transcribe one short segment |
| 2-5 min | [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] + [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] when needed | Check one pitch, reading, register, rhythm, or suspect-clip issue |
| 1 min | [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]] or daily note | Record the exact segment, local clip set, and one register or rhythm issue to revisit |

When time is short, keep one native sentence and one local clip. Phase 4 should never become silent grammar reading.

## Four-Block Native Audio Plan

| Phase 4 block | Study focus | Authentic audio target | Local companion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | N3 formal expressions, conjunctions, nominalization, quotation | Transcripted speech or course/tutor audio with longer clauses and reporting patterns | [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 1 |
| 2 | N3 kanji, adult-life vocabulary, work, nature, weather | News, workplace, weather, or daily-life segment where vocabulary appears in context | [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 2 |
| 3 | Keigo, business, seasonal greetings, idioms, numbers in culture | Formal role-play, service/workplace audio, or tutor/native-speaker examples with clear register | [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 3 |
| 4 | Advanced listening, conversation fluency, plateau strategy | One native-speed segment with text/subtitles plus one shadowing or transcription target | [[Phase 4 Local Audio Practice]] Block 4 |

## Quality Bar

Treat audio as usable for Phase 4 only if it meets these conditions:

- The segment is human-recorded, official-course audio, public broadcaster/native media, or tutor/native-speaker audio.
- You can replay the exact same segment across multiple days.
- There is transcript, subtitles, article text, lesson context, or a reliable way to verify what was said.
- The segment is short enough to shadow, transcribe, or summarize actively.
- Register and keigo claims are checked against a human/native model, not local TTS alone.
- Pitch-accent, kanji-reading, and suspect-clip issues are checked through [[Pronunciation and Audio Accuracy]] and logged in [[Pronunciation Correction Log]] when needed.

## Weekly Proof

At the end of each week, record evidence in [[Phase 4 Weekly Review]]:

- Which native-speed source and exact segment did I repeat?
- Which Phase 4 page did it support?
- Which local clip set made the grammar, word, register, or rhythm clearer?
- Which pronunciation, pitch, reading, or register issue did I check?
- Can I shadow or summarize one short segment without reading?
- What is next week's exact native-speed segment?
