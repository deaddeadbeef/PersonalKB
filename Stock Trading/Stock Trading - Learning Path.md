# Stock Trading - Learning Path

This path relearns stocks from the ground up. It separates market literacy, account mechanics, analysis, risk control, and practice so strategy does not arrive before basic survival.

## Pass Structure

| Pass | Read | Goal | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 - Safety frame | This page, [[Stock Trading Book Reading Spine]], and [[Position Sizing and Trade Journaling]] | Know what must be bounded before any trade | One written no-live-capital rule |
| 1 - What a stock is | [[Stocks and Ownership]], [[Returns Risk and Time Horizon]] | Understand ownership, return, risk, time horizon | Ten-sentence mental model |
| 2 - How trades execute | [[How Stock Markets Work]], [[Order Types and Execution]], [[Accounts Settlement and Margin]] | Understand orders, settlement, account types, and margin boundaries | Order-type comparison table |
| 3 - How to read a company | [[Reading Company Filings and Fundamentals]], [[Company Filing Worksheet]] | Read a filing and identify business, risks, financials, ratios, and invalidation evidence | One completed company filing worksheet |
| 4 - How prices move | [[Price Action Momentum and Volatility]] | Recognize volatility, momentum, beta, and price-action traps | One annotated chart journal entry |
| 5 - Practice loop | [[Stock Market Basics - Review Drill]], [[Position Sizing and Trade Journaling]], [[Paper Trading Lab]] | Paper-trade only, with review discipline | Ten paper trades with planned risk and postmortems |

## First Seven Sessions

1. Read [[Stocks and Ownership]]. Write down what a share is and what it is not.
2. Read [[Returns Risk and Time Horizon]]. Decide which money is not eligible for trading practice.
3. Read [[How Stock Markets Work]]. Explain bid, ask, spread, liquidity, and halt in plain language.
4. Read [[Order Types and Execution]]. Build a table of market, limit, stop, and stop-limit orders.
5. Read [[Accounts Settlement and Margin]]. Write cash-account and margin-account danger notes.
6. Read [[Reading Company Filings and Fundamentals]]. Open one 10-K on EDGAR and identify business, risk factors, MD&A, and financial statements.
7. Complete [[Company Filing Worksheet]] for one company before looking at a chart.
8. Read [[Position Sizing and Trade Journaling]]. Create a paper-trade journal row template.
9. Start [[Paper Trading Lab]] only after every earlier artifact exists.

## Rules for Advancement

- Do not move to Pass 2 until you can explain why a market order has execution certainty but not price certainty.
- Do not move to Pass 3 until you can explain cash settlement and margin risk without notes.
- Do not move to Pass 4 until a company thesis has filing evidence and a written invalidation condition.
- Do not move to paper trading until every journal row has planned entry, invalidation, size, exit, and review fields.
- Do not use real capital from this learning path alone.
