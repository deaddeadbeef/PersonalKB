## Scenario Prompts

1. A stock last traded at $20.00. The bid is $19.80 and the ask is $20.25. You place a market buy order. What price are you guaranteed?
2. You want to buy only if the stock can be bought at $30 or less. Which order type expresses that intent?
3. You own a stock at $50 and want an exit trigger at $46. Why is the actual sale price not guaranteed?
4. You sell a stock on Monday in a normal U.S. settlement cycle. Under the current T+1 baseline, when should it generally settle?
5. A company has high revenue growth but persistent losses and heavy debt. Which filing sections and ratios should you inspect before forming a thesis?
6. Your planned paper entry is $40 and invalidation is $38. If planned risk is $100, what share count equals `1R` before slippage?
7. A stock has a strong chart, but earnings are after the close. What risk is missing from a pure chart read?
8. Your paper trade made money, but the order type, invalidation, and planned size were not written before entry. What process grade should it receive?

## Minimum Passing Standard

Before using any live brokerage account, answer every prompt above in plain language and complete ten paper journal rows with pre-trade risk and post-trade review.
