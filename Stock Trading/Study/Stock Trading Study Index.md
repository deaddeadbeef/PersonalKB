## First Practice Loop

1. Read [[Stock Trading - Learning Path]].
2. Explain [[Stocks and Ownership]] from memory.
3. Compare market, limit, stop, and stop-limit orders from [[Order Types and Execution]].
4. Explain T+1 settlement and the cash-account/margin-account split from [[Accounts Settlement and Margin]].
5. Open one 10-K using EDGAR and fill the worksheet in [[Reading Company Filings and Fundamentals]].
6. Create ten paper journal rows using [[Position Sizing and Trade Journaling]].
7. Review misses with [[Stock Market Basics - Review Drill]].

## Competency Gates

| Gate | Passing evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Vocabulary | Can define stock, share, ticker, bid, ask, spread, market order, limit order, stop order, settlement, margin, volatility, beta, and diversification |
| Market mechanics | Can explain why last price is not execution price |
| Account mechanics | Can explain T+1 settlement and why margin can lose more than the deposit |
| Fundamental reading | Can find business, risks, MD&A, and financial statements in a 10-K |
| Risk process | Can calculate paper position size from entry, invalidation, and planned risk |
| Journal discipline | Has ten paper trades with pre-trade plan and post-trade review |
