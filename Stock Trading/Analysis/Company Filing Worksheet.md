# Company Filing Worksheet

## One-Line Summary

Use this worksheet to turn one SEC filing into a bounded business thesis before any chart read, price target, or paper trade.

## Rule

No company thesis is valid in this wiki until it names the filing, the period, the business model, the major risks, the financial condition, and what would make the thesis wrong.

## Output

Write exactly one paragraph:

```text
I understand [company] as [business model]. The main thesis is [claim]. The strongest evidence is [filing evidence]. The biggest risk is [risk]. The thesis is wrong if [invalidation evidence]. This is / is not ready for a paper trade because [reason].
```
