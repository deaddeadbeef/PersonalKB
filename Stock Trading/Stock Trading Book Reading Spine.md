## Book I: What A Stock Is

Build the object model: equity ownership, returns, risk, time horizon, and diversification.

- [[Stock Trading/Foundations/Returns Risk and Time Horizon|Returns Risk and Time Horizon]] — Stock learning starts with risk, time horizon, and diversification because the same price movement can be tolerable for a long-term investor and unacceptable for a short-term trader.
- [[Stock Trading/Foundations/Stocks and Ownership|Stocks and Ownership]] — A stock share is an equity claim on a company: it can benefit from company success through price appreciation or dividends, but it can also lose value, including all invested capital.

## Book II: Market Plumbing And Account Rules

Learn how orders, brokers, settlement, cash accounts, and margin rules shape every trade.

- [[Stock Trading/Market Mechanics/Accounts Settlement and Margin|Accounts Settlement and Margin]] — Account type controls what money you can trade with, when trades settle, whether you can borrow, and how quickly a mistake can become an account restriction.
- [[Stock Trading/Market Mechanics/How Stock Markets Work|How Stock Markets Work]] — Stock markets match buyers and sellers through brokers, exchanges, market centers, quotes, and rules; the visible price is only a snapshot of executable supply and demand.
- [[Stock Trading/Market Mechanics/Order Types and Execution|Order Types and Execution]] — Order types control execution behavior; they do not turn an uncertain trade into a certain profit.

## Book III: Evidence Before Thesis

Use filings, fundamentals, price action, momentum, and volatility as evidence layers rather than signals to chase.

- [[Stock Trading/Analysis/Company Filing Worksheet|Company Filing Worksheet]] — Use this worksheet to turn one SEC filing into a bounded business thesis before any chart read, price target, or paper trade.
- [[Stock Trading/Analysis/Price Action Momentum and Volatility|Price Action Momentum and Volatility]] — Price action shows what buyers and sellers are doing, but it does not explain by itself why a move is happening or whether it is sustainable.
- [[Stock Trading/Analysis/Reading Company Filings and Fundamentals|Reading Company Filings and Fundamentals]] — Fundamental stock analysis starts with how the company makes money, what risks it faces, what its filings disclose, and what price investors are paying for the business.

## Book IV: Systematic Strategy Research

Separate quantitative research and algorithmic execution, then keep both paper-only until risk controls are explicit.

- [[Stock Trading/Strategies/Algorithmic Trading|Algorithmic Trading]] — Algorithmic trading uses software rules to generate, route, modify, or execute orders; it is an automation layer, not proof that the trading idea has an edge.
- [[Stock Trading/Strategies/Quantitative Trading|Quantitative Trading]] — Quantitative trading turns a market hypothesis into a data-defined, testable model; the model is evidence to inspect, not a machine that removes uncertainty.
