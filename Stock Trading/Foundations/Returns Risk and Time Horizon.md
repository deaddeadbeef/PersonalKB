# Returns Risk and Time Horizon

## One-Line Summary

Stock learning starts with risk, time horizon, and diversification because the same price movement can be tolerable for a long-term investor and unacceptable for a short-term trader.

## Investing Versus Trading

Investing usually means putting money into assets with the expectation of return over time. Trading narrows the focus toward entry, exit, timing, and shorter feedback loops. The distinction is not moral; it is operational. A long-term investor can be wrong for years before a thesis resolves. A short-term trader can be forced out in minutes by price, liquidity, account rules, or margin requirements.

The beginner mistake is to borrow long-term language to justify short-term behavior. If the plan has a stop, a target, a short holding period, or intraday attention, treat it as a trade and manage it with explicit risk controls.

## Risk Types to Know First

| Risk | Meaning for stock trading |
| --- | --- |
| Market risk | Broad market movement can pull even strong companies down |
| Business risk | Company-specific decisions, competition, or execution can hurt value |
| Liquidity risk | A position may be hard to exit at the expected price |
| Volatility risk | Price swings can trigger emotional or forced decisions |
| Concentration risk | Too much money in one stock or theme can dominate outcomes |
| Leverage risk | Borrowed money can amplify losses beyond the original deposit |

FINRA's risk guidance is the correct first anchor: every investment has some degree of risk, and stocks can lose value, including their entire value.

## Time Horizon

Time horizon is the period before the money is needed. A long horizon can make temporary volatility more tolerable because the investor has time to recover from drawdowns. A short horizon turns the same volatility into a practical threat because the money may need to be converted back to cash during a bad market.

Trading money should be money that can be lost without damaging housing, food, debt payments, emergency reserves, taxes, or other obligations.

## Diversification

Diversification spreads exposure across securities, industries, and asset classes so one failure does not dominate the whole portfolio. It does not guarantee a profit or prevent losses in a broad market decline. It is a damage-control tool, not a shield.

For this wiki, diversification is a prerequisite concept before individual-stock selection. If a single trade can damage the entire account, the problem is position sizing, not stock picking.

## Beginner Boundary

The first stock-trading curriculum should avoid:

- Margin.
- Short selling.
- Options.
- Leveraged ETFs.
- Illiquid micro-cap stocks.
- Unverified social-media tips.
- Earnings-event gambling without a written risk plan.
