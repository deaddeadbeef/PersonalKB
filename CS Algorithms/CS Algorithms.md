# CS Algorithms

Master index for the *CS Algorithms* knowledge base. Built around Thomas H. Cormen's *Algorithms Unlocked* (MIT Press, 2013) as the primary entry point — an accessible introduction covering correctness, asymptotic analysis, sorting, searching, graphs, strings, cryptography, compression, and complexity theory.
> 📚 **New here?** Start with the [[CS Algorithms — Learning Path|Learning Path]] for a guided, progressive tour.



---

## Foundations and Analysis

Core vocabulary and proof machinery used throughout every other topic: asymptotic notation, loop invariants, recurrences, the Master Theorem, dynamic programming, and the comparison-sort lower bound.

→ **[[Foundations and Analysis Overview]]**

---

## Sorting and Searching

Comparison sorts, non-comparison sorts, the $\Omega(n \lg n)$ lower bound, and binary search. Binary Search is covered under Sorting as a searching technique on sorted arrays.

→ **[[Sorting Overview]]**

---

## Graphs and Shortest Paths

Graph vocabulary, topological ordering, traversal (BFS/DFS), shortest-path algorithms, minimum spanning trees, and network flow. Start at the Graphs hub; sub-hubs route you to the right algorithm.

→ **[[Graphs Overview]]**

---

## Greedy Algorithms

The greedy paradigm: making locally optimal choices to achieve globally optimal solutions. Activity selection, fractional knapsack, and the greedy-choice property.

→ **[[Greedy Overview]]**

---

## Divide and Conquer

Breaking problems into independent subproblems, solving recursively, and combining results. Recurrence analysis via the Master Theorem.

→ **[[Divide and Conquer Overview — Domain]]**

---

## Strings

Dynamic-programming and failure-function algorithms for pattern matching, sequence alignment, and string distance.

→ **[[Strings Overview]]**

---

## Cryptography

Classical ciphers through modern public-key cryptography, pseudorandom generation, and hybrid encryption.

→ **[[Cryptography Overview]]**

---

## Data Compression

Lossless compression: variable-length codes, prefix-free codes, Huffman coding, run-length encoding, and LZW.

→ **[[Data Compression Overview]]**

---

## Complexity Theory

Tractability limits: P vs NP, NP-completeness and reductions, undecidability (Halting Problem), and approximation algorithms for intractable problems.

→ **[[Complexity Theory Overview]]**

---

## Backtracking

Systematic trial-and-error with pruning: constraint-satisfaction problems, state-space trees, and the N-Queens problem.

→ **[[Backtracking Overview — Domain]]**

---

## Algorithmic Techniques

Cross-cutting patterns that appear across domains: two pointers, sliding window, and amortized analysis for algorithms.

→ **[[Techniques Overview]]**
