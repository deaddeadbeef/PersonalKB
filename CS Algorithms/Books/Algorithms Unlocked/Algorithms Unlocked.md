# Algorithms Unlocked

**Author:** Thomas H. Cormen  
**Publisher:** MIT Press, 2013  
**Level:** Introductory / General reader  
**Source:** [[Cormen 2013 - Algorithms Unlocked]]

A concise, accessible introduction to algorithms — what they are, how to evaluate them, and how they power modern technology from GPS routing to cryptography and web search.
