## Daily Cook Notes

### Monday

- **Lunch:** Chipotle Chicken Burrito -- prepped Sunday, grab-and-go from fridge.
- **Dinner:** Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl (double batch) -- sear 400g chicken
  thigh with premixed teriyaki sauce (~15 min). Assemble over prepped rice
  with edamame and pickled carrot. Pack second serving for Tuesday lunch.

### Tuesday

- **Lunch:** Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl (leftover from Mon dinner).
- **Dinner:** Beef & Black Bean Crunchwrap (double batch) -- reheat prepped
  beef-bean mix, assemble 2 crunchwraps fresh, sear in pan or air fry
  (~12 min). Pack one for Wed lunch.

### Wednesday

- **Lunch:** Beef & Black Bean Crunchwrap (leftover from Tue dinner).
- **Dinner:** Lemon Herb Salmon Stir-Fry -- sear 200g salmon (thawed since
  Tue morning), stir-fry asparagus and cherry tomatoes, finish with lemon
  and dill (~12 min). **Eat fresh -- no leftovers for Thu lunch (fish).**

### Thursday

- **Lunch:** Turkish Lamb Wrap (independent recipe) -- brown 180g ground lamb
  with spices (~8 min). Assemble with prepped pickled onion, yogurt sauce,
  and lavash. Wrap and pack.
  *Alternatively: prep the lamb Wed evening and assemble Thu morning.*
- **Dinner:** Chicken Katsu Bento (double batch) -- start 1¼ cups fresh dry rice
  in the cooker (~660 g cooked, covers Thu–Sat). Bread and air-fry 440g chicken breast
  (~18 min, two batches in air fryer). Assemble 2 bentos with fresh rice,
  pickled cucumber, and edamame. Pack second bento for Friday lunch.

### Friday

- **Lunch:** Chicken Katsu Bento (leftover from Thu dinner).
- **Dinner:** Ginger Beef Stir-Fry -- sear 200g sliced beef (thawed from
  freezer) in wok with prepped vegetables and premixed sauce (~10 min).
  Serve over rice. Enjoy the start of the weekend.

### Saturday

- **Lunch:** Flex / eat out.
- **Dinner:** Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowl -- beef thawed Thu→Fri, marinated
  overnight Fri. Sear in wok (~5 min). Wilt spinach, assemble over rice with
  kimchi. Quick and deeply flavorful.

### Sunday

- **Lunch:** Flex / eat out.
- **Dinner:** Prep day for next week. Plan meals, shop, and batch-prep.
