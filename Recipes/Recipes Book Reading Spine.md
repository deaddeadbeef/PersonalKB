## Book I: Portable High-Protein Meals

Read by category, then choose meals by protein target, prep burden, and flavor profile.

- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Bento Boxes/Bento Boxes|Bento Boxes]] — This category is built around rice, protein, vegetables, and a compact sauce. It is best when you want a tidy lunch that reheats predictably and keeps textures separated.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Bento Boxes/Chicken Katsu Bento|Chicken Katsu Bento]] — Crispy chicken katsu made in the air fryer — no deep frying, no oven. The panko crust stays reasonably crispy even after refrigeration and reheating.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Bento Boxes/Miso Tofu & Egg Bento|Miso Tofu & Egg Bento]] — High-protein vegetarian bento that proves you don't need meat to hit decent protein numbers. Firm tofu pan-seared until golden then glazed with a quick miso-mirin sauce.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Bento Boxes/Sesame Tempeh & Edamame Bento|Sesame Tempeh & Edamame Bento]] — Tempeh is one of the highest-protein plant foods — 19 g per 100 g, beating tofu handily. Pan-frying gives it a nutty, crispy exterior, and the sesame-soy glaze makes it genuinely delicious (not just "healthy-tasting").
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Bento Boxes/Teriyaki Turkey Meatball Bento|Teriyaki Turkey Meatball Bento]] — Turkey meatballs are a meal-prep powerhouse — make a big batch on Sunday and portion through the week. The teriyaki glaze caramelizes in the pan, giving each meatball a sticky, savory shell.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Burritos & Wraps/Burritos & Wraps|Burritos & Wraps]] — This category is built around tortilla, protein, vegetables, sauce, and a starch or bean base. It is best when portability matters more than plated presentation.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Burritos & Wraps/Chipotle Chicken Burrito|Chipotle Chicken Burrito]] — High-protein burrito with smoky chipotle heat. The filling reheats perfectly in the microwave, making it ideal for next-day lunches. Wrapping technique keeps everything compact and leak-free for transport.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Burritos & Wraps/Greek Chicken Souvlaki Wrap|Greek Chicken Souvlaki Wrap]] — Greek-flavored chicken in a pita — bright, herby, and filling without being heavy. The tzatziki keeps it cool and creamy.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Burritos & Wraps/Peri-Peri Turkey Wrap|Peri-Peri Turkey Wrap]] — A spicy, tangy wrap with high protein from turkey breast. The peri-peri seasoning gives Nando's-style heat, and the crunchy slaw keeps it fresh. Best assembled day-of so the tortilla doesn't get soggy from the slaw.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Burritos & Wraps/Turkish Lamb Wrap|Turkish Lamb Wrap]] — Mediterranean-style wrap with deep lamb flavor from cumin, sumac, and Aleppo pepper. The cucumber-yogurt sauce adds freshness and the pickled onion gives acidity that cuts through the richness.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Crunchwraps/Beef & Black Bean Crunchwrap|Beef & Black Bean Crunchwrap]] — Homemade crunchwrap with well-seasoned ground beef and black beans, a tostada shell in the middle for signature crunch. Seared in a pan or air fryer for a crispy exterior.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Crunchwraps/Chicken Tikka Crunchwrap|Chicken Tikka Crunchwrap]] — Indian-fusion crunchwrap where yogurt-marinated tikka chicken replaces the usual taco meat. Mint chutney and a tostada shell in the middle keep the texture interesting.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Crunchwraps/Crunchwraps|Crunchwraps]] — This category is built around large wrap, crisp center, seasoned filling, and a sealing fold. It is best when you want a high-protein meal that still feels like fast food.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Crunchwraps/Southwest Turkey Taco Crunchwrap|Southwest Turkey Taco Crunchwrap]] — A crunchwrap with Southwest taco flavors — the tostada layer stays crispy inside the sealed tortilla even after reheating. Ground turkey keeps the protein high and the fat moderate.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Crunchwraps/Spicy Gochujang Tofu Crunchwrap|Spicy Gochujang Tofu Crunchwrap]] — A vegetarian crunchwrap with real flavor depth from gochujang and kimchi. Pressing and pan-frying the tofu gives it a crispy exterior that holds up inside the sealed tortilla.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Rice Bowls/Chicken Shawarma Rice Bowl|Chicken Shawarma Rice Bowl]] — Middle-Eastern shawarma flavors in a meal-prep-friendly rice bowl. The warm spice blend (cumin, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon) makes the kitchen smell incredible, and the garlic sauce ties everything together.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Rice Bowls/Harissa Lamb Kofta Rice Bowl|Harissa Lamb Kofta Rice Bowl]] — Kofta — hand-formed spiced lamb patties — cook fast in a pan and pack intense flavor. The harissa adds North African heat, and the mint yogurt cools it down.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Rice Bowls/Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowl|Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowl]] — Quick-cooking bulgogi-style beef with a soy-sesame-pear marinade that tenderizes and adds deep savory-sweet flavor. Warm beef, rice, and cold kimchi on the side makes every bite interesting. Packs well and reheats great.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Rice Bowls/Rice Bowls|Rice Bowls]] — This category is built around rice or grain, protein, vegetables, sauce, and garnish. It is best when you want flexible leftovers and easy macro adjustment.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Rice Bowls/Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl|Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl]] — Pan-seared chicken thigh glazed in a quick homemade teriyaki sauce. Served over steamed rice with edamame and pickled carrot for color and crunch.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Skillets & Stir-Fries/Ginger Beef Stir-Fry|Ginger Beef Stir-Fry]] — Fast wok stir-fry with thinly sliced beef, fresh ginger, garlic, and crisp vegetables. The sauce is a simple soy-hoisin-ginger combo that coats everything in glossy umami.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Skillets & Stir-Fries/Ginger Sesame Tofu & Edamame Stir-Fry|Ginger Sesame Tofu & Edamame Stir-Fry]] — A fast vegetarian stir-fry with genuine protein substance. The tofu gets crispy in the wok, and the ginger-sesame sauce coats everything in an umami glaze.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Skillets & Stir-Fries/Lemon Herb Salmon Stir-Fry|Lemon Herb Salmon Stir-Fry]] — Light, fresh dinner that showcases salmon as the star protein. Seared in pieces then gently tossed with asparagus, cherry tomatoes, lemon juice, and fresh dill.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Skillets & Stir-Fries/Skillets & Stir-Fries|Skillets & Stir-Fries]] — This category is built around hot pan, quick protein, vegetables, sauce, and a rice/noodle pairing. It is best when speed and fresh texture matter.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Subs & Sandwiches/Grilled Chicken Pesto Sub|Grilled Chicken Pesto Sub]] — Simple, protein-heavy sub that comes together fast when chicken is prepped ahead. Basil pesto brings big flavor without effort, and jarred roasted red peppers add sweetness.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Subs & Sandwiches/Hot Honey Turkey Cutlet Sub|Hot Honey Turkey Cutlet Sub]] — The Nashville hot chicken sandwich trend, adapted to turkey and the air fryer. The panko crust gets properly crispy without deep frying, and the hot honey (honey + cayenne) gives that sweet-spicy kick.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Subs & Sandwiches/Philly Cheesesteak Sub|Philly Cheesesteak Sub]] — Classic Philly-inspired cheesesteak with thinly sliced beef sirloin, sautéed peppers and onions, and melted provolone. The thin beef sears in minutes, making it one of the fastest dinners in the rotation.
- [[Recipes/Recipe Library/Subs & Sandwiches/Subs & Sandwiches|Subs & Sandwiches]] — This category is built around bread, high-protein filling, sauce, crunch, and a packable wrap. It is best when you want cold or quick-reheat lunches with minimal containers.
