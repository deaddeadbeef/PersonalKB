# Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowl

![[korean-beef-bulgogi-bowl.jpg]]

> Thinly sliced marinated beef over rice with sesame spinach and kimchi.

## Why Make This

Quick-cooking bulgogi-style beef with a soy-sesame-pear marinade that
tenderizes and adds deep savory-sweet flavor. Warm beef, rice, and cold
kimchi on the side makes every bite interesting. Packs well and reheats great.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g beef sirloin, thinly sliced (2–3 mm)

**Bulgogi marinade**
- 15 ml soy sauce (~1 tbsp)
- 5 ml sesame oil (~1 tsp)
- 30 ml apple juice (~2 tbsp)
- 5 g garlic, minced (~1 clove)
- Pinch of black pepper

**Base**
- 180 g cooked white rice (~⅓ cup dry)

**Sides**
- 80 g spinach (fresh — wilts down to about 40 g)
- 50 g kimchi
- 5 g sesame seeds (~1 tsp)
- 15 g green onion, sliced (~1 stalk)

## Before You Start

- Partially freeze the beef for 30 min before slicing — makes thin slicing much easier.
- If marinated overnight (ideal), skip step 2 and go straight to searing.
- Start rice first.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and start rice in the cooker. It will be ready by the time you need it.

2. **Make bulgogi marinade and coat beef** (~3 min). Whisk soy sauce, sesame oil, apple juice, minced garlic, and black pepper in a bowl. Add the thinly sliced beef and toss to coat every piece. Let sit at least 10 min. Overnight in the fridge is better — the apple juice tenderizes the meat.

3. **Get the wok smoking hot** (~2 min). Set the wok over high heat. Wait until you see the faintest wisp of smoke. This is essential — a cold wok steams the beef instead of searing it.

4. **Sear the beef** (~4 min). Lift the beef out of the marinade (shake off excess). Spread in a single layer — work in two batches if needed. Don't touch it for 1.5–2 min to get a brown crust. Flip. Sear 1–2 min more. The edges should be caramelized. Set aside on a plate.

5. **Wilt the spinach** (~2 min). In the same wok (still hot), add spinach and a splash of water. Toss for 60 seconds until just wilted — it should still be bright green. Remove from heat. Drizzle with a tiny amount of sesame oil and a few sesame seeds.

6. **Assemble the bowl** (~2 min). Rice on the bottom. Arrange sliced bulgogi beef on one side, sesame spinach on another, kimchi on the third. Top with sliced green onion and remaining sesame seeds.

## Pack / Serve

Keep kimchi in a small separate container so its juices don't bleed into the
rice. The rest packs together well.

## Reheat

- Microwave 2 min at full power. Keep kimchi in a separate small container
  and add cold after reheating — it tastes better that way.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Slice beef thinly (partially freeze for 30 min first —
  makes slicing easier). Marinate overnight. Cook rice in bulk.
- **Daily cook:** Sear beef in wok (~5 min). Wilt spinach (~2 min). Assemble.
- **Fridge life:** Marinated raw beef 2 days. Cooked rice 4 days. Kimchi
  lasts indefinitely (fermented).

## Variations

- Swap sirloin for flank steak or skirt steak (similar macros).
- Add a fried egg for more protein (+70 cal, +6 g P).
- Substitute cauliflower rice for lower carbs (−40 g C, −180 cal).
