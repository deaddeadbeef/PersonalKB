# Chicken Shawarma Rice Bowl

![[chicken-shawarma-rice-bowl.jpg]]

> Shawarma-spiced chicken thigh over rice with pickled turnip, cucumber-tomato salad, and garlic sauce.

## Why Make This

Middle-Eastern shawarma flavors in a meal-prep-friendly rice bowl. The
warm spice blend (cumin, coriander, turmeric, cinnamon) makes the kitchen
smell incredible, and the garlic sauce ties everything together. Reheats
beautifully — the chicken stays juicy because thigh meat holds moisture
better than breast.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 220 g boneless, skinless chicken thigh (raw weight)

**Shawarma seasoning**
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp allspice
- Salt, pepper
- 5 ml olive oil

**Base + toppings**
- 150 g cooked white rice
- 40 g cucumber, diced
- 40 g tomato, diced
- 20 g pickled turnip (or pickled red onion)
- 15 g garlic sauce (toum) or 12 g mayo + 1 minced garlic clove + lemon squeeze

**Garnish**
- 5 g fresh parsley, chopped (optional)

## Before You Start

- Start rice in the rice cooker first — it runs unattended.
- Mix the shawarma spice blend in a small bowl.
- Dice cucumber and tomato, set aside.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and cook rice in the rice cooker. Will be done before the chicken.

2. **Season the chicken** (~1 min). Rub the thigh(s) with olive oil, then coat with the shawarma spice blend on all sides.

3. **Cook the chicken** (~10 min). Heat a pan over medium-high. Lay the thigh flat, presentation side down. Cook 5 min until deep golden with charred edges. Flip. Cook 4–5 min until 74°C / 165°F internal.

4. **Rest and slice** (~3 min). Rest on a cutting board 2–3 min. Slice into thin strips against the grain.

5. **Make the salad** (~2 min). Toss diced cucumber and tomato together. No dressing needed — the garlic sauce does the work.

6. **Assemble the bowl** (~2 min). Rice as the base. Fan the chicken strips on one side. Cucumber-tomato salad on another. Pickled turnip in a corner. Drizzle garlic sauce over the chicken. Sprinkle parsley if using.

## Pack / Serve

Pack in a compartmented container — rice and chicken together, salad
and pickles in a separate section. Garlic sauce in a small pot on the
side if you prefer freshness.

## Reheat

- Microwave the rice + chicken portion 2 min at full power. Add the
  cold salad and pickles after reheating — they're meant to be cold
  as a contrast.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Cook chicken in bulk with shawarma seasoning, slice
  and portion. Cook rice in rice cooker, portion. Make garlic sauce.
  Pickled turnip keeps for weeks.
- **Daily assembly:** 3 min — scoop rice, fan chicken, add toppings.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked chicken 4 days. Rice 4 days. Fresh salad
  veggies 3 days (dice day-of for best crunch).

## Variations

- Use chicken breast for leaner option — cook to exactly 74°C to avoid dryness.
- Swap rice for cauliflower rice to cut 120 kcal.
- Add 40 g hummus (+70 cal, +3 g P) as a base layer.
- Use lamb leg steaks for a richer shawarma bowl (see Harissa Lamb Kofta
  Rice Bowl for a lamb option).
