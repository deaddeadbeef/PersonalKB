# Harissa Lamb Kofta Rice Bowl

![[harissa-lamb-kofta-rice-bowl.jpg]]

> Spiced lamb kofta patties over rice with pickled onion, mint yogurt, and a harissa drizzle.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 50 g | 699 kcal | ~30 min | pan + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Kofta — hand-formed spiced lamb patties — cook fast in a pan and pack
intense flavor. The harissa adds North African heat, and the mint yogurt
cools it down. Lamb is fattier than chicken so the calorie count is higher,
but the protein is solid and the flavor payoff is worth the trade.

## Ingredients

**Kofta**
- 200 g ground lamb (lean if available, ~15% fat)
- 15 g harissa paste
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp coriander
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
- Salt, pepper

**Base + toppings**
- 150 g cooked white rice
- 30 g plain Greek yogurt
- 5 g fresh mint, chopped (or ½ tsp dried mint)
- 20 g pickled red onion
- 30 g cucumber, diced

**Cooking + garnish**
- 5 ml olive oil
- 5 g harissa paste (for drizzle)
- Lemon wedge

## Before You Start

- Start rice in the rice cooker first.
- Mix kofta ingredients in a bowl — hands work best. Form into patties
  before heating the pan.
- Make the mint yogurt: stir chopped mint into Greek yogurt with a pinch
  of salt.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and cook rice in the rice cooker.

2. **Mix the kofta** (~3 min). In a bowl, combine ground lamb, 15 g harissa paste, cumin, coriander, garlic powder, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix with your hands until the spices are evenly distributed. Don't over-mix — keeps the texture tender.

3. **Form patties** (~2 min). Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions. Roll each into a ball, then flatten into thick oval patties about 2 cm thick. Slightly thicker than you think — they'll shrink.

4. **Cook the kofta** (~10 min). Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high. Add the patties. Cook 4–5 min undisturbed until the bottom has a dark crust. Flip. Cook 4–5 min more. Done when firm to the touch and no longer pink inside. Rest 2 min.

5. **Make mint yogurt** (~1 min). Stir chopped mint (or dried mint) into Greek yogurt with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of lemon.

6. **Assemble the bowl** (~2 min). Rice as the base. Arrange kofta patties on top (halve them to show the interior). Dollop mint yogurt. Scatter pickled red onion and diced cucumber. Drizzle the remaining 5 g harissa thinned with a drop of olive oil.

## Pack / Serve

Pack rice and kofta together (they reheat as one). Mint yogurt, pickled
onion, and cucumber in a separate compartment — add after reheating.

## Reheat

- Microwave rice + kofta 2 min at full power. The lamb fat keeps the
  patties moist on reheat.
- Add cold toppings (yogurt, pickles, cucumber) after microwaving.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Mix and form kofta patties, cook in bulk. Store 4
  per container. Cook rice and portion. Make pickled red onion.
- **Daily assembly:** 3 min — reheat rice + kofta, add cold toppings.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked kofta 4 days. Rice 4 days. Mint yogurt 3 days.
  Pickled onion 7 days.

## Variations

- Swap lamb for lean ground beef (90/10) — less distinctly kofta but
  lower fat.
- Add 50 g roasted red pepper for sweetness (+15 cal).
- Use cauliflower rice to cut ~120 kcal.
- Swap harissa for ras el hanout for a more aromatic, less spicy version.
