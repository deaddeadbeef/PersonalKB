# Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowl

![[teriyaki-chicken-rice-bowl.jpg]]

> Caramelized teriyaki chicken thigh over steamed rice with quick-pickled veg.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |

| 61 g | 846 kcal | ~25 min | pan + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Pan-seared chicken thigh glazed in a quick homemade teriyaki sauce. Served
over steamed rice with edamame and pickled carrot for color and crunch. The
sauce keeps the rice from drying out during reheating — one of the best
pack-ahead bowls in the rotation.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g chicken thigh, boneless, skinless

**Base**
- 180 g cooked white rice (~⅓ cup dry)

**Teriyaki sauce**
- 15 ml soy sauce (~1 tbsp)
- 15 ml mirin (~1 tbsp)
- 10 g honey (~1 tsp heaping)

**Sides**
- 50 g edamame, shelled, cooked
- 40 g carrot, shredded and pickled (rice vinegar + pinch of sugar)
- 5 g sesame seeds (~1 tsp)

**Cooking**
- 5 ml vegetable oil
- Salt, pepper

## Before You Start

- Start rice first — it needs 15–20 min in the cooker.
- If pickled carrot and edamame are prepped from Sunday, just pull from fridge.
- Whisk the teriyaki sauce together so it's ready to pour.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse ⅓ cup dry rice until water runs mostly clear. Add to rice cooker with appropriate water. Start it — you won't touch it again for 18 min.

2. **Whisk the teriyaki sauce** (~1 min). In a small bowl, stir together soy sauce, mirin, and honey until the honey dissolves. Set aside.

3. **Prep the chicken** (~1 min). Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels — this is essential for a good sear. Season both sides with salt and pepper.

4. **Sear the chicken** (~9 min). Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Place chicken flat-side down (the smoother side). Don't move it for 5 minutes — let it develop a deep golden crust. Flip. Cook 4 min more until cooked through (no pink in the center, juices run clear).

5. **Glaze with teriyaki** (~3 min). Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour the teriyaki sauce into the pan. It will bubble immediately. Spoon the sauce over the chicken as it thickens, flipping once. Cook 2–3 min until the sauce is syrupy and coats the chicken in a glossy layer.

6. **Slice the chicken** (~2 min). Move chicken to a cutting board. Rest 2 min. Slice against the grain into 1 cm strips. You'll see the caramelized glaze on the outside.

7. **Assemble the bowl** (~2 min). Rice on the bottom. Fan the sliced chicken on top. Place edamame and pickled carrot on the sides. Drizzle any remaining pan sauce over everything. Sprinkle sesame seeds.

## Pack / Serve

Transfer to a container with a secure lid. The sauce keeps things moist so
it packs well for hours. No separate sauce container needed.

## Reheat

- Microwave with lid slightly ajar, 2 min at full power. The teriyaki
  sauce keeps everything moist. Stir the rice halfway through if possible.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Cook rice in bulk (3 cups dry). Pickle carrots (shred,
  toss with rice vinegar and pinch of sugar — lasts all week). Cook
  edamame and portion.
- **Daily cook:** Sear and glaze chicken fresh (~12 min). Assemble bowl.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked rice 4 days. Pickled carrot 7 days. Edamame 4 days.

## Variations

- Swap chicken thigh for chicken breast (leaner: +10 g P, −14 g F).
- Add a fried egg on top for extra protein (+70 cal, +6 g P).
- Use brown rice for more fiber (similar macros, nuttier flavor).
