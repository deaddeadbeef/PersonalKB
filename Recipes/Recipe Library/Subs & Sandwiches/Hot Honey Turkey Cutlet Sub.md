# Hot Honey Turkey Cutlet Sub

![[hot-honey-turkey-cutlet-sub.jpg]]

> Air-fried panko turkey cutlet on a sub roll with hot honey, pickles, and pepper jack.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 77 g | 871 kcal | ~25 min | air fryer | ✅ assemble day-of |

## Why Make This

The Nashville hot chicken sandwich trend, adapted to turkey and the air
fryer. The panko crust gets properly crispy without deep frying, and the
hot honey (honey + cayenne) gives that sweet-spicy kick. Turkey breast
keeps the protein sky-high. Best assembled fresh — the crust softens
after a few hours on the sub roll.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 220 g turkey breast cutlet (boneless, skinless, raw weight)

**Breading station**
- 15 g all-purpose flour
- 1 large egg (~50 g), beaten
- 30 g panko breadcrumbs
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder

**Sub assembly**
- 1 sub roll (~90 g) or hoagie roll
- 20 g pepper jack cheese, sliced
- 20 g dill pickles, sliced
- 15 g lettuce (romaine or iceberg)

**Hot honey**
- 15 g honey
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- Tiny pinch of salt

**Cooking**
- 2-sec spray of cooking oil spray
- Salt, pepper

## Before You Start

- If turkey cutlet is thick (over 2 cm), butterfly or pound to 1.5 cm
  even thickness.
- Set up the breading station: flour → egg → seasoned panko.
- Mix hot honey in a small bowl.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Prep the turkey cutlet** (~2 min). Season both sides with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. If thick, butterfly: place hand flat on top, slice horizontally to open like a book. Pound to even 1.5 cm thickness using a rolling pin or pan bottom.

2. **Set up breading** (~2 min). Plate 1: flour. Plate 2: beaten egg. Plate 3: panko mixed with smoked paprika and garlic powder.

3. **Bread the cutlet** (~2 min). Dredge in flour, shake off excess. Dip in egg, let excess drip. Press firmly into seasoned panko on both sides. Pat the crumbs in.

4. **Preheat the air fryer** (~2 min). Set to 200°C / 400°F. Spray the basket.

5. **Air fry the cutlet** (~12 min). Place breaded cutlet in a single layer. Spray the top lightly. Cook 6 min. Open, flip carefully. Spray new top. Cook 5–6 min more until golden and 74°C / 165°F internal.

6. **Make the hot honey** (~1 min). Stir cayenne and salt into honey. Microwave 10 sec to thin it slightly (optional but helps drizzling).

7. **Assemble the sub** (~2 min). Split the sub roll. Layer: lettuce on the bottom, air-fried cutlet, pepper jack on top of the hot cutlet (it'll melt slightly from residual heat), dill pickles, and drizzle hot honey over everything. Close the roll.

## Pack / Serve

For lunch transport, pack the cutlet and sub roll separately. The cutlet
stays crispier when not touching bread. Assemble at work. If pre-assembled,
wrap tightly in foil — accept some crust softening.

## Reheat

Designed to be eaten at room temp or freshly assembled. If the cutlet
is cold from the fridge, microwave 45 sec, then air fry 2 min at 200°C
to re-crisp (if air fryer is available). Otherwise, room-temp cutlet
on a fresh roll is perfectly good.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Bread the turkey cutlets (do NOT fry yet — store
  breaded and uncooked on parchment in a sealed container, separated
  by parchment paper). They hold 2 days breaded, uncooked.
- **Daily cook:** Air fry fresh in the morning (~12 min). Or cook at
  night, refrigerate, and re-crisp 2 min in the air fryer before packing.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked cutlets 3 days (crust declines after day 2).

## Variations

- Use chicken breast instead of turkey — same method.
- Swap pepper jack for provolone for milder heat.
- Add 30 g coleslaw on the sub for crunch (+40 cal).
- Increase cayenne in the hot honey to ½ tsp for serious heat.
