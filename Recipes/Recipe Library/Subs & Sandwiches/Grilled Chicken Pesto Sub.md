# Grilled Chicken Pesto Sub

![[grilled-chicken-pesto-sub.jpg]]

> Sliced chicken breast with basil pesto, mozzarella, and roasted red pepper on a sub roll.

## Why Make This

Simple, protein-heavy sub that comes together fast when chicken is prepped
ahead. Basil pesto brings big flavor without effort, and jarred roasted red
peppers add sweetness. The chicken stays moist during reheating thanks to the
pesto coating. Highest protein-per-calorie sub in the rotation.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g chicken breast, boneless, skinless

**Bread**
- 1 sub roll (~85 g)

**Toppings**
- 20 g basil pesto (store-bought is fine)
- 30 g mozzarella, sliced
- 40 g roasted red pepper, jarred, drained
- 15 g baby arugula

**Cooking**
- 5 ml olive oil
- Salt, pepper, pinch of garlic powder

## Before You Start

- If chicken was cooked in Sunday bulk prep, slice it and skip to step 2.
- Have all toppings laid out for fast assembly.
- Pat the chicken dry before seasoning — moisture = steaming, not searing.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Cook the chicken** (~14 min). Season breast with salt, pepper, and a pinch of garlic powder on both sides. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Lay the breast flat. Cook 5–6 min without moving — you want a golden crust. Flip. Cook 5–6 min more until the thickest part reads 74°C / 165°F. Rest on a cutting board for 3 min.

2. **Slice the chicken** (~1 min). Cut on the bias (diagonally) into thin strips, about 5 mm thick. The diagonal cut gives you wider pieces that cover more of the roll.

3. **Prep the roll** (~1 min). Slice the sub roll lengthwise. Spread pesto generously on both cut sides — this serves as your sauce and moisture barrier.

4. **Layer the fillings** (~2 min). On the bottom half: mozzarella slices first (they'll catch any warmth from the chicken), then sliced chicken in a single overlapping layer, roasted red pepper strips, and baby arugula on top.

5. **Close and pack** (~1 min). Press the top half down gently. Wrap tightly in foil for transport.

## Pack / Serve

The pesto-on-both-sides trick creates a moisture barrier that keeps the bread
from going soggy for hours. If packing for later, keep arugula separate and
add at lunchtime for maximum freshness.

## Reheat

- Remove arugula (add back after). Wrap sub in damp paper towel, microwave
  90 sec at 70% power. The pesto keeps the chicken moist.
- Arugula can be stored separately and added cold after reheating for a
  fresh element.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Cook chicken breast in bulk, slice and portion. Chicken
  stays moist for 3–4 days when sliced and stored with a drizzle of olive oil.
- **Daily assembly:** Spread pesto, layer fillings, wrap in foil. ~5 min.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked sliced chicken 4 days. Jarred roasted peppers
  last weeks in the fridge. Pesto 5–7 days once opened.

## Variations

- Swap pesto for sun-dried tomato pesto for a different flavor profile.
- Add 30 g sliced avocado for healthy fats (+50 cal, +5 g F).
- Use a ciabatta roll instead of a sub roll for a crustier bread.
