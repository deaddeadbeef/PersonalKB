# Philly Cheesesteak Sub

![[philly-cheesesteak-sub.jpg]]

> Thinly sliced beef with melted provolone, peppers, and onions on a hoagie roll.

## Why Make This

Classic Philly-inspired cheesesteak with thinly sliced beef sirloin, sautéed
peppers and onions, and melted provolone. The thin beef sears in minutes,
making it one of the fastest dinners in the rotation. Wraps well in foil and
reheats decently.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g beef sirloin, thinly sliced (2–3 mm)

**Bread**
- 1 hoagie roll (~85 g)

**Toppings**
- 30 g provolone cheese, sliced
- 60 g green bell pepper, sliced into strips
- 60 g yellow onion, sliced into half-moons

**Cooking**
- 10 ml olive oil (split: half for veg, half for beef)
- Salt, pepper

## Before You Start

- Partially freeze beef for 30 min first — this makes paper-thin slicing possible.
- Slice all vegetables before you start cooking. Once the pan is hot, everything moves fast.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Slice the beef paper-thin** (~3 min). If not already sliced, partially freeze the sirloin for 30 min. Slice against the grain as thin as you can — 2–3 mm. Thinner = more tender and faster to cook.

2. **Sauté the peppers and onions** (~6 min). Heat half the olive oil (5 ml) in a pan over medium-high. Add sliced peppers and onions. Cook 5–6 min, stirring occasionally, until softened with slight char on the edges. They should be wilted but not mushy. Remove and set aside on a plate.

3. **Sear the beef** (~4 min). Add remaining olive oil to the same pan. Spread beef slices flat in a single layer — work in 2 batches if the pan is crowded. Let sit without stirring for 1.5 min to get a brown crust. Flip or stir. Cook 1–2 min more. Season with salt and pepper. Chop the beef up a bit with the edge of a spatula as it finishes.

4. **Combine and melt cheese** (~1 min). Return peppers and onions to the pan. Toss together with the beef. Lay provolone slices on top. Cover the pan with a lid (or foil) for 30 seconds until the cheese is melted and gooey.

5. **Build the sub** (~1 min). Slice the hoagie roll lengthwise (don't cut all the way through — hinge it). Pile in the beef-cheese-pepper mixture. Press gently to compact.

## Pack / Serve

Wrap tightly in aluminum foil. The foil holds the sub together and keeps the
steam in, which helps melt the cheese further.

## Reheat

- Wrap sub in a damp paper towel. Microwave 90 sec at 70% power. The
  paper towel keeps the bread from getting tough.
- Best reheated: separate the filling from the roll. Microwave filling
  90 sec, toast roll separately if possible, reassemble.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Slice beef (freeze slightly first) and portion into 200 g
  bags. Slice peppers and onions, store in a container.
- **Daily cook:** Sauté everything and assemble (~12 min total). Fast weeknight
  meal.
- **Fridge life:** Sliced raw beef 2 days (freeze portions for later in the week).
  Sliced peppers/onions 4 days.

## Variations

- Swap provolone for American cheese (more melt) or Cheez Whiz (classic Philly).
- Add mushrooms (60 g sliced, sauté with the peppers) for umami (+10 cal).
- Use a whole wheat hoagie roll for more fiber.
