# Chicken Tikka Crunchwrap

![[chicken-tikka-crunchwrap.jpg]]

> Tikka-spiced chicken thigh with mint chutney sealed in a crispy tortilla.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 63 g | 888 kcal | ~30 min | pan + air fryer | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Indian-fusion crunchwrap where yogurt-marinated tikka chicken replaces the
usual taco meat. Mint chutney and a tostada shell in the middle keep the
texture interesting. The yogurt marinade keeps the chicken moist through
reheating, so it works great as a next-day lunch.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 180 g chicken thigh, boneless, skinless

**Marinade**
- 30 g Greek yogurt, 2%
- 1 tsp garam masala, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp paprika, pinch cayenne, salt

**Wrap + crunch layer**
- 1 flour tortilla, 12" (~95 g)
- 1 tostada shell (~14 g)

**Toppings**
- 30 g red onion, sliced into thin rings
- 30 g mozzarella, shredded
- 40 g tomato, diced
- 20 g mint chutney (store-bought jar)

**Cooking**
- 5 ml olive oil

## Before You Start

- Best if chicken is marinated overnight (Sunday prep). If not, 15 min minimum.
- If using precooked tikka chicken from the fridge, reheat in the microwave (60 sec) and skip to step 3.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Marinate the chicken** (~2 min active, 15+ min rest). Mix yogurt with garam masala (1 tsp), turmeric (½ tsp), cumin (½ tsp), paprika (½ tsp), a pinch of cayenne, and salt. Coat the chicken thighs thoroughly. Refrigerate at least 15 minutes — overnight is ideal.

2. **Cook the chicken** (~12 min). Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Lay the marinated thighs flat. Cook 5–6 min without disturbing — you want char marks from the yogurt marinade. Flip. Cook 5–6 min more. The marinade will form a golden crust. Rest 2 minutes, then dice into 1.5 cm pieces.

3. **Build the crunchwrap** (~3 min). Lay the tortilla flat. Pile diced chicken in the center. Top with red onion rings, diced tomato, and shredded mozzarella. Place the tostada shell on top. Spread mint chutney on the tostada.

4. **Fold into a hexagon** (~2 min). Starting from one edge, fold the tortilla up and over the tostada shell. Work your way around, creating 5–6 pleats. Press gently to compact.

5. **Sear until golden** (~5 min). Flip seam-side down into a dry pan over medium heat. Press gently. Cook 2–3 min until the bottom is golden and crispy. Flip. Cook the other side 2–3 min.

6. **Air fryer alternative** (~5 min). Instead of step 5: place seam-side down in the air fryer at 190°C / 375°F for 4–5 min, flipping once halfway.

## Pack / Serve

Let cool 2 minutes. Wrap in foil for transport. The flat shape packs well.
Cut in half for easier eating.

## Reheat

- Microwave 90 sec at full power. The yogurt marinade keeps the chicken
  moist even after reheating.
- For crispier shell: microwave 60 sec then air fry 2 min at 190°C.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Marinate chicken thighs overnight in yogurt-tikka spice.
  Cook them all Sunday, dice, and portion into 180 g containers.
- **Daily assembly:** Assemble and sear fresh (~8 min).
- **Fridge life:** Cooked tikka chicken 4 days. Mint chutney lasts weeks
  (store-bought, jarred).

## Variations

- Swap chicken thigh for chicken breast (leaner, +8 g P, −12 g F per serving).
- Add 30 g pickled red cabbage for crunch and color.
- Use naan bread instead of a tortilla for a different texture (adjust macros).
