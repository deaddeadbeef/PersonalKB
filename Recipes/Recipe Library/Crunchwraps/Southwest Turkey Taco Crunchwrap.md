# Southwest Turkey Taco Crunchwrap

![[southwest-turkey-taco-crunchwrap.jpg]]

> Taco-seasoned ground turkey with corn, black beans, cheese, and a crispy tostada layer in a sealed tortilla.

## Quick Look

## Why Make This

A crunchwrap with Southwest taco flavors — the tostada layer stays crispy
inside the sealed tortilla even after reheating. Ground turkey keeps the
protein high and the fat moderate. The black beans and corn add bulk and
fiber without piling on calories.

## Ingredients

**Protein + fillings**
- 200 g ground turkey (93% lean)
- 60 g black beans, canned, drained and rinsed
- 40 g corn kernels (canned or frozen, thawed)
- 1 tostada shell (~15 g)

**Wrap + toppings**
- 1 flour tortilla, 12" (~95 g)
- 25 g cheddar cheese, shredded
- 20 g salsa
- 15 g sour cream

**Seasoning**
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika
- Salt, pepper
- 5 ml olive oil

## Before You Start

- Drain and rinse the black beans. Thaw corn if frozen.
- Mix the dry spices in a small bowl so they're ready to go.
- Have the tostada shell, tortilla, cheese, salsa, and sour cream set out.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Brown the turkey** (~7 min). Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high. Add ground turkey, breaking it into small crumbles with a spatula. Cook 5–7 min, stirring occasionally, until no pink remains and you see some browning.

2. **Season and add fillings** (~3 min). Add chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper to the turkey. Stir well. Add black beans and corn. Cook 2 min until everything is heated through and well combined. Remove to a bowl.

3. **Build the crunchwrap** (~3 min). Lay the tortilla flat. Spoon the turkey-bean-corn mixture into the center. Top with shredded cheddar. Place the tostada shell flat on top (it acts as a structural divider). Spread sour cream on the tostada, then add salsa on top. Fold the edges of the tortilla up and over, pleating as you go to seal the center. You should end up with a flat hexagonal package.

4. **Toast the crunchwrap** (~4 min). Place the crunchwrap seam-side down in a dry pan over medium heat. Press gently with a spatula. Cook 2 min until golden. Carefully flip. Cook 2 min more until the other side is golden and crisp.

5. **Rest and cut** (~1 min). Let stand 1 min, then cut in half to check the seal.

## Pack / Serve

Wrap in foil for transport. The sealed construction travels well —
no dripping or falling apart. Can be eaten at room temperature or
reheated.

## Reheat

- Microwave 90 sec at full power. For crispier exterior, follow with
  30 sec per side in a dry pan if available.
- From fridge, add 30 sec to microwave time.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Brown turkey with seasoning in bulk, portion into
  containers. Drain beans, portion corn.
- **Daily assembly:** ~10 min. Layer, fold, toast. Fastest if filling
  is already cooked.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked turkey filling 4 days. Assembled crunchwraps
  hold well 1–2 days in the fridge (toast again to recrisp).

## Variations

- Swap turkey for lean ground beef (90/10) — slightly higher fat.
- Add 30 g pickled jalapeños for more heat.
- Use a whole-wheat tortilla for extra fiber.
- Swap sour cream for plain Greek yogurt (+protein, similar tang).
