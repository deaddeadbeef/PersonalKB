# Beef & Black Bean Crunchwrap

![[beef-black-bean-crunchwrap.jpg]]

> Seasoned ground beef and black beans sealed in a crispy tortilla hexagon.

## Quick Look

## Why Make This

Homemade crunchwrap with well-seasoned ground beef and black beans, a tostada
shell in the middle for signature crunch. Seared in a pan or air fryer for a
crispy exterior. The flat shape reheats evenly in the microwave and holds
together for transport.

## Ingredients

**Protein + fillings**
- 180 g ground beef, 90/10
- 60 g black beans, canned, drained and rinsed

**Wrap + crunch layer**
- 1 flour tortilla, 12" (~95 g)
- 1 tostada shell (~14 g)

**Toppings**
- 30 g cheddar cheese, shredded
- 20 g sour cream
- 40 g tomato, diced
- 20 g shredded lettuce

**Spices**
- 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, pepper

## Before You Start

- If beef-bean mixture is prepped from Sunday, just reheat in the microwave (90 sec) and skip to step 3.
- Set out the tostada shell, tortilla, and toppings so assembly goes fast.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Brown the beef** (~6 min). Set a pan over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and break into small crumbles with a spatula. Keep chopping as it cooks — you want pieces no bigger than a pea. Cook until no pink remains and fat has rendered, about 6 minutes.

2. **Drain and season** (~2 min). Tilt the pan and spoon off excess fat. Add chili powder (1 tsp), cumin (½ tsp), garlic powder (½ tsp), salt, and pepper. Stir in black beans. Cook 2 more minutes until beans are heated through. Remove from heat.

3. **Build the crunchwrap** (~3 min). Lay the tortilla flat on a clean surface. Spoon the beef-bean mixture into the center. Top with shredded cheddar, a dollop of sour cream, shredded lettuce, and diced tomato. Place the tostada shell flat on top of the pile. Add a small smear of sour cream on the tostada to help it stick.

4. **Fold into a hexagon** (~2 min). Starting from one edge, fold the tortilla up and over the tostada shell. Work your way around, creating 5–6 pleats that overlap each other, sealing the tostada inside. Press gently to compact.

5. **Sear until golden** (~5 min). Flip the crunchwrap seam-side down into a dry pan over medium heat. Press gently with a spatula. Cook 2–3 min until the bottom is golden brown and crispy. Carefully flip. Cook the other side 2–3 min.

6. **Air fryer alternative** (~5 min). Instead of step 5: place seam-side down in the air fryer basket. Cook at 190°C / 375°F for 4–5 min, flipping once halfway through.

## Pack / Serve

Let cool 2 minutes before wrapping in foil. The flat shape fits perfectly in
a rectangular container. Cut in half for easier eating.

## Reheat

- Microwave 90 sec at full power. The tostada loses some crunch but the
  overall texture stays good.
- For crispier results: microwave 60 sec, then air fry 2 min at 190°C.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Brown beef+bean mixture in bulk. Store in portions.
- **Daily assembly:** Assemble and sear fresh (~10 min). The searing step is
  fast and makes a big difference in quality.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked beef-bean mix 4 days. Assembled crunchwraps keep
  overnight but sear quality drops — best assembled day-of.

## Variations

- Use ground turkey (93% lean) for lower fat (−6 g F per serving).
- Add pickled jalapeños for heat.
- Swap cheddar for pepper jack cheese.
