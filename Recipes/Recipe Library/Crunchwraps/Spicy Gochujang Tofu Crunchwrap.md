# Spicy Gochujang Tofu Crunchwrap

![[spicy-gochujang-tofu-crunchwrap.jpg]]

> Crispy gochujang-glazed tofu with kimchi, shredded carrot, and sesame slaw in a sealed crunchwrap.

|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|

## Why Make This

A vegetarian crunchwrap with real flavor depth from gochujang and kimchi.
Pressing and pan-frying the tofu gives it a crispy exterior that holds up
inside the sealed tortilla. At 44 g protein, this is decent for a fully
plant-based meal — but be realistic that you'll need to lean harder on
protein elsewhere in the day.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 300 g extra-firm tofu (about ¾ of a standard block)

**Gochujang glaze**
- 20 g gochujang paste
- 10 ml soy sauce
- 5 ml sesame oil
- 5 ml rice vinegar
- 1 tsp maple syrup or honey

**Wrap + fillings**
- 1 flour tortilla, 12" (~95 g)
- 1 tostada shell (~15 g)
- 40 g vegan kimchi (fish-free), roughly chopped
- 30 g shredded carrot
- 10 g sesame seeds

**Cooking**
- 10 ml neutral oil (vegetable or canola)

## Before You Start

- Press the tofu: wrap in paper towels, place a heavy pan on top, let it
  sit 10 min. This removes excess water so it crisps properly.
- Mix the gochujang glaze ingredients in a small bowl.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Press and slice the tofu** (~2 min active + 10 min press). After pressing, cut the tofu into 1 cm slabs, then into 1.5 cm cubes.

2. **Pan-fry the tofu** (~8 min). Heat neutral oil in a pan over medium-high. Add tofu cubes in a single layer — don't crowd. Cook without moving 3 min until the bottom is golden. Flip pieces. Cook 3 min more. You want at least two crispy sides.

3. **Glaze the tofu** (~2 min). Reduce heat to medium. Pour the gochujang glaze over the tofu. Toss gently for 1–2 min until the glaze coats every piece and turns sticky. Remove to a plate.

4. **Build the crunchwrap** (~3 min). Lay the tortilla flat. Pile glazed tofu in the center. Top with shredded carrot and half the sesame seeds. Place tostada shell on top. Add chopped kimchi on the tostada. Fold tortilla edges up and over, pleating to seal.

5. **Toast the crunchwrap** (~4 min). Place seam-side down in the dry pan over medium heat. Press gently. Cook 2 min until golden. Flip carefully. Cook 2 min. Sprinkle remaining sesame seeds on top.

## Pack / Serve

Wrap in foil. The sealed format keeps the kimchi contained and the
crunchwrap tidy for transport.

## Reheat

- Microwave 90 sec. The tofu won't re-crisp but the gochujang glaze
  keeps the flavor intense.
- For better texture: microwave 60 sec, then toast in a dry pan 1 min
  per side.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Press and cube tofu. Make gochujang glaze. Chop vegan
  kimchi. Shred carrot.
- **Daily cook:** Pan-fry tofu and glaze fresh (~12 min). Assemble and
  toast. The frying is hard to skip — pre-fried tofu gets rubbery.
- **Fridge life:** Glazed tofu 3 days (texture declines). Vegan kimchi
  keeps weeks. Shredded carrot 5 days.

## Variations

- Add 50 g shelled edamame to the filling (+30 cal, +5 g P).
- Swap vegan kimchi for quick-pickled cucumber if you prefer milder.
- Use a whole-wheat tortilla for more fiber.
- Add a fried egg on top for a non-vegan protein boost (+70 cal, +6 g P).
