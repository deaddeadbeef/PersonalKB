# Chicken Katsu Bento

![[chicken-katsu-bento.jpg]]

> Air-fried panko chicken cutlet with rice, pickled veg, and a tangy tonkatsu drizzle.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 72 g | 774 kcal | ~30 min | air fryer + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Crispy chicken katsu made in the air fryer — no deep frying, no oven. The
panko crust stays reasonably crispy even after refrigeration and reheating.
High protein from a thick chicken breast cutlet, and the bento format keeps
everything portioned and packable.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 220 g chicken breast, boneless, skinless (raw weight)

**Breading station**
- 15 g all-purpose flour
- 1 large egg (~50 g), beaten
- 30 g panko breadcrumbs

**Base + sides**
- 150 g cooked white rice (~¼ cup dry)
- 50 g edamame, shelled, cooked
- 50 g cucumber, sliced and quick-pickled

**Sauce + cooking**
- 15 g tonkatsu sauce
- 2-sec spray of cooking oil spray
- Salt, pepper

## Before You Start

- Start rice first — it runs unattended for 18 min.
- Set up the breading station before touching the chicken. Three plates in a row: flour → egg → panko.
- If pickled cucumber and edamame are prepped from Sunday, pull from fridge.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and cook rice in the rice cooker. It will be done before the chicken.

2. **Butterfly the chicken** (~3 min). Place the breast flat on the cutting board. With your hand flat on top, slice horizontally through the middle to open it like a book — don't cut all the way through. Open it up. If it's still thick (over 2 cm), cover with plastic wrap and pound gently with a rolling pin or the bottom of a pan until it's an even 1.5 cm thick. Season both sides with salt and pepper.

3. **Set up the breading station** (~2 min). Plate 1: flour. Plate 2: beaten egg. Plate 3: panko breadcrumbs. Line them up left to right.

4. **Bread the chicken** (~2 min). Dredge the chicken in flour — shake off excess. Dip into beaten egg — let excess drip off. Press firmly into panko on both sides, making sure every surface is covered. Pat the panko in so it sticks.

5. **Preheat the air fryer** (~2 min). Set to 200°C / 400°F. While it heats, spray the basket with cooking oil.

6. **Air fry the chicken** (~12 min). Place breaded chicken in a single layer (no overlap). Spray the top lightly with cooking oil. Cook 6 min. Open, flip carefully with tongs. Spray the new top side. Cook 5–6 min more. Done when the crust is deep golden and the thickest part reads 74°C / 165°F.

7. **Rest and slice** (~2 min). Let the katsu rest on a wire rack or cutting board for 2 min (keeps the bottom from getting soggy). Slice crosswise into 1.5 cm strips.

8. **Pack the bento** (~2 min). Rice on one side of the container, katsu strips on the other. Edamame and pickled cucumber in smaller compartments or corners. Tonkatsu sauce in a small container or drizzled on top.

## Pack / Serve

Pack the tonkatsu sauce separately if you want maximum crispness — adding
it later prevents sogginess during storage.

## Reheat

- Microwave the rice and chicken portions 2 min at full power. The panko
  won't be as crispy as fresh, but still has good texture.
- To restore crunch: microwave 90 sec, then air fry 2 min at 200°C (if
  air fryer is available).

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Cook rice in bulk. Prepare pickled cucumber (slice,
  toss with rice vinegar, sugar, pinch of salt — lasts 5 days). Cook
  edamame and portion.
- **Daily cook:** Bread and air-fry chicken fresh (~15 min). Assemble bento.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked rice 4 days. Pickled cucumber 5 days. Do NOT
  bread chicken ahead — the coating gets soggy.

## Variations

- Use chicken thigh for juicier katsu (higher fat: +10 g F per serving).
- Swap tonkatsu sauce for Japanese curry sauce for katsu curry bento.
- Add furikake rice seasoning to the rice for extra flavor.
