## How To Choose

This category is built around rice, protein, vegetables, and a compact sauce. It is best when you want a tidy lunch that reheats predictably and keeps textures separated. Pick from this page by deciding what constraint matters today: highest protein, lowest prep effort, best leftover texture, or easiest transport.

When the week is busy, choose one recipe from this category and batch only the slow component. Cook the protein, sauce, or grain ahead, then assemble close to eating time when possible. That keeps the meal from turning into a soggy leftover and makes it easier to swap vegetables or sauces without recalculating the whole plan.

## Prep Pattern

Use the linked recipes as templates. First, anchor the protein target. Second, choose the carb base that matches your energy needs. Third, add vegetables for volume and texture. Fourth, keep sauces measured so flavor does not silently blow up calories. If the recipe is lunch-safe, pack wet and crisp components separately unless the page says the texture survives full assembly.

A category hub is useful when it helps you make a decision quickly. After cooking, update the rating or notes on the recipe page with what actually happened: reheated well, too dry, needed more sauce, packed badly, or scaled cleanly. Those small observations make the recipe library personal instead of generic.
