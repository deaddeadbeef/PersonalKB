# Sesame Tempeh & Edamame Bento

![[sesame-tempeh-edamame-bento.jpg]]

> Pan-crisped sesame-soy tempeh with edamame, rice, and pickled ginger in a bento box.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 54 g | 890 kcal | ~25 min | pan + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Tempeh is one of the highest-protein plant foods — 19 g per 100 g, beating
tofu handily. Pan-frying gives it a nutty, crispy exterior, and the
sesame-soy glaze makes it genuinely delicious (not just "healthy-tasting").
Paired with edamame, this is one of the strongest vegetarian bentos for
protein.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g tempeh
- 80 g edamame, shelled, cooked

**Sesame-soy glaze**
- 15 ml soy sauce
- 10 ml rice vinegar
- 5 ml sesame oil
- 5 g honey or maple syrup
- 10 g sesame seeds

**Base + sides**
- 150 g cooked white rice
- 15 g pickled ginger (store-bought)
- 30 g shredded carrot

**Cooking**
- 10 ml neutral oil (vegetable or canola)

## Before You Start

- Start rice in the rice cooker.
- Slice the tempeh before heating the pan.
- Mix the sesame-soy glaze in a small bowl.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and cook in the rice cooker.

2. **Slice the tempeh** (~2 min). Cut the tempeh block into slabs about 5 mm thick. Then cut each slab into rectangular strips (~2 cm × 4 cm). Thinner = crispier.

3. **Pan-fry the tempeh** (~8 min). Heat neutral oil in a pan over medium-high. Lay tempeh strips in a single layer. Cook 3–4 min without moving until the bottom is deep golden. Flip. Cook 3 min more. You want a crispy, nutty crust on both sides.

4. **Glaze the tempeh** (~2 min). Reduce heat to medium. Pour the sesame-soy glaze over the tempeh. Toss gently for 1–2 min as it reduces and coats each strip. Sprinkle half the sesame seeds and toss once more. Remove to a plate.

5. **Warm the edamame** (~1 min). In the same pan or microwave, heat edamame 30–60 sec.

6. **Pack the bento** (~2 min). Rice on one side. Glazed tempeh strips fanned on top — sprinkle remaining sesame seeds. Edamame in a compartment. Pickled ginger and shredded carrot in the remaining space.

## Pack / Serve

Close the bento and transport. The glaze keeps the tempeh flavorful
even at room temperature. Pickled ginger adds a palate-cleansing bite
between components.

## Reheat

- Microwave the rice + tempeh section 90 sec at full power. The tempeh
  texture holds up well — it doesn't get rubbery like tofu can.
- Edamame, carrot, and pickled ginger are fine cold.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Slice and pan-fry tempeh in bulk. Store glazed strips
  in containers (they firm up nicely in the fridge). Cook edamame and
  portion. Cook rice.
- **Daily assembly:** 2 min — scoop rice, add tempeh, pack sides.
- **Fridge life:** Glazed tempeh 4 days. Rice 4 days. Edamame 4 days.
  Pickled ginger keeps weeks.

## Variations

- Add a sliced hard-boiled egg for +70 cal, +6 g protein.
- Swap sesame-soy glaze for teriyaki glaze (see Teriyaki Turkey Meatball
  Bento for the recipe).
- Add 30 g vegan kimchi (fish- and shellfish-free) in place of pickled ginger for a Korean fusion twist.
- Use brown rice for more fiber.
