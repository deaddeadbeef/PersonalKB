# Miso Tofu & Egg Bento

![[miso-tofu-egg-bento.jpg]]

> Pan-seared miso-glazed tofu and jammy eggs over rice with sesame greens.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 44 g | 707 kcal | ~25 min | pan + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

High-protein vegetarian bento that proves you don't need meat to hit decent
protein numbers. Firm tofu pan-seared until golden then glazed with a quick
miso-mirin sauce. Paired with soy-marinated soft-boiled eggs, sesame spinach,
and rice. Every component reheats well.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g firm tofu, pressed
- 2 large eggs (~100 g total)

**Miso glaze**
- 15 g white miso paste (~1 tbsp)
- 10 ml mirin (~2 tsp)
- 5 ml soy sauce (half for glaze, half for spinach)

**Base + sides**
- 150 g cooked white rice (~¼ cup dry)
- 60 g spinach (fresh — wilts down)
- 5 ml sesame oil (~1 tsp)
- 5 g sesame seeds (~1 tsp)

**Cooking**
- 5 ml vegetable oil

## Before You Start

- If you prepped soy-marinated eggs on Sunday, pull 2 from the fridge and skip step 2.
- Press the tofu now (or during step 1). Wrap in paper towels, put a heavy pan on top, 10 min.
- Start rice first.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice and boil water** (~2 min active). Start rice in the cooker. Fill a small pot with enough water to cover 2 eggs. Bring to a rolling boil.

2. **Soft-boil the eggs** (~10 min). Gently lower eggs into boiling water with a spoon. Set a timer for exactly 7 minutes. When the timer goes off, immediately transfer eggs to an ice bath (bowl of cold water + ice). Let them cool 5 min. Peel carefully — start from the wider end where the air pocket is.

3. **Press and cut the tofu** (~2 min). If not already pressed, wrap tofu in paper towels and press under a heavy pan for 10 min. Cut into slabs about 1.5 cm thick (you'll get 4–5 slabs).

4. **Make the miso glaze** (~1 min). In a small bowl, whisk together miso paste, mirin, and half the soy sauce until smooth. It should be the consistency of thin yogurt.

5. **Sear the tofu** (~7 min). Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium-high. When it shimmers, lay tofu slabs flat. Don't move them for 3 minutes — let a golden crust form. Flip carefully with a spatula. Sear the other side 3 min. You want crispy golden edges with a soft interior.

6. **Glaze the tofu** (~2 min). Reduce heat to low. Spoon miso glaze over the tofu slabs. Flip once to coat both sides. Cook 1 minute more — watch carefully, miso burns easily. The glaze should look sticky and caramelized. Remove from pan.

7. **Wilt the spinach** (~2 min). In the same pan (still warm), add spinach and a splash of water. Toss for 60 seconds until just wilted. Drizzle with sesame oil and remaining soy sauce. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.

8. **Pack the bento** (~2 min). Rice in one section. Miso-glazed tofu slabs in another. Halved eggs next to the tofu. Sesame spinach on the side. Drizzle any remaining miso glaze over the tofu.

## Pack / Serve

The eggs are fine at fridge temperature or reheated with the rest. If
you prefer the yolk runny, pack them separately and add after reheating.

## Reheat

- Microwave rice and tofu compartments 90 sec at full power. Eggs can be
  reheated with the rest or kept at room temp (they're good both ways).
  The miso glaze keeps the tofu moist.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Soft-boil eggs in a batch (make 6–8 for the week). Store
  peeled in a container with a splash of soy sauce (soy-marinated eggs).
  Cook rice in bulk. Press and cut tofu into slabs, store dry.
- **Daily cook:** Sear and glaze tofu (~10 min). Wilt spinach (~2 min). Assemble.
- **Fridge life:** Soy eggs 4 days. Pressed tofu (raw) 4 days. Cooked rice 4 days.

## Variations

- Add 50 g shelled edamame for +5 g protein (+60 cal).
- Swap tofu for extra-firm for crispier sear.
- Use brown rice or mixed grain rice for more fiber.
