# Teriyaki Turkey Meatball Bento

![[teriyaki-turkey-meatball-bento.jpg]]

> Teriyaki-glazed turkey meatballs with rice, edamame, and quick-pickled cucumber in a bento box.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 67 g | 890 kcal | ~30 min | pan + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Turkey meatballs are a meal-prep powerhouse — make a big batch on Sunday
and portion through the week. The teriyaki glaze caramelizes in the pan,
giving each meatball a sticky, savory shell. The bento format with rice,
edamame, and pickled cucumber makes it a complete, balanced, portable meal.

## Ingredients

**Meatballs (makes ~10)**
- 250 g ground turkey (93% lean)
- 15 g panko breadcrumbs
- 1 large egg white (~30 g)
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (or ½ tsp ground)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 10 ml soy sauce
- Salt, pepper

**Teriyaki glaze**
- 20 ml soy sauce
- 15 ml mirin (or 10 ml rice vinegar + 5 g sugar)
- 10 g honey
- 5 ml sesame oil

**Base + sides**
- 150 g cooked white rice
- 50 g edamame, shelled, cooked
- 50 g cucumber, sliced and quick-pickled

**Cooking**
- 5 ml neutral oil (vegetable or canola)
- Sesame seeds for garnish (~3 g)

## Before You Start

- Start rice in the rice cooker.
- Mix teriyaki glaze ingredients in a small bowl.
- If edamame and pickled cucumber are prepped, pull from fridge.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and cook in the rice cooker.

2. **Mix the meatball base** (~3 min). In a bowl, combine ground turkey, panko, egg white, ginger, garlic, 10 ml soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Mix with your hands until just combined — over-mixing makes them dense.

3. **Form the meatballs** (~3 min). Wet your hands slightly (prevents sticking). Roll into ~10 meatballs, about 3 cm diameter each. Slightly smaller than a golf ball.

4. **Cook the meatballs** (~10 min). Heat neutral oil in a pan over medium heat. Add meatballs in a single layer. Cook 3 min, then gently roll to the next side. Continue rotating every 2–3 min until browned on all sides and cooked through (~10 min total). The interior should not be pink.

5. **Glaze the meatballs** (~2 min). Pour the teriyaki glaze into the pan. Increase heat to medium-high. Toss and roll the meatballs in the glaze for 1–2 min as it reduces to a sticky coating. Every meatball should be glossy.

6. **Pack the bento** (~2 min). Rice on one side. Glazed meatballs on the other — sprinkle with sesame seeds. Edamame and pickled cucumber in smaller compartments.

## Pack / Serve

The bento format is inherently portable. Close the lid and go. The
teriyaki glaze acts as a flavor sealant — meatballs don't dry out.

## Reheat

- Microwave the rice + meatball section 2 min at full power. The glaze
  keeps them moist.
- Edamame and pickled cucumber are fine cold — don't microwave them.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Make all meatballs and glaze in one batch. Store
  5–6 per container. Cook rice, portion. Prepare edamame and pickled
  cucumber.
- **Daily assembly:** 2 min — scoop rice, add meatballs, pack sides.
- **Fridge life:** Glazed meatballs 4 days (the glaze preserves moisture).
  Rice 4 days. Pickled cucumber 5 days.

## Variations

- Use ground chicken instead of turkey — similar macros.
- Add 30 g shredded carrot to the bento for color and crunch (+12 cal).
- Swap white rice for brown rice for more fiber (+10 cal per 150 g).
- Double the meatball batch and freeze half — reheat from frozen in
  microwave (add 1 min).
