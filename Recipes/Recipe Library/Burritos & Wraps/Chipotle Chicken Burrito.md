# Chipotle Chicken Burrito

![[chipotle-chicken-burrito.jpg]]

> Smoky chipotle chicken with black beans and rice wrapped in a big flour tortilla.

## Why Make This

High-protein burrito with smoky chipotle heat. The filling reheats perfectly
in the microwave, making it ideal for next-day lunches. Wrapping technique
keeps everything compact and leak-free for transport.

## Ingredients

**Protein + fillings**
- 200 g chicken breast (boneless, skinless)
- 80 g black beans, canned, drained and rinsed
- 100 g cooked white rice

**Wrap + toppings**
- 1 flour tortilla, 12" (~95 g)
- 30 g cheddar cheese, shredded
- 15 g chipotle in adobo, minced (about 1 pepper + sauce)
- 30 g salsa

**Cooking**
- 5 ml olive oil
- Salt, pepper, cumin (~1 tsp), smoked paprika (~½ tsp)

## Before You Start

- If chicken is prepped from Sunday, just warm it through. Skip step 1.
- Have rice cooked (rice cooker or leftover). Cold rice from the fridge is fine.
- Mince the chipotle pepper and set aside on a small plate.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Season the chicken** (~1 min). Rub both sides of the breast with salt, pepper, 1 tsp cumin, and ½ tsp smoked paprika. You want an even coating — no bare spots.

2. **Sear the chicken** (~12 min). Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high until it shimmers. Lay the breast flat. Cook 5–6 min without moving — you want a golden-brown crust. Flip. Cook another 5–6 min until the thickest part reads 74°C / 165°F on an instant-read thermometer. If it's browning too fast, drop to medium.

3. **Rest and dice** (~3 min). Move chicken to a cutting board. Let it rest 3 minutes (keeps it juicy). Dice into roughly 1.5 cm cubes.

4. **Warm the beans** (~2 min). Microwave black beans in a small bowl for 90 seconds. Drain any extra liquid.

5. **Warm the tortilla** (~1 min). In the same pan over medium heat, warm the tortilla 15 seconds per side. It should be soft and pliable, not crispy. Stack on a plate.

6. **Layer the fillings** (~2 min). Lay tortilla flat. In the center bottom third, layer in this order: rice → black beans → diced chicken → minced chipotle → shredded cheddar → salsa. Keep a 5 cm border on all sides.

7. **Fold the burrito** (~1 min). Fold the bottom edge up over the fillings. Fold both sides inward. Roll away from you tightly, tucking as you go. The seam should end up on the bottom.

8. **Seal the seam** (~1 min). Place the burrito seam-side down in the dry pan over medium heat. Press gently with a spatula. Cook 30 seconds until the seam is lightly toasted and sealed.

## Pack / Serve

Wrap tightly in aluminum foil for transport. If eating within 2 hours, it
travels well at room temperature. For lunch, refrigerate promptly at work.

## Reheat

- Wrap in a damp paper towel. Microwave at 50% power for 2 min, flip,
  then 1 min at full power. Let stand 1 min before eating.
- For crispier exterior: reheat unwrapped in the microwave first, then
  30 sec per side in a dry hot pan (if available at work, optional).

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Cook and dice chicken in bulk. Portion into 200g bags.
  Cook rice in rice cooker, portion into containers.
- **Daily assembly:** Warm tortilla, layer fillings, wrap fresh. Takes ~5 min
  if protein and rice are prepped.
- **Fridge life:** Prepped chicken lasts 4 days. Assembled burritos last
  1–2 days wrapped in foil — best assembled fresh.

## Variations

- Swap chicken for ground turkey (93% lean) — brown and season the same way.
- Add 50 g corn kernels for sweetness (+40 cal, +1 g P).
- Use a whole-wheat tortilla for more fiber.
