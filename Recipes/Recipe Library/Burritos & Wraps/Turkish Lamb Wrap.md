# Turkish Lamb Wrap

![[turkish-lamb-wrap.jpg]]

> Spiced ground lamb with pickled onion, cucumber-yogurt sauce, and greens in a lavash wrap.

## Quick Look

| Protein | Calories | Active Time | Equipment | Lunch-Safe |
|---------|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| 61 g | 734 kcal | ~25 min | pan | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Mediterranean-style wrap with deep lamb flavor from cumin, sumac, and Aleppo
pepper. The cucumber-yogurt sauce adds freshness and the pickled onion gives
acidity that cuts through the richness. Rolls up tight, reheats well, and
packs serious protein.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 180 g ground lamb (lean)

**Wrap + fresh toppings**
- 1 lavash flatbread sheet (~100 g)
- 40 g Greek yogurt, 2%
- 50 g cucumber, finely diced
- 30 g red onion, thinly sliced (for quick-pickling)
- 30 g mixed greens
- 50 g tomato, diced

**Cooking + spices**
- 5 ml olive oil
- 2 g sumac (~1 tsp)
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp Aleppo pepper (or sweet paprika)
- Salt, black pepper
- Splash of red wine vinegar + pinch of sugar (for pickled onion)
- Squeeze of lemon juice (for yogurt sauce)

## Before You Start

- If onions are pickled from Sunday prep, skip step 1.
- If yogurt sauce is made ahead, skip step 2.
- Have a plate or tray ready for assembly — lavash is big and floppy.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Quick-pickle the onion** (~2 min active, 10 min rest). Thinly slice the red onion into half-moons. Toss in a small bowl with a good splash of red wine vinegar and a pinch of sugar. Set aside — even 10 minutes makes a difference. They'll turn bright pink.

2. **Make the yogurt sauce** (~2 min). Finely dice the cucumber (3–4 mm pieces). Mix into Greek yogurt with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of lemon juice. Taste — it should be tangy and cool.

3. **Brown the lamb** (~7 min). Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high. Add ground lamb and break it into small crumbles with a spatula as it cooks. Keep stirring and chopping — you want no large chunks. Cook until browned and no pink remains, about 6 minutes.

4. **Drain and season** (~1 min). Tilt the pan and spoon off any excess fat. Add 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp sumac, ½ tsp Aleppo pepper, salt, and pepper. Stir through for 30 seconds until fragrant. Remove from heat.

5. **Warm the lavash** (~1 min). Wipe the pan clean. Over medium heat, warm the lavash 20 seconds per side. It should be soft and pliable — you just want to take the fridge chill off.

6. **Assemble the wrap** (~2 min). Lay the lavash flat. Spread yogurt sauce in a line down the center. Layer on: seasoned lamb, drained pickled onion, diced tomato, and mixed greens. Leave 5 cm free at each end.

7. **Roll tightly** (~1 min). Fold the bottom edge up over the fillings, tuck both sides in, then roll away from you firmly. Slice in half diagonally if packing for lunch.

## Pack / Serve

Wrap tightly in foil or cling wrap. If the yogurt sauce makes you nervous
about sogginess, pack it in a small container and add cold at lunchtime.

## Reheat

- Wrap in a damp paper towel. Microwave 2 min at 70% power. The yogurt
  sauce survives reheating — it just melts into the lamb slightly.
- Alternatively, keep the yogurt sauce in a small container and add cold
  after reheating the wrap.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Brown lamb in bulk (360 g for two wraps). Pickle a jar
  of onions (lasts a week). Make yogurt sauce in a jar.
- **Daily assembly:** Warm lavash, assemble, roll. ~5 min.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked lamb 4 days. Pickled onion 7+ days. Yogurt sauce 3 days.

## Variations

- Swap lamb for ground beef (90/10) — similar macros, milder flavor.
- Add 30 g crumbled feta for extra tang (+75 cal, +4 g P).
- Use a large flour tortilla instead of lavash if easier to find.
