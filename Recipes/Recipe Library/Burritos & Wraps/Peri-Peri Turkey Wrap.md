# Peri-Peri Turkey Wrap

![[peri-peri-turkey-wrap.jpg]]

> Peri-peri spiced turkey breast with slaw, pickled onion, and garlic mayo in a flour tortilla.

## Why Make This

A spicy, tangy wrap with high protein from turkey breast. The peri-peri
seasoning gives Nando's-style heat, and the crunchy slaw keeps it fresh.
Best assembled day-of so the tortilla doesn't get soggy from the slaw.
Turkey cooks fast, so this is one of the quickest wraps in the library.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 220 g turkey breast, boneless, skinless (raw weight)

**Peri-peri seasoning**
- 5 ml olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Salt, pepper

**Wrap + toppings**
- 1 flour tortilla, 12" (~95 g)
- 40 g cabbage slaw (shredded cabbage + carrot)
- 15 g pickled red onion
- 15 g garlic mayo (or mix 12 g mayo + 1 small clove garlic, minced)

## Before You Start

- If turkey is prepped from the weekend, just slice and layer — skip step 1–3.
- Make pickled red onion ahead: slice thin, pour over hot rice vinegar with
  a pinch of sugar and salt. Lasts a week in the fridge.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Season the turkey** (~1 min). Mix smoked paprika, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Rub the turkey breast on both sides with the spice mix and lemon juice.

2. **Sear the turkey** (~10 min). Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high. Cook turkey breast 5 min per side, pressing gently with a spatula for even contact. Done when internal temp reads 74°C / 165°F. Turkey breast overcooks fast — pull it as soon as it hits temp.

3. **Rest and slice** (~3 min). Rest on a cutting board 2 min. Slice into 1 cm strips against the grain.

4. **Warm the tortilla** (~1 min). In the same pan over medium, warm tortilla 15 sec per side.

5. **Assemble the wrap** (~2 min). Lay tortilla flat. Spread garlic mayo in a line down the center. Layer: cabbage slaw → turkey strips → pickled red onion. Fold bottom up, sides in, roll tightly.

## Pack / Serve

Wrap tightly in foil or parchment. For lunch, pack slaw and turkey
separately and assemble at work for best texture. If pre-assembled,
eat within 3–4 hours.

## Reheat

Designed to be eaten cold or room temp. If you want warm turkey,
microwave the sliced turkey 40 sec before assembling.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Cook turkey breast in bulk, slice and portion.
  Make pickled red onion (lasts 7 days). Shred cabbage and carrot.
- **Daily assembly:** ~5 min. Warm tortilla, layer, roll.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked turkey 4 days. Slaw mix 4 days (undressed).

## Variations

- Swap turkey for chicken breast — same cook method.
- Add 30 g avocado slices (+50 cal, +0.5 g P) for creaminess.
- Use a whole-wheat wrap for extra fiber.
- Increase cayenne to 1 tsp for proper Nando's extra-hot heat.
