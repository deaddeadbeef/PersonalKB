# Greek Chicken Souvlaki Wrap

![[greek-chicken-souvlaki-wrap.jpg]]

> Oregano-lemon marinated chicken thigh with tzatziki, tomato, and red onion in a warm pita.

## Why Make This

Greek-flavored chicken in a pita — bright, herby, and filling without being
heavy. The tzatziki keeps it cool and creamy. Best assembled fresh in the
morning since the pita softens if wrapped too early with wet ingredients.
Chicken can be prepped in advance so morning assembly takes 5 minutes.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g boneless, skinless chicken thigh (raw weight)

**Marinade**
- 15 ml olive oil
- 15 ml lemon juice (~½ lemon)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Salt, pepper

**Wrap + toppings**
- 1 large pita bread (~80 g) or flatbread
- 40 g tzatziki (store-bought or homemade)
- 40 g tomato, diced
- 20 g red onion, thinly sliced
- 15 g romaine lettuce, shredded

## Before You Start

- Marinate the chicken at least 15 min (overnight in the fridge is ideal).
- If chicken is prepped from the weekend, skip straight to warming it.
- Have tzatziki, tomato, and onion prepped and ready to go.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Marinate the chicken** (~2 min active + wait). Combine olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Add chicken thigh and coat evenly. Let sit at least 15 min at room temperature or up to 24 hours in the fridge.

2. **Cook the chicken** (~10 min). Heat a pan over medium-high. No extra oil needed — the marinade has enough. Lay the chicken flat. Cook 5 min undisturbed until the underside has a golden char. Flip. Cook 4–5 min more until cooked through (74°C / 165°F internal). Thighs are forgiving — slightly over is still juicy.

3. **Rest and slice** (~3 min). Transfer to a cutting board. Rest 2–3 min. Slice into thin strips across the grain, roughly 1 cm wide.

4. **Warm the pita** (~1 min). In the same pan over medium heat, warm the pita 20 seconds per side. It should be soft and pliable.

5. **Assemble the wrap** (~2 min). Lay pita flat. Spread tzatziki down the center. Layer lettuce, then chicken strips, then diced tomato and red onion on top. Fold the bottom edge up, then fold both sides in to form an open-topped wrap. Alternatively, roll tightly if using a large flatbread.

## Pack / Serve

For lunch transport, pack the chicken and toppings in a container and the
pita separately. Assemble at lunch. If you must assemble ahead, wrap
tightly in foil — but the pita will soften after a few hours.

## Reheat

This is designed to be eaten cold or at room temperature — no reheating
needed. If you prefer warm chicken, microwave the sliced chicken 45 sec
before assembling.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Marinate and cook chicken in bulk. Slice and portion
  into containers. Dice tomato and slice onion, store separately.
- **Daily assembly:** 5 min — warm pita, layer fillings. Fastest if
  chicken is pre-sliced.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked chicken 4 days. Pre-sliced tomato and onion 3 days.
  Tzatziki 5 days (store-bought) or 3 days (homemade).

## Variations

- Swap chicken thigh for breast — leaner but drier. Add a splash of
  lemon juice when reheating to compensate.
- Add 30 g crumbled feta (+60 cal, +4 g P) for a richer wrap.
- Use whole-wheat pita for more fiber.
- Add pickled banana peppers for tang.
