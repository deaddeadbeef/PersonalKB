# Ginger Beef Stir-Fry

![[ginger-beef-stir-fry.jpg]]

> Wok-seared beef strips with ginger, garlic, broccoli, and snap peas over rice.

## Quick Look

| 63 g | 787 kcal | ~20 min | wok + rice cooker | ✅ reheat OK |

## Why Make This

Fast wok stir-fry with thinly sliced beef, fresh ginger, garlic, and crisp
vegetables. The sauce is a simple soy-hoisin-ginger combo that coats everything
in glossy umami. Reliable weeknight dinner that also reheats well — the sauce
keeps the rice from drying out and the vegetables hold their texture.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g beef sirloin, thinly sliced (2–3 mm)

**Stir-fry sauce**
- 15 ml soy sauce (~1 tbsp)
- 15 ml hoisin sauce (~1 tbsp)
- 5 g cornstarch dissolved in 30 ml water (slurry)

**Vegetables**
- 80 g broccoli florets (bite-sized pieces)
- 60 g sugar snap peas, trimmed

**Aromatics**
- 10 g fresh ginger, minced (~1 tbsp)
- 5 g garlic, minced (~1 clove)

**Base + cooking**
- 180 g cooked white rice (~⅓ cup dry)
- 10 ml vegetable oil (split: half for beef, half for veg)

## Before You Start

- Start rice first. Prep all vegetables and mince ginger/garlic before heating the wok.
- Mix the stir-fry sauce in a bowl so it's ready to pour.
- If sauce and veg are prepped from Sunday, this comes together in under 10 min.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and start in the rice cooker. It will be ready by the time everything else is done.

2. **Mix the stir-fry sauce** (~1 min). In a small bowl, whisk soy sauce, hoisin sauce, and the cornstarch slurry (5 g cornstarch dissolved in 2 tbsp water). Set beside the stove.

3. **Get the wok smoking hot** (~2 min). Set wok over high heat. Wait until you see a faint wisp of smoke. A properly heated wok is the difference between a stir-fry and a steam-fry.

4. **Sear the beef** (~4 min). Add half the vegetable oil. Spread beef in a single layer — don't pile it. Let it sit untouched for 1.5–2 min until you see a brown crust forming on the bottom. Flip or toss. Sear 1–2 min more. The edges should be caramelized. Remove to a plate.

5. **Stir-fry the vegetables** (~4 min). Add remaining oil to the wok (still on high). Add minced ginger and garlic — stir for 30 seconds until fragrant. Do not burn. Add broccoli and snap peas. Toss constantly for 3–4 min. The broccoli should be bright green and crisp-tender. Add a splash of water (1 tbsp) if the wok gets dry — the steam helps cook the broccoli.

6. **Combine and glaze** (~1 min). Return beef to the wok. Pour in the sauce. Toss everything together for 60 seconds. The sauce will thicken quickly from the cornstarch and form a glossy coating on the beef and vegetables.

7. **Serve over rice** (~1 min). Scoop rice into a bowl or container. Top with the stir-fry. Scrape any remaining sauce from the wok over the top.

## Pack / Serve

Packs well in a single container — the sauce prevents the rice from drying out.
No separate sauce container needed.

## Reheat

- Microwave in a container with the lid cracked, 2 min at full power.
  The sauce prevents drying. Vegetables stay reasonably crisp — they
  soften slightly but don't get mushy.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Slice beef (partially freeze first for thin slices), portion
  into bags. Cut broccoli into florets, trim snap peas. Pre-mix sauce in a jar.
  Cook rice in bulk.
- **Daily cook:** Wok cooking is fast — 8 min total once everything is prepped.
- **Fridge life:** Sliced raw beef 2 days (freeze for later in week). Cut
  vegetables 4 days. Pre-mixed sauce 5 days.

## Variations

- Swap beef for chicken thigh (similar cook time, different flavor).
- Add water chestnuts or baby corn for texture.
- Use cauliflower rice for lower carbs.
