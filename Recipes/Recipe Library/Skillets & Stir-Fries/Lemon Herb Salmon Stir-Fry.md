# Lemon Herb Salmon Stir-Fry

![[lemon-herb-salmon-stir-fry.jpg]]

> Pan-seared salmon broken into flakes with lemon, dill, asparagus, and cherry tomatoes.

| 48 g | 772 kcal | ~20 min | wok + rice cooker | :x: dinner only |

## Why Make This

Light, fresh dinner that showcases salmon as the star protein. Seared in
pieces then gently tossed with asparagus, cherry tomatoes, lemon juice, and
fresh dill. **Dinner only** -- fish does not reheat well for packed lunches and
is excluded from the leftover-as-lunch rotation per dietary constraints.

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 200 g salmon fillet, skin removed

**Vegetables**
- 100 g asparagus, trimmed, cut into 4 cm pieces
- 80 g cherry tomatoes, halved

**Aromatics + finishing**
- 15 ml lemon juice (~1 tbsp, about half a lemon)
- 5 g fresh dill, chopped (~2 tbsp)
- 5 g garlic, minced (~1 clove)

**Base + cooking**
- 180 g cooked white rice (~⅓ cup dry)
- 10 ml olive oil (split: half for salmon, half for veg)
- Salt, pepper

## Before You Start

- Start rice first.
- Pat the salmon dry -- moisture on the surface prevents browning.
- Trim asparagus by snapping off the woody ends (they break naturally at the right spot).
- Halve cherry tomatoes day-of (they get watery if cut ahead).

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and start in the rice cooker.

2. **Cut and season the salmon** (~2 min). Cut the fillet into roughly 3 cm chunks. Season all sides with salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon juice.

3. **Sear the salmon** (~5 min). Heat half the olive oil in a wok over medium-high. When the oil shimmers, add salmon chunks. Let them sear undisturbed for 2 min -- a golden crust should form on the bottom. Gently flip each piece. Cook 2 min more. The center should be slightly translucent (it will finish cooking when returned to the wok later). Remove carefully with a spatula and set aside.

4. **Stir-fry the asparagus** (~4 min). Add remaining oil to the wok. Add asparagus pieces. Stir-fry 3--4 min until bright green and tender-crisp -- they should still have a slight snap when you bite one. Add minced garlic in the last 30 seconds. Stir until fragrant.

5. **Add tomatoes** (~1 min). Add halved cherry tomatoes to the wok. Toss for 60 seconds -- you want them just warmed through and starting to soften, not cooked down.

6. **Combine gently** (~1 min). Return salmon to the wok. Squeeze the remaining lemon juice over everything. Sprinkle chopped dill. Give one or two gentle tosses -- don't break up the salmon pieces.

7. **Serve over rice** (~1 min). Scoop rice into a bowl. Arrange the salmon and vegetables on top. Spoon any pan juices over the rice.

## Pack / Serve

Serve immediately -- this dish is best fresh. It is not designed for leftovers.

## Reheat

- **Not recommended for reheating.** This is a dinner-only recipe. Fish
  reheats poorly (texture degrades, strong smell in shared spaces). Cook
  and eat fresh.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** None needed for salmon (cook fresh). Trim and cut asparagus,
  store in a damp-towel-lined container. Halve cherry tomatoes day-of (they
  get watery if cut ahead). Cook rice in bulk.
- **Daily cook:** Sear salmon and stir-fry veg (~12 min). All fresh.
- **Fridge life:** Raw salmon 2 days from purchase. Trimmed asparagus 3 days.

## Variations

- Swap salmon for cod or tilapia (white fish, lower fat, milder flavor).
- Add capers (15 g) for a briny kick.
- Use zucchini instead of asparagus when not in season.
- **Note:** Any fish swap keeps this dinner-only. Do not assign leftovers to lunch.
