# Ginger Sesame Tofu & Edamame Stir-Fry

![[ginger-sesame-tofu-edamame-stir-fry.jpg]]

> Crispy ginger-sesame tofu with edamame, snap peas, and bell pepper over rice.

## Why Make This

A fast vegetarian stir-fry with genuine protein substance. The tofu gets
crispy in the wok, and the ginger-sesame sauce coats everything in an
umami glaze. Edamame adds an extra protein bump, and the snap peas give
a satisfying crunch. Wok cooking is fast and the whole thing comes
together in one pan (plus the rice cooker).

## Ingredients

**Protein**
- 250 g extra-firm tofu
- 80 g edamame, shelled, cooked

**Vegetables**
- 60 g sugar snap peas, trimmed
- 50 g bell pepper (any color), sliced

**Ginger-sesame sauce**
- 20 ml soy sauce
- 10 ml rice vinegar
- 5 ml sesame oil
- 10 g fresh ginger, grated (about 2 cm knob)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 5 g honey or maple syrup
- 5 g cornstarch + 15 ml water (slurry for thickening)

**Base + garnish**
- 150 g cooked white rice
- 5 g sesame seeds
- 1 green onion, sliced (optional)

**Cooking**
- 10 ml neutral oil (vegetable or canola)

## Before You Start

- Press the tofu: wrap in paper towels, press with a heavy pan for 10 min.
- Start rice in the rice cooker.
- Mix the sauce ingredients (except cornstarch slurry) in a small bowl.
- Mix cornstarch slurry separately.
- Prep all vegetables — stir-fry moves fast once you start.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Start the rice** (~1 min active). Rinse and cook in the rice cooker.

2. **Cube and dry the tofu** (~3 min). Cut pressed tofu into 2 cm cubes. Pat dry with paper towels one more time — the drier, the crispier.

3. **Crisp the tofu** (~8 min). Heat neutral oil in a wok over high heat until shimmering. Add tofu cubes in a single layer. Let them cook without moving 3 min until the bottom is golden. Flip or toss. Cook 2–3 min on the next side. Continue until crispy on at least 3 sides. Remove to a plate.

4. **Stir-fry the vegetables** (~3 min). In the same wok (add a splash of oil if dry), add snap peas and bell pepper. Stir-fry 2–3 min until bright and barely tender. They should still have a crunch.

5. **Add edamame and sauce** (~2 min). Return tofu to the wok. Add edamame. Pour in the ginger-sesame sauce. Toss everything together over high heat for 1 min. Add the cornstarch slurry. Toss 30 sec until the sauce thickens and coats everything in a glossy sheen.

6. **Serve** (~1 min). Scoop rice into a bowl or container. Pile the stir-fry on top. Sprinkle sesame seeds and sliced green onion.

## Pack / Serve

Pack rice and stir-fry together in a container. The sauce keeps
everything moist and flavorful. Travels well — no drippy components.

## Reheat

- Microwave 2 min at full power. The tofu won't be as crispy, but the
  sauce keeps it from being dry. Stir halfway through for even heating.

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Weekend prep:** Press and cube tofu. Cook edamame. Make
  ginger-sesame sauce (stores well 5 days). Slice veggies.
- **Daily cook:** Stir-fry is best fresh (~12 min active). Pre-cubed
  tofu speeds things up.
- **Fridge life:** Cooked stir-fry 3 days (vegetables soften). Sauce 5 days.

## Variations

- Add 100 g chicken breast for a non-vegetarian version (+165 cal, +31 g P).
- Swap snap peas for broccoli florets — cook 1 min longer.
- Add chili flakes or sriracha to the sauce for heat.
- Use brown rice or cauliflower rice.
