# Micronutrient Profile — Beyond Macros


---

## Overview

Your MacroFactor data tracks over 50 micronutrients across 260 days of food logging. While macros drive body composition, micronutrients drive performance, recovery, and long-term health — especially important during an extended caloric deficit.

---

## Key Micronutrients for Body Recomposition

### Critical During a Cut

| Nutrient | Role in Recomp | Risk During Deficit |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Vitamin D | Testosterone, muscle function, bone health | High — less food = less intake |
| Magnesium | Sleep quality, recovery, 300+ enzyme reactions | Moderate — depleted by exercise |
| Zinc | Testosterone production, immune function | Moderate — lost through sweat |
| Iron | Oxygen transport, training performance | Low-moderate — depends on diet |
| Calcium | Bone health (bones lose density during weight loss) | Moderate |
| B12 | Energy metabolism, red blood cell production | Low — if eating meat |
| Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) | Inflammation, brain function, muscle recovery | Moderate |

### Your Fiber Gap

| Metric | Your Average | Target | Gap |
|--------|-------------|--------|-----|
| Fiber | 19 g/day | 25-35 g/day | 6-16 g short |

This is your most actionable nutrition gap. Fiber supports:
- **Satiety** — helps you feel fuller in a deficit
- **Gut microbiome** — diverse bacteria improve nutrient absorption
- **Blood sugar** — reduces insulin spikes
- **Cholesterol** — soluble fiber binds bile acids

**Easy fixes:**
| Addition | Fiber | Calories |
|----------|-------|----------|
| 1 tbsp psyllium husk in water | +5g | +15 kcal |
| 1 cup broccoli | +5g | +55 kcal |
| 2 tbsp chia seeds | +10g | +140 kcal |
| ½ cup black beans | +7.5g | +115 kcal |

## Evidence-Based Supplement Recommendations

Only supplements with strong research support:

| Supplement | Dose | Evidence | Priority |
|-----------|------|----------|----------|
| **Creatine monohydrate** | 5g/day | Strongest supplement evidence for muscle + strength | 🔴 High |
| **Vitamin D3** | 2000-5000 IU/day | Most people are deficient, especially if limited sun | 🔴 High |
| **Omega-3 (fish oil)** | 1-2g EPA+DHA/day | Anti-inflammatory, cardiovascular, recovery | 🟡 Medium |
| **Magnesium** (glycinate) | 200-400mg/day | Sleep, recovery, most diets are deficient | 🟡 Medium |
| **Whey protein** | As needed to hit 184g | Convenient, high leucine | 🟢 As needed |

### Creatine — Special Note
Creatine is the most researched sports supplement in history. During a cut:
- Maintains muscle cell hydration and volume
- Preserves strength better than placebo
- May slightly increase water weight (1-2 kg) — this is intramuscular, not fat
- No loading phase needed — just 5g/day consistently
