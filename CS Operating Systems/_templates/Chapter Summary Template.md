# Chapter Summary Template

> **Usage:** Duplicate for each chapter. Rename to `MOS - Chapter NN.md`.
> Fill in `chapter`, `status`, and `chunk_count` to match what you actually create.

```yaml
---
id: mos-ch-NN
type: book-chapter
chapter: N
book: "Modern Operating Systems"
author: "Andrew S. Tanenbaum"
status: seeded
chunk_count: 0
source: "[[Tanenbaum 2015 - Modern Operating Systems]]"
tags:
  - csos
  - book-chapter
up: "[[Chapter Index]]"
---
```
