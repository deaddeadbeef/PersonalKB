# Modern Operating Systems

**Author:** Andrew S. Tanenbaum; Herbert Bos  
**Publisher:** Pearson, 2015 (4th edition)  
**Level:** Advanced undergraduate / Graduate  
**Source:** [[Tanenbaum 2015 - Modern Operating Systems]]

A definitive, comprehensive textbook on operating system theory and practice. Covers every major OS subsystem from processes and threads through virtualisation, multiprocessors, and security, with deep case studies on Linux/Android and Windows.
