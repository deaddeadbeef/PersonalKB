## Falcon 1 Era

**Q1. List the four Falcon 1 flight dates and their outcomes.**
> **A1.**
> | Flight | Date | Outcome |
> |--------|------|---------|
> | 1 | 24 Mar 2006 | Failure (engine fire at T+25 s) |
> | 2 | 21 Mar 2007 | Failure (roll oscillation, 2nd stage shutdown at T+5 min) |
> | 3 | 3 Aug 2008 | Failure (stage separation issue — 1st stage recontacted 2nd) |
> | 4 | 28 Sep 2008 | **Success** — first privately developed liquid-fuel rocket to orbit |
> A fifth flight on 14 Jul 2009 carried RazakSAT successfully.

**Q2. What payload did Falcon 1 Flight 4 carry to orbit?**
> **A2.** A 165 kg dummy payload (RatSat / mass simulator) for Falcon 1's qualification flight.

## Key Firsts

**Q3. When did SpaceX first successfully land a Falcon 9 booster?**
> **A3.** 22 December 2015, Orbcomm OG2 mission — landing at LZ-1, Cape Canaveral.

**Q4. When did SpaceX first re-fly a recovered Falcon 9 booster?**
> **A4.** 30 March 2017, SES-10 mission (booster B1021, previously flown on CRS-8).

**Q5. When did SpaceX first launch astronauts (Crew Dragon)?**
> **A5.** 30 May 2020, Demo-2 mission — astronauts Doug Hurley and Bob Behnken to ISS. This was the first crewed orbital launch from US soil since STS-135 (July 2011).

**Q6. What was the first Falcon 9 drone-ship landing?**
> **A6.** 8 April 2016, CRS-8 mission — booster B1021 landed on OCISLY (Of Course I Still Love You) in the Atlantic.

## CRS Program

**Q7. What is the CRS program and how many phases does it have?**
> **A7.** Commercial Resupply Services — NASA's ISS cargo delivery program. Two phases: CRS-1 (12 flights, 2012–2020, using Dragon 1) and CRS-2 (ongoing, using Cargo Dragon 2). SpaceX was awarded both phases.

**Q8. What was the date and cause of the CRS-7 failure?**
> **A8.** 28 June 2015. A steel strut in the second-stage LOX tank (holding a helium COPV) failed at ~2.5 g, well below its rated 10,000 lb. This caused LOX release, overpressure, and vehicle breakup at T+2 min 19 s.

## Notable Missions

**Q9. Name two significant national security missions flown by Falcon 9 / Heavy.**
> **A9.** USSF-44 (Nov 2022, Falcon Heavy, first NSSL-certified FH mission) and GPS III SV01 (Dec 2018, first SpaceX USAF mission). Also notable: NROL-108, NROL-87, and the X-37B (USSF-52 on Falcon Heavy).

**Q10. What commercial mission marked Falcon 9's first GTO delivery?**
> **A10.** SES-8, launched 3 December 2013 — SpaceX's first commercial GEO-transfer mission.

**Q11. What was the Inspiration4 mission?**
> **A11.** Launched 16 September 2021 — the first all-civilian orbital mission. Four private citizens (led by Jared Isaacman) orbited Earth for ~3 days in a Crew Dragon, reaching ~585 km altitude (higher than ISS or Hubble).

## Launch Cadence & Records

**Q12. Roughly how many Falcon 9 missions were launched in 2023?**
> **A12.** 91 Falcon-family launches in 2023 (a then-record year for SpaceX).

**Q13. How many orbital launches did SpaceX achieve in 2024?**
> **A13.** Over 130 Falcon-family launches in 2024, setting a new annual record and accounting for roughly half of all global orbital launches that year.

**Q14. What is the shortest turnaround achieved for a Falcon 9 booster between two flights?**
> **A14.** ~21 days (booster B1058, among others). The pad-turnaround record at SLC-40 has been as low as ~4 days between different boosters.

**Q15. What is Falcon 9's overall mission success rate (as of late 2024)?**
> **A15.** Over 99% — with only two failures (CRS-7 in June 2015 and Amos-6 pad anomaly in September 2016) and one partial failure (CRS-1 secondary payload, Oct 2012) across 300+ missions.
