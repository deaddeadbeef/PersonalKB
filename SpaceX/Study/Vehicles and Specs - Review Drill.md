# Vehicles and Specs — Review Drill

> 15 Q&A pairs · Mix of quick-fire recall and conceptual questions
> **How to use:** Cover the **A** column, answer from memory, then check.

---

## Quick-Fire Round

**Q1. What is the total height of a Falcon 9 (v1.2 / Block 5)?**
> **A1.** ~70 m (229.6 ft) including the payload fairing.

**Q2. What is the diameter of the Falcon 9 first and second stages?**
> **A2.** 3.7 m (12 ft) for both stages.

**Q3. How many Merlin 1D engines power the Falcon 9 first stage?**
> **A3.** Nine Merlin 1D engines in an octaweb arrangement.

**Q4. What is the Falcon 9 Block 5 payload capacity to LEO?**
> **A4.** ~22,800 kg (50,265 lb) in expendable mode.

**Q5. What is the Falcon 9 Block 5 payload capacity to GTO?**
> **A5.** ~8,300 kg (18,300 lb) in expendable mode; ~5,500 kg when recovering the booster.

**Q6. How many cores make up a Falcon Heavy?**
> **A6.** Three cores — one center core plus two side boosters (all derived from Falcon 9 Block 5).

**Q7. What is the total liftoff thrust of Falcon Heavy?**
> **A7.** ~22,819 kN (~5.13 million lbf) from 27 Merlin 1D engines.

**Q8. Name two landmark Falcon Heavy missions.**
> **A8.** Demo flight (Feb 2018, carried Elon's Tesla Roadster) and Arabsat-6A (Apr 2019, heaviest GTO payload to date for FH). Also notable: USSF-44 and Psyche.

**Q9. What is the total height of the Starship stack (Super Heavy + Ship)?**
> **A9.** ~121 m (397 ft).

**Q10. What is Starship's diameter?**
> **A10.** 9 m (30 ft) for both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship upper stage.

## Conceptual & Detail Round

**Q11. What is Starship's target payload capacity to LEO (fully reusable)?**
> **A11.** 100–150 tonnes (potentially 200+ t expendable, though expendable mode is not planned operationally).

**Q12. What primary material is Starship built from, and why was it chosen over carbon composites?**
> **A12.** 304L stainless steel. Chosen because it is cheap (~$3/kg vs ~$130/kg for CF), has excellent cryogenic strength (actually gets stronger when cold), tolerates high temperatures (~1,100 K) before losing significant strength, and is easy to weld and repair in the field.

**Q13. How many engines does Super Heavy carry, and what is its approximate total sea-level thrust?**
> **A13.** 33 Raptor engines producing ~7,590 tf (~74.4 MN) of sea-level thrust — the most powerful rocket booster ever flown.

**Q14. What is the design life of a Falcon 9 Block 5 booster?**
> **A14.** Designed for 10 flights without major refurbishment and up to 100 flights with periodic maintenance. In practice, individual boosters have exceeded 20 flights.

**Q15. What are the Crew Dragon's crew capacity and pressurized volume?**
> **A15.** Up to 4 crew for NASA missions (rated for up to 7). Pressurized volume is ~9.3 m³ (328 ft³).
