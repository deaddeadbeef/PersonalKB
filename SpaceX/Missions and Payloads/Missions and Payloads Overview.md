# Missions and Payloads Overview

SpaceX's mission manifest is the empirical record of everything the company has achieved — from the first Falcon 1 flight to over 300 Falcon 9 launches and beyond. Analyzing missions by customer, orbit, and payload type reveals how SpaceX built its track record and expanded into every segment of the launch market.

## Commercial Missions

Commercial satellite launches form a core revenue stream for SpaceX. Customers range from established operators like SES, Intelsat, and Eutelsat to newer entrants in LEO broadband and Earth observation. SpaceX's standard launch service agreement offers competitive pricing, reliable scheduling, and the option for rideshare on dedicated missions. Commercial contracts drove Falcon 9 adoption before government business scaled up.

## NASA Missions

SpaceX has flown dozens of missions for NASA spanning cargo resupply (CRS), crew transport (Commercial Crew), scientific payloads, and now lunar program hardware. Notable missions include the first commercial ISS berthing (COTS Demo, 2012), the return of American crewed launches (Demo-2, 2020), and science payloads like DART (asteroid deflection) and Europa Clipper. NASA remains SpaceX's anchor institutional customer.

## Rideshare and SmallSat

SpaceX's Transporter rideshare program offers dedicated sun-synchronous orbit missions where dozens to hundreds of small satellites share a single Falcon 9 launch. At prices starting around $275,000 per satellite, these missions have democratized orbital access for universities, startups, and small nations. The program has deployed thousands of smallsats across multiple missions.

## National Security Missions

SpaceX has won and executed an increasing share of U.S. national security space launch contracts through the NSSL program. These missions launch classified reconnaissance satellites, military communications systems, GPS satellites, and Space Force payloads. Certification for NSSL required years of proven reliability and represents significant institutional trust in SpaceX's capabilities.

## Starship Test Flights

The Starship integrated flight test campaign represents a new category of SpaceX missions — iterative, uncrewed test flights designed to progressively validate full-stack performance. Each flight test expands the envelope, from first stage separation through booster catch, ship reentry, and eventually orbital insertion. These tests generate engineering data that feeds directly into hardware and software updates.

## Launch Statistics and Records

SpaceX holds numerous records: highest annual launch rate by any provider, most reflights of an orbital-class booster, fastest turnaround between launches from the same pad, and the most satellites deployed by a single operator. Tracking these statistics provides a quantitative picture of SpaceX's operational maturity and its growing dominance in global launch share.
