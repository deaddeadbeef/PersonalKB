# Hughes Jupiter 3 Heaviest Commercial GEO Payload

Hughes Jupiter 3, launched July 28, 2023, via Falcon Heavy from LC-39A, was among the heaviest commercial geostationary communications satellites ever launched at approximately 9,200 kg. The payload required Falcon Heavy's additional performance beyond Falcon 9's capacity, demonstrating SpaceX's ability to serve the full mass range of the commercial GEO market with its vehicle family.
