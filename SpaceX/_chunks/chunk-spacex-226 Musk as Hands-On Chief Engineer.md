# Musk as Hands-On Chief Engineer

Elon Musk functions not merely as CEO but as self-described chief engineer at SpaceX, making hands-on technical decisions down to the component level. He reportedly reviews welding schedules, engine turbopump designs, and heat shield tile configurations personally. This centralized technical decision-making is a key enabler of SpaceX's rapid iteration pace, allowing architecture-level decisions to be made quickly without the extensive review boards and committee processes that characterize traditional aerospace primes.
