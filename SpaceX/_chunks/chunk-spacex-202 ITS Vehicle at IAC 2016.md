# ITS Vehicle at IAC 2016

The Interplanetary Transport System (ITS) was revealed at IAC 2016 in Guadalajara as SpaceX's first detailed Mars colonization vehicle. It featured a 12-meter fuselage diameter, a quoted Mars surface payload of approximately 300 metric tons, and capacity for 100 or more passengers. Industry reaction was mixed — technically ambitious but economically questionable at that scale, with no credible funding path presented for a vehicle of such size.
