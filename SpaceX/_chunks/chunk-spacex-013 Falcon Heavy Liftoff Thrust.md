# Falcon Heavy Liftoff Thrust

Falcon Heavy generates approximately 5.1 million pounds-force (22,819 kN) of thrust at liftoff from 27 Merlin 1D engines distributed across three Falcon 9 first-stage cores. The center core was structurally reinforced to withstand the asymmetric aerodynamic and thrust loads imposed by the two attached side boosters. SpaceX originally planned propellant cross-feed between boosters and center core but abandoned the concept as unnecessary.
