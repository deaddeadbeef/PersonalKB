# Grasshopper Test Vehicle Program

Grasshopper was a modified Falcon 9 v1.0 first stage fitted with fixed landing legs, serving as SpaceX's first vertical takeoff and vertical landing (VTOL) test vehicle. It completed 8 test flights at McGregor, Texas between September 2012 and October 2013, reaching a maximum altitude of 744 meters. Its successor, F9R Dev, added deployable landing legs and grid fins and flew from April to August 2014 before SpaceX transitioned to operational recovery attempts on orbital missions.
