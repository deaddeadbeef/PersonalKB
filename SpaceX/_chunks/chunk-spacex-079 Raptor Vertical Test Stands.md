# Raptor Vertical Test Stands

Raptor engines are tested on vertical stands at McGregor that replicate flight orientation, accommodating the engine's higher thrust levels and gimbal actuation under load. Unlike Merlin's horizontal test configuration, Raptor's vertical testing matches its operational conditions more closely. Hundreds of Raptor test firings at McGregor supported the iterative development of the full-flow staged combustion cycle — a technically demanding engine architecture where both fuel and oxidizer drive separate turbopumps in a closed loop.
