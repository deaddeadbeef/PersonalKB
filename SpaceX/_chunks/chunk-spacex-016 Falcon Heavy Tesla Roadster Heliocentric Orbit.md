# Falcon Heavy Tesla Roadster Heliocentric Orbit

The Falcon Heavy demonstration flight carried Elon Musk's personal midnight cherry Tesla Roadster as its payload, with a spacesuit-clad mannequin named "Starman" in the driver's seat. The Roadster was placed into a heliocentric orbit with an aphelion beyond the orbit of Mars, reaching approximately 1.66 AU from the Sun. Each side booster separates at approximately T+2 minutes 29 seconds during Falcon Heavy flights.
