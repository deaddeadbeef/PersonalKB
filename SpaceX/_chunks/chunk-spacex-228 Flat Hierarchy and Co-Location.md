# Flat Hierarchy and Co-Location

SpaceX operates with a relatively flat hierarchy compared to traditional aerospace primes like Boeing and Lockheed Martin. The Hawthorne, California headquarters features an open-plan layout with engineering, manufacturing, and mission control co-located in a single facility — deliberately designed to minimize communication barriers. This contrasts with traditional aerospace culture characterized by extensive review boards, conservative testing schedules, and cost-plus contracting incentives that discourage rapid iteration and highlight why SpaceX achieves development timelines that legacy primes consider impossible.
