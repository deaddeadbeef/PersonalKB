# IFT Campaign Five Flights in 18 Months

SpaceX conducted five Starship Integrated Flight Tests between April 2023 and October 2024, embodying an iterative "test, fail, learn, fly again" philosophy. Each flight carried instrumentation and telemetry packages rather than operational payloads, maximizing engineering data return. The campaign compressed a development timeline that would traditionally span a decade into under two years.
