# Self-Sustaining Mars Definition

Elon Musk has defined Mars self-sustainability as the threshold where the colony could survive indefinitely even if all supply ships from Earth stopped arriving. This requires full ISRU industrialization for propellant and construction materials, completely closed-loop life support systems, and local manufacturing capabilities. The long-term vision encompasses not just survival but expansion — asteroid mining for resources beyond what Mars provides and eventual expansion to the outer solar system, measured in timescales of decades to centuries.
