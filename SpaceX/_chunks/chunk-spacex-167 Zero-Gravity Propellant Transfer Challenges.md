# Zero-Gravity Propellant Transfer Challenges

Zero-gravity propellant transfer requires settling thrust or surface-tension propellant management devices to ensure liquid (not gas) flows through transfer lines. Ullage control maintains the pressure differential between donor and receiver tanks, critical for driving propellant flow without mechanical pumps in the baseline transfer architecture. Cryogenic fluid management—including boiloff minimization and ullage pressure control in microgravity—is the primary technical challenge.
