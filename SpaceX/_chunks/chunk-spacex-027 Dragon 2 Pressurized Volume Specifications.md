# Dragon 2 Pressurized Volume Specifications

Dragon 2 crew variant provides 9.3 cubic meters of pressurized habitable volume, accommodating up to 4 astronauts in standard ISS crew rotation configuration, expandable to 7 for contingency scenarios. The cargo variant offers 12.1 cubic meters of pressurized volume by removing crew seats and life support equipment, plus additional unpressurized trunk volume for external cargo and solar arrays.
