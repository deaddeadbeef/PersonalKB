# Merlin 1D Performance Specifications

Merlin 1D debuted on the Falcon 9 v1.1 in September 2013, producing 654 kN of thrust at sea level with a thrust-to-weight ratio exceeding 150:1. The gas generator cycle was deliberately chosen over staged combustion for manufacturing simplicity and rapid iteration. SpaceX vertically integrated Merlin production, manufacturing all engines in-house at Hawthorne, California.
