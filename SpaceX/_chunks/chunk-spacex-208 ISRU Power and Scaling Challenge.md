# ISRU Power and Scaling Challenge

Producing a single Starship's propellant load on Mars requires approximately 100 kW of continuous electrical power over the roughly 26-month period between landing and the next departure window. NASA's MOXIE experiment aboard the Perseverance rover provided a proof of concept, demonstrating oxygen extraction from Martian CO2 at approximately 10 grams per hour. However, operational Starship requirements demand roughly 1000 times MOXIE's throughput — a leap from laboratory demonstration to industrial-scale chemical processing in an environment with extreme temperature swings, pervasive dust, and no existing infrastructure.
