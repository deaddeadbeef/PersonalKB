# Commercial Crew Fixed-Price Savings

NASA estimated the Commercial Crew Program saved $20–30 billion compared to traditional cost-plus crew vehicle development. The fixed-price contract structure incentivized efficiency and risk management by the providers. Boeing's OFT-1 in December 2019 failed to reach the ISS due to two critical software errors—one of which could have caused a catastrophic collision with the service module—demonstrating that even well-funded traditional aerospace primes face challenges under fixed-price contracts.
