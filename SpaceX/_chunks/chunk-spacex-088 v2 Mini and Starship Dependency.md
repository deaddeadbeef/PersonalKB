# v2 Mini and Starship Dependency

The v2 Mini satellite (~800 kg) represents the maximum satellite capability achievable within Falcon 9 fairing volume and mass constraints, fitting 21–23 per launch compared to 60 for v1.0. The full-size v2 (~1,250 kg) is too large for Falcon 9 and requires Starship, which had not achieved operational cargo deployment as of early 2024. This creates a critical strategic dependency where Starship delays directly constrain Starlink's future capacity growth.
