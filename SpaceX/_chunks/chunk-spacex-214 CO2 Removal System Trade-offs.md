# CO2 Removal System Trade-offs

CO2 removal is the most immediately critical life support function for Mars transit. Two primary approaches exist: lithium hydroxide (LiOH) canisters, which remove CO2 via irreversible chemical reaction but are single-use and mass-intensive; and regenerable amine swing-bed systems, which adsorb CO2 and release it when heated, allowing indefinite cycling. The trade-off is simplicity and reliability (LiOH) versus mass efficiency and indefinite reuse (amine systems). ISS water recovery systems achieve approximately 90-93% reclamation from urine, humidity condensate, and wastewater.
