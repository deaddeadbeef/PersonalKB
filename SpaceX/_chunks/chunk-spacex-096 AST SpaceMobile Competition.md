# AST SpaceMobile Competition

AST SpaceMobile is SpaceX's primary competitor in direct-to-cell satellite service. AST launched the BlueWalker 3 test satellite in September 2022 with a 64 m² phased array—larger than SpaceX's 25 m²—and demonstrated voice calls and data streaming from unmodified phones. However, AST must build its entire constellation from scratch, whereas SpaceX can leverage its existing thousands of deployed Starlink satellites, providing a massive deployment advantage.
