# SpaceX EVA Suit Debut

SpaceX's purpose-built EVA suit debuted on Polaris Dawn, distinct from the standard intravehicular pressure suit used on previous Crew Dragon missions. The suit features a 3D-printed helmet with HUD visor, thermal micrometeorite garment layers, new joint bearings for mobility, and a tethered umbilical for life support. The suit positions SpaceX for future Starship lunar and Mars operations requiring extravehicular capability.
