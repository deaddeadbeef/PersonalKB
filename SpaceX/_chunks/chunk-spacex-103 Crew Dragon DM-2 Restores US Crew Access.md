# Crew Dragon DM-2 Restores US Crew Access

SpaceX's DM-2 mission launched May 30, 2020, with NASA astronauts Doug Hurley and Bob Behnken aboard Crew Dragon Endeavour, docking with the ISS for a 64-day mission. This restored US crew launch capability after nine years and validated commercial human spaceflight to the ISS. SpaceX met milestones methodically: DM-1 (uncrewed, March 2, 2019), in-flight abort test (January 2020), then DM-2. By 2024, SpaceX had completed eight operational crew rotations.
