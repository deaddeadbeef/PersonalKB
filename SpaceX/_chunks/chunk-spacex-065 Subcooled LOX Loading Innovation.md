# Subcooled LOX Loading Innovation

Falcon 9 uses deeply subcooled liquid oxygen chilled to approximately -207°C (66 K), roughly 24°C below its standard boiling point. This subcooling increases LOX density by approximately 8%, allowing more oxidizer mass in the fixed tank volume — a significant performance gain introduced with Falcon 9 Full Thrust (v1.2) in 2015. The tradeoff is a finite launch window of approximately 30-45 minutes before warming degrades the density advantage and performance falls below acceptable margins.
