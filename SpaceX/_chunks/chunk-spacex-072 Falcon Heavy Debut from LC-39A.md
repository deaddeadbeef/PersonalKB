# Falcon Heavy Debut from LC-39A

Falcon Heavy's demonstration flight (February 2018) was the most powerful rocket to launch from LC-39A since Saturn V. The mission launched Elon Musk's Tesla Roadster on an Earth-Mars transfer orbit as a demonstration payload. LC-39A required wider strongback and hold-down modifications to accommodate Falcon Heavy's triple-core configuration. Managing shared use of the pad between high-cadence Starlink missions, occasional Falcon Heavy flights, and crew Dragon missions presents ongoing scheduling challenges, partially mitigated by the availability of SLC-40.
