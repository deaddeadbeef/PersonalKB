# IFT-1 Pad Damage and Flight Termination

IFT-1 (April 20, 2023) ended when the Flight Termination System activated at approximately T+4 minutes after the vehicle failed to achieve stage separation, with multiple Raptor engines lost during ascent. The launch caused substantial damage to the Orbital Launch Mount at Starbase, leading SpaceX to install a steel-plate water-deluge flame deflector system before subsequent flights.
