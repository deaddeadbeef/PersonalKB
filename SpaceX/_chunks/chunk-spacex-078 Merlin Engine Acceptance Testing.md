# Merlin Engine Acceptance Testing

Every Merlin 1D engine undergoes an individual full-duration acceptance firing at McGregor before vehicle integration. Merlin engines are tested on horizontal stands, an orientation that simplifies engine installation, instrumentation hookup, and post-test inspection compared to vertical testing. This test-everything-before-flight philosophy means every engine that flies has demonstrated its performance under controlled conditions. The facility now processes hundreds of engines per year, reflecting SpaceX's growth from startup to the world's highest-cadence launch provider.
