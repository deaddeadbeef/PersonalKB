# Super Heavy First Successful Tower Catch

Booster 13 (B13) achieved the first successful tower catch on Integrated Flight Test 5 (IFT-5) in October 2024, marking a historic milestone in rocket reusability. Earlier, Booster 9 (B9) had become the first Super Heavy to perform a controlled descent and near-catch maneuver during IFT-4 in June 2024. SpaceX's rapid iterative approach saw construction and testing of boosters B4 through B14 in approximately three years at Starbase, Boca Chica, Texas.
