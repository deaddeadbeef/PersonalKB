# Starbase Site Selection and Latitude

SpaceX obtained FAA launch site operator licenses for Boca Chica, Texas in 2014, originally for Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy commercial launches. At 25.99°N latitude, Starbase is the southernmost continental U.S. launch site — closer to the equator than Cape Canaveral (28.5°N), offering favorable orbital inclination access. The site provides over-water eastward trajectories without overflying populated areas and benefits from lower land costs. SpaceX pivoted the site to Starship development circa 2018, fundamentally changing its scope.
