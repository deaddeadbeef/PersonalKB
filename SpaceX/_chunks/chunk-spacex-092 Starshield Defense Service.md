# Starshield Defense Service

Starshield is the defense-oriented Starlink variant featuring enhanced encryption, government-specific ground infrastructure, and classified capability tiers. It is sold to the US military and allied nations with classified pricing, operating through a separate service tier from commercial Starlink. The government and defense segment runs through Starshield, positioning SpaceX as a provider of military-grade satellite communications alongside its commercial broadband service.
