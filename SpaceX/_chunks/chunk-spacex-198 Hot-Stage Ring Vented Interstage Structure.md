# Hot-Stage Ring Vented Interstage Structure

SpaceX developed a dedicated hot-stage ring—a cylindrical steel structure with large venting ports positioned atop the Super Heavy booster—that redirects the Ship's engine exhaust outward and away from the booster's forward dome during the ignition overlap period. The ring must withstand direct exposure to multiple Raptor exhaust plumes (temperatures exceeding 3,000°C in the core flow) for several seconds while maintaining structural integrity for clean separation.
