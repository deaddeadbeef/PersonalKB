# Bandwagon Mid-Inclination Rideshare Series

SpaceX introduced the Bandwagon mid-inclination rideshare series in 2024, expanding the rideshare program beyond the sun-synchronous orbit corridor to serve defense and commercial customers needing ~45° orbital inclinations. Bandwagon-1 launched April 7, 2024, to a ~45° inclination orbit, carrying payloads for the Space Development Agency (SDA) and commercial customers. This expansion signaled SpaceX's intent to serve the full spectrum of orbital inclinations.
