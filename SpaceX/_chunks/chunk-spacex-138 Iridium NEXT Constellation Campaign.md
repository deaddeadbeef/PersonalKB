# Iridium NEXT Constellation Campaign

The Iridium NEXT campaign comprised 10 Falcon 9 launches between January 2017 and January 2019, deploying all 75 operational satellites (plus spares) for the Iridium constellation replacement. All launches occurred from Vandenberg SLC-4E with a mean interval of approximately 10 weeks between launches. The campaign demonstrated constellation-scale reliability and cadence, establishing SpaceX as the preferred launch provider for large satellite deployments.
