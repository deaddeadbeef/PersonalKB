# Military Cargo Transport Application

Military logistics applications of point-to-point Starship transport have received more serious consideration than commercial passenger service. US Transportation Command (USTRANSCOM) has publicly expressed interest in rocket-based rapid global cargo delivery, targeting approximately 80 tons of material delivered to any point on Earth in under one hour. Military applications face lower safety-certification barriers (accepting higher risk for cargo), carry no passenger-noise concerns, and address a genuine capability gap in strategic airlift response time that no existing system can fill.
