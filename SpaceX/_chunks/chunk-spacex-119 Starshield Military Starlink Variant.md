# Starshield Military Starlink Variant

Starshield was publicly announced by SpaceX in December 2022 as a military variant of Starlink. It is distinguished from commercial Starlink by government-controlled encryption keys, dedicated ground stations, anti-jamming capabilities, and classified capability tiers. Starshield represents SpaceX's strategy to capture military satellite communications and sensing markets by adapting proven Starlink infrastructure with defense-grade enhancements, positioning the company as an integrated defense space infrastructure provider.
