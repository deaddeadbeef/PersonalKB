# Krypton Ion Thrusters Cost Reduction

Starlink v1.0 satellites use krypton rather than xenon for their Hall-effect ion thrusters. This choice reduces propellant costs by roughly an order of magnitude at the expense of some specific impulse. The cost-optimized thruster design reflects SpaceX's emphasis on manufacturing economics at constellation scale, where thousands of satellites must be produced and fueled affordably.
