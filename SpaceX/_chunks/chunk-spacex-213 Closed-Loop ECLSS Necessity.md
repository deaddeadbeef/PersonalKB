# Closed-Loop ECLSS Necessity

Closed-loop Environmental Control and Life Support Systems (ECLSS) are essential for Mars-duration missions because open-loop systems — carrying all consumables from Earth — scale catastrophically with crew size and mission duration. The ECLSS must sustain crew health across two different operating regimes: the microgravity transit phase (6-9 months) and the partial-gravity Mars surface phase (0.38g). Closed-loop systems recycle air, water, and waste, reducing resupply mass but introducing mechanical complexity and additional failure modes that must be managed.
