# FAA Environmental Assessment for Starbase

The FAA completed a Programmatic Environmental Assessment for Starbase in June 2022, issuing a Mitigated Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) that allows launches subject to 75+ environmental mitigation measures. These measures address wildlife protection, debris management, noise limits, and water quality. Starbase is adjacent to the Lower Rio Grande Valley National Wildlife Refuge, creating ongoing environmental tension — operations require coordination with U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service regarding piping plovers and other nesting shorebirds.
