# Booster Turnaround Time Compression

SpaceX compressed Falcon 9 booster turnaround time from over 12 months for the first reflight (B1021, 2017) to under 21 days in the fastest cases. Block 5's design-for-reuse features — durable thermal protection, improved turbopump coatings, and bolted (rather than welded) landing legs — significantly reduced the scope of between-flight refurbishment. The standard refurbishment process includes post-flight inspection, engine and structural checks, consumable replacement, and a static fire before flight clearance.
