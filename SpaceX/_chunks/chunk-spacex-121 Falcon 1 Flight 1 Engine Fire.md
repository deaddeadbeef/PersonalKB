# Falcon 1 Flight 1 Engine Fire

Falcon 1 Flight 1 (March 24, 2006) failed at T+25 seconds when a corroded aluminum B-nut on a fuel line fitting caused a Merlin engine fire. The corrosion was exacerbated by the salt-air environment of the Kwajalein Atoll launch site on Omelek Island in the tropical Pacific. The vehicle was lost shortly after liftoff.
