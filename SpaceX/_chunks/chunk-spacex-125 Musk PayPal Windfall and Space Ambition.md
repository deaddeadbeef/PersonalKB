# Musk PayPal Windfall and Space Ambition

Elon Musk received approximately $165 million (pre-tax) from his 11.7% ownership stake in PayPal when eBay acquired it in October 2002. He committed $100 million of personal funds to SpaceX, which was incorporated May 6, 2002, and officially founded June 28, 2002. His initial concept, "Mars Oasis," aimed to send a greenhouse to Mars carrying seeds in dehydrated nutrient gel to reignite public interest in space exploration.
