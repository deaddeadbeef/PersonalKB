# Soyuz Dependency After Shuttle Retirement

The Space Shuttle's final mission (STS-135, Atlantis) launched July 8, 2011, ending 30 years of Shuttle operations. This created a nine-year gap in US crew launch capability (2011–2020), during which NASA was completely dependent on Russia's Soyuz for ISS crew transport. Soyuz seat prices escalated from approximately $21.3 million per seat in 2006 to over $80 million by 2018, reflecting Russia's monopoly pricing power.
