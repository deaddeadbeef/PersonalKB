# Mars Base Power Architecture Evolution

Power architecture for the Mars base evolves across phases. Initial operations rely on deployable solar arrays, which perform adequately at Mars's orbital distance — roughly 43% of Earth's solar flux at the equator — for small crew sizes. As power demand grows with ISRU propellant production, habitat expansion, and industrial processes, nuclear fission reactors become necessary. NASA's Kilopower project targets 10 kW-class reactors suitable for Mars surface deployment. Site selection prioritizes low elevation for atmospheric benefits and near-equatorial latitude for optimizing solar energy.
