# Crew Dragon Mark 3 Parachute System

The Crew Dragon Mark 3 parachute system deploys in two stages: two drogue chutes first stabilize the capsule at supersonic speeds, followed by four main parachutes that slow the descent to approximately 7.6 m/s at splashdown. The system features redundancy design allowing safe crew recovery even with one main parachute failure. The all-glass touchscreen cockpit replaced traditional mechanical switches, representing a fundamental departure from prior crewed spacecraft.
