# Dragon Rad-Hardened Avionics for Crew Safety

Dragon crew vehicles employ radiation-hardened processors for crew safety, reflecting NASA human-rating requirements demanding higher single-unit reliability for crewed missions—a different philosophy from Falcon 9's COTS triple-redundancy approach. Dragon's touchscreen-based crew interface runs on separate display computers from the flight-critical avionics, ensuring that a display system failure cannot affect vehicle control.
