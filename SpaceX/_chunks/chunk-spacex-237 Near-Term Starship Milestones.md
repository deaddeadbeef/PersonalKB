# Near-Term Starship Milestones

SpaceX's critical near-term objectives center on achieving routine Starship operations: successful orbital flights, reliable booster catch-and-reuse, payload deployment for both commercial and government customers, and execution of the Artemis Human Landing System (HLS) contract requiring SpaceX to deliver a Starship lunar lander for NASA astronaut Moon landings. These milestones gate all subsequent Mars ambitions — without operational Starship reliability, the Mars campaign model cannot begin.
