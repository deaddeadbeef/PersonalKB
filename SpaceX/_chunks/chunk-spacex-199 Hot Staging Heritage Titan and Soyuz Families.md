# Hot Staging Heritage Titan and Soyuz Families

Hot staging has operational heritage in multiple rocket families. Titan II, III, and IV rockets used hot staging for decades, with the second stage igniting through a hole in the interstage while the first stage was still thrusting. Soviet and Russian rockets including the Soyuz family derived from the R-7 employ a form of hot staging where strap-on boosters separate while the core stage fires, demonstrating the technique across thousands of flights.
