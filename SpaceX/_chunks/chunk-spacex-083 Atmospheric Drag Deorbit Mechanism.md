# Atmospheric Drag Deorbit Mechanism

Satellites at 550 km altitude experience sufficient atmospheric drag to naturally deorbit within approximately five years, serving as a built-in debris mitigation mechanism. At the lower 340 km near-polar shell, decay occurs even faster, within one to two years. This deliberate choice of low-altitude orbits with guaranteed deorbit timelines established a new industry standard for responsible space operations.
