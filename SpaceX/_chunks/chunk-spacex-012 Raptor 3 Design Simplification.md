# Raptor 3 Design Simplification

Raptor 3 targets approximately 280 tonnes-force of thrust with a further simplified and more compact design compared to Raptor 2. Key changes include removal of the outer engine heat shield and gimbal actuator cowling, significantly reducing mass and part count. As of mid-2024, SpaceX had produced over 300 Raptor engines with a target production rate exceeding one engine per day.
