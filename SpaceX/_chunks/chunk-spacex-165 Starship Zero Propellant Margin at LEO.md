# Starship Zero Propellant Margin at LEO

Starship reaches low Earth orbit with effectively zero remaining propellant margin for beyond-LEO maneuvers, making orbital refueling a mission-critical capability rather than an optional enhancement. While the vehicle can deliver over 100 tonnes to LEO in expendable mode, it arrives with nearly empty tanks insufficient for translunar injection or Mars departure burns.
