# Stage Separation and Parallel Operations

Falcon 9's first stage burns for approximately 2 minutes 30 seconds to main engine cutoff (MECO). Stage separation via pneumatic pushers then initiates two parallel operational paths: the second stage Merlin Vacuum engine ignites for orbit insertion, while the first stage executes its recovery burn sequence — boostback burn (if returning to launch site), three-engine entry burn for atmospheric deceleration, and single-engine landing burn for precision touchdown. Payload fairing deployment occurs approximately 3 minutes after launch.
