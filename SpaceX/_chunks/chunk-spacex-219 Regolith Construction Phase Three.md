# Regolith Construction Phase Three

Phase 3 of the Mars base envisions large-scale construction using In-Situ Resource Utilization — specifically sintered or 3D-printed Martian regolith structures. Processed regolith provides radiation shielding, structural mass, and thermal insulation without the extreme cost of transporting building materials from Earth. This phase scales the base from a small outpost toward Musk's long-term stated aspiration of a self-sustaining Mars city of approximately one million people over multiple decades, though the intermediate growth path remains largely unspecified.
