# Gap Filler Erosion Primary TPS Failure Mode

IFT reentry data revealed localized heating anomalies at tile gaps and flap hinge lines, where aerodynamic flow channeling concentrates thermal energy. Gap filler erosion was identified as a primary failure mode, leading to localized overheating of the steel substrate. Flap hinge lines are particularly challenging because gap geometry changes dynamically during reentry as control surfaces actuate, creating variable-width gaps exposed to hypersonic flow.
