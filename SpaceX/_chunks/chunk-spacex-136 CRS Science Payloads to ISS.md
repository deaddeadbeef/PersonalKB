# CRS Science Payloads to ISS

Across both CRS contract phases, Dragon delivered critical science payloads to the ISS including the Cold Atom Lab (quantum physics research in microgravity), ECOSTRESS (plant water stress monitoring from orbit), and hundreds of CubeSats. These deliveries demonstrated Dragon's role not just as a logistics vehicle but as an enabler of fundamental science research that depended on reliable and frequent access to the orbital laboratory.
