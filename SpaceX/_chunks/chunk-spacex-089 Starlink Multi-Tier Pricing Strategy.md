# Starlink Multi-Tier Pricing Strategy

Starlink operates a diversified multi-tier pricing model. US residential service costs approximately $120/month plus a $599 one-time terminal fee. Business tier offers 150–500 Mbps with priority network access at $250–$500/month. Maritime service starts at approximately $5,000/month for 50 Mbps, with major cruise lines like Royal Caribbean deploying fleet-wide. Maritime customers pay roughly 40x the residential rate, demonstrating significant revenue extraction across segments.
