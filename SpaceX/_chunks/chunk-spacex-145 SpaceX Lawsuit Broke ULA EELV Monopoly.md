# SpaceX Lawsuit Broke ULA EELV Monopoly

SpaceX filed suit against the US Air Force in April 2014, challenging the sole-source award to ULA of a block-buy contract for 36 EELV cores worth billions of dollars. The lawsuit argued the contract violated competitive procurement law and unfairly excluded qualified providers. The settlement opened future competitions, and the Air Force certified Falcon 9 for NSSL (then called EELV) missions in May 2015 after SpaceX completed required certification flights.
