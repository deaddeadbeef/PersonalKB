# 18000 Hexagonal Tiles Mechanical Attachment

Approximately 18,000 hexagonal heat shield tiles cover the windward surface of Starship, each mechanically fastened to studs welded to the stainless steel substrate. Each tile is approximately 15–20 centimeters across and designed as a standalone thermal protection unit, meaning a single damaged tile can be swapped without removing or disturbing neighboring tiles. The hexagonal geometry minimizes thermal expansion gaps and distributes aerodynamic loads more evenly than rectangular tiles.
