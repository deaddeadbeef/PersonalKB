# Falcon 9 Stage Separation Parameters

Falcon 9 stage separation occurs at approximately T+2 minutes 33 seconds into flight, at roughly 70 km altitude and 2.3 km/s velocity. Second engine start (SES-1) follows separation by approximately 8 seconds, with the second-stage burn duration varying by mission profile.
