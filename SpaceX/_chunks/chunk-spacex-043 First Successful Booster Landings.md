# First Successful Booster Landings

The OG-2 mission (December 2015) achieved the first-ever return-to-launch-site (RTLS) landing of an orbital-class rocket booster at Cape Canaveral's Landing Zone 1. Four months later, CRS-8 (April 2016) accomplished the first successful autonomous drone ship landing at sea. The drone ship capability was critical because high-energy missions lack sufficient fuel margin to reverse course and return to the launch site, making at-sea recovery the only viable path.
