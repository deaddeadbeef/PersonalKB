# Smallsat Rideshare Program

SpaceX's Smallsat Rideshare Program created a new market tier on dedicated Transporter missions, offering prices starting at approximately $5,500 per kg for rideshare slots. This made orbit accessible for startups, universities, and small nations, enabling constellation-scale deployments by organizations that previously could not afford dedicated launches. The program democratized space access by providing routine, affordable rideshare opportunities to low Earth orbit.
