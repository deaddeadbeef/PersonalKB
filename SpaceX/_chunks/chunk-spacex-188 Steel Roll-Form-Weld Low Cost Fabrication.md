# Steel Roll-Form-Weld Low Cost Fabrication

Starship's stainless steel construction enables simplified fabrication: 304L steel sheets are roll-formed into cylindrical sections and welded using automated submerged arc or MIG welding processes, far less complex than carbon composite layup. This can be performed with standard industrial metalworking equipment, dramatically lowering capital tooling costs compared to carbon fiber autoclaves or aluminum-lithium friction stir welding fixtures.
