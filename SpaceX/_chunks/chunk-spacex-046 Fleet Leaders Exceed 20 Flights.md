# Fleet Leaders Exceed 20 Flights

By 2024, Falcon 9 fleet leaders B1058, B1060, and B1061 each surpassed 20 flights, far exceeding Block 5's original 10-flight design target. B1058 first flew on Demo-2 (May 2020), SpaceX's inaugural crewed ISS mission, then accumulated most subsequent flights on Starlink missions. B1060 entered service on a GPS III mission (June 2020). By this point, new boosters were manufactured only to replace retiring cores or expand the fleet — reused hardware became the norm.
