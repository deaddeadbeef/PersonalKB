# Airline Model Economics Analogy

The airline analogy for rocket reuse is structurally accurate: a Boeing 737 costing $100 million flies approximately 30,000 flights over its lifetime, amortizing capital cost to roughly $3,300 per flight. A Falcon 9 booster costing $40-45 million and flying 20+ times amortizes to approximately $2 million per flight. At 90+ missions per year with predominantly reused boosters, SpaceX's annual savings relative to expendable operations are estimated at $3-4 billion.
