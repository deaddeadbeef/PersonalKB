# Legacy FTS Range Bottleneck

The legacy ground-commanded Flight Termination System (FTS) required dedicated ground-based radar tracking infrastructure and a human range safety officer to monitor vehicle trajectory and send destruct commands if the vehicle deviated from its approved corridor. This system limited each range to one active launch at a time, effectively constraining Cape Canaveral's Eastern Range to roughly one launch per month. The personnel and infrastructure requirements created a fundamental bottleneck on launch cadence.
