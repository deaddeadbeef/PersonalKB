# Stainless Steel Higher Temperature Tolerance

Stainless steel's melting point (approximately 1,400–1,450°C) compared to aluminum alloys (~660°C) means the Starship airframe can tolerate significantly more heat soak-through during reentry before structural compromise, reducing thermal protection system mass requirements. Musk has publicly noted that steel's reflectivity also provides a modest thermal advantage, reflecting a portion of reentry radiative heating that a dark carbon fiber surface would absorb.
