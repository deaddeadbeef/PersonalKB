# 304L Low Carbon Variant Cryo Advantages

The 304L designation indicates a low-carbon variant of 304 stainless steel, which improves weldability and resistance to intergranular corrosion—critical properties for a welded cryogenic pressure vessel undergoing repeated thermal cycling. Carbon fiber composites can experience microcracking at cryogenic temperatures due to differential thermal expansion between fiber and matrix, potentially creating leak paths in tank walls—a risk stainless steel does not share.
