# Block 5 Booster Flight and Turnaround Records

Individual Block 5 boosters have exceeded 20 flights each, with booster B1058 among the highest-flown cores, well surpassing the original 10-flight design target. Turnaround time between flights for a single booster has been reduced to as little as 21 days, compared to months of refurbishment required for earlier Falcon 9 versions. A fleet of 10+ active boosters supports a manifest of 60+ launches per year.
