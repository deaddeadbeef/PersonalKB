# Suborbital Intercity Transport Concept

SpaceX's point-to-point (P2P) Earth transport concept, first presented at IAC 2017 alongside the BFR reveal, proposes using Starship for suborbital intercity passenger travel. Headline claims include New York to Tokyo in approximately 30 minutes and London to Sydney in under an hour. The concept envisions Starship launching from offshore platforms, flying a suborbital ballistic trajectory reaching space, and landing on a similar platform near the destination city. Suborbital ballistic trajectories are dramatically faster than subsonic air travel for intercontinental distances.
