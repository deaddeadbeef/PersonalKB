# Ariane 6 Structural Disadvantage

Arianespace retired Ariane 5 on July 5, 2023 (final mission VA261) after 27 years of service. Ariane 6 completed its maiden flight on July 9, 2024, approximately four years behind the original schedule. The vehicle is expendable with no funded reusable program, creating a structural disadvantage against SpaceX's reusable economics. Russia's launch industry has also contracted sharply post-sanctions, conducting approximately 19 orbital launches in 2023 serving only domestic or allied-nation customers.
