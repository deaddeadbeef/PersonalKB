# Starship Variants Share 9m Platform

All Starship variants share the same 9-meter outer diameter, overall length of approximately 50 meters for the Ship, and six-Raptor engine configuration (three sea-level, three vacuum-optimized). This modularity allows SpaceX to address diverse mission profiles—from crewed Mars transit to military applications—without designing entirely new vehicles. Manufacturing commonality across variants amortizes factory tooling and reduces per-unit costs.
