# First Commercial Spacecraft to Dock with ISS

Dragon C2+ (COTS 2) became the first commercial spacecraft to dock with the International Space Station on May 25, 2012, demonstrating autonomous rendezvous and berthing capabilities. This milestone validated NASA's investment in commercial cargo development and confirmed SpaceX's ability to deliver operational space logistics services.
