# SDA Proliferated LEO Launch Contracts

The Space Development Agency awarded SpaceX contracts for Tranche 0/1 Transport Layer satellite deployment, contributing to a planned mesh of hundreds of LEO military satellites for missile tracking. SpaceX's demonstrated rapid launch cadence provides unique national security value for responsive space—the ability to quickly replace or reconstitute satellite constellations during a conflict. This positions SpaceX as a critical launch provider for the military's next-generation proliferated LEO architecture.
