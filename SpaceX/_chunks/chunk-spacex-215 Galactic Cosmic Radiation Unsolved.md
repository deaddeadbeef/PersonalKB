# Galactic Cosmic Radiation Unsolved

Radiation represents the most intractable life support challenge for Mars transit. Solar particle events (SPEs) from coronal mass ejections can deliver acute lethal doses but are intermittent and somewhat predictable. Galactic cosmic radiation (GCR) — high-energy heavy ions from outside the solar system — delivers chronic low-level exposure for which no practical shielding solution exists at reasonable mass budgets. Partial mitigation approaches include water walls, polyethylene shielding, and regolith burial on the Martian surface, but none adequately address the transit-phase GCR exposure.
