# Starlink Autonomous Collision Avoidance

Starlink satellite avionics include autonomous collision avoidance systems that process conjunction data and execute maneuvers without ground operator intervention, managing a constellation of thousands of satellites in low Earth orbit. The satellites use krypton-fueled Hall-effect ion thrusters for orbit raising and station-keeping, controlled by onboard avionics that autonomously plan and execute maneuvers based on orbital parameters and conjunction warnings.
