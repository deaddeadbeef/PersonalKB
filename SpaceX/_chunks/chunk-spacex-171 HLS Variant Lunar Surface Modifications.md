# HLS Variant Lunar Surface Modifications

The Starship HLS variant omits the standard thermal protection system since it will not perform Earth atmospheric reentry. It features high-thrust RCS landing thrusters positioned high on the vehicle to minimize lunar regolith disruption, preventing exhaust from excavating regolith into ballistic trajectories that could damage hardware. The design includes a cargo airlock for deploying equipment to the lunar surface.
