# Starshield Military Variant Classified

The Starshield variant is a military/national security configuration with classified modifications, developed in partnership with the U.S. Department of Defense for missions that may include reconnaissance, communications, or space domain awareness. Publicly acknowledged by SpaceX in 2022 with a dedicated webpage, but technical details remain largely classified under government contracts.
