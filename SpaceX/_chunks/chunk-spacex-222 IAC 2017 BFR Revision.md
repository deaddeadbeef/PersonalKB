# IAC 2017 BFR Revision

At IAC 2017 in Adelaide, Australia, Musk presented "Making Life Multiplanetary," revealing the BFR as a substantially revised architecture. The vehicle shrank to 9 meters in diameter with 31 Raptor engines and a quoted payload-to-LEO of approximately 150 metric tons in fully reusable configuration. The single-vehicle-family strategy consolidated satellite deployment, crew transport, ISS resupply, lunar missions, Mars operations, and Earth point-to-point transport. Industry analysts considered the 2017 talk more credible because it presented a plausible business case.
