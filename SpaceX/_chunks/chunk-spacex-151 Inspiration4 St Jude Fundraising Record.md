# Inspiration4 St Jude Fundraising Record

Inspiration4 raised over $240 million for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, including Jared Isaacman's personal $100 million contribution. Each crew member represented a charitable pillar — Arceneaux (hope), Proctor (prosperity), Sembroski (generosity) — establishing spaceflight as a philanthropy platform. Netflix's *Countdown: Inspiration4 Mission to Space* provided unprecedented public access across five episodes in September 2021.
