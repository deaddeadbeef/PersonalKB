# Polaris Dawn Record Altitude Orbit

Polaris Dawn, launched September 10, 2024, from LC-39A, achieved an apogee of approximately 1,400 km — the highest crewed Earth orbit since Apollo 17 in 1972. The high orbit placed the crew (Jared Isaacman, Scott Poteet, Sarah Gillis, Anna Menon) in the inner Van Allen radiation belt for several orbits before lowering to ~700 km for the EVA phase, demonstrating that private missions can achieve exploration-class objectives.
