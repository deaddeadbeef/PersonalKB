# ITS-to-BFR Funding Realism Shift

The shift from the ITS to the BFR between IAC 2016 and 2017 was driven primarily by funding realism. The ITS presented no credible funding path for a vehicle of its scale. The smaller BFR serving multiple markets — satellite deployment, ISS resupply, lunar missions, Mars, and point-to-point — could generate revenue across all those segments to cross-subsidize Mars development. Industry reception shifted from "visionary but impractical" (2016) to "ambitious but plausible" (2017) as the business case became more coherent.
