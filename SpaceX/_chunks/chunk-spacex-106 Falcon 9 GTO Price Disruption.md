# Falcon 9 GTO Price Disruption

Falcon 9 reusability, operational from 2017, cut commercial GTO launch prices from the $100–200 million range (typical for Ariane 5 or Proton) to approximately $60–70 million—a roughly 50–65% reduction. Previously, Arianespace dominated the GTO market with approximately 50% share, ULA served US government exclusively, and Russia's Proton competed on price. The competitive pressure from SpaceX has contributed to the decline or retirement of multiple legacy launch systems.
