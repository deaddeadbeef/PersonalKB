# SmallSat Rideshare Program Pricing Disruption

SpaceX announced the SmallSat Rideshare Program in August 2019 with a starting price of $1 million for 200 kg to sun-synchronous orbit, later adding a $275,000 minimum for 50 kg payloads. This pricing undercut dedicated small satellite launchers by roughly 5–10× on a per-kilogram basis, fundamentally pressuring the business cases of competitors like Rocket Lab, Virgin Orbit (now defunct), and Astra.
