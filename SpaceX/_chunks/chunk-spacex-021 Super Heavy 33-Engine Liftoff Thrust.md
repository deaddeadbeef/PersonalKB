# Super Heavy 33-Engine Liftoff Thrust

Super Heavy's 33 Raptor engines produce approximately 7,590 tonnes-force (74,400 kN) of total liftoff thrust, roughly double that of the Saturn V. The booster carries approximately 3,400 tonnes of subcooled LOX/LCH4 propellant, constituting roughly 80% of the full stack's propellant mass. Super Heavy stands approximately 71 meters tall and 9 meters in diameter.
