# Laser Inter-Satellite Links

Laser inter-satellite links introduced with Starlink v1.5 (September 2021) fundamentally changed the network architecture by removing mandatory ground station relay hops. These links use optical wavelengths and achieve approximately 100 Gbps throughput, with latency advantages over terrestrial fiber for long-distance routing. The capability is essential for providing coverage over oceans and polar regions where ground stations are impractical.
