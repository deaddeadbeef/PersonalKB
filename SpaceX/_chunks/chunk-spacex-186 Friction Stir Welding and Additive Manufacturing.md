# Friction Stir Welding and Additive Manufacturing

Friction stir welding (FSW)—a solid-state joining process using a rotating tool to plastically deform and join metal without melting—is used extensively for Falcon 9 aluminum-lithium alloy tank manufacturing, producing welds with superior fatigue life. Additive manufacturing (3D printing) is used for critical engine components, including the SuperDraco Inconel combustion chambers on Dragon printed as a single piece—one of the first flight-qualified 3D-printed rocket engine chambers.
