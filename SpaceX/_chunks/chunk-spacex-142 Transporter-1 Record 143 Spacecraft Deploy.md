# Transporter-1 Record 143 Spacecraft Deploy

Transporter-1 launched January 24, 2021, from SLC-40, Cape Canaveral, carrying 143 spacecraft to a ~525 km sun-synchronous orbit, shattering the prior single-launch deployment record of 104 satellites. Subsequent Transporter missions have flown at roughly quarterly cadence, establishing a regular and predictable rideshare schedule that allows smallsat operators to plan missions with reliable launch dates.
