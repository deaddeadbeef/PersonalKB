# LC-39A Historical Significance

Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center is the most historically significant active U.S. launch pad. It launched Apollo 11 (July 1969), Apollo 13, the Skylab space station, and 82 Space Shuttle missions including STS-1, the first Shuttle flight (April 1981), and STS-135, the final Shuttle mission (July 2011). After the Shuttle program's retirement in 2011, the pad sat dormant until NASA signed a 20-year exclusive-use lease with SpaceX in April 2014, selected over a competing bid from Blue Origin.
