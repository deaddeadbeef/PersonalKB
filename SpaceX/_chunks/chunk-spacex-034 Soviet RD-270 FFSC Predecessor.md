# Soviet RD-270 FFSC Predecessor

The Soviet Union's RD-270 engine was the first serious full-flow staged combustion cycle development program, designed in the 1960s for the UR-700 heavy-lift vehicle. The RD-270 targeted approximately 260 bar chamber pressure using N2O4/UDMH propellants but suffered from combustion instability and was cancelled around 1970 without ever reaching flight status. Raptor is the first FFSC engine to overcome these challenges and achieve flight.
