# Gen1 Gen2 Constellation Scale

The Starlink Gen1 FCC authorization approved approximately 12,000 satellites across multiple orbital shells. The Gen2 authorization in 2022 added roughly 30,000 more satellites, bringing the total to approximately 42,000 authorized spacecraft. This represents the largest coordinated satellite deployment in history and set precedents for how regulators evaluate constellation-scale approvals.
