# Starship Reusable LEO Payload Capacity

Starship is designed to deliver 100–150 tonnes to low Earth orbit in fully reusable configuration, far exceeding the payload capacity of any existing or historical launch system. The vehicle's 9-meter outer diameter provides a payload volume of approximately 1,000 cubic meters, the largest of any rocket ever built. The payload bay uses a large clamshell door design enabling deployment of satellites up to 8 meters in diameter.
