# Propellant Depot Orbital Storage Concept

The propellant depot concept envisions a specialized Starship variant serving as an orbital storage facility with enhanced thermal insulation, active cryogenic management, and standardized docking interfaces for tanker vehicles. Each tanker Starship can deliver 100–150 tonnes of propellant per flight. Boiloff—the gradual vaporization of cryogenic propellants due to heat leak—is a primary engineering concern, as even small daily losses compound across the weeks needed to accumulate propellant.
