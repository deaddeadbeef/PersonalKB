# Inspiration4 First All-Civilian Orbital Mission

Inspiration4, launched September 15, 2021 from LC-39A, was the first orbital spaceflight crewed entirely by private citizens with no professional astronauts aboard. The crew — Jared Isaacman (commander), Hayley Arceneaux (youngest American in orbit at 29), Sian Proctor (first Black woman to pilot an orbital spacecraft), and Chris Sembroski — orbited at approximately 575 km altitude, higher than both ISS (~420 km) and Hubble (~540 km). The mission splashed down September 18 after three days in orbit.
