# Merlin Vacuum Engine Performance

The Merlin Vacuum (MVac) variant uses a larger niobium alloy nozzle extension optimized for operation in the vacuum of space. It produces 981 kN of thrust with a specific impulse of 348 seconds in vacuum. The turbopump spins at approximately 36,000 RPM, driven by a gas generator burning a small fraction of the LOX/RP-1 propellant supply.
