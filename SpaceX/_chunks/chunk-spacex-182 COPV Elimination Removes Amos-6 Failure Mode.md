# COPV Elimination Removes Amos-6 Failure Mode

Falcon 9 relies on helium stored in composite overwrapped pressure vessels (COPVs) submerged in the LOX tank—a system associated with the September 2016 Amos-6 pad explosion caused by solid oxygen formation in COPV liner wrinkle voids. Autogenous pressurization eliminates this failure mode entirely. COPV removal also eliminates approximately dozens of high-pressure vessels per vehicle, reducing mass and integration labor for helium system leak checks and proof testing.
