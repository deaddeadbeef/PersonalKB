# Sabatier Reaction for Mars Propellant

The Sabatier reaction (CO2 + 4H2 → CH4 + 2H2O) is the baseline chemical process for producing methane propellant on Mars. It combines carbon dioxide — abundant in Mars's approximately 95% CO2 atmosphere — with hydrogen to produce methane and water. The water byproduct is then fed into electrolysis cells (2H2O → 2H2 + O2), splitting it into hydrogen for recycling back into the Sabatier reactor and oxygen for both propellant oxidizer and life support.
