# Transpiration Cooling Future TPS Concept

SpaceX has explored transpiration cooling as a future TPS augmentation, where a small amount of fluid is forced through a porous tile surface to create an evaporative cooling boundary layer. This could potentially enable even higher reentry velocities and dramatically extend thermal margins. However, it remains a research concept rather than near-term implementation, requiring porous tile materials and a fluid supply system that adds complexity.
