# Customer Acceptance of Flight-Proven Boosters

Customer perception of reused Falcon 9 boosters shifted dramatically between 2017 and 2022. Early customers demanded new boosters or price discounts for accepting reflown hardware. By approximately 2021-2022, flight heritage became a positive reliability indicator. The decisive milestone came with Crew-4 (April 2022), when NASA approved previously-flown boosters for crew rotation missions to the ISS — signaling full institutional acceptance that reused hardware met human-rated safety standards.
