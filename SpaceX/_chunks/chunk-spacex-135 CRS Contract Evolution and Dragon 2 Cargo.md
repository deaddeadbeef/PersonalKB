# CRS Contract Evolution and Dragon 2 Cargo

The CRS-1 contract, awarded in December 2008, was valued at approximately $1.6 billion for 12 missions (~$133 million per mission). The CRS-2 contract, awarded January 2016, transitioned to Dragon 2 cargo featuring autonomous docking (replacing Canadarm2 berthing), increased internal volume, upgraded solar arrays, and the ability to remain docked for up to 75 days. CRS-2 was shared with Northrop Grumman (Cygnus) and Sierra Nevada Corporation (Dream Chaser).
