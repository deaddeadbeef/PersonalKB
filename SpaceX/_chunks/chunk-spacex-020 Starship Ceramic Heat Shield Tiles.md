# Starship Ceramic Heat Shield Tiles

Starship uses an active heat shield system with approximately 18,000 ceramic hexagonal tiles bonded to the windward side of the vehicle for atmospheric re-entry protection. The total propellant capacity for the full Starship stack is approximately 4,600 tonnes of subcooled liquid oxygen and liquid methane. On-orbit propellant transfer between Starship vehicles is planned to enable deep-space missions including lunar landing and Mars transit.
