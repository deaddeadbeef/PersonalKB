# CRS-7 COPV Strut Failure

CRS-7 failed on June 28, 2015, at T+139 seconds when an industrial steel strut securing a helium composite overwrapped pressure vessel (COPV) failed at approximately 20% of its rated strength, causing structural breakup. The return to flight occurred December 21, 2015 (Orbcomm OG-2), after a six-month stand-down. The failure led to implementation of 100% acceptance testing of all critical struts.
