# El Segundo Warehouse and Early Team

SpaceX's first headquarters was a ~75,000 sq ft warehouse at 1310 East Grand Avenue in El Segundo, California — cramped but affordable and close to aerospace suppliers. The initial core team of approximately 12 engineers included Chris Thompson (structures), Hans Koenigsmann (avionics), and several recruits from TRW, Boeing, and Northrop Grumman's aerospace divisions. This lean startup culture set the template that scaled to Falcon 9, Dragon, and Starship.
