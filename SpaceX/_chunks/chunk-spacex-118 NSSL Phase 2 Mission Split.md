# NSSL Phase 2 Mission Split

NSSL Phase 2 Lane 1 contracts were awarded in August 2020, splitting missions approximately 60% to ULA with Vulcan Centaur and 40% to SpaceX with Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy. This validated Falcon 9 as a trusted national security launch platform. SpaceX's GPS III SV01 launch on December 23, 2018, had already marked its first GPS satellite delivery and a milestone in national security launch certification. SpaceX also conducted the classified USSF-44 Falcon Heavy mission in November 2022.
