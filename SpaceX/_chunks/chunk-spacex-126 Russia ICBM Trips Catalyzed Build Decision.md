# Russia ICBM Trips Catalyzed Build Decision

Musk made two trips to Russia in 2001–2002 to buy refurbished ICBMs from NPO Lavochkin and Kosmotras representatives, but sellers repeatedly raised prices and accounts describe dismissive treatment. On the flight home, Musk's spreadsheet analysis showed that rocket raw materials were approximately 3% of launch price, confirming massive room for disruption through vertical integration and in-house manufacturing.
