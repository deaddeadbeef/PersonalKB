# Third-Party Deployer Ecosystem

Third-party deployers serve as critical aggregators in the SpaceX rideshare ecosystem. D-Orbit's ION Satellite Carrier is a free-flying orbital transfer vehicle that precisely deploys multiple CubeSats after Falcon 9 separation. Exolaunch provides EXOpod mechanical deployers and CarboNIX separation systems for microsatellites. Momentus's Vigoride uses water-plasma propulsion to deliver smallsat payloads to custom orbits, extending mission flexibility beyond the shared deployment plane.
