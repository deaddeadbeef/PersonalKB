# Starship 304L Stainless Steel Airframe

SpaceX selected stainless steel 304L alloy for the Starship airframe due to its favorable strength-to-weight ratio at both cryogenic and re-entry temperatures. Unlike carbon composites or aluminum-lithium alloys, 304L actually gains strength at cryogenic temperatures and maintains structural integrity at re-entry heating levels. The material's low cost and ease of fabrication were additional deciding factors.
