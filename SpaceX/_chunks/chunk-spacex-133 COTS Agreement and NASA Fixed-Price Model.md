# COTS Agreement and NASA Fixed-Price Model

The COTS Space Act Agreement, signed in August 2006 between NASA and SpaceX, provided $396 million in milestone-based payments contingent on demonstrated progress toward orbital cargo delivery. This pioneered NASA's fixed-price commercial development model, co-funding Falcon 9 and Dragon development that SpaceX leveraged into market dominance — arguably the highest-return government technology investment of the 21st century.
