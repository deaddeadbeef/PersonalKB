# Launch Noise and Offshore Platforms

Noise is perhaps the most immediate barrier to point-to-point Starship transport — a launch produces acoustic energy measured in the hundreds of decibels at close range, far exceeding any commercial airport noise standard. Offshore launch and landing platforms mitigate but do not eliminate this problem, and they introduce their own logistical challenges: passenger transfer from shore to platform, weather sensitivity for maritime operations, and the capital cost of building floating spaceports. These practical obstacles remain largely unresolved.
