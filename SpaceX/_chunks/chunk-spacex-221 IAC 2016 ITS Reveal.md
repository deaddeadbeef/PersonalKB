# IAC 2016 ITS Reveal

At IAC 2016 in Guadalajara, Mexico, Elon Musk delivered the landmark presentation "Making Humans a Multiplanetary Species," revealing the Interplanetary Transport System for the first time. The ITS booster was designed with 42 Raptor engines producing a combined thrust of approximately 128 MN. The presentation included detailed animations of orbital refueling, Mars entry, and surface operations. Audience reaction was electric with standing ovations, but expert commentary divided between admiration for the engineering vision and skepticism about the economics and timeline.
