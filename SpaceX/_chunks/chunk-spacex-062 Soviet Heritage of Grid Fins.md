# Soviet Heritage of Grid Fins

Grid fin technology originated in Soviet missile design programs of the 1950s-1960s, developed for guided munitions and interceptors requiring compact, foldable aerodynamic control surfaces. SpaceX adapted this underutilized engineering concept for orbital booster recovery at a scale far beyond its original military application. The adaptation exemplifies SpaceX's approach of drawing on proven but underexploited technologies and applying them to novel aerospace problems.
