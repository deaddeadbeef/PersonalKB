# SpaceX LC-39A Pad Conversion

SpaceX invested over $100 million to convert LC-39A from Shuttle-era vertical integration to a horizontal integration architecture supporting Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, and eventually Starship. The Shuttle Fixed and Rotating Service Structures were demolished. A Horizontal Integration Facility (HIF) was built approximately 400 meters from the pad, where vehicles are assembled horizontally. A Transporter Erector (TE) — a wheeled structure — carries the assembled rocket horizontally to the pad and hydraulically raises it to vertical for propellant loading and launch.
