# Dragon 2 Capsule Reuse Capability

Dragon 2 capsules have demonstrated extensive reuse capability, with individual capsules flying up to 5 or more missions after heat shield and parachute refurbishment between flights. This reuse is enabled by the PICA-X (Phenolic Impregnated Carbon Ablator) heat shield, a SpaceX-developed variant of NASA's PICA material designed specifically to support capsule reflight.
