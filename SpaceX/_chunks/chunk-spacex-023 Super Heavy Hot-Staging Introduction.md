# Super Heavy Hot-Staging Introduction

Hot-staging was adopted for the Starship system starting with Integrated Flight Test 2 (IFT-2). In this approach, the Starship upper stage's engines ignite before stage separation while still attached to the Super Heavy booster. A vented interstage structure called the hot-staging ring redirects ship engine exhaust away from the booster during the staging event, adding approximately 3 seconds of overlap thrust.
