# Steel 50x Cheaper Than Carbon Fiber

Raw material cost for 304L stainless steel is approximately $2.50 per kilogram, compared to $130 or more per kilogram for aerospace-grade carbon fiber prepreg—roughly a 50x cost reduction in material alone. This cost difference makes SpaceX's iterative prototyping approach—building, testing to failure, and rapidly rebuilding—economically feasible in a way that would be prohibitively expensive with carbon fiber.
