# Falcon 1 Flight 4 First Private Orbit

Falcon 1 Flight 4 (September 28, 2008) became the first privately developed liquid-fueled rocket to reach Earth orbit, launching a dummy mass payload from Kwajalein Atoll. By mid-2008, SpaceX had nearly exhausted its funding and Musk's personal reserves, making Flight 4 effectively a last-chance attempt before the company would have folded. Its success validated the low-cost development model and directly enabled the NASA COTS award that funded Dragon and Falcon 9.
