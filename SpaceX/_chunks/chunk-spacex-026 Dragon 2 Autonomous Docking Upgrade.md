# Dragon 2 Autonomous Docking Upgrade

Dragon 2 upgraded from Dragon 1's berthing approach to fully autonomous docking via NASA's International Docking Adapter (IDA), eliminating the need for ISS Canadarm2 robotic arm capture. Dragon 2 was a fundamental redesign rather than an incremental upgrade, featuring autonomous docking capability, a modern avionics suite, and crew life support systems. The Dragon 2 supports both cargo and crew configurations with minimal hardware changes.
