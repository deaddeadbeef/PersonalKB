# Hot vs Cold Staging 10 Percent Payload Gain

Traditional cold staging involves sequential booster shutdown, physical separation, a brief coast phase, then upper stage ignition—during which gravity losses reduce payload performance. Hot staging ignites the Ship's Raptor engines while still mechanically connected to Super Heavy, with separation occurring milliseconds to seconds after engine confirmation. This eliminates coast-phase gravity loss entirely, yielding approximately 10% increased payload to orbit without hardware mass increases.
