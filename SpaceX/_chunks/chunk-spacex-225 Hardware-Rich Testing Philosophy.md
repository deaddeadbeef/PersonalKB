# Hardware-Rich Testing Philosophy

SpaceX's "hardware-rich" testing philosophy is its most operationally distinctive trait: the company builds and destroys test articles at rates that traditional aerospace considers reckless, on the principle that physical testing reveals failure modes faster and cheaper than simulation alone. This approach produced rapid iteration on Starship prototypes at Boca Chica, where serial numbers SN8 through SN15 each flew, crashed, and informed the next build in a cadence unprecedented in rocket development. The contrast with Boeing and Lockheed Martin's simulation-heavy, review-board-intensive approach is stark.
