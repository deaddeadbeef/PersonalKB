# Starship Prototype Landing Campaign SN8 to SN15

Starship prototypes SN8 through SN14 (2020–2021) experienced a series of landing failures during high-altitude test flights. SN8 (December 9, 2020) reached ~12.5 km but crash-landed due to low header tank pressure causing insufficient thrust during the landing flip maneuver. SN10 (March 3, 2021) touched down successfully but toppled and exploded approximately 8 minutes later. SN15 successfully launched and landed on May 5, 2021, incorporating improvements to propulsion, avionics software, and structural design from all previous failures.
