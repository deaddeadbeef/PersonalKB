# Methane Fuel Selection Rationale

Methane (CH4) was chosen as Starship's fuel specifically because it can be synthesized from resources available on Mars, unlike RP-1 (kerosene) which requires complex hydrocarbon feedstocks. The Sabatier reaction combines Martian atmospheric CO2 with hydrogen derived from subsurface water ice to produce methane directly. This ISRU compatibility was a primary driver of the Raptor engine's methalox (methane/liquid oxygen) design, making the fuel choice an architecture-level decision rather than merely an engine-performance optimization.
