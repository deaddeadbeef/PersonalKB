# Hot Staging First Demonstrated on IFT-2

IFT-2 (November 18, 2023) successfully demonstrated hot-stage separation for the first time, where Ship Raptor engines ignite before physical separation from the booster. This technique improves payload performance by approximately 10%. The booster experienced a rapid unscheduled disassembly during boostback, and the Ship reached space before being lost near its planned engine relight.
