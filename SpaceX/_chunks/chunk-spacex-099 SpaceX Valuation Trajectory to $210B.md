# SpaceX Valuation Trajectory to $210B

SpaceX's valuation grew approximately 200x over twelve years: roughly $1 billion in 2012, $12 billion after the 2015 Google/Fidelity round, $74 billion in February 2021 driven by Starlink deployment, $137 billion in December 2023 secondary share transactions, and exceeding $210 billion in mid-2024 tender offers. This makes SpaceX the most valuable private company in the world. SpaceX has never gone public, with Musk indicating a potential Starlink IPO once revenue becomes more predictable.
