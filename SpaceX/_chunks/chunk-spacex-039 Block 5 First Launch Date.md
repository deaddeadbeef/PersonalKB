# Block 5 First Launch Date

The first Falcon 9 Block 5 launched on May 11, 2018, carrying the Bangabandhu Satellite-1 for Bangladesh from LC-39A at Kennedy Space Center. NASA required 7 successful flights of the Block 5 configuration before certifying it for crewed Commercial Crew missions. Block 5 boosters are identified by serial numbers starting with B1046, which was this first Block 5 core.
