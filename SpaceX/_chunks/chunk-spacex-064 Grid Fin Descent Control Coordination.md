# Grid Fin Descent Control Coordination

During Falcon 9 booster descent, the four grid fins coordinate with cold gas nitrogen thrusters and engine gimbal to maintain continuous attitude control across all flight regimes. At supersonic speeds (Mach 3+) during the entry burn, grid fins provide primary aerodynamic steering. Through subsonic deceleration they maintain authority while working alongside cold gas thrusters. Near touchdown, control authority hands off to engine gimbal for final precision landing. This layered approach ensures seamless control from hypersonic reentry through landing.
