# Aluminum to Titanium Grid Fin Evolution

Early Falcon 9 grid fins (2015-2018) were cast aluminum — effective for aerodynamic guidance but single-use, visibly charring during supersonic reentry and requiring replacement before each reflight. The Block 5 upgrade (May 2018) introduced titanium grid fins: single-piece castings weighing approximately 100 kg each with superior heat resistance. Titanium fins are designed for indefinite reuse with no between-flight replacement, and they feature larger surface area and improved geometry for greater control authority compared to the aluminum predecessors.
