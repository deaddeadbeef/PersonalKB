# First Stage Cost Dominance

The Falcon 9 first stage constitutes approximately 60-70% of total vehicle cost, estimated at $40-45 million out of SpaceX's published $67 million launch price. The stage comprises nine Merlin 1D engines, aluminum-lithium tankage, avionics, and landing hardware (legs, grid fins). This cost dominance makes first stage recovery the highest-leverage reusability target — recovering and reflying it transforms the economic model from expendable to an amortized capital asset.
