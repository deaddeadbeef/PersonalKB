# Starlink Depends on Reusability

The Starlink constellation requires continuous replenishment of 6,000+ satellites in low Earth orbit. At expendable launch pricing (~$67M per mission carrying approximately 60 satellites), launch cost alone would exceed the constellation's revenue potential, making the business case economically unviable. Reusable launch at $15-20M marginal cost per mission is the enabling condition that makes Starlink financially feasible — without booster reuse, the constellation could not be built or maintained.
