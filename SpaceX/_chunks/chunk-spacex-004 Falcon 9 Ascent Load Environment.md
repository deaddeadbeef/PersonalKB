# Falcon 9 Ascent Load Environment

Maximum axial quasi-static acceleration during Falcon 9 ascent is approximately 6g at main engine cutoff (MECO). The acoustic environment inside the payload fairing peaks at approximately 131 dB overall sound pressure level at liftoff. These parameters define the structural and vibration design envelope for payloads integrating onto Falcon 9.
