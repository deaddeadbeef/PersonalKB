# Inspiration4 Medical Research Program

Inspiration4 conducted medical experiments during its three-day mission including ultrasound imaging, cognitive testing, and blood and saliva sample collection for post-flight immunological and genomic analysis. Researchers at Weill Cornell Medicine and Baylor College of Medicine led the studies, generating civilian spaceflight health data that complemented decades of professional astronaut research and supported future commercial crew missions.
