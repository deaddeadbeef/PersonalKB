# Multi-Shell Multi-Band Architecture

Starlink employs multiple orbital shells for global coverage: 550 km at 53° inclination for populated areas, 570 km at 70° for higher latitudes (Canada, UK, Scandinavia), and 340 km at 97.6° near-polar for Arctic and Antarctic regions. Spectrum allocations across Ku-band, Ka-band, and V-band provide layered capacity and frequency diversity. The V-band (Gen2) offers significantly higher bandwidth but requires larger terminals and is more susceptible to rain fade.
