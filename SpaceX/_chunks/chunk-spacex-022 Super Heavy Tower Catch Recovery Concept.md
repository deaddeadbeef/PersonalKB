# Super Heavy Tower Catch Recovery Concept

Super Heavy is designed for recovery via mechanical catch arms (nicknamed "chopsticks") mounted on the Mechazilla launch tower, eliminating the mass penalty of landing legs entirely. Four stainless steel grid fins, each approximately 2 meters by 4 meters, provide aerodynamic steering during descent through the atmosphere. This approach enables potentially faster turnaround than any landing-leg-based recovery method.
