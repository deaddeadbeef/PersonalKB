# Raptor 2 Sea-Level Thrust Output

Raptor 2 produces approximately 230 tonnes-force (2,256 kN) of sea-level thrust, a significant increase from the approximately 185 tf of the original Raptor 1. Specific impulse is approximately 327 seconds at sea level and 350 seconds in the vacuum-optimized configuration. The engine uses dual preburners: an oxidizer-rich preburner driving the LOX turbopump and a fuel-rich preburner driving the methane turbopump.
