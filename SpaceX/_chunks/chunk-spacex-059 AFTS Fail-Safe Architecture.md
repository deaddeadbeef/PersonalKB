# AFTS Fail-Safe Architecture

AFTS is designed to be fail-safe: any system malfunction — including GPS receiver loss, IMU failure, or processor fault — defaults to vehicle termination rather than allowing unmonitored flight to continue. SpaceX became the first company to certify AFTS for orbital launch vehicles, coordinating with the FAA and the 45th Space Wing (now Space Launch Delta 45). Certification involved GPS receiver testing under launch vibration, IMU accuracy validation, software verification and validation, and failure mode analysis. The system first flew operationally on Falcon 9 in 2017.
