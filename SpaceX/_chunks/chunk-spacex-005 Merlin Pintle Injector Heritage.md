# Merlin Pintle Injector Heritage

The Merlin engine's pintle injector design traces directly to Tom Mueller's work at TRW before he co-founded SpaceX. The pintle injector configuration provides inherent combustion stability, eliminating the need for expensive acoustic baffles that complicate other engine designs. This design choice was foundational to Merlin's simplicity, robustness, and low production cost.
