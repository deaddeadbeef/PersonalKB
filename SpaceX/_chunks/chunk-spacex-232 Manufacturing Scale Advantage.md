# Manufacturing Scale Advantage

Starshield benefits enormously from SpaceX's Starlink manufacturing base — the ability to produce satellites at thousands-per-year rates gives Starshield a cost and schedule advantage over traditional defense satellite programs that produce single-digit units over multi-year timelines. This represents a structural shift in defense space procurement: moving from exquisite, small-number constellations built by traditional defense primes to proliferated, rapidly replaceable constellations leveraging commercial manufacturing scale.
