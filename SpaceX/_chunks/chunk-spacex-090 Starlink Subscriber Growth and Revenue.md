# Starlink Subscriber Growth and Revenue

Starlink subscriber growth surpassed 4 million by mid-2024 across more than 100 countries. Estimated 2024 revenue is approximately $6.6 billion, making Starlink SpaceX's largest revenue source and likely surpassing launch services revenue. SpaceX's total estimated 2024 revenue across all business lines is approximately $13–15 billion, with Starlink representing roughly half. This validates the mega-constellation economic thesis that many analysts initially doubted.
