# FFSC Zero Propellant Dump Loss

In a full-flow staged combustion cycle (FFSC), all propellant mass contributes to thrust generation and specific impulse because there is no overboard turbine exhaust dump. By contrast, gas generator cycles like Merlin dump approximately 2–3% of propellant overboard after driving the turbines, representing a direct specific impulse loss. This zero-dump characteristic is a fundamental thermodynamic advantage of the FFSC architecture.
