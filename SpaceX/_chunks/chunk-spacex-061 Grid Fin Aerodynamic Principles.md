# Grid Fin Aerodynamic Principles

Grid fins are lattice-structured aerodynamic control surfaces mounted near the top of the Falcon 9 first stage, providing roll, pitch, and yaw authority during descent and landing. Their open lattice (waffle) pattern maintains aerodynamic effectiveness through the transonic flight regime, where conventional planar fins suffer control reversal — a dangerous loss or inversion of control authority. During ascent, the four grid fins fold flat against the interstage to minimize aerodynamic drag and deploy after stage separation.
