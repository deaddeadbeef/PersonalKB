# SES-8 First GTO Mission

SES-8, launched December 3, 2013, from SLC-40 at Cape Canaveral, was SpaceX's first geostationary transfer orbit mission. The 3,170 kg SES-8 communications satellite was delivered to a supersynchronous transfer orbit for SES of Luxembourg. This mission demonstrated Falcon 9's capability to serve the high-value commercial communications satellite market, opening the door to GTO customers.
