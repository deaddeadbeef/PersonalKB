# Crew Dragon DM-2 First Crewed Launch

Demo Mission 2 (DM-2) launched on May 30, 2020, carrying NASA astronauts Doug Hurley and Bob Behnken to the International Space Station. This was the first crewed orbital launch from U.S. soil since STS-135 (the final Space Shuttle mission) in July 2011. DM-1 had previously launched uncrewed on March 2, 2019, successfully docking with the ISS autonomously and returning via splashdown in the Atlantic.
