# Raptor Methane Propellant for Mars ISRU

Raptor's LOX/CH4 (liquid oxygen/methane) propellant combination was selected specifically to enable in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) on Mars. Methane and liquid oxygen can theoretically be synthesized from the Martian CO2 atmosphere and subsurface water ice using the Sabatier process. This propellant choice is a cornerstone of SpaceX's Mars colonization architecture, allowing return-trip refueling without Earth-supplied propellant.
