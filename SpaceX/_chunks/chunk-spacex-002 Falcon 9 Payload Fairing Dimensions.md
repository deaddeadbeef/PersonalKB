# Falcon 9 Payload Fairing Dimensions

The Falcon 9 payload fairing has an outer diameter of 5.2 meters with an internal usable diameter of approximately 4.6 meters. The fairing stands 13.1 meters tall, providing a usable cylindrical payload volume of roughly 100 cubic meters. This is one of the largest commercially available payload volumes on any operational launch vehicle.
