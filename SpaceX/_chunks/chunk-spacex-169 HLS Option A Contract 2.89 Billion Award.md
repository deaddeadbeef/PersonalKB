# HLS Option A Contract 2.89 Billion Award

NASA awarded the HLS Option A contract to SpaceX on April 16, 2021, for $2.89 billion—significantly less than competing bids from Blue Origin's National Team (~$5.99B) and Dynetics (~$8.5B). The contract covered development, an uncrewed lunar landing demonstration, and one crewed landing mission (Artemis III) with two astronauts. NASA's source selection cited SpaceX's technical approach, lower price, and higher self-funding commitment as key discriminators.
