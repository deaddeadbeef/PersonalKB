# Hosted Payload Capability

Starshield's most significant capability beyond encrypted communications is hosted payloads — government-furnished sensors and instruments that ride on Starshield satellites. Potential applications include missile tracking (detecting and tracking ballistic and hypersonic missile launches from LEO), intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) imaging, and signals intelligence. This transforms the constellation from a communications network into a multi-mission sensing platform. Starshield integrates with the Space Development Agency's proliferated LEO architecture as part of the broader National Defense Space Architecture (NDSA).
