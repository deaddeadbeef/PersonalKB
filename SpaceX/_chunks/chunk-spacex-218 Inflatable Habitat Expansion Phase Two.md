# Inflatable Habitat Expansion Phase Two

Phase 2 of the Mars base transitions to pressurized structures constructed from components delivered as cargo or repurposed from landing equipment. Inflatable habitat modules, rigid connectors, and airlocks allow expansion beyond the Starship hulls, providing more ergonomic living and working spaces. Dedicated laboratory, medical, and agricultural modules become feasible at this stage, moving the base from a survival-oriented outpost toward a functional settlement with specialized capabilities.
