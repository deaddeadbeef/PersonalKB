# SpaceX Intense Work Culture

SpaceX's work culture features 60-to-80-hour weeks widely reported as standard, particularly during development sprints and launch campaigns. This has generated significant controversy, with former employees describing burnout and work-life balance issues, while defenders argue the pace is necessary for the company's ambitious timelines and that participation is voluntary. Employee turnover is notably higher than industry averages, but SpaceX recruits aggressively from top-tier universities and maintains a deep applicant pipeline, positioning the grueling culture as a filtering mechanism that retains the most mission-driven engineers.
