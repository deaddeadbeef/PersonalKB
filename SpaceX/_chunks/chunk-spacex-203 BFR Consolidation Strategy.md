# BFR Consolidation Strategy

The BFR (Big Falcon Rocket) redesign unveiled at IAC 2017 in Adelaide scaled the vehicle down from the ITS's 12-meter diameter to 9 meters, adopting a "one vehicle for everything" philosophy. This single vehicle family would serve Earth orbit, Moon, Mars, and point-to-point transport missions. The consolidation addressed the ITS funding critique by enabling revenue generation across multiple markets, improving economic viability. SpaceX renamed the vehicle to "Starship" (upper stage) and "Super Heavy" (booster) in 2018.
