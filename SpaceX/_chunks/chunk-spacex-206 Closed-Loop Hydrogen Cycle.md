# Closed-Loop Hydrogen Cycle

The ISRU propellant production system creates a partially closed hydrogen loop that dramatically reduces mass imports from Earth. Water produced by the Sabatier reaction is fed into electrolysis cells, which split it into hydrogen for recycling back into the Sabatier reactor and oxygen for propellant oxidizer. Only an initial hydrogen seed stock and water ice harvested from Martian subsurface deposits are needed as inputs. A full Starship propellant load consists of roughly 240 metric tons of liquid methane and 860 metric tons of liquid oxygen.
