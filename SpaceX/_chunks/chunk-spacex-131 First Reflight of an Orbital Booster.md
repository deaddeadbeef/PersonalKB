# First Reflight of an Orbital Booster

Falcon 9 booster B1021 became the first orbital-class booster to be reflown when it launched SES-10 to geostationary transfer orbit on March 30, 2017. The booster had previously flown on the CRS-8 mission. This milestone proved that refurbishment and reuse of orbital-class hardware was operationally and commercially viable, fundamentally changing launch industry economics.
