# GPS III SV01 First NSSL Mission

GPS III SV01 ("Vespucci"), launched December 23, 2018, from SLC-40, Cape Canaveral, was SpaceX's first National Security Space Launch mission. The mission delivered the first modernized GPS satellite to its medium Earth orbit. The booster was expended to maximize performance for the MEO mission profile, demonstrating SpaceX's willingness to forgo recovery when mission requirements demanded it.
