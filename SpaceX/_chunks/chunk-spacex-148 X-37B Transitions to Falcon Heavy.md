# X-37B Transitions to Falcon Heavy

USSF-52, launched December 28, 2023, placed the X-37B autonomous military spaceplane on Falcon Heavy — its first flight on a commercial vehicle, transitioning from ULA's Atlas V. SpaceX also supported SDA's Proliferated Warfighter Space Architecture with Tranche 0 satellites launched in April 2023 aboard a Falcon 9 rideshare, establishing the initial demonstrator layer for missile warning and data transport.
