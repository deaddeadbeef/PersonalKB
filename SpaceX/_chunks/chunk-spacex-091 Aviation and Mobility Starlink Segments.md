# Aviation and Mobility Starlink Segments

Aviation Starlink has been adopted by JSX and Hawaiian Airlines, with several major US carriers evaluating it for passenger Wi-Fi replacement. The enterprise and mobility segments (maritime, aviation, government) generate disproportionately high revenue relative to subscriber count. Starlink revenue is the primary internal funding mechanism for Starship development, creating a financial dependency between the two programs. A potential Starlink IPO could value the division at $100B+ and provide capital for SpaceX's Mars ambitions.
