# SpaceX As Its Own Largest Customer

Internal Starlink launches accounted for roughly 60 of SpaceX's 96 missions in 2023, making self-demand the largest single driver of launch cadence. SpaceX is simultaneously the world's largest launch provider and its own largest customer. This self-generated demand creates compounding advantages: high launch cadence reduces per-unit costs, which attracts more external customers, which funds more Starlink satellites, forming a reinforcing economic flywheel.
