# Fairing Cost and Recovery Economics

Each Falcon 9 payload fairing set (two halves) costs approximately $6 million to manufacture, representing roughly 10% of total vehicle cost. This makes the fairing the second most expensive expendable component after the first stage. At SpaceX's high launch cadence (50+ Starlink missions per year), fairing reuse saves an estimated $200M+ annually by recovering and reflying fairing halves rather than building new ones for each mission.
