# Starshield Military Satellite Program

Starshield, publicly announced by SpaceX in late 2022, represents the company's formal entry into the national security space market. It is architecturally separate from commercial Starlink, with distinct security requirements, dedicated ground infrastructure, and government-specific capabilities. The program was motivated by Starlink's ad hoc deployment in Ukraine beginning February 2022, which demonstrated transformative military communications utility but exposed limitations — lack of government-standard encryption, shared commercial infrastructure, and no formal military C2 integration.
