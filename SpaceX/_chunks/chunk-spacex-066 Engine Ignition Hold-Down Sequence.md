# Engine Ignition Hold-Down Sequence

At T-3 seconds in the Falcon 9 countdown, the nine first-stage Merlin 1D engines ignite in a staggered sequence over approximately 0.5 seconds. The vehicle is held by launch clamps while engines ramp to full thrust, allowing automated health verification of all nine engines before committing to flight. Clamps release at T-0 only after systems confirm nominal operation. Engine chill prior to ignition circulates liquid nitrogen and LOX through Merlin turbopump bearings and combustion chambers to prevent thermal shock.
