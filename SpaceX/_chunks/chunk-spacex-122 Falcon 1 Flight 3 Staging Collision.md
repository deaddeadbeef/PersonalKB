# Falcon 1 Flight 3 Staging Collision

Falcon 1 Flight 3 (August 2, 2008) suffered a stage separation collision caused by residual thrust from the upgraded Merlin 1C engine. The first stage re-contacted the second stage moments after separation, destroying both stages and all payloads — including NASA's NanoSail-D and two Department of Defense experimental satellites. The fix for Flight 4 involved a longer pneumatic pusher delay to allow Merlin 1C thrust tail-off before separation, implemented in just seven weeks.
