# Block 5 Titanium Grid Fins

Falcon 9 Block 5 introduced titanium grid fins to replace the previous aluminum fins, which required replacement after each flight due to re-entry heating damage. The Octaweb engine mounting structure was also changed from a welded design to a bolted configuration, simplifying engine removal and inspection between flights. Retractable landing legs replaced the earlier fixed-configuration crush-core legs, enabling faster post-landing processing.
