# Merlin Vacuum Restart Capability

The Falcon 9 second stage Merlin Vacuum (MVac) engine supports coast phases and multiple in-space restarts, enabling delivery to a wide range of orbits and trajectories. This restart capability allows complex mission profiles including precise low Earth orbit plane targeting, geostationary transfer orbit (GTO) insertion, direct geostationary orbit (GEO) delivery, and interplanetary injection. The T-38 minute countdown — shorter than most launch vehicles — is enabled by highly automated sequences and streamlined procedures.
