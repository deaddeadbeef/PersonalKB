# Starfactory Manufacturing Facility

Starbase facilities evolved from ad-hoc open-air fabrication tents to the Starfactory — a large enclosed manufacturing building designed for high-rate Starship and Super Heavy production. The Starfactory represents a transition from prototype-scale fabrication to industrial-scale manufacturing, supporting SpaceX's goal of producing Starship vehicles at the rate needed for Mars colonization and high-cadence orbital operations. SpaceX purchased most Boca Chica Village properties to expand the site footprint; remaining residents experience road closures during operations.
