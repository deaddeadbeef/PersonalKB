# Falcon 9 LEO Payload Capacity

Falcon 9 can deliver 22,800 kg to low Earth orbit (LEO) in fully expendable configuration. In reusable mode, the vehicle retains 8,300 kg of capacity to geostationary transfer orbit (GTO). These figures make Falcon 9 the lowest-cost-per-kilogram operational launch vehicle, supporting LEO, GTO, polar, sun-synchronous, and interplanetary trajectories.
