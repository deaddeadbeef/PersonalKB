# Early Barge Landing Attempts

SpaceX's first autonomous spaceport drone ship (ASDS) landing attempt on CRS-5 (January 2015) reached the barge but crashed due to hydraulic fluid depletion in the grid fin actuators. The second attempt on CRS-6 (April 2015) achieved touchdown but the booster tipped over post-landing. These near-misses validated that propulsive landing from orbital velocity was physically viable while revealing specific hardware limitations that needed resolution before operational recovery.
