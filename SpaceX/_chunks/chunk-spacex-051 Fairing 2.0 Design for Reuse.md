# Fairing 2.0 Design for Reuse

Fairing 2.0, introduced alongside the Block 5 upgrade, incorporated several reuse-enabling features: improved environmental sealing to protect against saltwater ingress, structural reinforcement for water-impact loads, and modular electronics for easier between-flight servicing. The refurbishment process for recovered fairings involves freshwater rinsing, structural joint inspection, pyrotechnic bolt replacement, electronics testing, and acoustic liner recertification before the halves are cleared for reflight.
