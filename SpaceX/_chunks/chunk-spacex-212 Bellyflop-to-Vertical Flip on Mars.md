# Bellyflop-to-Vertical Flip on Mars

Starship's Mars landing leverages its bellyflop orientation during atmospheric entry, presenting maximum cross-section for peak drag. After decelerating through the hypersonic and supersonic regimes, the vehicle executes its signature flip maneuver — rotating from horizontal belly-down to vertical orientation for propulsive final landing. This maneuver, proven in Earth-based Starship tests, must be re-validated for Mars conditions: 0.38g surface gravity (3.72 m/s²) and the thin CO2 atmosphere significantly reduce aerodynamic control surface authority.
