# Block 5 Reuse Design Targets

Falcon 9 Block 5 was explicitly designed for 10 or more flights between major inspections and a total operational life of 100+ flights per booster. It was engineered from the ground up for rapid and extensive reuse, incorporating lessons from dozens of prior booster recoveries and refurbishment cycles. Block 5 represented the final planned Falcon 9 upgrade, allowing SpaceX to shift engineering resources toward Starship development.
