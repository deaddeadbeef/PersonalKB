# SpaceX 60-70 Percent Launch Market Share

SpaceX has captured an estimated 60–70% of global commercial launch market share by mass delivered to orbit, an unprecedented concentration for a single launch provider. SpaceX conducted 96 orbital launches in 2023 and is on pace for approximately 140+ in 2024, compared to roughly 67 total launches by China (all providers combined) in 2023. The global commercial launch market generates approximately $10–15 billion annually as of 2024.
