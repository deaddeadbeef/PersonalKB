# Tom Mueller Foundational Propulsion Hire

Tom Mueller was SpaceX's critical early hire, joining from TRW as VP of Propulsion and becoming the architect of the Merlin engine family. Mueller had previously built and hot-fired a 13,000 lbf thrust LOX/kerosene engine as part of the Reaction Research Society amateur rocketry group. His expertise established propulsion as SpaceX's core competency from the very beginning.
