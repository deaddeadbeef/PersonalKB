# First Commercial EVA from Crew Dragon

Polaris Dawn conducted the first commercial EVA in history on flight day 3. Isaacman exited the Dragon hatch first, followed by Gillis, each performing structured mobility tests on the skywalker handrail mounted outside the hatch. Since Crew Dragon lacks an airlock, the full cabin was depressurized, meaning all four crew members were simultaneously exposed to vacuum conditions in pressurized suits — a unique operational approach distinct from ISS EVA procedures.
