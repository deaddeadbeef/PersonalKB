# FFSC Dual Preburner Temperature Management

In Raptor's FFSC cycle, the oxidizer-rich preburner operates at a relatively mild temperature of approximately 700K because excess oxygen keeps combustion temperatures low, reducing turbine blade erosion. The fuel-rich preburner similarly operates at manageable temperatures of approximately 800K, with methane-rich exhaust that is far less corrosive to turbine components than oxygen-rich gas. This dual-preburner approach distributes thermal stress across two moderate-temperature systems rather than one extreme-temperature system.
