# Starshield Enhanced Encryption

Starshield addresses commercial Starlink's defense limitations through enhanced encryption meeting or exceeding Department of Defense standards for classified data handling. The secure ground segment separates military traffic from commercial Starlink entirely, with hardened ground stations and dedicated network operations centers under government oversight. This addresses the fundamental vulnerability exposed in Ukraine — that a commercial communications system, regardless of how useful, lacks the security architecture required for sustained formal defense operations.
