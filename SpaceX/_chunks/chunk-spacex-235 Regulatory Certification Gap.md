# Regulatory Certification Gap

Safety certification for point-to-point Starship transport presents a regulatory challenge without precedent. Commercial aviation achieves roughly one fatal accident per ten million flights — a reliability level no rocket has approached by orders of magnitude. The FAA has no framework for certifying a rocket as a commercial passenger transport vehicle, and developing one requires a safety record that cannot be established without extensive operational history — a chicken-and-egg problem. Additional hurdles include international airspace coordination and sovereign overflight rights across multiple nations.
