# Google Fidelity Billion Dollar Investment

Google and Fidelity invested $1 billion in January 2015 for approximately 8.33% of SpaceX, valuing the company at roughly $12 billion. This was a strategic inflection point that provided validation from tier-one institutional investors and capital specifically earmarked for Starlink satellite constellation development. The investment marked the transition from SpaceX as primarily a launch company to one with broadband constellation ambitions.
