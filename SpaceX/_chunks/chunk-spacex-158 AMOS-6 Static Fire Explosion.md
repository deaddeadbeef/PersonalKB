# AMOS-6 Static Fire Explosion

AMOS-6 was destroyed on September 1, 2016, during a pre-flight static fire when solid oxygen (SOX) formed in buckled COPV liner overwrap layers and ignited under pressure. The return to flight occurred January 14, 2017 (Iridium-1), after a 4.5-month stand-down. The failure drove a comprehensive COPV redesign and revised helium loading procedures to prevent oxygen solidification in future vehicles.
