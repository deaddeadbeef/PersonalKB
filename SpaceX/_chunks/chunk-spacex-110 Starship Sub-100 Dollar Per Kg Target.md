# Starship Sub-100 Dollar Per Kg Target

Starship targets sub-$100/kg to LEO through full rapid reusability of both stages, which would represent a further 97% reduction from Falcon 9 costs. The target launch cost of $2–10 million per flight with a 100–150 metric ton LEO capacity yields this figure. At this cost, space launch economics approach those of premium intercontinental air freight, potentially enabling entirely new categories of space utilization including orbital manufacturing and affordable habitats.
