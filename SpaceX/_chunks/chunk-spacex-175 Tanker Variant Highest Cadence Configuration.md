# Tanker Variant Highest Cadence Configuration

The Tanker variant eliminates the standard payload bay and maximizes internal propellant volume, purpose-built for orbital refueling missions where it delivers liquid methane and liquid oxygen to depot or mission vehicles. It is expected to be the highest-cadence Starship configuration, with potentially dozens of flights required to support a single beyond-LEO mission.
