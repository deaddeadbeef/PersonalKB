# Marginal Reflight Cost Advantage

The marginal cost of reflying a Falcon 9 with a reused booster is estimated at $15-20 million per mission, covering refurbishment, consumables, propellant, a new second stage, fairing, and range fees. Against SpaceX's published launch price of $67 million, this creates approximately $47-52 million in gross margin per commercial flight. Internal Starlink launches benefit further since no commercial margin is needed, with marginal cost potentially as low as $15-20M per mission.
