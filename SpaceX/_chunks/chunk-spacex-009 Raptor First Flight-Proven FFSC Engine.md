# Raptor First Flight-Proven FFSC Engine

Raptor is the first flight-proven full-flow staged combustion cycle (FFSC) engine in history. Its chamber pressure of approximately 300 bar (4,400 psi) exceeds that of any previously flown rocket engine, including the RS-25 Space Shuttle Main Engine at approximately 206 bar. Both propellants enter the combustion chamber fully gasified, improving mixing and combustion efficiency.
