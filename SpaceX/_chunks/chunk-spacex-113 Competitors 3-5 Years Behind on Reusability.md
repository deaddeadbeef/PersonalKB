# Competitors 3-5 Years Behind on Reusability

No current competitor can match SpaceX's combination of reusable economics, flight heritage, and launch cadence. The closest challengers are estimated at 3–5 years behind on reusability. Competitors must match not just current SpaceX pricing but its ongoing trajectory of cost reduction. The competitive response to SpaceX has nonetheless catalyzed global investment in reusable launch technology, with nearly every major space-faring nation pursuing some form of booster reuse.
