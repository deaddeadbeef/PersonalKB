# Carbon Fiber to Stainless Steel Switch 2018

SpaceX originally designed Starship (BFR) using carbon fiber composite construction with prototype tooling in development, before Musk directed the switch to 304L stainless steel in approximately November–December 2018. The counterintuitive rationale: austenitic stainless steel's yield and ultimate tensile strength actually increase at cryogenic temperatures (yield strength approximately doubles), the opposite behavior of aluminum and carbon fiber. This means propellant tanks are strongest when fully loaded.
