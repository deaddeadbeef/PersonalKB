# Starship IFT-1 and IFT-2 Orbital Attempts

Starship IFT-1 (April 20, 2023) had its flight termination system activated approximately 4 minutes after liftoff when multiple Raptor engines shut down and the vehicle began tumbling, triggering autonomous destruction. IFT-2 (November 2023) achieved stage separation — a significant advance — but lost both the booster and ship to separate anomalies. SpaceX's iterative "test, fail, fix, fly" philosophy compressed years of traditional development into months of rapid public testing with zero crew casualties across all crewed missions.
