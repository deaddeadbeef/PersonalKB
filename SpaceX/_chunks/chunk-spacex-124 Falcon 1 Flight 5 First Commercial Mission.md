# Falcon 1 Flight 5 First Commercial Mission

Falcon 1 Flight 5 (July 14, 2009) successfully delivered RazakSAT, a Malaysian remote-sensing satellite, to a near-equatorial orbit, marking SpaceX's first paying commercial orbital mission. The Kwajalein Atoll launch site was selected for its equatorial location and US military presence, providing both performance advantages and regulatory simplicity for early SpaceX launches.
