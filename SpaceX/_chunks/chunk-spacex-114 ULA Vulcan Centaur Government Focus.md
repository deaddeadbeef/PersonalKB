# ULA Vulcan Centaur Government Focus

ULA's Vulcan Centaur is strategically positioned for government and national security missions rather than competing with SpaceX on commercial price, effectively conceding the commercial market. Vulcan Centaur first launched successfully on January 8, 2024 (Cert-1 mission carrying Peregrine lunar lander), using Blue Origin BE-4 engines after years of engine delivery delays. The vehicle is expendable and cannot match SpaceX's reusable economics.
