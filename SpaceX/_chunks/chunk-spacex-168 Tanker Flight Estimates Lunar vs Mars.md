# Tanker Flight Estimates Lunar vs Mars

SpaceX estimates approximately 5–10 tanker flights are required to fully fuel a Starship for a lunar mission, while a Mars transit mission could require 15 or more tanker flights depending on payload mass and trajectory. Mars missions need substantially more propellant due to higher delta-v for Earth departure, longer transit, and the need for propulsive Mars orbit insertion or direct entry. The NASA HLS propellant transfer demonstration is a contractual gate that must be cleared before the crewed Artemis III lunar landing.
