# Starlink Satellite Generation Progression

Starlink satellites have evolved through five hardware generations since 2019. The v0.9 batch of 60 prototypes launched May 23, 2019, validating flat-pack deployment. The v1.0 (~260 kg) entered production in November 2019 with 60-per-launch flat-panel stacking. The v1.5 added laser inter-satellite links in September 2021. The v2 Mini (~800 kg) launched February 2023 with roughly 4x throughput versus v1.5, and the full-size v2 (~1,250 kg) requires Starship for deployment.
