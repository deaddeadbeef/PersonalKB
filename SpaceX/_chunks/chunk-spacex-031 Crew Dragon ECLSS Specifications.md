# Crew Dragon ECLSS Specifications

The Crew Dragon Environmental Control and Life Support System (ECLSS) maintains cabin pressure at 14.7 psia with a standard nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere. The system supports up to 7 crew members for up to 210 hours of free flight. NASA's CCtCap certification requirements mandated a loss-of-crew probability no greater than 1 in 270 over the mission lifecycle.
