# Beal Aerospace Site Acquisition

SpaceX purchased its McGregor, Texas test facility from Beal Aerospace assets in 2003. Beal Aerospace had invested approximately $200 million in rocket development before ceasing operations in 2000, leaving behind substantial test infrastructure. The acquisition provided SpaceX with existing engine test stands and support facilities at a fraction of new-build cost during the company's capital-constrained early years. The facility covers 4,000+ acres approximately 20 miles west of Waco, providing isolation and buffer zones for large-scale rocket engine testing.
