# Dragon 1 Down-Mass Capability

Dragon 1 carried approximately 3,310 kg of upmass (pressurized trunk combined) and could return approximately 2,500 kg to Earth via ocean splashdown. This made it the only ISS cargo vehicle capable of returning significant payloads intact for post-flight analysis — a unique capability unavailable from Russian Progress, Japanese HTV, or ESA ATV cargo vehicles. SpX-CRS-1, the first operational resupply mission, launched October 7, 2012.
