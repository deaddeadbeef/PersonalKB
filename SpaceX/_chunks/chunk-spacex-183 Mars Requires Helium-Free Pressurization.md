# Mars Requires Helium-Free Pressurization

Mars compatibility is a primary design driver for autogenous pressurization: there is no known accessible helium source on Mars, so any vehicle intended for Mars surface refueling and return must use pressurants that can be sourced or manufactured locally. Helium is also expensive ($30–100+ per kg), supply-constrained as a byproduct of natural gas extraction, and subject to periodic global shortages that could limit launch cadence for dependent vehicles.
