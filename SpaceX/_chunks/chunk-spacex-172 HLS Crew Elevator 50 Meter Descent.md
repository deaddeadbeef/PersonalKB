# HLS Crew Elevator 50 Meter Descent

The Starship HLS includes a crew elevator system to transport astronauts from the pressurized cabin to the lunar surface, approximately 50 meters below the crew compartment. This addresses the unprecedented height of the Starship HLS—the tallest lunar lander ever designed—requiring a mechanical system to safely lower suited astronauts. An uncrewed demonstration landing on the lunar surface is required before NASA will authorize the crewed Artemis III mission.
