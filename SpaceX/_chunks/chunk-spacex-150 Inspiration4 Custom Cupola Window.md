# Inspiration4 Custom Cupola Window

For Inspiration4, SpaceX designed and installed a custom cupola dome window in the Crew Dragon's forward nose cone, replacing the docking mechanism not needed for the free-flight mission. The cupola was a multi-layer fused silica assembly providing the largest continuous window flown in space, offering the crew panoramic Earth views during their three-day orbital mission.
