# Dragon 1 Commercial Resupply Record

Dragon 1 completed 20 successful Commercial Resupply Services (CRS) missions to the International Space Station between 2012 and 2020, establishing commercial cargo delivery as routine. The first Dragon 1 mission (COTS Demo Flight 2, May 2012) made Dragon the first commercial spacecraft to berth with the ISS. Dragon 1 used a berthing approach requiring the ISS Canadarm2 robotic arm to grapple and attach the capsule to a Common Berthing Mechanism (CBM) port.
