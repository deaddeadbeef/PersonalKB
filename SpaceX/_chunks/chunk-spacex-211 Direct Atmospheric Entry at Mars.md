# Direct Atmospheric Entry at Mars

Starship bypasses traditional Mars Orbit Insertion (MOI) entirely, performing direct atmospheric entry from the interplanetary trajectory. The MOI-equivalent delta-v of approximately 0.6 km/s is shed through aerodynamic braking rather than propulsive burn, saving hundreds of tons of propellant. Mars entry velocity is approximately 5.5 km/s, lower than Earth reentry but complicated by Mars's thin atmosphere — surface pressure is roughly 0.6% of Earth's — which provides less deceleration per unit altitude and extends the heating period.
