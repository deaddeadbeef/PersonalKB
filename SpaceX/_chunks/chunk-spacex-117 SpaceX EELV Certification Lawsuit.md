# SpaceX EELV Certification Lawsuit

SpaceX filed a lawsuit against the US Air Force in April 2014 challenging the block-buy sole-source contract awarded to ULA, which effectively held a monopoly on national security launches. The suit was settled after SpaceX received access to compete for EELV missions, achieving certification in 2015. This legal challenge fundamentally changed the competitive landscape for defense launch procurement and opened the door for SpaceX's growing national security role.
