# Falcon Heavy Payload Mass Capabilities

Falcon Heavy can deliver 63,800 kg to low Earth orbit (LEO), 26,700 kg to geostationary transfer orbit (GTO), and 16,800 kg to Mars transfer orbit in fully expendable configuration. These capabilities made it the most capable operational launch vehicle by payload mass to orbit at the time of its debut, at a fraction of the cost of competing heavy-lift vehicles.
