# BulgariaSat-1 Breaks Reuse Acceptance Barrier

BulgariaSat-1, launched June 23, 2017, was the first commercial mission to fly on a previously-flown Falcon 9 first stage. The booster (B1029.2) had previously launched Iridium-1, making BulgariaSat the first commercial operator to accept a flight-proven booster. This broke the reusability acceptance barrier for commercial customers, validating that premium operators would trust reflown hardware.
