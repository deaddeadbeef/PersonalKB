# USSF-44 First Falcon Heavy Military Mission

USSF-44, launched November 1, 2022, from LC-39A, was the first Falcon Heavy national security mission. The flight deployed payloads directly to geostationary Earth orbit for the US Space Force. Both side boosters were recovered at LZ-1 and LZ-2, while the center core was expended for the direct GEO insertion profile that required maximum performance from the vehicle.
