# 134 Launches in 2024 and Mechazilla Catch

Falcon 9 achieved 134 launches in calendar year 2024, setting the record for most orbital launches by a single vehicle type in one year and accounting for the majority of global orbital launches. The same year, the Starship Super Heavy booster was caught by the Mechazilla chopstick arms on the launch tower during IFT-5 (October 13, 2024) — the first time a launch vehicle first stage was caught by tower-mounted mechanical arms.
