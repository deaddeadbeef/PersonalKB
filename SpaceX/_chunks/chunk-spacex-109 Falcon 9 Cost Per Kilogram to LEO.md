# Falcon 9 Cost Per Kilogram to LEO

Falcon 9 reusable has reduced launch costs to approximately $2,700/kg to LEO, representing a 75–85% reduction from pre-SpaceX expendable launch vehicle costs. The marginal launch cost is estimated at $15–20 million for a reused booster with a new upper stage, yielding that figure for a full 22,800 kg LEO payload. For comparison, the Space Shuttle's per-kilogram cost was approximately $54,500/kg when total program expenditures (~$209 billion over 135 flights) are fully amortized.
