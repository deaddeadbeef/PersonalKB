# Falcon Heavy Demo Flight Dual Booster Landing

The Falcon Heavy demonstration flight launched on February 6, 2018 from LC-39A at Kennedy Space Center—the same pad used for Apollo 11. Both side boosters successfully performed return-to-launch-site (RTLS) landings at LZ-1 and LZ-2, touching down nearly simultaneously in an iconic moment. The center core missed the drone ship "Of Course I Still Love You" by approximately 100 meters due to insufficient TEA-TEB igniter fluid for the re-entry burn.
