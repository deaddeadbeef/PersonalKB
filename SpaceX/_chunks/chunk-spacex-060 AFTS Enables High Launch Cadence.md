# AFTS Enables High Launch Cadence

AFTS's removal of ground radar and range safety officer requirements enabled multiple launches per week from Cape Canaveral, up from roughly one per month historically. Following SpaceX's successful certification, the U.S. Space Force mandated AFTS as the standard for all Eastern Range orbital launches, with ULA and other providers subsequently adopting the technology. Without AFTS, SpaceX's 100+ annual launches from shared ranges would be physically impossible given legacy ground infrastructure and personnel constraints.
