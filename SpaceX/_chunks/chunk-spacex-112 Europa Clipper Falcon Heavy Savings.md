# Europa Clipper Falcon Heavy Savings

NASA's Europa Clipper mission, originally designed for SLS, was transferred to Falcon Heavy at a cost of $178 million—roughly one-tenth the estimated SLS launch cost. This enabled the mission to proceed without SLS schedule dependency, demonstrating how SpaceX's cost reduction directly benefits science missions. The cost revolution has democratized space access, enabling universities, startups, and small nations to deploy orbital assets for the first time through affordable rideshare options.
