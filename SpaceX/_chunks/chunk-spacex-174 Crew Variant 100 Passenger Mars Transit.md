# Crew Variant 100 Passenger Mars Transit

The Crew variant is designed to carry 100 or more passengers on interplanetary transits to Mars, featuring pressurized cabin volume with windows, life support systems, radiation shielding, and communal living areas for multi-month missions. The life support system must sustain 100+ passengers for approximately six months, requiring closed-loop water recycling, CO₂ scrubbing, and food storage systems far exceeding current spacecraft capabilities.
