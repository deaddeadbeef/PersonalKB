# Hohmann Transfer Duration and Windows

The baseline Starship Mars trajectory follows a near-Hohmann transfer orbit, requiring approximately 6 to 9 months of cruise time depending on specific launch window geometry and available propellant margin. These windows open roughly every 26 months due to the synodic period of Earth and Mars orbital alignment. Faster trajectories are possible but require significantly more propellant, directly reducing payload mass to the surface — a trade that may favor cargo missions optimizing for mass over crew missions optimizing for reduced radiation exposure.
