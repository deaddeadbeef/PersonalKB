# FFSC Full Gasification Eliminates Injector Erosion

Full gasification of both propellants before injection into the main combustion chamber eliminates injector-face erosion caused by impinging liquid streams, a common failure mode in high-pressure rocket engines. The RS-25, by comparison, uses oxygen-rich staged combustion but only gasifies the hydrogen side, making it a staged combustion design (not full-flow) that achieves approximately 206 bar chamber pressure. Raptor's full-flow approach enables approximately 300 bar.
