# Starfactory One Starship Per Week Target

Starfactory, the expanded manufacturing facility at Boca Chica, Texas, is designed to achieve a production target of one complete Starship upper stage per week, with parallel Super Heavy booster production lines. Raptor engine production targets have been publicly stated as one engine per day, a cadence necessary to support the envisioned flight rate of multiple Starship launches per week.
