# First Fairing Reflight Milestone

The first operational reuse of a payload fairing on an orbital launch vehicle occurred in November 2019, when a previously-flown fairing half was reflown on a Starlink mission. By 2022, reused fairings became standard on Starlink flights, with most missions carrying at least one previously-flown fairing half. Each reuse saves an estimated $4-5M per mission, and at 50+ Starlink missions per year this translates to over $200M in annual savings.
