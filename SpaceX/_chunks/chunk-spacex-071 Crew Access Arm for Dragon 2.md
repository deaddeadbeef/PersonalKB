# Crew Access Arm for Dragon 2

A Crew Access Arm (CAA) was added to the LC-39A launch tower to enable Dragon 2 crew boarding — a retractable enclosed walkway allowing astronauts to walk from the tower to the spacecraft at the top of the Falcon 9 stack. The CAA was first used operationally during Demo-2 (May 2020), SpaceX's inaugural crewed mission to the International Space Station. Its installation was required for NASA Commercial Crew program certification of the launch site for human spaceflight.
