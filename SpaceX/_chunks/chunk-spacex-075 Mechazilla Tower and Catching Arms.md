# Mechazilla Tower and Catching Arms

Mechazilla is a 146-meter steel integration tower at Starbase equipped with massive mechanical arms nicknamed "chopsticks" designed to catch returning Super Heavy boosters and Starship vehicles mid-air. This approach eliminates the need for landing legs and their associated mass penalty on the vehicle. Mechazilla first successfully demonstrated catching a Super Heavy booster in October 2024 — a milestone in SpaceX's strategy of maximizing payload capacity by removing all non-essential mass from the flight vehicle.
