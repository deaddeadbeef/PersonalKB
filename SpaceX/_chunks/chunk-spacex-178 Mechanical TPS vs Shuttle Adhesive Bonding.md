# Mechanical TPS vs Shuttle Adhesive Bonding

Unlike the Space Shuttle's TPS, which used RTV adhesive bonding—a labor-intensive process requiring days of cure time per tile—Starship's mechanical attachment system uses metallic studs resistance-welded to the steel skin with tiles secured via clips or pins. This design accommodates differential thermal expansion between the ceramic tile and steel substrate, targeting maintenance timelines measured in hours rather than weeks for rapid field replacement.
