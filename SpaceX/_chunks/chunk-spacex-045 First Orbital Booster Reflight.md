# First Orbital Booster Reflight

Booster B1021 became the first orbital-class liquid-fueled rocket booster to be reflown when it launched SES-10 on March 30, 2017. B1021 had originally flown on the CRS-8 mission in April 2016, making the turnaround approximately 11 months. This milestone validated the fundamental business case for booster reuse — proving that an orbital-class first stage could survive reentry, be refurbished, and successfully fly a commercial payload a second time.
