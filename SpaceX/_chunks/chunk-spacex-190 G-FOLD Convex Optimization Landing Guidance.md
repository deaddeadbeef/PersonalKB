# G-FOLD Convex Optimization Landing Guidance

The G-FOLD (Guidance for Fuel-Optimal Large Diverts) algorithm, based on convex optimization principles, enables real-time computation of fuel-optimal landing trajectories for Falcon 9 booster recovery and is adapted for Starship landing guidance. It solves a convex optimization problem in real time to compute a trajectory from any point in the landing approach envelope to the target site, accounting for aerodynamic drag, thrust constraints, and exclusion zones.
