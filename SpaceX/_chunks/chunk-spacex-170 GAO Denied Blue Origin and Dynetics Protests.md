# GAO Denied Blue Origin and Dynetics Protests

Blue Origin and Dynetics filed formal protests with the Government Accountability Office after NASA selected only SpaceX for HLS, arguing the single-selection approach deviated from the original solicitation's intent to fund two providers. The GAO denied both protests in July 2021. The Option B sustainment contract, awarded in 2023 for approximately $1.15 billion, subsequently extended SpaceX's HLS role to additional Artemis missions beyond the initial demonstration.
