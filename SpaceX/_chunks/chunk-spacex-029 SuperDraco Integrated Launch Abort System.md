# SuperDraco Integrated Launch Abort System

Crew Dragon's integrated launch escape system uses 8 SuperDraco engines embedded in the capsule walls, capable of activating at any point from pad to orbit. Each SuperDraco produces approximately 73 kN (16,400 lbf) of thrust, giving the abort system a combined 584 kN to pull the capsule away from a failing rocket in under 8 seconds. The in-flight abort test on January 19, 2020 demonstrated capsule separation and crew survival during maximum aerodynamic pressure (Max-Q).
