# Musk Personal $100M Investment

Elon Musk invested approximately $100 million of his $180 million PayPal proceeds into SpaceX from 2002 to 2008, funding the company through three consecutive Falcon 1 failures that nearly bankrupted both SpaceX and Musk personally. Founders Fund led a $20 million investment round in 2008, providing critical capital just as Musk's personal funds were exhausted and Falcon 1 Flight 4 finally succeeded.
