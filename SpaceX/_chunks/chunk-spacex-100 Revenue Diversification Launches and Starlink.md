# Revenue Diversification Launches and Starlink

SpaceX's revenue has diversified from being launch-dependent to roughly 50/50 between launch services and Starlink, dramatically improving financial resilience. Starlink generates an estimated $6.6 billion of approximately $13–15 billion total 2024 revenue. Secondary share sales provide employee liquidity and investor returns without the regulatory and disclosure requirements of a public offering, enabling SpaceX to remain private while maintaining financial flexibility.
