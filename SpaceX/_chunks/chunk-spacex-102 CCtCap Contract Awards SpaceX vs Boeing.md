# CCtCap Contract Awards SpaceX vs Boeing

The critical CCtCap contracts awarded in September 2014 allocated $2.6 billion to SpaceX and $4.2 billion to Boeing for crew vehicle development and six operational missions each—the largest commercial space procurement to that date. SpaceX's contract was $1.6 billion less than Boeing's, yet SpaceX delivered operational crew transport four years earlier. Boeing's Starliner suffered repeated delays, with its crewed flight not occurring until June 2024.
