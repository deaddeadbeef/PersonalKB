# Direct-to-Cell LTE from Orbit

SpaceX's direct-to-cell technology enables cellular connectivity from Starlink v2 Mini satellites to unmodified smartphones by broadcasting standard LTE signals on T-Mobile's PCS mid-band spectrum (approximately 1900 MHz). The partnership was announced August 25, 2022. Any existing LTE phone connects without modifications—the satellite mimics an eNodeB with adjustments for the ~3.5 ms one-way propagation delay at 550 km and Doppler shift compensation.
