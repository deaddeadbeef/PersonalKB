# First Orbital Booster Landing

The first successful orbital-class booster landing occurred on December 21, 2015, when Falcon 9 B1019 returned to Landing Zone 1 at Cape Canaveral after deploying 11 Orbcomm OG-2 satellites. This breakthrough proved that propulsive vertical landing of an orbital-class first stage was operationally viable, setting the foundation for SpaceX's reusability program.
