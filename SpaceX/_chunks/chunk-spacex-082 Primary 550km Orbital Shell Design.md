# Primary 550km Orbital Shell Design

The primary Starlink shell sits at 550 km altitude with a 53° inclination, using 72 orbital planes with 22 satellites per plane (1,584 satellites in the first shell). SpaceX originally proposed a higher 1,150 km shell but amended its filing to 550 km specifically to address debris concerns raised by the FCC and other stakeholders. The 550 km altitude balances latency, population coverage, and built-in deorbit via atmospheric drag.
