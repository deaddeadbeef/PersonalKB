# Hot-Stage Ring Jettison After Separation

After IFT-2 demonstrated successful hot staging on November 18, 2023, subsequent flights (IFT-3 through IFT-5) continued to use the technique. The hot-stage ring was eventually designed to be jettisoned after separation to reduce booster return mass. During the hot staging sequence, the Ship's engines ignite and ramp to thrust while separation bolts hold the stack together; once engine performance is confirmed, the release is commanded and stages separate.
