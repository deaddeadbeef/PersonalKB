# China Emerging Launch Competitors

China's state and commercial launch industry represents the most credible long-term competitive threat to SpaceX, driven by government investment, manufacturing scale, and demonstrated rapid technical progress. State provider CASC and commercial companies like Galactic Energy and LandSpace are pursuing methane-fueled reusable vehicles. Galactic Energy's Ceres-1 achieved orbit in 2022, and its Pallas-1 medium-lift vehicle aims for methane reusability comparable to Falcon 9.
