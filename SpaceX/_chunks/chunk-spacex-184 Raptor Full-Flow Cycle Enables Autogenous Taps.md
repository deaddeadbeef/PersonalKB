# Raptor Full-Flow Cycle Enables Autogenous Taps

The Raptor engine's full-flow staged combustion cycle is particularly well-suited to autogenous pressurization because both propellants are fully gasified before entering the main combustion chamber, providing convenient tap points for pressurization gas. The system must precisely regulate gas temperature and flow rate to maintain stable tank pressure without overcooling (causing condensation and pressure collapse) or overheating (risking tank wall damage).
