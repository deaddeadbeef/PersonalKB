# SpaceX Vertical Integration Manufacturing

SpaceX manufactures approximately 80% of Falcon 9 and Merlin engine components in-house at its Hawthorne, California facility, compared to traditional aerospace primes that subcontract 60–80% of components. This vertical integration is one of three reinforcing mechanisms driving SpaceX's cost advantage, alongside first-stage reuse and high manufacturing cadence (one booster every 4–5 days). The approach gives SpaceX direct control over quality, schedule, and component costs.
