# Merlin Thrust Evolution from 1A to 1D Plus

The Merlin engine family evolved from 340 kN of thrust on the Merlin 1A to 845 kN on the Merlin 1D+ variant, a 2.5x thrust increase achieved while simultaneously reducing cost per engine. Merlin 1A powered the first three Falcon 1 flights (2006–2008) using an ablatively cooled chamber. The Merlin 1C then introduced regenerative cooling, increasing specific impulse to approximately 304 seconds at sea level.
