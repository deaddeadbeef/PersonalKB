# Mars Oasis Origin Concept

Mars Oasis was conceived in 2001 as a small greenhouse experiment to be landed on Mars, predating SpaceX's founding by one year. The concept aimed to inspire public support for space exploration rather than advance any specific technical capability. When Musk discovered that existing launch costs made even this modest mission prohibitively expensive, he pivoted to founding SpaceX in 2002 to reduce launch costs directly — making Mars Oasis the conceptual catalyst for the entire company.
