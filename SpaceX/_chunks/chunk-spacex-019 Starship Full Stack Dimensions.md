# Starship Full Stack Dimensions

The full Starship stack stands approximately 121 meters (397 feet) tall, making it the tallest rocket ever built. The vehicle diameter is 9 meters (approximately 30 feet), consistent across both the Super Heavy booster and Starship upper stage. Super Heavy is powered by 33 Raptor engines, while the Starship upper stage uses 6 Raptors (3 sea-level and 3 vacuum-optimized).
