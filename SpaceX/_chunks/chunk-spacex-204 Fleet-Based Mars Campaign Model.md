# Fleet-Based Mars Campaign Model

SpaceX's current Mars campaign model relies on fleet-based launches timed to the approximately 26-month synodic transfer windows when Earth and Mars reach favorable orbital alignment. Cargo Starships are sent first to pre-position supplies, habitats, and ISRU propellant-production equipment. Crewed vehicles follow on subsequent windows once surface infrastructure is validated. This logistics-chain approach — more reminiscent of Antarctic base resupply than Apollo-style exploration — reduces risk by ensuring critical systems are operational before humans arrive.
