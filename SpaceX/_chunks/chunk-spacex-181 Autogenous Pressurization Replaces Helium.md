# Autogenous Pressurization Replaces Helium

Starship's autogenous pressurization system replaces helium with heated propellant vapors—gaseous methane for the CH₄ tank and gaseous oxygen for the LOX tank—tapped from the Raptor engine's preburner cycle. A small fraction of propellant is diverted through a heat exchanger, vaporized, and routed to the top of the respective propellant tank to maintain ullage pressure. This eliminates helium dependency entirely, simplifies vehicle plumbing, and removes COPV failure modes.
