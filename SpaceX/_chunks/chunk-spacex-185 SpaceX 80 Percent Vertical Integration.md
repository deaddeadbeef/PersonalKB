# SpaceX 80 Percent Vertical Integration

SpaceX maintains over 80% vertical integration, manufacturing the majority of avionics, engines, structures, and ground support equipment internally—a stark contrast to traditional aerospace primes like Boeing or Lockheed Martin, which rely heavily on subcontractor supply chains involving thousands of suppliers across dozens of countries. This approach enables rapid design iteration, direct quality control, and cost discipline impossible in a disaggregated supply chain.
