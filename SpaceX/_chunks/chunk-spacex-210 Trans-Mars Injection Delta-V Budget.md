# Trans-Mars Injection Delta-V Budget

The delta-v budget for Trans-Mars Injection (TMI) from low Earth orbit is approximately 3.6 km/s. Faster trajectories that shorten the 6-to-9-month cruise time require proportionally more propellant, directly reducing the payload mass deliverable to the Martian surface. This creates a fundamental trade-off: cargo missions may favor slower, more efficient trajectories to maximize delivered mass, while crewed missions may accept payload penalties for shorter transits that reduce radiation exposure and consumables requirements.
