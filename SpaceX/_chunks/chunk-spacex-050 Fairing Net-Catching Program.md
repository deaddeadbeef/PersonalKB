# Fairing Net-Catching Program

SpaceX initially attempted mid-air fairing recovery using the vessel Mr. Steven (later renamed Ms. Tree), joined by Ms. Chief — high-speed offshore supply vessels each retrofitted with a large net on extending arms. After separation at ~110 km altitude, fairing halves deployed parafoils and guided themselves toward recovery vessels. Net-catching was abandoned circa late 2020 due to low operational reliability caused by wind variability, parafoil behavior, and the precision required for boat-fairing coordination. SpaceX transitioned to controlled water landings instead.
