# Falcon 9 Users Guide

## Summary
The Falcon 9 Users Guide is SpaceX's official payload planning document for customers integrating spacecraft onto the Falcon 9 launch vehicle. It details the vehicle's performance capabilities, payload fairing specifications, launch environments, and standard mission profiles. The guide serves as the primary technical interface document between SpaceX and payload providers.

## Key Claims
- Falcon 9 can deliver 22,800 kg to low Earth orbit (LEO) in fully expendable configuration
- Geostationary transfer orbit (GTO) capacity is 8,300 kg in reusable mode, higher in expendable
- The 5.2-meter diameter composite fairing provides one of the largest commercially available payload volumes
- Falcon 9 achieves the lowest cost per kilogram to orbit of any operational launch vehicle
- The vehicle supports a wide range of mission profiles including LEO, GTO, polar, sun-synchronous, and interplanetary trajectories

## Atomic Facts
1. Payload fairing outer diameter is 5.2 meters with an internal usable diameter of approximately 4.6 meters
2. Fairing height is 13.1 meters, providing a usable cylindrical payload volume of roughly 100 cubic meters
3. Maximum axial quasi-static acceleration during ascent is approximately 6g at main engine cutoff (MECO)
4. Acoustic environment inside the fairing peaks at approximately 131 dB overall sound pressure level at liftoff
5. Stage separation occurs at approximately T+2 minutes 33 seconds, at roughly 70 km altitude and 2.3 km/s velocity
6. Second engine start (SES-1) follows separation by approximately 8 seconds, with a burn duration varying by mission profile

## Significance
The Falcon 9 Users Guide is the foundational reference for understanding Falcon 9's capabilities and constraints. It defines the technical envelope that has made Falcon 9 the world's most frequently launched orbital rocket, with over 200 successful missions by 2024.
