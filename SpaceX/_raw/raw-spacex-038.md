## Summary

Inspiration4, the first all-civilian orbital spaceflight, launched September 15, 2021, from LC-39A and splashed down September 18. Funded by Jared Isaacman (Shift4 Payments founder), the crew represented charitable pillars: Hayley Arceneaux (hope, St. Jude physician assistant and cancer survivor), Sian Proctor (prosperity, geoscientist), and Chris Sembroski (generosity, Air Force veteran). The mission orbited at ~575 km — higher than ISS (~420 km) and Hubble (~540 km). The Dragon capsule featured a custom cupola dome window replacing the docking port. Over three days the crew conducted medical experiments and live events. The mission raised over $240 million for St. Jude, and Netflix's *Countdown: Inspiration4 Mission to Space* provided unprecedented public access to the flight.

## Key Claims

- Inspiration4 (September 15–18, 2021) was the first orbital spaceflight crewed entirely by private citizens with no professional astronauts aboard.
- The mission orbited at approximately 575 km altitude, the highest crewed Earth orbit since the Gemini program and higher than both ISS and Hubble orbital altitudes.
- Jared Isaacman funded the mission and selected crew members to represent charitable pillars: hope (Arceneaux), prosperity (Proctor), and generosity (Sembroski).
- The Crew Dragon was fitted with a custom cupola dome window in place of the docking mechanism, providing the largest continuous window flown in space.
- The mission raised over $240 million for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, including Isaacman's personal $100 million contribution.

## Atomic Facts

1. Launch: September 15, 2021, 8:02 PM EDT from LC-39A, Kennedy Space Center; splashdown September 18, 2021, in the Atlantic Ocean off the Florida coast.
2. Crew: Jared Isaacman (commander), Hayley Arceneaux (medical officer, youngest American in orbit at 29), Sian Proctor (pilot, first Black woman to pilot an orbital spacecraft), Chris Sembroski (mission specialist).
3. Orbital altitude of ~575 km exceeded ISS (~420 km) and Hubble (~540 km); the orbit was inclined at 51.6° to match ISS parameters for abort contingency planning.
4. The cupola dome window was a custom-fabricated multi-layer fused silica assembly designed by SpaceX, installed in the forward nose cone where the docking adapter normally resides.
5. Medical experiments conducted during the mission included ultrasound imaging, cognitive testing, blood and saliva sample collection for post-flight immunological and genomic analysis by researchers at Weill Cornell Medicine and Baylor College of Medicine.
6. Netflix's *Countdown: Inspiration4 Mission to Space* was released in five episodes across September 2021, providing real-time and behind-the-scenes coverage that served as both documentary and public engagement vehicle.

## Significance

Inspiration4 marked the inflection between government-monopolized and commercially accessible crewed spaceflight. Four civilians completing a multi-day orbital mission validated Dragon's autonomous capabilities and opened the door for Axiom and Polaris missions. The St. Jude fundraising established spaceflight as a philanthropy platform.
