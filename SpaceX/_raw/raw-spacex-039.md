## Summary

The Polaris Program is Jared Isaacman's privately funded multi-mission initiative with SpaceX, announced February 2022. Polaris Dawn launched September 10, 2024, from LC-39A with crew: Isaacman (commander), Scott Poteet (pilot), Sarah Gillis (mission specialist), and Anna Menon (medical officer). The mission reached ~1,400 km apogee — the highest crewed Earth orbit since Apollo — entering the inner Van Allen belt. The centerpiece was the first commercial EVA: Isaacman and Gillis performed excursions using SpaceX's new EVA suit while the full cabin was depressurized (Dragon lacks an airlock). The mission also tested Starlink laser inter-satellite comms with Dragon as a relay node. Polaris Dawn splashed down after ~5 days. Future missions include a second Dragon flight and an intended early crewed Starship mission.

## Key Claims

- Polaris Dawn (September 2024) achieved an apogee of approximately 1,400 km, the highest crewed Earth orbit since the Apollo 17 mission in 1972.
- The mission conducted the first commercial EVA in history, with Isaacman and Gillis performing extravehicular excursions from Crew Dragon using SpaceX's new EVA suit.
- SpaceX's EVA suit debuted on Polaris Dawn as a purpose-built extravehicular garment, distinct from the standard intravehicular pressure suit used on previous Crew Dragon missions.
- Crew Dragon's lack of an airlock required full cabin depressurization for the EVA, meaning all four crew members were exposed to vacuum conditions in pressurized suits simultaneously.
- The mission tested Starlink laser inter-satellite link communications using Dragon as a non-Starlink relay node, validating optical crosslink compatibility beyond the Starlink constellation.

## Atomic Facts

1. Polaris Dawn launched September 10, 2024, from LC-39A, KSC; crew: Jared Isaacman (commander), Scott Poteet (pilot), Sarah Gillis (mission specialist), Anna Menon (medical officer/mission specialist).
2. Apogee of ~1,400 km placed the crew in the inner Van Allen radiation belt; the high orbit was maintained for several orbits before lowering to ~700 km for the EVA phase.
3. The EVA was conducted on flight day 3; Isaacman exited the Dragon hatch first, followed by Gillis; each performed a structured series of mobility tests on the skywalker handrail mounted outside the hatch.
4. SpaceX EVA suit features: 3D-printed helmet with HUD visor, thermal micrometeorite garment layers, new joint bearings, and tethered umbilical for life support.
5. Starlink laser communications test transmitted data via optical inter-satellite links between Dragon and operational Starlink satellites, demonstrating cross-platform laser relay capability.
6. The Polaris Program's third mission is intended as one of the first crewed Starship flights; no firm launch date has been announced, with the schedule contingent on Starship development milestones.

## Significance

Polaris Dawn showed private missions can achieve exploration-class objectives previously exclusive to government agencies. The EVA suit positions SpaceX for Starship lunar/Mars operations. The Starlink laser test validates crewed spacecraft leveraging the constellation for connectivity. The Dragon-to-Starship progression charts a path toward commercial deep-space human operations.
