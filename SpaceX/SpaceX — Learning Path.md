## Pass 2 — Core Mechanics (~8 hours)

Re-read each page's ⚙️ Core Mechanics and 🏋️ Warm-Up sections. Understand *how* SpaceX technology works.

### Suggested order
Follow the same sequence as Pass 1. Spend extra time on:
- **Propulsive landing** — understand the entry-burn, landing-burn, hoverslam sequence
- **Raptor engine cycle** — trace fuel and oxidizer through the full-flow staged combustion cycle
- **Falcon 9 architecture** — understand stage separation, boostback, and reentry
- **Starship flight tests** — follow IFT progression and lessons learned
- **Reusability economics** — compare expendable vs reusable cost models

### Checkpoints
After this pass you should be able to:
- [ ] Describe the Falcon 9 flight profile from launch to booster landing
- [ ] Explain the full-flow staged combustion cycle advantage over gas-generator
- [ ] Trace the evolution from Falcon 1 to Starship
- [ ] Explain Starlink constellation design and business model
- [ ] Describe the Mars colonization architecture at a high level
