# Chunk NES 099 — Ricoh Custom Silicon

Ricoh manufactured both NES custom chips. The 2A03 CPU combined a MOS 6502 core (licensed from MOS Technology with BCD mode disabled to avoid patent royalties) with the APU's five audio channels on a single die — eliminating a separate sound chip and reducing board cost. The 2C02 PPU was a dedicated graphics processor handling tile-based background rendering, hardware scrolling, sprite management, and palette lookup entirely in silicon. This two-chip architecture (plus RAM) comprised the entire NES console electronics, achieving remarkable capability from minimal hardware complexity.
