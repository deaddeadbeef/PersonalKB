# Chunk NES 085 — The 10NES Lockout System

The NES included the CIC (Checking Integrated Circuit) lockout chip, known as 10NES, as a security system. Licensed cartridges contained a matching CIC chip that performed a handshake with the console's chip on power-up. Without this handshake, the NES would reset repeatedly (the blinking power light). This gave Nintendo control over third-party publishing through mandatory licensing and content approval. Tengen (Atari subsidiary) circumvented the chip using voltage spike techniques, leading to landmark legal battles. The lockout system was not present on the Japanese Famicom.
