# Chunk NES 059 — NES Regional Hardware Differences

The Famicom (Japan) used a 60-pin cartridge connector with hardwired controllers (including a microphone on controller 2), a front expansion port, and RF-only output. The NES (North America/Europe) redesigned with a 72-pin front-loading ZIF connector, detachable controllers, bottom expansion port (rarely used), and both RF and composite output. The front-loading design differentiated from top-loading consoles associated with the 1983 crash, but the ZIF connector was prone to reliability issues causing the infamous blinking screen problem. The NES also included the 10NES lockout chip for third-party licensing control.
