# Chunk NES 063 — NES vs Sega Master System

The Master System (1986) had more raw resources: 8 KB RAM (vs NES 2 KB), 16 KB VRAM (vs 2 KB), 64 colors from 256 (vs 54 from 64), and a faster Z80 CPU at 3.58 MHz (vs 6502 at 1.79 MHz). However, the NES's mapper system enabled far greater effective capability growth over the console's lifetime. The NES sprite-0 hit flag and flexible scroll registers enabled visual tricks harder on SMS. The Z80's 16-bit address calculations were easier to program, but the 6502's zero-page addressing provided fast variable access. Both supported 64 sprites with 8-per-scanline limits.
