# Chunk NES 087 — NES vs Commodore 64

The C64 (1982) used the 6510 CPU at 1.02 MHz (slower than NES's 1.79 MHz) with the VIC-II graphics chip and legendary SID audio chip. The SID's 3 channels with analog filters and ring modulation produced far superior audio to the NES APU. VIC-II had fewer but larger sprites (8 at 24x21 versus NES's 64 at 8x8). The NES PPU was better optimized for scrolling tile-based games while the C64 excelled at static-screen games and demos. From an emulation standpoint, the C64 is harder to emulate due to VIC-II badline behavior and SID's analog filter requiring DSP modeling.
