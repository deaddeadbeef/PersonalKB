# Chunk NES 057 — Famicom and NES Launch History

The Famicom launched July 15, 1983 in Japan for 14,800 yen. Initial units had a defective chip causing freezes; Nintendo recalled all units. The NES launched October 18, 1985 in US test markets (New York), bundled with R.O.B. to market as a toy and distance from the 1983 video game crash. Nationwide US launch followed in 1986. By 1988, the NES was in 33% of US households. The last official North American game was Warios Woods (1994). Production ended in 1995 with lifetime sales of approximately 61.9 million units worldwide across all regions.
