# Chunk NES 086 — NES vs Atari 7800

The Atari 7800 (1986) shared the 6502C CPU at 1.79 MHz with the NES but used the MARIA chip for display-list-based rendering rather than tile-based PPU. MARIA supported 100+ sprites of varied widths versus the NES's 64 fixed-size sprites, but its display-list architecture consumed massive CPU time while the NES PPU rendered autonomously. The 7800's greatest weakness was audio — it defaulted to the ancient Atari 2600 TIA chip with only 2 channels of primitive sound, unless the optional POKEY addon was present. The NES's integrated 5-channel APU was vastly superior for game audio.
