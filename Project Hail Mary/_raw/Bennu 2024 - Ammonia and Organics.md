# Bennu 2024 — Ammonia and Organics

## What This Source Is

Analysis of asteroid Bennu samples returned by NASA's OSIRIS-REx mission, published in *Nature* and related journals (2024). Reports the detection of ammonia-bearing minerals, amino acids, and complex organic molecules in pristine extraterrestrial material.

## Why It Matters to PHM

Rocky's biology uses ammonia as a solvent. A common objection to ammonia-based life is "where would ammonia come from in sufficient quantities?" Bennu answers this: ammonia and ammonia-bearing compounds are cosmically available, incorporated into primordial asteroid material, and delivered to planetary surfaces via impacts. This strengthens the case that ammonia-rich prebiotic chemistry is plausible in other star systems.

## Key Takeaways

- Bennu samples contain ammonium (NH₄⁺) salts incorporated into clay minerals, indicating aqueous processing with dissolved ammonia in the early solar system.
- Complex organic molecules including amino acids were detected, consistent with prebiotic chemistry occurring on the asteroid's parent body.
- The ammonia is not trace — it is a significant component of the mineral matrix, suggesting ammonia was abundant in the local chemical environment.
- These findings support models where ammonia-water mixtures were common in the outer solar system and delivered to inner planets by impacts.
