# Chris West — Hail Mary Ship Analysis

## What This Source Is

Fan-physicist deep-dive into the *Hail Mary*'s design as described in the novel. West works through the stated mass, thrust, fuel fraction, and mission profile to check whether the numbers are self-consistent. Part of a broader community of engineers and physicists stress-testing the novel's internal physics.

## Why It Matters to PHM

This is the most detailed independent analysis of the ship as an engineering system. It highlights where Weir's numbers hold up (drive concept, thrust-gravity regime) and where they strain (fuel budget for the full accel-decel-return profile). It also contextualizes the ship's extreme energy density requirements — the drive only works because Astrophage stores energy at levels far beyond chemical or even nuclear fuels.

## Key Takeaways

- The Hail Mary's drive depends on an energy density approaching matter-antimatter annihilation, far beyond any chemical or nuclear fuel.
- The stated ship mass and fuel load are tight for the full acceleration + deceleration + return mission profile. The mass ratio may not fully close.
- Thrust-gravity during powered flight is physically sound (equivalence principle), but the coast-phase gravity arrangement is underspecified in the novel.
- The ship's lab, beetles (EVA craft), and life-support systems are constrained by the mass budget — every kilogram matters when fuel is the dominant mass.
- Rocky's modifications to the ship (xenonite pressure hull, thermal isolation) are engineering plausible given the stated materials.
