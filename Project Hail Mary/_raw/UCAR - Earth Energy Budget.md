# UCAR — Earth Energy Budget

## What This Source Is

Educational overview from the University Corporation for Atmospheric Research (UCAR) explaining Earth's energy balance — how much solar energy arrives, how it's reflected and absorbed, and why small changes destabilize the climate.

## Why It Matters to PHM

The Astrophage crisis in the novel is fundamentally an energy-budget crisis. Understanding the real numbers (~1361 W/m² solar constant, ~340 W/m² global average after geometry, ~240 W/m² absorbed after albedo) is essential for evaluating whether Weir's "a few percent dimming collapses civilization" premise is credible. It is.

## Key Takeaways

- The solar constant at Earth's orbit is approximately 1361 W/m².
- Geometric spreading over the sphere means global-average insolation is about one quarter of that: ~340 W/m².
- Earth's average albedo (~0.30) reflects about 100 W/m², leaving ~240 W/m² absorbed.
- The current radiative imbalance from greenhouse gases is only ~1 W/m², yet it drives measurable warming. The system is extraordinarily sensitive.
- Ice-albedo feedback is a well-established positive feedback: more ice → higher reflectivity → less absorption → more cooling → more ice. A sustained solar dimming of several percent would risk triggering this runaway loop.
