# Science Accuracy Scorecard

A compact assessment of the major scientific claims in [[Project Hail Mary]], categorized by how well they hold up against real physics, biology, and engineering.

**Categories:**
- ✅ **Grounded** — real science, correctly applied
- 🔶 **Extrapolated** — based on real principles, extended beyond current knowledge
- ❌ **Impossible** — contradicts known physics/biology
- ❓ **Uncertain** — plausible but unverifiable, or the novel is ambiguous

## Summary

The novel's **strongest science** is in astronomy (spectroscopy, energy budgets, star selection) and communication theory (math-first contact, tonal language). Its **biggest departures** cluster around Astrophage thermodynamics — the energy storage and propulsion claims that make the plot possible but violate known physics by many orders of magnitude. The **middle ground** includes ammonia biochemistry, multi-year torpor, and mass-ratio questions — areas where real science provides a foundation but the novel extrapolates optimistically.

> [!tip] Weir's Approach
> Weir consistently builds from real science and then turns one dial to an extreme setting. The novel works because the *one* impossible thing (Astrophage energy density) is the load-bearing assumption, and everything else follows logically from it.
