## Astrophage and Extremophile Science

### Dadachova 2007
Dadachova, E. et al. "Ionizing Radiation Changes the Electronic Properties of Melanin and Enhances the Growth of Melanized Fungi." *PLoS ONE* 2(5), 2007. — Landmark paper showing melanized fungi harvest ionizing radiation for metabolic energy. Core inspiration for Astrophage's radiation-eating concept.

### Casadevall 2017
Casadevall, A. et al. "Melanized Fungi and Radiation." In *Melanins and Melanosomes*, 2017. — Review of how melanin acts as a radioprotectant and possible energy-harvesting pigment in fungi exposed to high-radiation environments.

### Shunk 2020
Shunk, G. K. et al. "Growth of *Cladosporium sphaerospermum* on the International Space Station." *bioRxiv*, 2020. — ISS experiment confirming that melanized fungi grow toward radiation, supporting the radiotrophic-organism concept behind Astrophage.

### Royal Institution Article
"The Science Behind Science Fiction: Project Hail Mary." Royal Institution blog. — Accessible overview of which PHM concepts map to real science and where Weir took liberties.

### NASA PHM Science
"The Science Behind Project Hail Mary." NASA outreach materials. — NASA's own breakdown of the real physics and biology underpinning the novel.

## Astronomy and Climate

### NASA Earth Energy Budget
NASA Earth Observatory, "Earth's Energy Budget" fact sheets. — Authoritative numbers: solar constant ≈ 1361 W/m², global-average insolation ≈ 340 W/m², absorbed ≈ 240 W/m².

### NOAA Incoming Sunlight
NOAA Climate.gov, "Incoming Sunlight" and Maunder Minimum resources. — Historical evidence that even modest solar-output drops (like the Maunder Minimum) correlate with significant cooling.

### OpenStax Spectroscopy
OpenStax *Astronomy*, Chapter on Spectroscopy. — Standard textbook treatment of how spectral absorption/emission lines identify elements and molecules—the real-world basis for the Petrova-line detection method.

### Britannica PHM Science
"The Science Behind Project Hail Mary." Encyclopaedia Britannica, Science & Tech. — Accessible summary contextualizing PHM's astronomy within real astrophysics.

### Tabby's Star Overview
Boyajian, T. et al. and associated popular-science coverage of KIC 8462852 ("Tabby's Star"). — The best real-world analog for anomalous stellar dimming; caused by dust/debris, not biology, but illustrates how dimming triggers investigation.

## Propulsion and Spaceflight

### Baez Relativistic Rocket
Baez, J. "The Relativistic Rocket." UC Riverside Physics FAQ. — Standard derivation of relativistic rocket equations, time dilation, and fuel requirements. Baseline for evaluating the Hail Mary's performance claims.

### Chris West Ship Analysis
West, C. "Analyzing the Hail Mary." Blog post / online analysis. — Fan-physicist deep-dive into the ship's stated mass, thrust, and fuel fractions; identifies where Weir's numbers hold up and where they don't. Extended analysis covers energy density requirements and mission profile feasibility. Raw note: [[Chris West - Hail Mary Ship Analysis]].

### Ashish Fuel Critique
Ashish (online). "Project Hail Mary Fuel Calculation Critique." — Highlights the mass-ratio problem: the ship may not carry enough Astrophage to both accelerate and decelerate at the stated performance.

### Morgan 1998
Morgan, D. L. "Neutrino Propulsion." Journal reference, 1998. — Theoretical discussion of using neutrino beams for thrust; real physics but absurdly low thrust-to-power ratio, contextualizing Astrophage's fictional IR/neutrino drive.

### NASA STASH Torpor
NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts, "Torpor Inducing Transfer Habitat for Human Stasis to Mars" (STASH). — NASA-funded study on therapeutic hypothermia for long-duration spaceflight; current state-of-the-art is days, not years.

### Atomic Rockets Artificial Gravity
Winchell Chung, "Atomic Rockets: Artificial Gravity." ProjectRho.com. — Encyclopedic reference on spin gravity, thrust gravity, and human-factors constraints for spacecraft design.

### Northeastern Accuracy Discussion
Northeastern University / phys.org coverage. "How Scientifically Accurate Is *Project Hail Mary*?" — Broad fact-check across biology, physics, and engineering claims in the novel.

## Xenobiology and Communication

### Frontiers Extremophiles 2019
Merino, N. et al. "Living at the Extremes: Extremophiles and the Limits of Life in a Planetary Context." *Frontiers in Microbiology*, 2019. — Comprehensive review of known extremophile diversity; grounds the plausibility of life in exotic conditions.

### MDPI Silicon 2020
Bains, W. et al. "Silicon as a Building Block for Life." *Life (MDPI)*, 2020. — Evaluates silicon's potential as a carbon substitute; concludes it is chemically inferior in most known solvent systems.

### CUNY Alternative Biochemistry
CUNY Academic Works, Astrobiology chapter on alternative biochemistry. — Textbook-level overview of ammonia, methane, and other candidate biosolvents beyond water.

### Linguistic Discovery Eridian
"The Language of Eridians." *Linguistic Discovery* or affiliated linguistics analysis. — Academic-style analysis of Rocky's tonal/chord-based language system and how it maps to real conlang and SETI communication principles.

### Cetacean Biosonar 2024
Madhusudhana, Sharma; Gavrilov, Alexander; Erbe, Christine. "Cetacean click detection" (review). arXiv:2402.04735, 2024. — Reviews automated detection methods for cetacean echolocation clicks with extensive background on biosonar physics and neural processing in odontocetes. Grounds the plausibility of Eridian acoustic-primary cognition. Raw note: [[Cetacean Biosonar - Neural Processing and Information Bandwidth]].

## New Raw-Material Sources (2024–2025)

### Frontiers 2022 ISS Cladosporium
Shunk, Kalber, Bodart, Casadevall et al. "Ionizing Radiation Selects for Growth of *Cladosporium sphaerospermum*." *Frontiers in Microbiology*, 2022. — ISS experiment showing melanized fungi grow ~21% faster under space radiation and provide modest radiation attenuation. Raw note: [[Frontiers 2022 - ISS Cladosporium Growth]].

### UCAR Energy Budget
UCAR Center for Science Education, "Earth's Energy Budget" overview, 2023. — Clear treatment of the solar constant (1361 W/m²), global-average insolation (340 W/m²), albedo, and ice-albedo feedback. Raw note: [[UCAR - Earth Energy Budget]].

### Bailer-Jones 2025
Bailer-Jones, Coryn A. L. "The Physics of Interstellar Travel." 2025. — Rigorous review of relativistic rocket equations, photon rocket performance, mass-ratio constraints, and the deceleration problem. Raw note: [[Bailer-Jones 2025 - Physics of Interstellar Travel]].

### Frontiers 2024 Synthetic Torpor
Cerri, Tinganelli, Squarcio et al. "Synthetic Torpor — A Realistic Prospect for Space Travel?" *Frontiers in Physiology*, 2024. — Current review of synthetic torpor research: rodent demonstrations, translational gap to humans, and radiation-interaction hypotheses. Raw note: [[Frontiers 2024 - Synthetic Torpor Review]].

### Bennu 2024 Ammonia and Organics
Lauretta, Connolly et al. OSIRIS-REx Bennu sample analysis, *Nature*, 2024. — Detection of ammonium salts, amino acids, and complex organics in pristine asteroid material, supporting cosmic availability of ammonia. Raw note: [[Bennu 2024 - Ammonia and Organics]].

### NSA Extraterrestrial Communication
Callimahos, Lambros D. "Communication with Extraterrestrial Intelligence." NSA, 1966 (declassified). — Treats alien-signal decoding as cryptanalysis; argues mathematics is the safest bootstrap language. Raw note: [[NSA - Communication with Extraterrestrial Intelligence]].

### Responsibility to Defend Earth 2024
Policy/ethics discussion on global coordination for existential threats, 2024. — Examines the governance gap between what existential crises demand and what current institutions can deliver. Raw note: [[Responsibility to Defend Earth 2024]].

## Taumoeba and Predatory Microbiology

### Bdellovibrio Sockett 2009
Sockett, R. E. "*Bdellovibrio bacteriovorus*: Predation, Evolution and Ecology." *Annual Review of Microbiology* 63, 2009. — Comprehensive review of *Bdellovibrio* predatory lifecycle, host-range, and ecological role. Primary academic anchor for the predatory-microbiology basis of Taumoeba. Raw note: [[Microbial Predator-Prey Ecology - Bdellovibrio and Analogues]].

### Bdellovibrio Ecology Jurkevitch 2006
Jurkevitch, E. (ed.). *Predatory Prokaryotes: Biology, Ecology and Evolution*. Springer, 2006. — Volume-length treatment of predatory bacteria ecology, taxonomy, and evolutionary origins. Covers *Bdellovibrio*, *Vampirovibrio*, *Micavibrio* and others. Raw note: [[Microbial Predator-Prey Ecology - Bdellovibrio and Analogues]].

### Bdellovibrio Coevolution Rotem 2015
Rotem, O. et al. "Predator-Prey Coevolution in *Bdellovibrio bacteriovorus*." *ISME Journal* 9, 2015. — Experimental demonstration of arms-race co-evolution between *Bdellovibrio* and prey; shows oscillating resistance/counter-resistance dynamics and stable coexistence outcomes. Raw note: [[Microbial Predator-Prey Ecology - Bdellovibrio and Analogues]].
