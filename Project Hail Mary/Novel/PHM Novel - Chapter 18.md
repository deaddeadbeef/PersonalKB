# PHM Novel - Chapter 18

← [[PHM Novel - Chapter 17]] | [[Chapter Index]] | [[PHM Novel - Chapter 19]] →

> **One-line summary** — The Adrian sampling mission works, the Hail Mary is nearly destroyed in the process, and Rocky sacrifices himself to save Grace.

## 🎯 Intuition
**The Core Idea:** Friendship stops being conversational and becomes a life-risking act.
**Why It Matters:** This chapter is one of the novel's emotional and physical climaxes. It preserves the precious sample needed for the scientific solution while proving that Grace and Rocky's bond has become strong enough to override even species-level survival instincts.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics
### Summary

Rocky has now accepted relativity. Chain assembly is complete. Grace and Rocky execute the sampling plan: Grace tilts the Hail Mary at 60° and slows to 127.5 m/s (carefully calculated), Rocky deploys 20 spools of xenonite chain into Adrian's atmosphere while Grace maintains altitude with the spin drives. Rocky designs a self-contained life-support sample container for the collected organisms. After a tense wait, Grace performs a spacewalk to retrieve the sampler as the chain is winched back up. He grabs it — and immediately things go catastrophically wrong. The Hail Mary begins tilting uncontrollably; the horizon rises; the ship is thrashing. Grace scrambles across the hull, clutching the sample, as the ship lurches and the tether system becomes unreliable. He barely gets back to the airlock. Inside: the hull has a massive melt-hole — 20 metres long, half as wide — from atmospheric blowback. A fuel bay has breached and is burning; the exhaust is thrusting erratically, spinning the ship and building up crushing centripetal force. Grace, barely able to breathe under the mounting g-forces, makes a last-ditch decision to jettison a fuel bay. The wrong one — the force reverses, throwing him the other direction. He is pinned, lungs unable to expand, fading into unconsciousness. Then the groaning stops. Rocky has come through the large dormitory airlock — through Grace's oxygen-rich atmosphere — to save him. Rocky's last words before slumping are: "Save...Earth...Save...Erid..."

### Characters Present

- **Ryland Grace** — executes the EVA; retrieves the sample; nearly dies from centripetal force; is saved by Rocky
- **Rocky** — designs the life-support sample container; deploys the chain spools; crosses into Grace's oxygen atmosphere to physically rescue him; sustains severe oxygen burns

## 🔬 Deep Dive
### Science and Technology

- Chain deployment dynamics: ship tilted at 60°, slowed to 127.5 m/s orbital velocity, spin drives maintaining altitude while the chain hangs into the Petrova-line breeding zone — see [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]].
- Rocky's life-support sample container: maintains Adrian atmospheric conditions passively (status quo maintenance, no active control needed) — elegant engineering under resource constraint.
- Atmospheric blowback hull damage: Adrian's thick atmosphere (9 atm at surface) generates aerodynamic forces at low orbital velocity that melt a 20m × ~10m hole in the hull.
- Fuel-bay jettison: emergency procedure; Grace selects wrong bay (fifty-fifty), worsening forces momentarily before stabilising.
- Oxygen toxicity for Eridians: Rocky's biology requires very-low-oxygen ammonia atmosphere; exposure to Grace's oxygen-rich air causes severe chemical burns — see [[Rocky and the Eridians]].

### Narrative Analysis
Weir strips away flashbacks and keeps the chapter entirely in present action, which creates relentless momentum. Precise engineering details make the disaster feel believable, and Rocky's rescue lands harder because it erupts out of pure procedural chaos.
