# PHM Novel - Chapter 01

[[Chapter Index]] | [[PHM Novel - Chapter 02]] →

> **One-line summary** — Grace wakes alone and amnesiac while a brief Petrova flashback plants the mystery that will define the novel.

## 🎯 Intuition
**The Core Idea:** The chapter establishes Grace's total disorientation and links it to the first hint of the Astrophage crisis.
**Why It Matters:** Weir opens by making the reader learn the situation at the same speed as Grace. The tiny Petrova flashback widens the scope from a survival puzzle aboard ship to a civilization-scale scientific mystery.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics
### Summary
Ryland Grace regains consciousness in an oval medical bed with total amnesia. A computer voice repeats arithmetic questions—starting with "What's two plus two?"—to probe cognitive recovery while robot arms remove medical tubes from his body. He can barely speak at first, slowly fighting through paralysed muscles to form words and open his eyes against blinding light. He discovers he is connected to dozens of tubes and biosensors and that the room holds two other oval beds with mummified crewmates who cannot be roused. A fragmentary flashback surfaces of a diner where Grace reads an email from astronomer Dr. Irina Petrova to an astronomy mailing list: she has noticed a thin infrared arc stretching between the Sun and Venus—"The Thin Red Line"—and is soliciting theories from colleagues. Back in the present, Grace manages to sit up and stand but still cannot answer the computer's final question: what is your name?
