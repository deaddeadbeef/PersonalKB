# PHM Novel - Chapter 29

← [[PHM Novel - Chapter 28]] | [[Chapter Index]] | [[PHM Novel - Chapter 30]] →

> **One-line summary** — After months of searching, Grace finds the stranded Blip-A and reunites with Rocky, proving his rescue gamble was worth it.

## 🎯 Intuition
**The Core Idea:** Grace's decision to turn back pays off when he finds Rocky alive and delivers the one piece of knowledge that can still save Erid.
**Why It Matters:** This chapter is the emotional payoff to the moral choice made in Chapter 28. It also confirms that Grace's rescue is not merely sentimental: his scientific knowledge about xenonite permeability is exactly what Rocky needs.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics
### Summary

Chapter 29 opens approximately three months into the search. The decision and the Beetle launch are already history — both occurred in Chapter 28. Grace has been methodically sweeping the Petrovascope through three-dimensional arcs, with attitude-control thrusters feeding Astrophage as a slow rotating light source in the Petrova wavelength. The technique finally pays off. A faint return signal appears in one arc. Grace closes the range over hours, matches velocity with the contact, and confirms it is Blip-A — dead in space, no drive signature, no response to radio or light.

With both ships at matched velocity and zero relative drift, Grace EVAs across on a tether. He reaches Blip-A's hull and knocks with a wrench until Rocky hears him. The airlock tunnel extends. Rocky's physical state tells the story immediately: burns along one side, two arms injured from failed attempts to stop the Taumoeba infestation. His emotional state is another matter. His first words confirm he is alive and astonished: "You are here!" Grace explains that he knew about the xenonite penetration problem — something Rocky had no way to discover — and came specifically because of it. Rocky's reply is the chapter's emotional peak: "You save me and you save Erid!"

## 🔬 Deep Dive
### Science and Technology

- Petrovascope search execution: the improvised radar technique set up in Chapter 28 (attitude-control thrusters as rotating Petrova-band light source, scope detecting reflected signal) completes its purpose here. The search geometry requires three-dimensional arc sweeping across positional uncertainty in all six degrees. See [[Astrophage Biology]] and [[PHM Novel - Chapter 28]] for the technique's design.
- Blip-A fuel depletion confirmed: total absence of Astrophage drive signature over the search period is consistent with Taumoeba-82.5 spreading through the all-xenonite fuel system without Grace's intervention. Rocky had no means of containment without knowing the penetration trait existed.
- EVA tether-crossing to a matched-velocity derelict: zero relative drift between the Hail Mary and Blip-A makes the EVA approach feasible without a thruster pack. Tether length and closing speed are the only constraints.

### Plot Connections

- The reunion here is the pay-off of the rescue decision and Beetle launch made in Chapter 28; see [[PHM Novel - Chapter 28]] for the choice and its logic.
- The xenonite warning Grace delivers at this moment is the practical information Rocky needed and could not have obtained any other way; its downstream effect is the successful Eridian response implied in the Epilogue.
- Grace's decision, whose emotional core is on display here, mirrors and inverts the original crew's one-way commitment: a different kind of irrevocable act. See [[Resolution and Aftermath]].
- See [[Rocky and the Eridians]] for the relationship context that makes Grace's EVA legible as an act of friendship rather than pure mission logic.

### Narrative Analysis
This chapter delays gratification by skipping ahead three months, making the eventual reunion feel hard-won rather than immediate. The pacing is lean and linear, with the technical search procedure giving way to one of the novel's simplest and strongest emotional beats. Rocky's line works because it collapses friendship and planetary stakes into the same moment.
