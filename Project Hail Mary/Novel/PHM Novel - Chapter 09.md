# PHM Novel - Chapter 09

← [[PHM Novel - Chapter 08]] | [[Chapter Index]] | [[PHM Novel - Chapter 10]] →

> **One-line summary** — Grace makes first visual and auditory contact with Rocky and establishes the first shared unit of time.

## 🎯 Intuition
**The Core Idea:** The chapter shows first contact becoming real communication through material clues, sound, and measurement.
**Why It Matters:** This is the moment the alien ceases to be an abstraction and becomes a collaborator-in-progress. The time-unit exchange also shows that scientific communication can begin before full language, giving the relationship a rigorous foundation.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics
### Summary
Grace knocks back on the tunnel wall three times, mirroring what the alien did. He examines the hex wall on the far end of the tunnel—roughly forty hexagonal panels, each apparently made of a different material, suggesting the alien is presenting a range of compounds for Grace to interact with or assess. He leaves the tunnel to let the aliens inspect whatever reactions occur. Returning, he notices a small triangular object near one of the hexes: it moves, bends at articulated joints, and resolves into a three-fingered claw—his first visual contact with a living Eridian. He is desperate to see more but the view is limited. Shortly after, the alien is replaced by what appears to be a robot, and the tunnel is depressurised to vacuum on the Eridian side, suggesting the aliens are systematically sampling the tunnel environment and Grace's hull material. Then, unexpectedly, a sound comes from the tunnel: the alien is communicating by vibrating the xenonite wall with direct physical contact, producing distinct tones—a language that requires no mouth. Grace is astonished. The alien—who Grace has begun to think of as "Rocky"—then holds up a clock with raised symbols rotating on three concentric rotors, clearly responding to a clock Grace had taped to his side of the wall earlier. Together they time out the duration of one full rotation to establish a shared time unit: one Eridian second equals approximately 2.366 Earth seconds.
