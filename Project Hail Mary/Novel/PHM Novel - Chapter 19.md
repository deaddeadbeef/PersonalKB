# PHM Novel - Chapter 19

← [[PHM Novel - Chapter 18]] | [[Chapter Index]] | [[PHM Novel - Chapter 20]] →

> **One-line summary** — Grace stabilizes the crippled ship, protects the sample, and saves Rocky with a first-principles treatment for alien oxygen burns.

## 🎯 Intuition
**The Core Idea:** After the rescue, survival depends on calm reasoning and care instead of sheer daring.
**Why It Matters:** The chapter proves that Grace can reciprocate Rocky's sacrifice in the language both of them trust most: applied science. It also preserves the mission's only viable sample, turning a disaster sequel into the bridge between emotional payoff and the upcoming research breakthrough.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics
### Summary

Rocky is motionless; Grace is barely conscious under crushing centripetal force. Grace's first priority is stopping the spin: he extends the centrifuge cables from 20 metres to 75 metres, reducing the centripetal force to one-fourteenth, allowing him to breathe and move. He climbs hand-over-hand (the ship is effectively upside down) to the navigation console and brings the Hail Mary into a stable orbit. Rocky lies collapsed in the dormitory — unmoving, body burning hot. Grace cannot touch him. His second priority is the sample: the organism container survived, cushioned by the EVA suit in the airlock. He confirms it is intact and the contents are viable. Then the medical challenge: how do you treat an Eridian with severe oxygen burns? Grace reasons from first principles. Rocky's radiator vents on his carapace are the critical organ — coated in combustion products and oxides, they have lost heat-conductivity. Grace improvises a tool: a drill bit tipped with a xenonite chip (epoxied on) inside a sealed steel box that can pressurise with Eridian air via a small drilled port, all controlled remotely. He uses it as a directed air blast into Rocky's carapace vents — and Rocky's vents erupt with black smoke and debris, clearing the blockage. Signs of recovery. The chapter ends with Grace tending to Rocky, the sample safely secured, and the immediate emergency resolved — transition from crisis to investigation.

### Characters Present

- **Ryland Grace** — stabilises the ship; secures the sample; improvises Rocky's oxygen-burn treatment
- **Rocky** — recovering from oxygen exposure; carapace vents cleared; shows first signs of response
