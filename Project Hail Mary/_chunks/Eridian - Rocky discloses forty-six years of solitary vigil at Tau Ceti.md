# Rocky discloses forty-six years of solitary vigil at Tau Ceti

## Context

In Chapter 14, Rocky casually reveals that he has been at Tau Ceti for forty-six Earth years. His crew died during transit (from radiation sickness, as established in Chapter 13), and Rocky continued the mission alone — investigating, observing, and maintaining his ship in a system where no help could reach him. Rocky's tone is matter-of-fact; for him, this is simply what the mission required. The mismatch between the emotional weight of the disclosure and Rocky's calm delivery is one of the novel's most effective characterisation beats.

## Why It Matters

The forty-six-year figure reframes everything about Rocky. His engineering skills, his emotional stability, his willingness to cooperate instantly with Grace — all are products of a being who has spent nearly half a century alone with a planet-saving mission and no one to share it with. The disclosure also explains Rocky's surplus fuel (his ship was designed for a shorter mission; the time-dilation effect meant he arrived early) and his accumulated knowledge of the Tau Ceti system. It deepens the partnership by establishing that Grace is not just Rocky's collaborator but his first companion in decades.
