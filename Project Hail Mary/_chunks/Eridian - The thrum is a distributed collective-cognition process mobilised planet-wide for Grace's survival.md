# The thrum is a distributed collective-cognition process mobilised planet-wide for Grace's survival

## Context

In the Epilogue, Grace reports that the Eridian scientific community responded to his nutritional deficiency diseases by executing a planet-wide collective-intelligence process — a 'thrum.' Grace describes this as the second-largest science-oriented thrum ever, exceeded only by the original Astrophage response plan. The thrum is presented as a distinctive Eridian cognitive capability: many individuals contributing to a distributed group mind.

## Why It Matters

The thrum is one of the novel's most distinctive pieces of xenobiology worldbuilding. It suggests that Eridian cognition operates on a fundamentally different social scale than human intelligence — collective rather than individual for large problems. The fact that Grace's survival warranted the second-largest science thrum in history also indicates the value Eridian civilisation places on the interspecies relationship.
