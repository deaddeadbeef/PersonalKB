# Rocky enters Grace's oxygen atmosphere to save him accepting lethal exposure

## Context

The chain-retrieval EVA goes catastrophically wrong: atmospheric blowback melts a hole in the hull, a fuel bay breaches and burns, and the ship spins out of control. Grace is pinned under crushing g-forces, unable to breathe. Rocky could have waited safely in his ammonia environment and engineered a solution. Instead, he crosses into Grace's lethal oxygen atmosphere, sustaining severe burns, to physically stabilise the ship. His collapse — 'Save...Earth...Save...Erid...' — is the emotional climax of their friendship.

## Why It Matters

Rocky's oxygen crossing is the most costly act of the partnership and the novel's clearest statement about what their relationship means. It is not calculated mutual benefit — it is sacrifice accepted for a friend. This moment is what makes Grace's later decision (Chapter 28) to turn back for Rocky feel earned. See [[Arc - Grace and Rocky]] for the relationship arc and [[Rocky and the Eridians]] for the biological cost.
