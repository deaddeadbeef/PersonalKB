# Ice albedo feedback amplifies solar dimming

## Context

Ice-albedo feedback is one of the strongest positive feedback mechanisms in Earth's climate system. When temperatures drop, ice and snow cover increase, raising the planet's albedo (reflectivity). Higher albedo means less solar energy is absorbed, which drives further cooling, which creates more ice. In paleoclimate, this mechanism is implicated in snowball-Earth episodes where glaciation extended to equatorial latitudes.

## Why It Matters

This is the mechanism that makes the Astrophage crisis existential rather than merely inconvenient. A few percent solar dimming doesn't just cause proportional cooling — it can trigger a self-reinforcing loop. The Maunder Minimum (~0.1% solar variation) correlated with the Little Ice Age. A multi-percent reduction, sustained and accelerating as Astrophage populations grow, could push Earth past the tipping point into runaway glaciation. The novel's ticking-clock urgency is physically justified.
