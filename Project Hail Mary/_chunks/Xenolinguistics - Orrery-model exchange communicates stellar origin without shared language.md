# Orrery-model exchange communicates stellar origin without shared language

## Context

In Chapter 7 (present-timeline), Grace disassembles the alien cylinder and finds a miniature orrery — whisker-like rods of different lengths arranged to represent a stellar system. He deduces that the star's position corresponds to 40 Eridani, identifying the alien's home system. He then fabricates a matching model marking the Sun and sends it back. The alien responds with another orrery marking one of 40 Eridani's planets — an identification of the Eridian homeworld. No words, no shared alphabet, and no shared sensory modality are required: both parties share physical reality (the geometry of stellar systems) and can represent it in three dimensions.

## Why It Matters

This exchange is the first genuine information transfer between the two species. It demonstrates the real-world SETI principle that physical referents and mathematical/spatial structure can serve as a communication substrate before any symbolic language exists. The orrery protocol is more powerful than signal mirroring (which only proves mutual awareness): it conveys specific factual content — origin — with verifiable fidelity. It also establishes cooperative intent: the alien's prompt reciprocation of a marked homeworld signals a willingness to share, not merely to respond.
