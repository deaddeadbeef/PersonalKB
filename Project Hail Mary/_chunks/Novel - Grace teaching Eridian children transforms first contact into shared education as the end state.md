# Grace teaching Eridian children transforms first contact into shared education as the end state

## Context

The Epilogue closes with Grace walking to his teaching room. Thirty young Eridians bounce on the other side of a divider wall. Grace sits at a keyboard that encodes Eridian tonal communication with full inflections and mood markers — the mature form of the xenolinguistics arc. The novel ends here: a human teacher and an alien class.

## Why It Matters

This final image reframes the entire novel. Grace was a teacher before the mission, and he is a teacher at the end — but now teaching across species rather than within one. The organ keyboard represents the full development of the xenolinguistics arc from first clumsy exchanges to fluent classroom instruction.
