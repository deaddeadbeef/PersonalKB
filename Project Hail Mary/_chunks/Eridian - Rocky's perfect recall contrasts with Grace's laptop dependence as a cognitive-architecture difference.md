# Rocky's perfect recall contrasts with Grace's laptop dependence as a cognitive-architecture difference

## Context

During the Chapter 11 vocabulary-building marathon, Grace keeps careful records on his laptop — noting each new word, its Eridian tonal form, its confirmed referent, and its usage context. Rocky keeps no equivalent external record. He reproduces every agreed term precisely from session to session, including numerical calibrations and multi-stage definitions. The contrast is not presented as Rocky showing off: it appears to be a baseline cognitive difference. Rocky treats external storage as Grace's problem to solve; he simply has the lexicon in his head.

## Why It Matters

This detail raises genuinely interesting questions about Eridian cognitive architecture. An acoustic-primary species relying heavily on echolocation for real-time spatial modelling would likely have exceptional working memory for acoustic patterns — the neural machinery for processing rapid pulse-echo sequences might generalise to strong phonological and lexical retention. Whether the novel intends this as a direct biological consequence or a character trait is ambiguous, but the pattern is consistent throughout: Rocky functions as a walking archive for the shared technical vocabulary. The contrast also illuminates Grace's role — his value to the collaboration is partly his external tooling (laptop, laboratory instruments) compensating for human memory limits. See [[Eridian Sensory Biology]] for the acoustic-cognition context.
