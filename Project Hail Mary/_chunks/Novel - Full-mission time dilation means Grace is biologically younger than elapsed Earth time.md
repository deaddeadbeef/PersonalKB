# Full-mission time dilation means Grace is biologically younger than elapsed Earth time

## Context

The Epilogue shows Grace years into his life on Erid. He has aged, but less than the calendar time since he left Earth. The relativistic time-dilation effects mean that everyone he knew on Earth has aged more than he has. When Rocky offers to send him home, Grace declines — the trip would consume years he no longer has, and his life is now on Erid.

## Why It Matters

Time dilation is not just a physics concept in the novel — it is the mechanism that permanently separates Grace from his Earth life. The asymmetric aging means there is literally no going back to the world he left. This adds emotional weight to his rescue decision and his Epilogue contentment.
