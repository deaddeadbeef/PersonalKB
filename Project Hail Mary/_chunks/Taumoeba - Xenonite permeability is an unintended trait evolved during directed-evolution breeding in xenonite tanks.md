# Xenonite permeability is an unintended trait evolved during directed-evolution breeding in xenonite tanks

## Context

Grace's systematic investigation reveals that every xenonite breeder farm is leaking Taumoeba while Eridian-steel mini-farms are not. The explanation: during breeding, Taumoeba that could penetrate xenonite's molecular structure escaped the lethal nitrogen inside the tanks. Evolution selected for this trait alongside nitrogen tolerance. A confirmation experiment proves only Taumoeba-82.5 can pass through xenonite; the original strain cannot. The horror: Rocky's entire ship is xenonite.

## Why It Matters

Xenonite permeability is the novel's sharpest unintended-consequence beat. The same directed evolution that produced Earth's saviour created an existential threat to Eridian civilisation. It is also scientifically elegant: evolution does not optimise for one trait at a time. The distinction between directed Taumoeba movement and stochastic nitrogen diffusion is a clever piece of fictional biology.
