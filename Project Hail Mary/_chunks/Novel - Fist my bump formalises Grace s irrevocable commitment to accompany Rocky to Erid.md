# "Fist my bump" formalises Grace's irrevocable commitment to accompany Rocky to Erid

## Context

At the close of Chapter 30, with the xenonite-threat briefing delivered and the Taumoeba food solution established, Grace and Rocky seal the plan: Grace will accompany Rocky back to Erid. Rocky says "Settled. Fist my bump." — using the cross-species commitment protocol they developed during their months together. The fist-bump gesture, originally a human social convention Grace introduced, has become their shared binding-agreement ritual: the Eridian-language equivalent of a handshake on a deal. Once performed, the commitment is structural — Grace's food supply does not allow another Earth transit, and the course to 40 Eridani has been set.

## Why It Matters

The "fist my bump" seals a three-part logic that the chapter has assembled: Rocky is rescued (Ch29) → Grace can survive on Taumoeba (Ch30 food solution) → the plan is committed (this beat). Without the formalization, the rescue decision could read as still-open; the fist-bump closes it. It also demonstrates, economically, how much their shared language has grown: Rocky uses a human cultural import — correctly, affectionately, at exactly the right moment — to bind the most consequential agreement of both their lives.
