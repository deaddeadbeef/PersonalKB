# Petrovascope first activation confirms Astrophage is present at Tau Ceti

## Context

Grace calculates the Hail Mary's velocity at 7,595 km/s during deceleration and waits five days for engine cutoff so the Petrovascope can operate without drive interference. When he activates it, the instrument immediately shows a Petrova Line at Tau Ceti — Astrophage is here. The mission's premise is confirmed: Tau Ceti's dimming immunity is not because Astrophage is absent but because something is suppressing it. While observing, Grace notices a sharp discontinuity in the Petrova Line that cannot be a lens artefact. Switching to visible light reveals a solid object — the first glimpse of what will become Rocky's ship, the Blip-A.

## Why It Matters

This is the present timeline's first major scientific payoff. The Petrova Line detection reframes the mission from "investigate why Tau Ceti is immune" to "find what is suppressing Astrophage here" — a question whose answer (Taumoeba) drives the entire second half of the novel. The solid-object detection also initiates the first-contact arc.
