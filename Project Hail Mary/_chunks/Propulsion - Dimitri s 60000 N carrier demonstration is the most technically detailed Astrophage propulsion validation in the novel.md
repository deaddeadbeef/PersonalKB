# Dimitri's 60,000 N carrier demonstration is the most technically detailed Astrophage propulsion validation in the novel

## Context

Chapter 8's flashback shows Dimitri Komorov's Astrophage breeder team — nicknamed "Dimitri's Denizens" — conducting a controlled combustion experiment aboard the carrier. The result is 60,000 Newtons of thrust, and the team's reaction is described as stunned celebration. This is a separate scene from the Chapter 4 international briefing where Dimitri similarly announces the 60,000 N figure. The Chapter 4 briefing scene frames the result as a mission-enabling revelation for Stratt's assembled international audience. The Chapter 8 carrier scene shows the research team on the ground, working in their own facility, achieving and measuring the result — engineering bench work rather than briefing-room report. The stunned quality of the team's reaction underlines that the result exceeded expectations even among the scientists producing it.

## Why It Matters

The 60,000 N figure is the novel's central propulsion validation. It is the moment that converts Astrophage from a biological curiosity into a viable drive fuel. The Chapter 8 carrier scene grounds this validation in practical laboratory reality: a specialist team, a controlled experiment, a measured result, and the researchers' own surprise at the magnitude. This is the closest the novel comes to depicting the actual bench-science moment when interstellar propulsion became achievable within the story's world.
