# Xenonite pressure sphere is a transit vehicle across incompatible atmospheres

## Context

In Chapter 14, Rocky completes the xenonite pressure sphere he began building in Chapter 13. The sphere allows him to physically enter the Hail Mary and move through its interior while maintaining his native atmospheric conditions. Grace describes Rocky as 'as heavy as a motorcycle' inside the device. This is the engineering solution that makes direct shared-space collaboration possible.

## Why It Matters

The pressure sphere is the structural turning point that upgrades the Grace-Rocky partnership from wall-separated exchange to shared laboratory work. It demonstrates xenonite's engineering versatility (see [[Xenonite - Eridian Structural Material]]) and Rocky's practical problem-solving approach. Unlike the docking tunnel (which is a static neutral zone), the sphere is a mobile personal environment.
