# Chain-deployment mechanics tilt the ship at 60 degrees to drag a 10km xenonite chain through the breeding band

## Context

The Petrova-line breeding band sits 10 km below minimum safe orbit. Grace and Rocky's solution is a 10 km xenonite chain with a life-support sample container at the end, deployed by tilting the ship and reducing speed while maintaining altitude with the engines. The 200,000 links were hand-made over weeks from melted-down ship beds. The EVA to retrieve the sampler goes catastrophically wrong when atmospheric blowback punches a 20-metre melt-hole in the hull.

## Why It Matters

The chain-deployment sequence is the novel's most elaborate joint engineering feat. It demonstrates the complementary expertise theme: Rocky builds the xenonite chain and container; Grace calculates the orbital mechanics. The catastrophe during retrieval leads directly to Rocky's oxygen-atmosphere rescue. See [[Xenonite - Eridian Structural Material]] for the material and [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the biological outcome.
