# Biochemical compatibility with Earth life makes Taumoeba viable as human food for Grace's survival

## Context

After the rescue, Grace faces a food problem: he cannot survive the trip to Erid on remaining supplies, and Eridian food is toxic due to heavy metals. Rocky proposes Taumoeba. The organism's biochemistry is fundamentally compatible with Earth life because it evolved from the same Astrophage-seeded genetic lineage. The Epilogue confirms Taumoeba is calorically viable but nutritionally incomplete — Grace eventually develops deficiency diseases before Eridian scientists develop supplementation.

## Why It Matters

Taumoeba-as-food is the narrative mechanism that makes Grace's survival on Erid physically possible. It ties back to the Chapter 13 panspermia hypothesis: if Astrophage seeded all DNA-based life, then Adrian-origin organisms should be biochemically compatible with human digestion. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the full profile and [[Resolution and Aftermath]] for the long-term outcome.
