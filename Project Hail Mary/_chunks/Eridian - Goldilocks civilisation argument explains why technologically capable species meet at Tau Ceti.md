# Goldilocks civilisation argument explains why technologically capable species meet at Tau Ceti

## Context

After the radiation lesson, Grace and Rocky discuss why they met at all. Grace proposes that a civilisation too primitive to build interstellar ships would simply die, while one advanced enough to handle Astrophage locally would never need to travel. Only species in the narrow middle band — capable of the journey but not the local fix — would converge on Tau Ceti. Rocky confirms his civilisation fits the same description.

## Why It Matters

The Goldilocks civilisation argument retroactively explains the novel's entire premise. It transforms the coincidence of two species meeting at the same star from narrative convenience into an in-universe inevitability — a self-selecting filter that makes convergence the expected outcome rather than a miracle.
