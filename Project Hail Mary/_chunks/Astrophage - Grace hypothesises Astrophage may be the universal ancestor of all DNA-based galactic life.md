# Grace hypothesises Astrophage may be the universal ancestor of all DNA-based galactic life

## Context

During the Chapter 13 biological exchange, Grace reasons that if Astrophage migrates between stars and carries genetic material, it could have seeded the precursors of DNA-based life throughout the galaxy over billions of years. He frames this explicitly as speculation, not certainty.

## Why It Matters

This hypothesis connects Astrophage's established interstellar migration (see [[Astrophage Life Cycle and Migration]]) to the broader question of why life at both Earth and Erid shares fundamental biochemical features. It is the novel's boldest speculative claim and is clearly marked as such in the text. See [[Alternative Biochemistry]] for the ammonia-vs-water biochemistry context this hypothesis reframes.
