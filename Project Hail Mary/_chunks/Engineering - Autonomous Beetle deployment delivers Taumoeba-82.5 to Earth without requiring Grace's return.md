# Autonomous Beetle deployment delivers Taumoeba-82.5 to Earth without requiring Grace's return

## Context

In Chapter 28, after confirming Blip-A's engines are dark (Rocky is stranded), Grace first replaces all xenonite Taumoeba tanks with aluminum and then launches the Beetles — pre-loaded with Taumoeba-82.5 farms in sealed Eridian-steel capsules. The probes are programmed for autonomous navigation to the Solar System. This act discharges Grace's mission obligation: Earth will receive the solution regardless of what he does next.

## Why It Matters

The Beetle launch is what makes Grace's subsequent rescue decision possible. By decoupling his personal fate from the mission outcome, he creates the moral space to choose Rocky over Earth. The Epilogue confirms the Beetles arrived and the solution worked. See [[Hail Mary Ship Design and Systems]] for the Beetle specifications and [[Resolution and Aftermath]] for the outcome.
