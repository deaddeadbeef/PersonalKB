# Adrian is the first named investigation target for anomalous Astrophage suppression

## Context

In Chapter 15, after Rocky offers surplus fuel and the mission's emotional register shifts from certain death to potential survival, Grace and Rocky set course for Tau Ceti e — eleven days out. Grace names it Adrian. The planet's selection is driven by the core mission question: why is Astrophage suppressed here and not at Sol?

## Why It Matters

Adrian's naming and targeting marks the transition from the relationship-building arc to the scientific investigation arc. Everything that follows — the homeworld discovery, the chain sampler, the Taumoeba identification — flows from this decision. See [[Tau Ceti and 40 Eridani]] for the real-star context and [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for what Adrian yields.
