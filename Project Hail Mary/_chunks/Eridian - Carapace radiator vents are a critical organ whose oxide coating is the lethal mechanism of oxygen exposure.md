# Carapace radiator vents are a critical organ whose oxide coating is the lethal mechanism of oxygen exposure

## Context

After Rocky's oxygen-atmosphere rescue in Chapter 18, he lies motionless with a burning-hot carapace. Grace reasons from first principles: the radiator vents are coated in combustion products and oxides that insulate them, blocking the heat conductivity Rocky needs to survive. Grace builds a sealed treatment tool — a xenonite-tipped drill bit inside a pressurised box fed with Eridian air — and uses it to blast debris from the vents. Black smoke erupts and Rocky begins to recover.

## Why It Matters

This chunk establishes the radiator vents as Rocky's most critical and vulnerable organ. The oxide-coating mechanism explains precisely why oxygen is lethal to Eridians: not through respiratory failure (they don't breathe) but through heat-regulation failure. Grace's first-principles medical reasoning across alien biology is one of the novel's best demonstrations of scientific thinking under pressure.
