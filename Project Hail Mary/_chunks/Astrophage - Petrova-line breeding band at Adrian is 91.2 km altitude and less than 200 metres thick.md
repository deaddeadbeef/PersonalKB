# Petrova-line breeding band at Adrian is 91.2 km altitude and less than 200 metres thick

## Context

Using the Petrovascope, Grace measures the exact altitude and thickness of Adrian's Astrophage breeding zone. At 91.2 km altitude and under 200 metres wide, the band is a precision engineering target — too low for safe orbit but too high for direct descent. This measurement drives the chain-sampler design in Chapter 17.

## Why It Matters

The breeding band's precise dimensions transform a vague 'go sample the atmosphere' problem into a specific engineering challenge with exact parameters. The thinness of the band also implies concentrated predator activity, which is confirmed when the chain sample yields Taumoeba. See [[Stellar Dimming and the Petrova Line]] for the Petrova Line concept and [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the sampling outcome.
