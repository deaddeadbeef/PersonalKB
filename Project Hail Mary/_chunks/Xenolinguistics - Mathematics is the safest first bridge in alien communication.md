# Mathematics is the safest first bridge in alien communication

## Context

Both Callimahos (1966) and the broader SETI community converge on mathematics as the most reliable starting point for interspecies communication. The reasoning: any civilization capable of building a radio transmitter (or, in PHM's case, an interstellar ship) must understand counting, arithmetic, and basic logical relations. Mathematical truths are objective and demonstrable — you can show that 2 + 3 = 5 by tapping without any shared language, biology, or cultural context. Freudenthal's Lincos (1960) formalized this approach into a complete constructed language built up from natural numbers.

## Why It Matters

Grace begins communicating with Rocky by tapping prime numbers and demonstrating arithmetic. This is not just a narrative convenience — it is the consensus approach in real SETI/METI thinking. The novel's depiction of math-first bootstrapping, followed by physical referents, then verbs, then abstract concepts, closely parallels how linguists and communication theorists have imagined first contact would actually proceed.
