# Organ-keyboard teaching scene marks xenolinguistics maturing to classroom instruction

## Context

In the Epilogue, years after the main narrative, Grace lives on Erid in a purpose-built habitat dome and teaches a class of thirty young Eridians. His instrument is a modified organ keyboard that encodes the full range of Eridian-human communication: inflections, tones, and emotional markers. The keyboard represents the final evolution of the communication system that began with mirrored Petrova-band flashes in Chapter 6, progressed through vibration language, clock synchronisation, and object exchange, and now supports structured pedagogy. The novel ends on this image: a human teacher and a class of alien children.

## Why It Matters

The closing image completes two arcs simultaneously. First, the xenolinguistics arc: from desperate first-contact improvisation to fluent enough communication for teaching. The organ keyboard is not just a tool but evidence of a mature, systematised language — one that can be taught to the next generation, not just used between its two inventors. Second, Grace's character arc: he began the novel as a middle-school science teacher and ends it as a teacher again — but now his students are alien children on another world. The symmetry is deliberate and emotionally resonant. See [[Arc - Xenolinguistics Progression]] for the full communication arc and [[Resolution and Aftermath]] for the ending analysis.
