# Beetle probes are autonomous mission-insurance vehicles at 500g acceleration to 0.93c

## Context

In a Chapter 17 flashback, inventor Steve Hatch introduces Grace to the Beetle programme aboard the carrier. The Beetles are tiny autonomous vehicles carrying sealed Astrophage fuel, capable of extreme acceleration. Hatch names his prototype after Pete Best (the pre-Ringo Beatle). The probes are designed so that mission-critical findings can reach Earth regardless of the crew's fate.

## Why It Matters

The Beetles are the novel's key redundancy mechanism. In Chapter 28, Grace loads them with Taumoeba-82.5 and launches them toward Earth before turning back for Rocky — ensuring the solution arrives even though he will not. See [[Hail Mary Ship Design and Systems]] for the ship's full complement and [[The Hail Mary Drive]] for the propulsion physics.
