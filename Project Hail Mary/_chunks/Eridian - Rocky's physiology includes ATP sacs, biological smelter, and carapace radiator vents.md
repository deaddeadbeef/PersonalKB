# Rocky's physiology includes ATP sacs, biological smelter, and carapace radiator vents

## Context

In Chapter 13, during the radiation lesson that explains the death of Rocky's crewmates, Rocky provides an extended biological disclosure. His ATP sacs store metabolic energy; his biological smelter extracts oxygen from mineral compounds rather than from an atmosphere; and his carapace radiator vents dissipate waste heat. These details establish that Eridian life is built on fundamentally different biochemical machinery than Earth life, while still being functionally recognisable as a working metabolism.

## Why It Matters

This is the novel's most concentrated xenobiology exposition. The ATP sacs, smelter, and radiator vents collectively define Rocky as a plausible alien organism rather than a fantasy creature — each organ has a clear functional role. The radiator vents become medically critical in Chapter 19, when oxide coating from oxygen burns blocks them and nearly kills Rocky.
