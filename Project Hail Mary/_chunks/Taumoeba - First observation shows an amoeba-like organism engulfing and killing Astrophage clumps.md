# First observation shows an amoeba-like organism engulfing and killing Astrophage clumps

## Context

Using Rocky's sealed xenonite extraction box to safely prepare a slide from the Adrian sample, Grace watches through the microscope as an amoeba-like organism engulfs Astrophage. The prey are paralysed — unable to accelerate away — before the predator completes encirclement, suggesting a chemical exudate. Grace names it Taumoeba. He and Rocky then set up Venus and Threeworld atmospheric simulation experiments to test survivability.

## Why It Matters

This is the pivotal scientific moment of the novel: the transition from theoretical predator hypothesis to confirmed, named, and characterised organism. The observation of chemical paralysis before engulfment implies a sophisticated predation mechanism. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the full scientific profile and [[Arc - Taumoeba Discovery]] for the discovery arc context.
