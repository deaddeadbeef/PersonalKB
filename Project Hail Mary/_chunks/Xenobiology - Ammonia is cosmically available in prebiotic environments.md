# Ammonia is cosmically available in prebiotic environments

## Context

NASA's OSIRIS-REx mission returned pristine samples from asteroid Bennu in 2023. Analysis published in 2024 revealed ammonium (NH₄⁺) incorporated into clay minerals, along with amino acids and complex organics. The ammonia is not trace contamination — it is a structural component of the mineral matrix, indicating that ammonia-water solutions were present during aqueous alteration on Bennu's parent body billions of years ago.

## Why It Matters

A recurring objection to ammonia-based biochemistry (like Rocky's) is availability: would ammonia be present in sufficient quantities on other worlds? Bennu answers yes. Ammonia-bearing compounds are incorporated into primordial solar-system material and delivered to planetary surfaces via impacts. If ammonia is abundant in our solar system's asteroid belt, it is likely common in other star systems with similar formation histories — including 40 Eridani, Rocky's home system. This strengthens the plausibility of the novel's ammonia-based alien biochemistry.
