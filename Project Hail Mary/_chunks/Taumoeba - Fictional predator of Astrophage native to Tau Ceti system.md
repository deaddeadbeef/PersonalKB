# Taumoeba is a fictional single-celled predator of Astrophage native to the Tau Ceti system

## Context

The novel depicts Taumoeba as the biological solution to the Astrophage crisis. It is an organism native to a planet (or planetary atmosphere) in the Tau Ceti system that evolved to consume Astrophage as its primary food source. Taumoeba can survive the same extreme temperature and pressure conditions Astrophage exploits, and its presence in the Tau Ceti system explains why that star has not dimmed to the same catastrophic degree as the Sun. The novel makes clear that Taumoeba is host-specific: it consumes Astrophage and not other organisms. This is a fictional extrapolation, not a description of any known organism.

## Why It Matters

Taumoeba is the pivot point of the entire resolution arc. The discovery that a biological predator can control Astrophage population reframes the problem from "physics challenge" to "ecology problem," and Grace's scientific method — studying the organism, understanding its host-specificity, testing it in controlled conditions — drives the climax. The real-world anchor is predatory microbiology: organisms like *Bdellovibrio bacteriovorus* demonstrate that microbial predator-prey dynamics are real and can be host-specific.
