# Living fungal shields can attenuate radiation

## Context

In the same ISS experiment, a ~1.7 mm layer of *C. sphaerospermum* was measured for its radiation-blocking properties. The fungal layer provided modest but detectable attenuation of the ambient radiation field beneath it. Because the fungus is alive, it self-repairs and self-replicates — a property no passive shielding material has.

## Why It Matters

This connects to two PHM themes. First, it reinforces Astrophage's plausibility: if melanized organisms interact with radiation strongly enough to attenuate it, they interact strongly enough to harvest it. Second, it has practical spaceflight implications — biological shielding is a real research direction for long-duration missions, and the *Hail Mary* crew faces radiation hazards at relativistic speeds. The novel doesn't use bio-shielding, but the science behind it strengthens the worldbuilding.
