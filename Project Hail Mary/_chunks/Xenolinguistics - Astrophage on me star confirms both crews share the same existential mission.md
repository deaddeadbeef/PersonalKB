# "Astrophage on me star" confirms both crews share the same existential mission

## Context

By Chapter 11, Grace and Rocky have built enough shared vocabulary for Rocky to communicate his reason for being at Tau Ceti. His statement is simple but transformative: "Astrophage on me star. Bad bad bad." Both species sent ships to the same system for the same reason — Tau Ceti's anomalous resistance to Astrophage. The revelation reframes the entire relationship: Grace and Rocky are not just fellow travellers but allies in a shared existential crisis. Neither can solve the problem alone.

## Why It Matters

This is the pivot from first-contact as intellectual exercise to first-contact as survival partnership. Until this moment, Grace could treat Rocky as a fascinating puzzle. After it, cooperation is not optional — it is the only rational strategy for both species. The statement also demonstrates how far the xenolinguistics arc has progressed: Rocky can now express abstract concepts (stellar infestation, urgency) using a vocabulary built from weeks of painstaking exchange. The shared-mission confirmation is the emotional and strategic foundation for everything that follows.
