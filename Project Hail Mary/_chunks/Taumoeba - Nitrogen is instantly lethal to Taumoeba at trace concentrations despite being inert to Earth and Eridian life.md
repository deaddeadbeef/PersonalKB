# Nitrogen is instantly lethal to Taumoeba at trace concentrations despite being inert to Earth and Eridian life

## Context

Grace and Rocky build controlled chambers with precise gas injection valves and test atmospheric components one at a time. CO₂ alone: Taumoeba thrives. Add nitrogen: every Taumoeba dies within seconds. The result is startling because nitrogen is biologically inert in every other context. Separate tests confirm sulfur dioxide is harmless but trace nitrogen is instantly lethal.

## Why It Matters

Nitrogen lethality is the pivotal experimental discovery that unlocks the entire solution pathway. It defines the problem (Taumoeba cannot survive Earth's 78% nitrogen atmosphere) and simultaneously suggests the solution strategy (directed evolution for nitrogen tolerance). It also provides the containment tool (nitrogen flood) used in Chapter 26. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the full scientific context.
