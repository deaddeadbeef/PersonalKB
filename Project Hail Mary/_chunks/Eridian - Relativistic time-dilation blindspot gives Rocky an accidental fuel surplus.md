# Relativistic time-dilation blindspot gives Rocky an accidental fuel surplus

## Context

Rocky has been puzzled since arrival: his trip took half the expected duration and he has far more fuel than his calculations predicted. Grace immediately recognises this as relativistic time dilation — at high fractions of lightspeed, Rocky's onboard clocks ran slower than the external frame. Eridian physics never developed this branch of knowledge because their planet's extreme atmospheric shielding means they never observed high-energy physics phenomena that would reveal it.

## Why It Matters

This is one of the novel's best characterisation moments: Rocky's civilisation built an interstellar ship without understanding relativity. The surplus fuel is a plot-significant asset (Rocky can fuel Grace's return), and the knowledge gap deepens the portrayal of Eridian science as advanced but differently evolved.
