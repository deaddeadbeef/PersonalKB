# Sol's recovery to pre-Astrophage brightness confirms the Beetle delivery succeeded

## Context

In the Epilogue, Rocky visits Grace in his Erid habitat dome and delivers the news: a thrum of Eridian astronomers has confirmed Sol's brightness has returned to normal. Grace's interior monologue is unambiguous: 'There's only one possible way that happens: Astrophage is gone.' The Beetles launched in Chapter 28 arrived, the Taumoeba-82.5 solution worked, and Earth survived.

## Why It Matters

Sol's recovery is the novel's resolution proof. It confirms that every link in the chain — Taumoeba discovery, directed evolution, Beetle loading, autonomous launch, interstellar transit, and planetary deployment — succeeded. It also validates Grace's rescue decision: he gave up his return to save Rocky, but the mission succeeded anyway. See [[Resolution and Aftermath]] for the full analysis and [[Stellar Dimming and the Petrova Line]] for the Petrova Line's significance.
