# Left-handed threading marks a distinct Eridian engineering convention

## Context

Examining the alien cylinder in Chapter 7, Grace notices that the fastener threading runs left-handed — counter to the right-hand convention that is effectively universal across all human engineering cultures. Right-hand threading dominates on Earth not for deep physical reasons but as an emergent cultural standard; there is nothing in physics that privileges one handedness. The Eridian left-hand convention is therefore a direct fingerprint of independent technological development: Eridian engineers converged on the same general solution (threaded fasteners) but with opposite chirality, suggesting their engineering tradition crystallised separately without any contact with Earth's.

## Why It Matters

The threading detail is minor in plot terms but significant as world-building evidence. It reinforces that Eridian technology is genuinely alien — not a translation of human engineering but a convergent parallel path. Convergent evolution is well documented in biology (eyes, wings, echolocation); the novel extends this logic to technology. Eridians independently developed fasteners, threading conventions, and cylindrical containers — but the chirality broke the other way. This is the kind of detail that makes the alien feel architecturally coherent rather than merely cosmetically different.
