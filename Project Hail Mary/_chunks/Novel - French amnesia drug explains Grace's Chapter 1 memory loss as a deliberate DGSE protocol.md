# French amnesia drug explains Grace's Chapter 1 memory loss as a deliberate DGSE protocol

## Context

After Grace refuses the mission for the final time, Stratt reveals her contingency. A Russian soldier blocks Grace's exit. He will be held until launch, sedated, and the amnesia drug — developed by the French DGSE for anti-terrorism operations — will erase his recent episodic memory while leaving his scientific training intact. This explains everything about Chapter 1: the amnesia, the intact competence, and the gradual memory recovery through flashbacks.

## Why It Matters

This chunk closes the novel's central structural mystery. The amnesia framing device — which has been driving the reader's curiosity since page one — is not a medical accident but a deliberate choice by Stratt. It also represents her most extreme coercive act: not merely forcing Grace onto the mission but deliberately erasing his memory of being forced.
