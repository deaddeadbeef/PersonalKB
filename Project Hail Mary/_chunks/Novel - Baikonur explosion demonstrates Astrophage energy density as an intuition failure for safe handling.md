# Baikonur explosion demonstrates Astrophage energy density as an intuition failure for safe handling

## Context

The Baikonur explosion is introduced in Chapter 20's flashback (the detonation and its immediate aftermath) and its mechanism is detailed in Chapter 22 (Stratt's accident investigation). DuBois had requisitioned one nanogram of Astrophage for a generator test; the quartermaster issued one milligram. At Astrophage's energy density, that million-fold error produced catastrophic temperatures. The deaths of DuBois and Shapiro are what force Stratt to draft Grace as the replacement science specialist.

## Why It Matters

The Baikonur accident is both a plot mechanism (it puts Grace on the ship) and a science-communication beat. It demonstrates that Astrophage's energy density makes intuitive quantity judgements lethally unreliable — a lesson about working with materials whose physics exceed everyday experience.
