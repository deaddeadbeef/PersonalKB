# Adrian is reframed as the Astrophage homeworld with a co-evolved ecology

## Context

Chapter 16 delivers the novel's major scientific reframing. When Grace examines the ECU sample under the microscope, he finds far more than Astrophage: an entire ecosystem of co-evolving organisms. The logical conclusion — that Adrian is the ancestral home of Astrophage — transforms the mission from 'find what's suppressing Astrophage' to 'find the predator in a known ecological system.'

## Why It Matters

This is the pivotal scientific reframing of the novel. The shift from physics-and-chemistry to ecology framing unlocks the entire Taumoeba discovery arc. If Adrian is the homeworld, then predation is expected, not miraculous. See [[Astrophage Life Cycle and Migration]] for the lifecycle context and [[Astrophage Biology]] for the organism's profile.
