# Eridian heavy-metal ecology makes native food directly toxic to humans forcing dependence on Taumoeba

## Context

In Chapter 30, after the rescue, Rocky offers Eridian food as a survival solution for Grace on the journey to Erid. Grace rejects it immediately: Eridian planetary ecology is saturated with heavy metals — elements that are essential or tolerable within Eridian biochemistry (which evolved under those conditions) but directly toxic to human physiology at the concentrations present throughout the Eridian food chain. This is not a cultural or taste issue but a hard biochemical barrier. Rocky then proposes Taumoeba as an alternative, and the solution holds precisely because Taumoeba did not evolve on Erid — it evolved in the nitrogen-ammonia atmosphere of Adrian, which lacks the heavy-metal saturation of Eridian ecology. The food-problem beat and its Taumoeba resolution form the same scene's two consecutive steps: the obstacle, then the workaround.

## Why It Matters

This chunk establishes the negative side of the Ch30 food logic. Without it, the Taumoeba food solution (see [[Taumoeba - Biochemical compatibility with Earth life makes it viable as human food for Grace's survival|chunk-taumo-012]]) reads as one option among several rather than the only viable path. The heavy-metal toxicity constraint is also worldbuilding with long-term consequences: it means Grace's survival on Erid will always depend on either Taumoeba supplementation or imported Earth-compatible nutrients — a dependency consistent with the Epilogue's depiction of Grace developing nutritional deficiency diseases before Eridian scientists develop supplementation. See [[Alternative Biochemistry]] for the Eridian biochemistry context and [[Resolution and Aftermath]] for the Epilogue's survival-on-Erid outcomes.
