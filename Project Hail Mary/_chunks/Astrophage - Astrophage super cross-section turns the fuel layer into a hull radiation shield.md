# Astrophage super cross-section turns the fuel layer into a hull radiation shield

## Context

During Chapter 12's radiation-teaching sequence (where Grace explains ionising radiation to Rocky over three hours and fifty new words), Grace also explains how the Hail Mary survives interstellar radiation: Astrophage has an extraordinarily high absorption cross-section across the electromagnetic spectrum. The fuel tanks surrounding the crew compartment absorb incoming radiation before it reaches the habitable areas. This is not a designed feature but a fortunate consequence of Astrophage's biology — the same property that makes it a stellar parasite (absorbing starlight) also makes it an excellent radiation shield.

## Why It Matters

This is a classic Weir dual-purpose engineering insight: the fuel is also the shield. The super cross-section property connects Astrophage's biology (designed to absorb solar energy) to the ship's engineering requirements (radiation protection during a years-long interstellar transit). It also explains why the Eridians — who never evolved to deal with radiation — could survive their own transit: their Astrophage fuel provided the same inadvertent shielding.
