# Fuel contamination causes a complete shipwide power-loss cascade on the Hail Mary

## Context

Following the Chapter 20 cliffhanger ('Then the lights go out'), Chapter 21 reveals the cause: Taumoeba has escaped into the fuel lines and consumed the Astrophage that powers both generators. The ship is completely dark except for Rocky's equipment. Grace and Rocky improvise a recovery using Rocky's sealed Astrophage supply for emergency power and the four Beetle probes (John, Paul, George, Ringo) — whose self-contained fuel systems were isolated from contamination — as makeshift engines.

## Why It Matters

The power-loss cascade is the Taumoeba arc's dramatic turning point: the organism Grace intends to deploy as Earth's saviour has just demonstrated its destructive potential aboard his own ship. It also reveals a critical design strength — the Beetles' sealed fuel systems survive because of engineering redundancy. See [[Hail Mary Ship Design and Systems]] for the Beetle complement and [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the organism's dual role as solution and threat.
