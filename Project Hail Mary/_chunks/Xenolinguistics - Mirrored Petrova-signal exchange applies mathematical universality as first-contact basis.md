# Mirrored Petrova-signal exchange applies mathematical universality as first-contact basis

## Context

In Chapter 6, Grace has confirmed via telescope that the object blocking the Petrova Line is an alien spacecraft — angular, flat-paneled, and unlike any human design. It has matched his velocity at 217 metres. Grace decides cooperation is worth the risk and fires a one-second Spin Drive burst in the Petrova frequency. The alien ship fires back for exactly one second. He escalates to patterned flashes; the alien mirrors every pattern. The exchange proves three things: the other ship is intelligent, it is using the same Astrophage-based propulsion, and it is willing to communicate.

## Why It Matters

The mirrored-signal exchange is the novel's first-contact moment. It works because both ships share the same physical medium — Astrophage-powered engines emitting in the Petrova frequency — so neither party needs to assume anything about the other's sensory capabilities. The exchange demonstrates the mathematical-universality principle that underpins real SETI theory: shared physics is the most reliable basis for initial communication. This moment launches the xenolinguistics arc that progresses through orrery models, vibration language, clock synchronisation, and eventually full working conversation.
