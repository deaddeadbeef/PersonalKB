# Xenonite docking tunnel is an engineered neutral zone between incompatible atmospheres

## Context

In Chapter 8, the alien ship manoeuvres until it is physically docked against the Hail Mary's hull. The Eridians then construct a tunnel — built from xenonite, their structural material of choice — bridging the two hulls. Grace equalises his side with ship air and waits. The tunnel creates a shared physical space that both parties can access from their respective atmospheres without either entering the other's environment. The chapter ends with three deliberate knocks from the far side of the tunnel wall: the first use of that shared space as a communication channel.

## Why It Matters

The docking tunnel is architecturally significant because it makes the central relationship of the novel physically possible. Grace and Rocky's environments are mutually lethal: Rocky's ammonia-rich, high-pressure, high-temperature atmosphere would kill Grace immediately, and Grace's oxygen-nitrogen environment would be equally hostile to Rocky. The xenonite tunnel solves this with material engineering — strong enough to hold the pressure differential, impermeable enough to maintain atmospheric isolation. The construction also communicates intention: the Eridians chose to build a permanent connecting structure before any words had been exchanged, a cooperative act that carries its own meaning.
