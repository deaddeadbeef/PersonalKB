# Rocky's surplus Astrophage offer transforms the one-way sacrifice into a survivable mission

## Context

In Chapter 15, Rocky — characteristically enthusiastic — offers Grace his surplus Astrophage: "Let's save planets!" Rocky has more fuel than expected because his civilization never discovered relativistic time dilation. The offer means Grace could refuel the Hail Mary for a return trip to Earth. Grace, who accepted certain death in Chapter 5, is overwhelmed — his tearful relief is one of the novel's most emotionally raw moments.

## Why It Matters

This is the novel's single biggest mission-pivot. Before this moment, Grace is on a one-way trip; after it, he could go home. The transformation is entirely a product of the partnership: Rocky's accidental surplus exists only because of an Eridian knowledge gap that Grace diagnosed. The offer also sets up the novel's climactic choice: when Rocky's ship is later threatened by Taumoeba-82.5, Grace must decide between using his return fuel to go home or turning back to rescue Rocky. The fuel offer makes the rescue decision possible — and agonising. See [[The Hail Mary Drive]] for the mass-ratio constraints and [[Arc - Grace and Rocky]] for the relationship escalation.
