# Grace chooses to rescue Rocky instead of returning to Earth as the defining ethical act

## Context

Grace's decision logic is explicit: Earth's solution is already en route via the Beetles. Rocky is stranded because of xenonite permeability — something only Grace knows. Rocky could not have anticipated or prevented the threat. The rescue is the novel's emotional climax: a man who was originally a coward (his own self-assessment from Chapter 23) choosing certain personal sacrifice for friendship. He begins a Petrovascope search using attitude-control thrusters as a Petrova-band flashlight.

## Why It Matters

This is the novel's defining moment — the act that transforms Grace from reluctant participant to active moral agent. The decision only works because the Beetle launch has decoupled his fate from Earth's. It also inverts his original arc: he was forced onto the mission against his will, and now he voluntarily gives up his return.
