# ECU microscopy reveals multiple life forms co-evolving with Astrophage at Adrian

## Context

Grace's ECU collected Astrophage from near Adrian, but the microscope reveals far more than expected. The sample teems with diverse organisms. This is the first direct evidence that Adrian hosts a full biological community, not just isolated Astrophage. Rocky's population-dynamics reasoning — that where there is prey, there must be predators — aligns immediately.

## Why It Matters

The ECU microscopy result is the observational foundation for the entire Taumoeba arc. Without this evidence, the predator hypothesis would remain speculative. The diversity of organisms also supports the homeworld conclusion. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the full predator characterisation and [[Astrophage Life Cycle and Migration]] for the ecological implications.
