# Nitrogen-flood containment protocol sterilises the Hail Mary after a second outbreak

## Context

The second outbreak (detected at the end of Chapter 25 by smell) triggers a rapid, decisive containment response. Grace suits up in the Orlan EVA suit, vents the atmosphere, floods with nitrogen, and then systematically isolates every Taumoeba source in sealed plastic bins pressurised with nitrogen. The overpressure creates instrument-free monitoring: a deflating bin indicates a leaking farm.

## Why It Matters

The nitrogen-flood protocol demonstrates Grace's growth as an operator — compare his decisive, procedurally correct response here with the improvisational scramble of Chapter 21. It also applies the Chapter 23 discovery (nitrogen lethality) as a practical containment tool. The bin-isolation technique directly enables the Chapter 27 investigation that reveals xenonite permeability. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the scientific context and [[Hail Mary Ship Design and Systems]] for the ship systems involved.
