# Nitrogen cannot penetrate xenonite because it moves stochastically while Taumoeba navigates directionally

## Context

In Chapter 27, Grace investigates why Taumoeba-82.5 can pass through xenonite walls while nitrogen — which is lethal to Taumoeba — cannot follow. His explanation: xenonite's molecular structure is a complex protein-and-chemical chain with a labyrinthine internal architecture. Nitrogen, as an inert gas, moves by entropy — random stochastic diffusion — and cannot navigate the molecular maze. Taumoeba-82.5, as a living organism with sensory-response capability, moves directionally through the same structure, actively navigating barriers the way any microbe navigates a physical environment. The distinction is between passive diffusion and active biological locomotion at the molecular scale.

## Why It Matters

This explanation is the scientific linchpin of the novel's climax. If nitrogen could follow Taumoeba through xenonite, the containment problem would solve itself (the Taumoeba would die inside the walls). Because it cannot, Rocky's all-xenonite ship is fatally vulnerable: Taumoeba can reach his fuel, but nitrogen cannot reach the Taumoeba. The transport-mechanism distinction is also the reason metal containment works — metal has no molecular-scale pathways for either nitrogen or Taumoeba to traverse. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the contamination arc and [[Xenonite - Eridian Structural Material]] for the material properties.
