# Extending centrifuge cables reduces centripetal force enabling movement under deceleration

## Context

In Chapter 19, Grace faces a crisis: Rocky is dying from oxygen exposure and needs immediate treatment, but the Hail Mary is under heavy deceleration that pins Grace to the floor. He cannot move, let alone perform surgery. His solution is to extend the centrifuge cable system from 20 metres to 75 metres radius. At the same rotational speed, the longer radius produces proportionally lower centripetal force at the crew compartment — reducing effective gravity roughly 14-fold and allowing Grace to move. The modification is improvised under extreme time pressure with Rocky's life at stake.

## Why It Matters

This scene demonstrates Grace's engineering resourcefulness at its sharpest: a physics relationship (centripetal force varies inversely with radius at constant angular momentum) applied as an emergency survival hack. It is also a rare moment where the novel's propulsion and life-support systems interact directly — the same artificial-gravity system that kept Grace alive during normal operations becomes an obstacle during crisis and must be reconfigured. See [[Artificial Gravity and Induced Torpor]] for the gravity system and [[Ryland Grace]] for Grace's problem-solving pattern.
