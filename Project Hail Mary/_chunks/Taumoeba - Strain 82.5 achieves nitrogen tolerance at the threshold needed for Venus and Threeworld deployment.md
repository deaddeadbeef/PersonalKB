# Strain 82.5 achieves nitrogen tolerance at the threshold needed for Venus and Threeworld deployment

## Context

After weeks of iterative breeding starting with Taumoeba-35 (Chapter 24 opening), the final tanks yield Taumoeba-80 and then Taumoeba-82.5. Both pass confirmation tests in exact Venus (3.5% N₂) and Threeworld (8.25% N₂) atmospheric simulations. Rocky celebrates in a ceremonial Eridian outfit with gems and gloves; Grace finds Ilyukhina's vodka and toasts 'To Taumoeba-82.5! Savior of two worlds!'

## Why It Matters

Taumoeba-82.5 is the specific strain that will be delivered to Earth via the Beetles and to Erid via Rocky. This is the novel's mission-success moment — the scientific objective is achieved. The celebration scene is also a key emotional beat for the Grace-Rocky partnership. See [[Taumoeba and the Biological Solution]] for the full Taumoeba profile.
