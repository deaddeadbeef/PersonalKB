# Xenonite-threat briefing transmits the permeability warning that enables Erid's response

## Context

In Chapter 30, immediately after Rocky boards Blip-A through the airlock tunnel and the reunion makes his injuries visible, Grace raises the problem Rocky did not know about: Taumoeba-82.5, the nitrogen-tolerant strain they engineered together, has an unintended property — it can permeate xenonite. Rocky's civilisation uses xenonite extensively. If Erid deploys Taumoeba-82.5 to control Astrophage around their planet without accounting for this flaw, the organism could enter xenonite-enclosed spaces (equipment, structures, vehicles) and infect or contaminate them. Rocky absorbs the briefing and immediately moves to the next problem — his characteristic engineering-first response — but the information has been transmitted.

## Why It Matters

The xenonite permeability problem was identified in Chapter 27 and is the reason Rocky departed in the first place. But Rocky left because the threat to Erid was urgent, not because he fully understood the mechanism. Grace's briefing in Chapter 30 closes the information gap: Rocky now has the complete technical picture needed to carry back to Erid. Without this handoff, the Epilogue's implied successful Eridian response — where Erid deploys Taumoeba without triggering a secondary xenonite crisis — would be causally unexplained. The briefing is a quiet but load-bearing narrative beat. See [[Taumoeba - Xenonite permeability is an unintended trait evolved during directed-evolution breeding in xenonite tanks]] for the permeability mechanism, and [[Resolution and Aftermath]] for the broader resolution arc.
