# Training a Helpful and Harmless Assistant with RLHF

## What Is This?
Anthropic's detailed study on using RLHF to train AI assistants that are both helpful and harmless, including preference data collection, reward modeling, and the tension between helpfulness and harmlessness objectives.

## Why It Matters
Provided the most thorough empirical analysis of the helpfulness-harmlessness trade-off in RLHF, and introduced the HH-RLHF dataset that became a standard benchmark. Shaped how the field thinks about alignment as a multi-objective optimization problem.
