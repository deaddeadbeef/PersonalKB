# Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena

## What Is This?
Introduces MT-Bench (a multi-turn benchmark with GPT-4 as judge) and Chatbot Arena (a crowdsourced live evaluation platform with Elo ratings), studying when and how LLM-based evaluation agrees with human preferences.

## Why It Matters
Established the two most widely used LLM evaluation methods: automated judge-based scoring and human preference Elo rankings. Showed that strong LLM judges agree with human evaluators >80% of the time, making scalable evaluation practical for model development.
