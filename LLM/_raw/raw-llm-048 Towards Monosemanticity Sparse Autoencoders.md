# Towards Monosemanticity: Sparse Autoencoders

## What Is This?
Uses sparse autoencoders to decompose MLP activations in a one-layer Transformer into interpretable, monosemantic features — individual directions in activation space that correspond to human-understandable concepts.

## Why It Matters
Provided the most compelling evidence that superposition (multiple concepts encoded in overlapping neurons) can be disentangled at scale. Opened a practical path toward mechanistic interpretability of large language models, enabling feature-level understanding of model behavior.
