# Universal Adversarial Attacks on Aligned LLMs

## What Is This?
Introduces the Greedy Coordinate Gradient (GCG) method for automatically generating adversarial suffixes that cause aligned LLMs to produce harmful content, demonstrating transferability across models including black-box systems.

## Why It Matters
Revealed a fundamental vulnerability in RLHF-aligned models: optimized adversarial suffixes can bypass safety training, and these attacks transfer across architectures. Sparked urgent research into robust alignment and adversarial defenses for deployed LLMs.
