# GPT-2: Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners

## What Is This?
Scaled GPT to 1.5 B parameters and demonstrated zero-shot task performance without any fine-tuning, introducing the WebText dataset for higher-quality pre-training data.

## Why It Matters
Showed that language models implicitly learn to perform tasks when trained on large, diverse corpora — the conceptual leap toward in-context learning and the scaling hypothesis that drives modern LLM development.
