# Gemini: A Family of Highly Capable Multimodal Models

## What Is This?
Google DeepMind's natively multimodal model family (Ultra, Pro, Nano) trained jointly on text, image, audio, and video, designed to process interleaved sequences of different modalities from the ground up.

## Why It Matters
Demonstrated that native multimodal pre-training (rather than bolting vision onto a language model) can achieve state-of-the-art across 30+ benchmarks in language, coding, reasoning, and multimodal understanding — the first credible GPT-4-class competitor on broad evaluations.
