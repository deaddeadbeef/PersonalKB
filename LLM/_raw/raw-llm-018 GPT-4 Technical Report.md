# GPT-4 Technical Report

## What Is This?
OpenAI's technical report on GPT-4, a large-scale multimodal model that accepts image and text inputs and produces text outputs, achieving human-level performance on many professional and academic benchmarks.

## Why It Matters
GPT-4 marked a step change in LLM capability, passing bar exams and medical licensing tests, and demonstrated that scaling plus RLHF alignment could produce commercially transformative AI systems.

## Key Takeaways
1. Multimodal: accepts both text and image inputs (vision capability)
2. Achieves 90th percentile on the Uniform Bar Exam and high scores on AP, GRE, and medical exams
3. Introduced a predictable scaling framework: small-model loss predicts large-model performance
4. Extensive safety work with RLHF and red-teaming to reduce harmful outputs
