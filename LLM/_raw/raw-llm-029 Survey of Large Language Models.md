# A Survey of Large Language Models

## What Is This?
A comprehensive survey covering the development, architecture, training, alignment, utilization, and evaluation of large language models, synthesizing findings from hundreds of papers into a structured taxonomy.

## Why It Matters
Serves as the most widely cited reference overview of the LLM field, providing a structured map of pre-training, adaptation, prompting, and evaluation that helps newcomers and practitioners navigate the rapidly evolving landscape.

## Key Takeaways
1. Taxonomy of LLM families: GPT, LLaMA, PaLM, BLOOM, and others with architecture comparison
2. Pre-training recipe analysis: data collection, tokenization, scaling laws, and training stability
3. Alignment techniques: SFT, RLHF, DPO, and constitutional methods compared systematically
4. Utilization strategies: in-context learning, chain-of-thought, tool use, and planning
