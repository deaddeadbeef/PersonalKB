# Red Teaming Language Models to Reduce Harms

## What Is This?
A systematic study of manual and automated red teaming techniques applied to language models, cataloging attack taxonomies, scaling behaviors of harmfulness, and the effectiveness of RLHF in reducing successful attacks.

## Why It Matters
Established red teaming as a structured safety practice for LLM deployment. Showed that larger models are harder to red-team after RLHF but more capable of generating harmful content before alignment — a critical finding for safety planning.
