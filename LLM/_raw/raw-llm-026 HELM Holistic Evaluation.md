# Holistic Evaluation of Language Models

## What Is This?
HELM is a comprehensive benchmarking framework that evaluates LLMs across 42 scenarios and 7 metrics (accuracy, calibration, robustness, fairness, bias, toxicity, efficiency), providing a multi-dimensional view of model quality.

## Why It Matters
HELM moved LLM evaluation beyond single-number leaderboards by systematically measuring dimensions that matter for deployment — fairness, toxicity, robustness — establishing a standard for responsible evaluation.

## Key Takeaways
1. Taxonomy: 42 scenarios spanning question answering, summarization, information retrieval, toxicity detection, and more
2. Seven metric categories ensure models are evaluated on safety and fairness, not just accuracy
3. Revealed that no single model dominates all scenarios — different models excel in different areas
4. Open framework enabling reproducible, standardized comparison of new models
