# Mistral 7B Apache 2.0 Release Catalyzed Open Ecosystem

Mistral 7B was released under the Apache 2.0 license in September 2023, making it one of the most permissively licensed high-quality language models available. Unlike Meta's LLaMA models which had usage restrictions, Apache 2.0 imposes no constraints on commercial deployment or derivative works. This permissive licensing, combined with strong benchmark performance at a deployable 7B size, catalyzed a wave of community fine-tunes (OpenHermes, Zephyr, etc.) and established Mistral AI as a major player in the open-weight ecosystem within months of the company's founding.
