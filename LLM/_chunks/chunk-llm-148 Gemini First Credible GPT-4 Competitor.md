# Gemini Broke OpenAI's Frontier Lead

When released in December 2023, Gemini Ultra was the first non-OpenAI model to credibly match or exceed GPT-4 across a broad range of language, code, and multimodal benchmarks. This ended OpenAI's approximately 12-month period of unchallenged frontier dominance (GPT-4 launched March 2023). The result validated Google DeepMind's strategy of native multimodal training and demonstrated that the frontier was becoming a multi-lab competition rather than a single-leader race.
