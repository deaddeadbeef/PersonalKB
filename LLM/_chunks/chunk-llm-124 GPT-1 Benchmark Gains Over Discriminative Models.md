# GPT-1 Outperformed Discriminative Baselines on 9 of 12 Tasks

GPT-1 achieved state-of-the-art results on 9 out of 12 evaluated NLP benchmarks, surpassing task-specific discriminative models despite using a single pre-trained architecture. These tasks included natural language inference (SNLI, MNLI, QNLI), question answering, commonsense reasoning, and semantic similarity. The strong transfer performance demonstrated that unsupervised generative pre-training produces representations that generalize broadly, validating the approach over purely supervised methods.
