# PaLM 540B Set SOTA on Majority of BIG-Bench Tasks

PaLM 540B achieved state-of-the-art results on the majority of BIG-Bench's 150+ diverse language tasks, outperforming the previous best model (Gopher 280B) on aggregate scores. The strongest improvements came on tasks requiring multi-step reasoning, where chain-of-thought prompting unlocked capabilities that flat prompting could not elicit. However, PaLM also revealed that some BIG-Bench tasks showed no improvement with scale, suggesting that certain capabilities may require different approaches beyond simply increasing model size.
