# Agentic AI Learning Should End In Self-Assessment And Proof Artifacts

## Context

The survey ends with a substantial assessment section whose questions progress from architecture foundations through RL algorithms, systems design, and agentic AI topics.

## Claim

A progressive LLM route should end in recall, explanation, and proof artifacts. Reading more pages is not the terminal state; being able to answer, build, evaluate, and defend the system is.

## Why It Matters

This supports the PersonalKB pattern of routing LLM study into oral defense, claim ledgers, local inference evidence, evaluation results, and capstone work.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: What should end an LLM learning stage? -> A: A named explanation, proof artifact, score, or missing-evidence row.
- Q: Why include quizzes or oral defenses in a wiki? -> A: They turn passive notes into retrieval practice and expose gaps before the knowledge is used in implementation decisions.
