# GPT-4 Reasoning Benchmark Performance

## Context
GPT-4 achieved unprecedented scores on professional and academic benchmarks. On the Uniform Bar Exam, it scored in the ~90th percentile of test takers, compared to GPT-3.5's ~10th percentile — a massive jump in legal reasoning capability. It scored 298/400 on the USMLE (medical licensing exam), passed multiple AP exams with 5s (the highest score), achieved high scores on GRE quantitative and verbal sections, and outperformed prior models on SAT math and reading.

The breadth of these results was as significant as any individual score. GPT-4 demonstrated strong performance across domains that require very different capabilities: legal reasoning, medical knowledge, mathematical problem-solving, reading comprehension, and scientific understanding. This generality suggested that scaling (in model size, data, and alignment) was producing genuinely improved reasoning rather than domain-specific pattern matching.

## Why It Matters
GPT-4's exam results were a watershed moment for public perception of AI capability. The bar exam result in particular — going from 10th to 90th percentile in one generation — illustrated the rapid pace of improvement. These benchmarks also established the evaluation paradigm of testing LLMs on professional exams, which became standard practice for all subsequent frontier model releases.
