# DeepSeek R1 Distilled 14B Model Outperforms o1-mini

## Context

DeepSeek released distilled versions of R1 at multiple scales (1.5B to 70B), trained on reasoning traces from the full R1 model.

## Claim

DeepSeek's 14B-parameter model distilled from R1 outperformed OpenAI's o1-mini on several reasoning benchmarks, demonstrating that reasoning capabilities transfer effectively to much smaller architectures.

## Why It Matters

This proved that frontier reasoning is not exclusive to massive models, making advanced AI reasoning accessible on consumer hardware and at dramatically lower cost.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: How small can a reasoning model be while remaining effective? → A: DeepSeek showed even 7B-parameter distilled models can outperform 70B base models on reasoning tasks.
- Q: What technique produced the distilled models? → A: Fine-tuning on (problem, reasoning trace, answer) triples generated by the full R1 model.
