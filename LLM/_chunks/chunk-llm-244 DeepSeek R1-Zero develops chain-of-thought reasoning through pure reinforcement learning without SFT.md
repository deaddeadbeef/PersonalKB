# DeepSeek R1-Zero Develops Chain-of-Thought Through Pure RL

## Context

DeepSeek AI trained R1-Zero using only reinforcement learning, without any supervised fine-tuning data. The model spontaneously developed structured reasoning behaviours.

## Claim

DeepSeek R1-Zero, trained with pure RL and no supervised fine-tuning, spontaneously developed chain-of-thought reasoning including self-verification and introspective "aha moments."

## Why It Matters

This demonstrated that structured reasoning can emerge from reward signal alone, without explicit human demonstrations of step-by-step thinking.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: What makes R1-Zero unique compared to other reasoning models? → A: It was trained with pure RL (no SFT), yet spontaneously developed chain-of-thought reasoning, self-verification, and reflective "aha moments."
- Q: Why is emergent reasoning from RL significant? → A: It shows reasoning is not merely imitation of human examples but can arise from optimising for correct answers.
