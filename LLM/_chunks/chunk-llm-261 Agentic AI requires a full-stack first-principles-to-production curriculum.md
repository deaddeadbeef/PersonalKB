# Agentic AI Requires A Full-Stack First-Principles-To-Production Curriculum

## Context

The survey introduces agentic AI as the end of a stack that begins with transformer architecture and runs through training algorithms, hardware and systems constraints, reasoning, evaluation, and deployment architecture.

## Claim

Agentic AI should be learned as a full-stack progression. A reader who jumps directly to agents, tools, or frameworks will miss the model, training, systems, and evaluation assumptions that make those layers work or fail.

## Why It Matters

This supports using a progressive route for the LLM wiki: first model mechanics, then training and systems, then post-training and reasoning, then evaluation, and only then agent loops and harnesses.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: Why should agentic AI not be the first LLM topic? -> A: Because agent behavior depends on model architecture, training, inference cost, context limits, evaluation, and tool/harness boundaries.
- Q: What is the main curriculum shape from the survey? -> A: First principles first, production systems last, with agentic AI revealed after the reader understands the underlying stack.
