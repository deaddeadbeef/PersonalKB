# Reasoning Distillation Trains Small Models on Teacher Reasoning Traces

## Context

Classical knowledge distillation transfers output distributions from large to small models. Reasoning distillation extends this by also transferring the step-by-step reasoning process.

## Claim

Reasoning distillation fine-tunes small models on (problem, reasoning trace, answer) triples from a larger teacher model, transferring reasoning as a learnable generation style rather than as factual knowledge.

## Why It Matters

This technique decouples reasoning capability from model scale, enabling deployment of reasoning-capable models on consumer hardware at a fraction of frontier model inference costs.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: What is the training data format for reasoning distillation? → A: (problem, reasoning trace, answer) triples where the reasoning trace is generated by the teacher model.
- Q: Why does reasoning transfer as a "style"? → A: The student learns patterns of decomposition, verification, and step-by-step analysis rather than memorising specific facts.
