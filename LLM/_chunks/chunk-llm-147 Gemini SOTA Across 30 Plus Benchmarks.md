# Gemini Ultra Set SOTA on 30 of 32 Benchmarks

Gemini Ultra achieved state-of-the-art results on 30 out of 32 evaluated benchmarks spanning text, code, math, and multimodal reasoning. It was the first model to exceed 90% accuracy on MMLU (90.04%), surpassing GPT-4's 86.4%. On HumanEval code generation it scored 74.4%, and on multimodal benchmarks like MMMU and MathVista it outperformed all prior models. This broad benchmark dominance established Gemini as the first credible GPT-4-class competitor across the full range of evaluation categories.
