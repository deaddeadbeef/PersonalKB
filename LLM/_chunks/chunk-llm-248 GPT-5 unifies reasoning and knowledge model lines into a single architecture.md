# GPT-5 Unifies Reasoning and Knowledge Model Lines

## Context

OpenAI previously maintained separate model lines: GPT-series for general knowledge and o-series for reasoning. This fragmented the developer experience and required choosing between capabilities.

## Claim

OpenAI's GPT-5 series unified the previously separate o-series (reasoning) and GPT-series (general knowledge) into a single architecture combining reasoning, coding, and multimodal capabilities.

## Why It Matters

Unification simplifies model selection for developers and demonstrates that reasoning and broad knowledge can coexist in a single model without trade-offs.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: Why did OpenAI unify the o-series and GPT-series? → A: To eliminate the need for developers to choose between reasoning capability (o-series) and general knowledge (GPT-series).
- Q: What variants exist in the GPT-5 family? → A: GPT-5.1 through 5.4 (flagships), 5.3-codex and 5.2-codex (code-specialised), 5.4-mini and 5-mini (cost-efficient).
