# DeBERTa Was First Model to Surpass Human on SuperGLUE

DeBERTa (specifically the 1.5B parameter DeBERTa-xxlarge variant) became the first model to surpass human-level performance on the SuperGLUE benchmark in January 2021, scoring 90.3 versus the human baseline of 89.8. SuperGLUE was specifically designed to be more challenging than GLUE, including tasks like causal reasoning and coreference resolution. This milestone marked a symbolic threshold for NLU capabilities, though it reflects benchmark-specific optimization rather than general human-level language understanding.
