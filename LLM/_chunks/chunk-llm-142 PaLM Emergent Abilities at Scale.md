# PaLM Demonstrated Emergent Abilities at 540B Scale

PaLM provided the strongest evidence at its time for emergent abilities in LLMs. On BIG-Bench tasks, certain capabilities appeared as discontinuous jumps: multi-step arithmetic reasoning, joke explanation, logical sequence completion, and cause-and-effect identification performed at near-random levels at the 8B and 62B scales but improved dramatically at 540B. These results fueled both the scaling hypothesis (that sufficient scale unlocks qualitatively new capabilities) and the ongoing debate about whether emergence is a real phenomenon or a measurement artifact.
