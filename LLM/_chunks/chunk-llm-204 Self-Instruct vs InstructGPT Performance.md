# Self-Instruct GPT-3 Nearly Matches InstructGPT Without Human Annotations

When GPT-3 (davinci) was fine-tuned on 52K self-generated instruction-output pairs using the Self-Instruct pipeline, it achieved instruction-following performance within 5% of InstructGPT (text-davinci-001) on the Super-NaturalInstructions benchmark. This was significant because InstructGPT relied on expensive, carefully curated human annotation while Self-Instruct used zero human-labeled instruction data beyond the 175 seed tasks.

The comparison validated that synthetic instruction data generated by the model itself could substitute for human annotations in many scenarios. This finding motivated the broader adoption of distillation-based and self-play approaches to alignment, where a strong teacher model generates training data for cheaper student models.
