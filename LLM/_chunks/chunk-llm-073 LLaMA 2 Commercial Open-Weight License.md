# LLaMA 2 Commercial Open-Weight License

## Context
LLaMA 1 was released under a research-only license, limiting its use to academic and non-commercial settings. LLaMA 2 made the landmark decision to include a commercial license (the "Meta Llama 2 Community License"), allowing businesses with fewer than 700 million monthly active users to deploy the model in production. This was the first time a competitive open-weight foundation model was available for commercial use.

The commercial license transformed the open-weight ecosystem from an academic curiosity into a viable business infrastructure. Companies could now build products on LLaMA 2 without negotiating API access from OpenAI or Google, fine-tune the model for their specific needs, and serve it on their own hardware for data privacy and cost control. This spawned an ecosystem of startups, fine-tuned variants, and specialized models built on the LLaMA 2 foundation.

## Why It Matters
The commercial license was arguably as impactful as the model itself. It established the template for open-weight commercial releases that Mistral, Qwen, Gemma, and subsequent LLaMA versions would follow. By enabling businesses to build on open models, Meta created a competitive alternative to proprietary API providers and accelerated the decentralization of AI capabilities.
