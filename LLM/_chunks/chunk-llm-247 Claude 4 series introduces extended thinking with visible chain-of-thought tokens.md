# Claude 4 Series Introduces Extended Thinking with Visible Chain-of-Thought

## Context

Anthropic's Claude model family evolved from Claude 3.5 Sonnet through the Claude 4 series, adding new reasoning and tool-use capabilities at each generation.

## Claim

The Claude 4 family (Sonnet 4, Opus 4.5, Opus 4.6) introduced extended thinking — visible chain-of-thought reasoning tokens — along with computer use capability and deep MCP integration.

## Why It Matters

Unlike o1's hidden reasoning, Claude's extended thinking is visible to users, enabling transparency into the reasoning process while achieving comparable quality improvements.

## QnA Seeds

- Q: How does Claude's extended thinking differ from o1's chain-of-thought? → A: Claude's reasoning tokens are visible to the user; o1's are hidden.
- Q: What other capabilities arrived with the Claude 4 series? → A: Computer use (screenshot-based GUI interaction) and deep MCP integration for tool connectivity.
